🗣️ Event Pitch Scripts for Jamie

Copy these or adapt to your style. The key: be casual, lead with the dog, mention "free" early.

🐕 The Dog Opener (Best for walking around)

"Oh my gosh, what a beautiful [breed]! What's their name? ... [Chat about dog] ... So random question — do you ever get behind on picking up the yard? We do pet waste cleanup — first time's totally free. Here's my card if you ever need us!"

🏠 The Homeowner Angle (For people without dogs present)

"Hey! Do you have dogs at home? We do pet waste removal around St. Louis — basically we come scoop your yard weekly so you don't have to deal with it. It's like having a lawn service but for the gross part. First cleanup is free!"

🐾 The Adopter Special (For adoption events)

"Congratulations on your new family member! We do pet waste removal — clean up the yard so you can focus on the fun stuff. We'd love to give you a free first cleanup as a welcome-home gift. Here's my card — just text me when you're settled in!"

🤝 The Business Partnership Pitch

"Hey, I'm Jamie with Tidy Tails — we do pet waste removal. I noticed your business attracts a lot of dog owners. Would you be open to keeping a stack of our cards at your counter? We can send customers your way too. Win-win!"

🏢 The Rescue/Shelter Partnership

"We love what you guys do for animals in St. Louis. We'd like to offer a free month of yard cleanup to anyone who adopts through your organization — kind of a welcome-home package. Could we leave some cards at your adoption desk?"

💡 Jamie's Event Rules

  • Lead with the dog, not the pitch. Pet the dog first. Ask its name. THEN mention Tidy Tails.
  • Say "free" early. "First cleanup is free" removes all pressure.
  • Hand the card, don't push. "Here's my card IF you ever need us" > "Can I sign you up?"
  • Don't sell, connect. These are future neighbors/friends, not leads.
  • Follow up within 48 hours. Anyone who texted or showed interest, follow up.
  • Post about it. Photo + "Had a blast at [event]!" in FB groups builds credibility.
