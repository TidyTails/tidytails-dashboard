🧠 Posting Strategy

🔑 The #1 Rule: Don't Be Salesy

  • Be a person, not a business. Community groups hate ads. Write like you're talking to a neighbor.
  • Lead with VALUE or HUMOR — the pitch comes second
  • Ask questions to drive comments ("Anyone else dealing with this?")
  • Respond to EVERY comment — engagement boosts visibility

📅 Timing Matters

  • Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (avoid Monday chaos & weekend noise)
  • Best times: 7-9 AM (morning scroll) or 7-9 PM (evening wind-down)
  • Worst time: Friday afternoon — everyone's checked out
  • Post ONE group per day max — don't spam 5 groups at once

🔄 Rotation Strategy

  • Week 1: Spring-themed posts (deep clean focus)
  • Week 2: Humor/relatable posts (engagement focus)
  • Week 3: Value/price posts (conversion focus)
  • Week 4: Photo/before-after posts (social proof)
  • Never post the same copy in the same group twice

💬 Nextdoor-Specific Tips

  • Use "Recommendation" format — it looks organic, not like an ad
  • Neighbors trust Nextdoor more than Facebook for local services
  • Keep it SHORT — Nextdoor posts do better under 100 words
  • Include your neighborhood name ("here in Florissant...")
  • Reply to anyone asking for pet service recommendations

🚫 What NOT to Do

  • ❌ Don't post the same thing in 5 groups in one day (looks spammy)
  • ❌ Don't use ALL CAPS or too many emojis (screams "ad")
  • ❌ Don't just post and disappear — reply to EVERY comment
  • ❌ Don't post before reading group rules (some require admin approval)
  • ❌ Don't argue with negative comments — just stay helpful

🎯 Responding to Comments — Scripts
