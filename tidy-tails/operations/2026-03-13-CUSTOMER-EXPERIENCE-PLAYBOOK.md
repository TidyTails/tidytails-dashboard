## 📋 THE SERVICE VISIT WORKFLOW

### PHASE 1: PRE-SERVICE (Morning of Service Day)

**Step 1: Day-Before Reminder (Optional — for new customers first 3 visits)**
> Send evening before, ~7 PM

```
Hey [NAME]! 👋 Just a heads up — Tidy Tails is scheduled for your yard tomorrow. Anything I should know? (gate code changes, new dog, etc.) See you then! - TJ
```

**Why:** Sets expectations, catches gate code changes before you're standing at a locked gate, shows professionalism.

---

**Step 2: On-My-Way Text**
> Send when you're ~15 min away

```
🐕 On my way to [ADDRESS/NAME]! Should be there in about 15 min. 
```

Short version for regulars:
```
🐕 Headed your way! ~15 min
```

**Why:** This is Doggy Doods' secret weapon. Customers LOVE knowing when you're coming. It also:
- Lets them put dogs inside if needed
- Shows you're reliable and organized
- Creates a touchpoint that builds the relationship
- Distinguishes you from every other service that just shows up randomly

**Pro Tip:** Save these as text shortcuts on your phone:
- `omw1` → full on-my-way message
- `omw2` → short version for regulars

---

### PHASE 2: DURING SERVICE

**Step 3: The Gate Photo**
> Take a quick photo of the yard BEFORE you start (if it's bad) and AFTER

**Before Photo:** Only take if the yard is notably messy — this becomes your "before" for marketing (with permission) and proof of work.

**After Photo:** Take EVERY time. Quick snap of a clean yard. This is your receipt.

**Photo Rules:**
- No identifiable personal items (kids toys, addresses visible)
- Focus on the GRASS looking clean
- Good lighting (natural daylight)
- Takes literally 5 seconds

**Why:** 
- Proof of service if there's ever a dispute
- Before/after content for social media (with permission)
- Shows the customer you take pride in your work
- Doggy Doods does this and their reviews mention it specifically

---

**Step 4: Quick Yard Scan**
> While scooping, notice things:

- Is the grass getting long? → Mention it casually
- New dog toys? → "Cute new toy, [DOG NAME] must love it"
- Fence damage? → "Hey, noticed a loose board on your back fence — just wanted to let you know"
- Holiday decorations? → "Love the setup!"

**Why:** This isn't snooping — it's being observant. Customers are amazed when their service provider notices things. It shows you care about their property, not just scooping shit.

---

### PHASE 3: POST-SERVICE (Immediately After)

**Step 5: Service Complete Text**
> Send within 5 minutes of finishing

**Standard (first month of service):**
```
✅ All clean! Your yard's looking great today. [DOG NAME] was [watching from the window/hanging out with me/being adorable as usual]. See you next [DAY]! 🐕
```

**Regular customers (after first month):**
```
✅ Done! Yard's clean. See you [DAY]! 🐕
```

**If you noticed something:**
```
✅ All clean! Quick heads up — noticed [observation]. Not a big deal but wanted to mention it. See you next [DAY]!
```

**With photo (occasional — don't overdo it):**
```
✅ All done! Here's how it looks 👇 See you [DAY]! 🐕
[attach after photo]
```

**Why:** This is the MOST important text you send. It:
- Confirms you actually showed up and did the work
- Personalizes the service (using dog's name)
- Creates a positive touchpoint right when they're thinking about you
- Makes them feel like they're getting premium service for $70/mo

**Pro Tip:** Text shortcuts again:
- `done1` → standard complete message
- `done2` → short version for regulars

---

### PHASE 4: FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCES

**Step 6: The 3-Visit Check-In**
> After the 3rd service visit, send:

```
Hey [NAME]! Just checking in — how's everything going with the service? Anything I can do differently? I want to make sure [DOG NAME]'s yard stays perfect. 🐕
```

**Why:** This is where most services lose people. They sign up, get 3-4 visits, and quietly cancel because of some small issue they never mentioned. This text catches problems BEFORE they become cancellations.

**If they respond with ANY feedback:** Fix it immediately and confirm: "Done! Thanks for telling me — I want this to be perfect for you."

---

**Step 7: The 1-Month Review Ask**
> After ~4 visits (1 month of service)

```
Hey [NAME]! Been a month of clean yards — hope [DOG NAME] is enjoying it! 😄

Quick favor — Google reviews are HUGE for small businesses like mine. If you've been happy with the service, would you mind leaving a quick one? Takes 30 seconds:

[GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Totally no pressure — I appreciate your business either way! 🙏
```

**Why:** By month 1, they've seen consistent service. They're most likely to leave a review when:
- They're satisfied (you just confirmed at visit 3)
- It's easy (direct link, not "go find us on Google")
- It feels personal (not an automated email)

**CRITICAL:** Only ask ONCE. If they don't do it, don't ask again for at least 3 months. Nothing kills goodwill faster than nagging for reviews.

---

**Step 8: The Referral Seed**
> After ~8 visits (2 months), when they respond to any text:

```
By the way — if you know anyone in [THEIR NEIGHBORHOOD] who could use the service, I'm running a deal where you both get $10 off your next month. Just have them mention your name! 🐕
```

**Why:** Referrals from existing customers convert 3-5x better than cold leads. But you have to:
- Wait until they're established (not during month 1)
- Make it beneficial for BOTH parties
- Keep it casual, not salesy
- Mention their specific neighborhood (creates urgency — "oh yeah, my neighbor has 3 dogs...")

---

### PHASE 5: RETENTION & DELIGHT

**Step 9: Seasonal Touchpoints**
> Send these at natural seasonal transitions:

**Spring (March):**
```
Hey [NAME]! Spring's here and [DOG NAME]'s yard is about to get a LOT more use. Want me to do a deep clean to kick off the season? I'll throw it in free for monthly customers. 🌱
```

**Summer (June):**
```
Hey [NAME]! Summer's heating up — just a heads up I'll be out a little earlier on [DAY]s to beat the heat. [DOG NAME] and I will both appreciate it! ☀️
```

**Fall (October):**
```
Hey [NAME]! Fall cleanup season — leaves + dog waste = not fun. I'll make sure to get everything even under the leaf piles. Your yard's in good hands! 🍂
```

**Winter (December):**
```
Hey [NAME]! Just a note — I'll still be out every [DAY] rain, snow, or shine. [DOG NAME]'s yard doesn't take holidays! Stay warm out there. ❄️
```

**Why:** These remind customers of your value at exactly the moments they might think "do I still need this?" The answer becomes obvious: yes.

---

**Step 10: The Anniversary Text**
> On their 1-year anniversary as a customer:

```
Hey [NAME]! Fun fact — today marks 1 YEAR of clean yards for [DOG NAME]! 🎉 That's roughly [52 x visits/week] visits and probably [estimate] lbs of... you know what. 😂 

Thanks for sticking with Tidy Tails — you're one of my OG customers and I genuinely appreciate it. Here's to year 2! 🐕🎉
```

**Why:** This is the Zappos/Chewy move. Nobody does this. It costs nothing and creates an emotional moment that makes cancellation feel like breaking up with a friend.
