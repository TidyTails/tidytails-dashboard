The Partnership Offer

You give them referral cards for their dog-owning customers. If their customer signs up, you send them $20 cash or a 10% referral fee on month 1. Everyone wins — their customer gets a cleaner yard, you get a new customer, and they get paid to recommend something their clients already need.

🎯 Why Lawn Care is the #1 Untapped Channel for Tidy Tails

  • Their customers are already verified dog owners. Every customer with a fenced yard and a lawn service is 70%+ likely to have a dog. Zero qualification needed.
  • They visit the same yards weekly. Lawn crews build TRUST with homeowners — a recommendation from them carries 5x more weight than a Nextdoor post.
  • No competitive conflict. Lawn care companies do not pick up dog waste. They hate mowing around it. We solve THEIR problem and THEIR customer's problem simultaneously.
  • Easy conversation starter: "Do you notice a lot of yards have dog waste still out when you mow? Our partner Tidy Tails handles that — want cards for your customers?"
  • They're already thinking about it. Every lawn crew in St. Louis has mowed around dog poop this week. They'll laugh and immediately understand the pitch.

🔑 The Hook That Closes the Conversation

Lead with THEIR pain, not your pitch:

  • "Does it ever come up — customers with dogs and the yard isn't cleared before you get there?"
  • Let them answer. They'll almost always say yes, or laugh.
  • Then: "We solve that. We clean dog waste weekly. Would you want cards to give those customers? If they sign up, you get $20."

📋 Two Referral Models — Pick One Per Partner

  • Model A — Card Drop: You leave 20 business cards with a rubber band. They put them in welcome packets or hand out when the topic comes up. Low commitment, passive.
  • Model B — Active Endorsement: They text their customers directly recommending Tidy Tails. You give them a custom referral link or code. Higher conversion, better tracking.
  • Best fit: Model A for first contact. If they convert a customer within 30 days, upgrade to Model B. Start frictionless, earn trust.

⚡ Why April is Perfect Timing

  • Spring mowing season JUST started. Every lawn crew in STL is back in yards this week.
  • Dog waste from the entire winter is surfacing NOW — lawn crews are literally mowing around it right now.
  • Homeowners are calling lawn companies for spring cleanup — same trigger moment for pet waste removal.
  • The "spring reveal" problem is at its PEAK — this pitch is more resonant in April than any other month.

🤝 Who to Target: Small Local Operators, Not Franchises

  • Best partners: Owner-operated lawn crews (1-3 trucks), local Facebook businesses, solo operators who give their personal number.
  • Why not franchises: TruGreen, Lawn Doctor etc. have corporate policies against third-party referrals. Local operators can say yes on the spot.
  • The sweet spot: Company with 80–300 residential lawn customers in overlapping territory. Enough scale to produce 3–8 referrals/year. Small enough to care about the $20/convert.
  • Bonus targets: Landscapers who work Chesterfield/Creve Coeur/Ladue — where clients pay $70/mo without blinking.

📈 Why This Channel Compounds

  • Zero ongoing effort. Once cards are dropped, the partner does the work passively. No weekly maintenance required.
  • Seasonal alignment. Lawn care season = spring–fall = exact same season when dog waste removal is most in-demand. The referral opportunity peaks at the same time you need customers.
  • Network effect. Partner A refers a customer who tells her neighbor who calls directly. Referral-sourced customers also refer at higher rates than cold-acquired customers.
  • Referral customers churn less. Customers who come through a trusted recommendation stay longer (avg 20–22 months vs 14–16 for cold leads). Higher LTV per customer.
