## WHY MOTHER'S DAY WORKS DIFFERENTLY THAN EVERY OTHER CAMPAIGN

Most of TJ's campaigns target the dog owner directly. Mother's Day flips that. The buyer is the **husband, partner, or adult child** — and the recipient is the dog mom.

This unlocks three things:

1. **New buyer demographic.** Husbands searching for gifts who have never seen a Tidy Tails ad. Zero ad fatigue. Fresh eyes on the offer.
2. **Zero price resistance.** The person writing the check doesn't do the chore. $70/month for "never have to watch her do that gross thing again" is an easy yes.
3. **Pre-sold on the outcome.** The gift buyer already knows the recipient hates cleaning up dog poop. They're not convincing them of a problem. They're just solving it.

Also true for she sheds: the husband watching his wife dream about a she shed for two years and finally doing something about it on Mother's Day is a genuinely motivated buyer. Low-pressure. High intent.

Campaigns begin ramping up April 14 (4 weeks out). Hard close April 28-May 4 (last 2 weeks). One final urgency push May 8-9.
