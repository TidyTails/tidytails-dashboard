## WHY THIS PACK MATTERS

The Mother's Day pack targets husbands buying for wives and adult kids buying for moms. The Father's Day pack flips the gender and context:

- **Primary buyer:** Wives, partners, adult children buying for the "dog dad" who handles the yard
- **Secondary buyer:** Dog dads giving themselves permission — "treat yourself" angle
- **Third segment:** Dads buying their kids or spouse a shed for Father's Day (TuffShed she shed overlap)

Father's Day is the second-highest gift-purchase moment for home service businesses after Mother's Day. The emotional register is different: less "she deserves a break" and more "he's handled it every weekend for years." The yard labor angle converts well — it's a concrete, recognizable chore. "He mows, he cleans up after the dog, he does everything" is a universal domestic truth.

**Key insight:** The Father's Day blog post exists at `/blog/fathers-day-gift-dog-owner-st-louis.html`. These ads drive traffic there and to direct conversion. The Mother's Day window just closed — buyers who converted their wives/moms are now warm to the service and may upgrade or refer a dad who needs it.
