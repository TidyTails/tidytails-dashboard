## How to Find Them (2 minutes)
1. Open Facebook → Search bar
2. Search: "[City Name] MO" → click "Groups" tab
3. Sort by member count (bigger = better)
4. Join 3-5 per city. Request to join, wait for approval.

## Tips
- **Join as Jamie** (personal account), not Tidy Tails business page
- **Engage first** — like/comment on a few posts before dropping your own
- **Check group rules** — some ban business posts, some have designated days
- **"Service Saturday" or "Business Wednesday"** — many groups have specific promo days
- **One post per group per week MAX** — don't get flagged as spam
- Groups with 1,000+ members = worth the effort
- Groups under 200 members = skip unless very local/active
