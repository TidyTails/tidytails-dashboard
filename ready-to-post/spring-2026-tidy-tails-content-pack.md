## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Photo > No photo, always.** Even a phone pic of a clean yard beats a text-only post
2. **Reply to every comment** within 1 hour if possible — algorithm loves engagement
3. **Don't delete and repost** — algorithm penalizes this
4. **In dog groups:** Don't hard sell. Share a helpful tip first, mention Tidy Tails naturally
5. **On Nextdoor:** Be neighborly first, businessy second. Nextdoor users hate blatant ads
6. **Time your posts:** Morning (7-9 AM) or evening (7-9 PM) get the most eyes
7. **Rotate groups:** Never post the same thing in the same group twice in a month
8. **Save the posts that perform well** — reuse the format with fresh copy next month
