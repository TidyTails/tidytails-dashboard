# Commercial Lead Finder

Find high-quality commercial leads for Tidy Tails pet waste removal.

## Before Starting Research

1. Check existing leads: `memory/leads-commercial.md`
2. Never contact the same business twice

## Target Business Types

### Tier 1 (Highest Value)
- **Property Management Companies** — manage multiple properties, one contract = many yards
- **Apartment Complexes** — dog-friendly apartments especially
- **HOAs** — common areas, community dog parks

### Tier 2 (Good Value)
- **Veterinary Clinics** — outdoor areas, referral potential
- **Dog Daycares** — heavy use areas
- **Pet Stores** — outdoor areas, referral potential
- **Dog Parks** — municipal contracts

### Tier 3 (Opportunistic)
- **Senior Living Facilities** — residents with pets
- **Churches with grounds** — community spaces
- **Schools with fields** — maintenance contracts

## Service Area

St. Louis County and surrounding:
- St. Charles County
- O'Fallon, St. Peters
- Ferguson, Florissant
- Maryland Heights, St. Ann
- Target: Middle-class neighborhoods

## Research Process

1. **Search** for businesses in target categories + St. Louis area
2. **Verify** they have outdoor spaces (Google Maps, website photos)
3. **Find contact** — decision maker name, email, phone
4. **Qualify** — are they dog-friendly? Multiple units/properties?
5. **Check duplicates** — search `memory/leads-commercial.md`
6. **Add to tracker** with all details

## Qualification Criteria

**Strong Lead:**
- Has outdoor common areas
- Dog-friendly property/policy
- Multiple units or locations
- Decision maker identifiable
- In service area

**Weak Lead (skip):**
- No outdoor areas
- Single small property
- No pet policy visible
- Outside service area
