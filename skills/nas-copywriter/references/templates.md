# Copywriting Templates

## Headline Formulas

### Benefit Headlines
```
Get [Desirable Result] Without [Undesirable Thing]
The [Number] Secrets to [Outcome] That [Experts] Don't Tell You
How [Ordinary Person] [Achieved Result] in [Timeframe]
[Number] Ways to [Outcome] (Even If [Common Objection])
Finally, A [Product] That [Addresses Pain Point]
```

### Curiosity Headlines
```
What [Authority] Knows About [Topic] That You Don't
The [Adjective] Truth About [Topic]
Why [Common Belief] Is Costing You [Loss]
[Number] [Things] You Never Knew About [Topic]
The Hidden [Problem/Opportunity] in Your [Situation]
```

### Question Headlines
```
Do You Make These [Number] Mistakes in [Activity]?
Are You [Undesirable State]? Here's Why.
What Would You Do With [Benefit]?
Tired of [Problem]?
Why Do [Group] [Get Result] While Others Don't?
```

### Command Headlines
```
Stop [Bad Action] and Start [Good Action]
Don't [Action] Until You Read This
Give Me [Timeframe] and I'll Give You [Result]
Try [Product] Free for [Timeframe]
Discover [Benefit] in [Timeframe]
```

### Story Headlines
```
They Laughed When [I Did X] — But When [Result Happened]...
How a [Ordinary Person] [Achieved Extraordinary Result]
The [Time] I [Mistake/Discovery] Changed Everything
From [Bad State] to [Good State]: A [Story]
I Never Thought [Outcome] Until [Discovery]
```

## Body Copy Frameworks

### PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)

```
PROBLEM:
You know that [problem]? The one where [specific symptom]?

AGITATE:
It gets worse. Every day you [don't solve it], [negative consequence].
[Paint the pain vividly.] [Make it personal.] [Show the cost of inaction.]

SOLVE:
That's exactly why [Product/Service] exists.
[Explain how it solves the problem.]
[Specific benefit 1]
[Specific benefit 2]
[Specific benefit 3]

[Call to Action]
```

### AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)

```
ATTENTION:
[Headline/Hook that stops the scroll]

INTEREST:
Here's the thing: [Interesting fact or insight about their situation]
[Build on the hook with relevant information]
[Make them curious about the solution]

DESIRE:
Imagine [desirable outcome].
[Feature → Benefit 1]
[Feature → Benefit 2]
[Proof: testimonial, statistic, demonstration]
[More proof]

ACTION:
[Clear, specific call to action]
[Remove friction: "It only takes X minutes"]
[Add urgency if real: "Until [date]"]
```

### 4 Ps (Picture-Promise-Prove-Push)

```
PICTURE:
[Paint a vivid picture of life with the benefit]
Imagine waking up to [positive scenario].
[Sensory details that make it real]

PROMISE:
That's exactly what [Product/Service] delivers.
[Core promise in one clear sentence]

PROVE:
Don't take our word for it:
- [Testimonial or case study]
- [Statistic or data point]
- [Credential or recognition]

PUSH:
[Call to action with urgency]
[Risk reversal: guarantee, free trial, etc.]
[Make it easy: "Just [simple action]"]
```

### Before-After-Bridge

```
BEFORE:
Right now, you're [current painful state].
[Details about their current situation]
[Empathize with their frustration]

AFTER:
But imagine [transformed state].
[Details about their life after]
[Paint the contrast clearly]

BRIDGE:
[Product/Service] is the bridge from here to there.
[How it works]
[Why it's different]
[Call to action]
```

## Email Templates

### Cold Outreach (B2B)

```
Subject: [Specific benefit] for [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific observation about their business].

[One sentence about the problem this creates]

We help [similar companies] [achieve result]. For example, [brief proof point].

Would it be worth a 15-minute call to see if this makes sense for [Company]?

[Signature]

P.S. [Secondary benefit or social proof]
```

### Follow-Up Sequence

**Email 2 (3 days later):**
```
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

[Name],

Quick follow-up on my last note about [benefit].

[One new angle or piece of value]

Worth a quick conversation?

[Signature]
```

**Email 3 (5 days later):**
```
Subject: [Curiosity hook]

[Name],

I wanted to share something [relevant/interesting]: [insight or case study]

If [problem they have] is on your radar, I think [solution] could help.

Open to chatting?

[Signature]
```

**Email 4 (7 days later):**
```
Subject: Closing the loop

[Name],

I don't want to be a pest, so I'll make this my last note for now.

If [solving problem] becomes a priority, my info is below.

All the best,
[Signature]
```

### Promotional Email

```
Subject: [Benefit + Urgency if real]

Hey [Name],

[Hook: Question or bold statement]

[1-2 sentences on the problem/opportunity]

Here's what we've got:

[Offer details]
✅ [Benefit 1]
✅ [Benefit 2]  
✅ [Benefit 3]

[Price/deal if relevant]

[Call to action button/link]

[Urgency: deadline or scarcity if real]

[Signature]

P.S. [Restate key benefit or deadline]
```

## Landing Page Structure

```
ABOVE THE FOLD:
- Headline (main benefit)
- Subhead (supporting detail or how)
- Hero image or video
- Primary CTA button

SOCIAL PROOF BAR:
- Logos, review counts, trust badges

PROBLEM SECTION:
- Agitate the pain
- Show you understand

SOLUTION SECTION:
- Introduce your product/service
- Key benefits (3-5 max)
- How it works (3 simple steps)

PROOF SECTION:
- Testimonials with photos
- Case studies with numbers
- Before/after if applicable

OFFER SECTION:
- Clear pricing
- What's included
- Comparison if helpful

RISK REVERSAL:
- Guarantee
- Free trial
- Easy cancellation

FINAL CTA:
- Repeat the offer
- Urgency if real
- Make it easy

FAQ:
- Handle objections
- 5-7 common questions
```

## Quick Reference: Power Words

**Urgency:** Now, Today, Limited, Last chance, Deadline, Instant, Fast
**Exclusivity:** Secret, Insider, Private, Members-only, VIP
**Value:** Free, Save, Discount, Bonus, Extra, Guaranteed
**Trust:** Proven, Results, Tested, Certified, Guaranteed, Risk-free
**Curiosity:** Discover, Reveal, Secret, Hidden, Truth, Finally
**Emotion:** Love, Hate, Fear, Happy, Frustrated, Excited, Worried

---

## Quick Reference: CTA Phrases

**Soft:** Learn more, Find out how, See details, Discover
**Medium:** Get started, Try it now, Claim your spot, Reserve yours
**Hard:** Buy now, Sign up today, Call now, Start immediately
**Local:** Text/Call [number], DM me, Drop a comment, Stop by
