# Pitch Scoring Rubric — Detailed Criteria

## How to Use

Listen to/read the pitch. Score each dimension 0-10. Use the criteria below for calibration.

---

## 1. Hook/Opening (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Did they grab attention in the first 30 seconds?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Unforgettable opening. Story, stat, or statement that makes you lean in. |
| 7-8 | Strong opening. Clear, interesting, makes you want to hear more. |
| 5-6 | Adequate. Gets to the point but doesn't excite. |
| 3-4 | Weak. Boring intro, too much throat-clearing, "Um, so, we're building..." |
| 0-2 | No hook. Started with company name and features. Forgettable. |

**Best practice:** Open with the problem (visceral), a surprising stat, or a bold claim.

---

## 2. Problem Clarity (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Is the pain obvious and urgent?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Problem is visceral. You FEEL it. Real examples, real stakes. |
| 7-8 | Problem is clear and believable. Good supporting evidence. |
| 5-6 | Problem stated but generic. "Businesses struggle with X." |
| 3-4 | Problem is vague or unclear. Hard to understand who has this pain. |
| 0-2 | No clear problem. Solution in search of a problem. |

**Best practice:** Make the listener nod. Use specific examples, quotes, or scenarios.

---

## 3. Solution Fit (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Does the solution clearly solve the stated problem?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Perfect fit. Solution directly addresses every pain point mentioned. |
| 7-8 | Good fit. Clear connection between problem and solution. |
| 5-6 | Partial fit. Solves some of the problem, gaps remain. |
| 3-4 | Weak fit. Solution seems disconnected from stated problem. |
| 0-2 | No fit. Solution doesn't address the problem at all. |

**Best practice:** Mirror the problem structure. "You said X hurts. We do Y. Here's how Y fixes X."

---

## 4. Why Now (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Is there urgency/timing that makes this moment right?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Compelling timing. Market shift, technology unlock, regulation change. |
| 7-8 | Good timing story. Clear reason this couldn't work 5 years ago. |
| 5-6 | Some timing mentioned but not convincing. |
| 3-4 | No timing story. Could have been pitched anytime. |
| 0-2 | Timing works against them. Too early or too late. |

**Best practice:** "Three things changed: [trend], [technology], [behavior]. This creates a window."

---

## 5. Market Size (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Are TAM/SAM/SOM clear and believable?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Crisp market sizing. Bottom-up math. Believable path to $100M+. |
| 7-8 | Good market story. Numbers make sense. Clear target segment. |
| 5-6 | Market mentioned but hand-wavy. "It's a $50B market." |
| 3-4 | Unrealistic numbers or no segmentation. |
| 0-2 | No market sizing. Or clearly made up. |

**Best practice:** Bottom-up: "X customers × $Y/year = $Z TAM. We're targeting [segment] = $W SAM."

---

## 6. Traction/Proof (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Do they show evidence this works?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Strong traction. Revenue, customers, growth rate, retention. |
| 7-8 | Good early signals. Pilots, LOIs, waitlist, clear momentum. |
| 5-6 | Some proof. A few customers or successful tests. |
| 3-4 | Weak proof. "We talked to people and they said they'd buy." |
| 0-2 | No proof. Pure idea stage with nothing validated. |

**Best practice:** Show the hockey stick. Or: "We launched X weeks ago. Here's what happened."

---

## 7. Business Model (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Is it clear how they make money?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Crystal clear. Pricing, unit economics, margins all explained. |
| 7-8 | Good clarity. Revenue model clear, some unit economics. |
| 5-6 | Model mentioned but details fuzzy. |
| 3-4 | Vague. "We'll monetize through subscriptions or ads or something." |
| 0-2 | No business model. Or clearly unsustainable. |

**Best practice:** "We charge $X. It costs us $Y to deliver. Margin is Z%. Here's how that scales."

---

## 8. Team Credibility (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Do they seem like the right people?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Exceptional fit. Domain expertise, track record, unfair advantages. |
| 7-8 | Good fit. Relevant experience, credible backgrounds. |
| 5-6 | Adequate. Some relevant experience but not compelling. |
| 3-4 | Weak fit. Team doesn't obviously match the problem. |
| 0-2 | Red flags. No relevant experience. Or solo with complex problem. |

**Best practice:** "I spent X years doing Y. I saw this problem firsthand. I'm the right person because..."

---

## 9. Ask Clarity (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Is it clear what they want?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Crystal clear ask. Amount, use of funds, milestones, timeline. |
| 7-8 | Good ask. Clear amount and general use. |
| 5-6 | Ask mentioned but vague. "We're raising a seed round." |
| 3-4 | Unclear ask. Not sure what they want or why. |
| 0-2 | No ask. Or ask doesn't match the stage/traction. |

**Best practice:** "We're raising $X to achieve [milestone] in [timeframe]. Here's how we'll use it."

---

## 10. Confidence/Delivery (0-10)

**What to evaluate:** Do they sound like they believe it?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Commanding presence. Confident, clear, passionate but not manic. |
| 7-8 | Good delivery. Engaging, knows the material, good pace. |
| 5-6 | Adequate. Gets through it but not memorable. |
| 3-4 | Nervous or uncertain. Lots of filler words, unclear statements. |
| 0-2 | Poor delivery. Reading slides, monotone, or overly salesy. |

**Best practice:** Practice until you can deliver without notes. Vary pace. Pause for effect.

---

## Scoring Summary

| Total Score | Verdict |
|-------------|---------|
| 85-100 | **Ship It** — Ready for real pitches |
| 70-84 | **Almost There** — Polish the weak spots |
| 50-69 | **Needs Work** — Significant revisions needed |
| Below 50 | **Major Revision** — Rethink structure and story |

---

## After Scoring

1. Identify the **3 lowest-scoring dimensions** — these are priorities
2. Write **specific rewrites** for weak sections
3. Generate **3-5 hard questions** they should prepare for
4. Provide **concrete next actions** for improvement
