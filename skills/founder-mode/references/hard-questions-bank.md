# Hard Questions Bank — 50+ Investor/Advisor Questions

## How to Use

After reviewing a pitch or business plan:
1. Select 5-10 relevant questions from below
2. Simulate how the founder would answer
3. Identify gaps in their preparation
4. Provide suggested answers for weak spots

---

## Business Model Questions

1. **"Why won't [big incumbent] just do this themselves?"**
   - They're looking for: Defensibility, timing, focus argument

2. **"What's your moat? What stops someone from copying this tomorrow?"**
   - They're looking for: Network effects, data, switching costs, brand, expertise

3. **"How do you make money?"**
   - They're looking for: Clear revenue model, not hand-waving

4. **"What are your unit economics?"**
   - They're looking for: CAC, LTV, margin, payback period

5. **"How does this scale? Does it get easier or harder at 10x?"**
   - They're looking for: Operating leverage, not linear scaling

6. **"What's your pricing strategy? Why those numbers?"**
   - They're looking for: Value-based pricing logic, competitive positioning

7. **"Who pays? Who decides? Are they the same person?"**
   - They're looking for: Understanding of the sales motion

---

## Market Questions

8. **"How big is the market, really?"**
   - They're looking for: Bottom-up math, not top-down fantasy

9. **"Who's your customer? Be specific."**
   - They're looking for: Clear ICP, not "everyone"

10. **"What if this market doesn't grow as fast as you think?"**
    - They're looking for: Adjacent markets, pivot options, resilience

11. **"Who else is trying to solve this? Who's your real competition?"**
    - They're looking for: Competitive awareness, including non-obvious alternatives

12. **"Why hasn't anyone solved this already?"**
    - They're looking for: Market insight, timing story

13. **"What's the wedge? How do you get in?"**
    - They're looking for: Go-to-market strategy, beachhead

---

## Traction Questions

14. **"How do you get your first 10 customers?"**
    - They're looking for: Concrete GTM, not "marketing"

15. **"What's your sales cycle? How long from first touch to close?"**
    - They're looking for: Realistic timeline, pipeline management

16. **"What does your retention look like?"**
    - They're looking for: Churn rate, NRR, cohort analysis

17. **"Show me the growth curve. What's driving it?"**
    - They're looking for: Understanding of growth levers

18. **"What feedback are you getting from customers?"**
    - They're looking for: Customer voice, iteration, learning

19. **"What's your conversion rate at each stage?"**
    - They're looking for: Funnel metrics, bottleneck awareness

---

## Team Questions

20. **"Why are YOU the right person to build this?"**
    - They're looking for: Founder-market fit, unfair advantages

21. **"What's your unfair advantage?"**
    - They're looking for: Something competitors can't replicate

22. **"How did you meet your co-founder? How do you resolve disagreements?"**
    - They're looking for: Team dynamics, conflict resolution

23. **"What happens if you get hit by a bus?"**
    - They're looking for: Key-person risk, team depth

24. **"Who's the first hire after this raise?"**
    - They're looking for: Resource allocation, priorities

25. **"What are you bad at? How do you compensate?"**
    - They're looking for: Self-awareness, humility

## Financial Questions

    - They're looking for: Cash management, runway awareness

27. **"How long does this money last?"**
    - They're looking for: Runway calculation, milestone planning

28. **"When do you need to raise again?"**
    - They're looking for: Fundraising strategy, dilution awareness

29. **"What milestones does this money unlock?"**
    - They're looking for: Clear use of funds tied to value creation

30. **"What's your path to profitability?"**
    - They're looking for: Business sustainability, not perpetual fundraising

31. **"What assumptions are baked into your projections?"**
    - They're looking for: Intellectual honesty, scenario awareness

32. **"What if you only raise half?"**
    - They're looking for: Flexibility, prioritization skills

    - They're looking for: Risk awareness, honesty

34. **"What's the biggest risk you're NOT worried about but should be?"**

35. **"What assumption, if wrong, makes this worthless?"**

## Investor-Specific Questions

46. **"What do you need from investors besides money?"**

47. **"How did you arrive at this valuation?"**
    - They're looking for: Reasonable comp analysis, not ego pricing

48. **"Who else is in this round?"**
    - They're looking for: Social proof, round dynamics

49. **"What's been the hardest part so far?"**
    - They're looking for: Authenticity, resilience

50. **"What keeps you up at night?"**
    - They're looking for: Honesty, self-awareness

## Gotcha Questions (Designed to Trip You Up)

51. **"Tell me something that's true that almost nobody agrees with you on."**
    - (Thiel's classic — looking for contrarian insight)

52. **"If you had to bet your entire net worth on one thing going right, what is it?"**
    - (Forcing prioritization)

53. **"Walk me through a deal you lost. Why?"**
    - (Looking for learning orientation)

54. **"What's a mistake you made and how did you fix it?"**
    - (Self-awareness, growth mindset)

55. **"Tell me about a time you changed your mind about something important."**
    - (Intellectual flexibility)

## How to Prepare

For each question you might get:

1. **Write out your answer** (not bullet points — full sentences)
2. **Time yourself** — most answers should be 30-60 seconds
3. **Practice out loud** — hearing yourself helps
4. **Get someone to grill you** — simulate the pressure
5. **Update after every pitch** — add questions you got, improve answers

---

## Red Flag Answers to Avoid

❌ "We have no competition"
❌ "Our technology is 10x better"
❌ "We just need 1% of the market"
❌ "We're the Uber of X"
❌ "The product sells itself"
❌ "We're first movers"
❌ "Our projections are conservative"
❌ "We're a family"
❌ "VCs don't understand our space"
