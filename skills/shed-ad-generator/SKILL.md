# Shed Ad Generator

Generate shed ads that sound exactly like TJ wrote them.

## Before Writing Any Ad

1. Read the style guide: `memory/tj-ad-style-guide.md`
2. Check ad archive to avoid repeats: `memory/ad-copy-archive.md`
3. Scrape current promos: https://www.tuffshed.com/special-offers/

## TJ's Voice (Summary)

- Conversational, neighborly, NOT salesy
- Uses humor ("gossip at a hair salon", "haven't parked since '22")
- Checklist format with ✅ emojis
- Always includes: FREE delivery, financing options, "No pressure"

## Ad Structures

### PSA/Warning Hook
```
PSA: If you haven't parked in your garage since '22...
...it's time for a shed.
✅ Rent-to-own ✅ Traditional financing ✅ FREE delivery
No judgment—just neighborly help.
```

### Side Effects Humor
```
Warning: Side effects of our sheds may include:
✔ More space
✔ Less clutter
✔ Jealous neighbors
💥 FREE delivery
No pressure, but these are moving fast 💇
```

### Pain Point → Solution
```
Every year we add more stuff:
Holiday décor, Lawn tools, Sports gear, Kids toys

But the house doesn't grow.

A shed keeps clutter OUT of your home.
More space = less stress.
```

### "Not Just Storage" Reframe
```
A shed isn't just storage.

It can be:
✓ workshop
✓ home gym
✓ office
✓ man cave

Way cheaper than adding onto your house.
```

## Marketplace Listing Format

**Title:** [Size] + [Product Type] + [$XX/mo]
Example: "10x16 Utility Shed $208/mo"

**Price:** Full price (not monthly)

**Category:** Storage & Organization OR Outdoor Storage Boxes

**Description:** Hook + benefits checklist + soft CTA
