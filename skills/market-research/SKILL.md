# Market Research

Deep research on competitors, markets, and opportunities.

## Competitor Categories

### Pet Waste (Tidy Tails Competitors)
Research any pet waste removal service in St. Louis area:
- Pricing models
- Service areas
- Marketing tactics
- Website quality
- Reviews/reputation
- Weaknesses to exploit

### Sheds (TJ's Competitors)
Research any shed company that is NOT TuffShed or WoodMaster:
- TJ works FOR TuffShed and WoodMaster
- Anyone else selling sheds = competitor
- Look for pricing, tactics, weaknesses

## Research Framework

### 1. Identify Competitors
```
Search: "[service type] [location]"
Examples:
- "pet waste removal St. Louis"
- "poop scooping service St. Charles"
- "shed dealers St. Louis" (exclude TuffShed, WoodMaster)
```

### 2. Analyze Each Competitor

**Basic Info:**
- Company name
- Website URL
- Service area
- Years in business

**Pricing:**
- Weekly/biweekly/monthly rates
- One-time clean prices
- Commercial pricing (if visible)

**Marketing:**
- What platforms are they on?
- What's their messaging?
- What hooks do they use?
- Reviews (Google, Yelp, Facebook)

**Strengths:**
- What are they doing well?
- What can we learn?

**Weaknesses:**
- Where are they falling short?
- What gaps can we fill?
- Bad reviews — what complaints?

### 3. Synthesize Insights

After researching, answer:
1. What's the price range in this market?
2. What messaging is working?
3. What's missing that we could offer?
4. Where are competitors weak?
5. What differentiates Tidy Tails/TJ?
