# Morning Briefing Generator

Create TJ's daily morning briefing — actionable, categorized, value-packed.

## Briefing Structure

All briefings MUST be categorized by industry:
1. 🐕 DOG WASTE (Tidy Tails)
2. 🏠 SHEDS (TuffShed/WoodMaster)
3. ⚙️ SYSTEMS
4. 💡 NEWS & IDEAS

## Data Sources

### Tidy Tails
- Dashboard task board: `dashboard/TASKS.md`
- Operations: `memory/tidy-tails-operations.md`
- Lead tracker: `memory/leads-commercial.md`
- Goal: 25-30 customers by August 2025 (currently ~5)

### TuffShed/WoodMaster
- Pipeline: `memory/tuffshed.md` (12 quoted, 15 pre-quote)
- Current promos: https://www.tuffshed.com/special-offers/
- TJ's Salesforce notes (if available)

### News
Search for 1 relevant story:
- AI and automation
- Small business growth
- Sales techniques
- Pet industry trends
- Shed/outdoor living trends
- Entrepreneurship

### Business Ideas
Generate 2 realistic business ideas TJ could start:
- Leverage existing skills (sales, marketing, local service)
- Low startup cost
- Can run alongside current work
- St. Louis market opportunity

### Self-Improvement Tasks
Identify 4 tasks Franklin could handle but isn't yet:
- Look at gaps in current automation
- Manual work that could be systematized
- Research that would help the business
- Tools/integrations that could help
