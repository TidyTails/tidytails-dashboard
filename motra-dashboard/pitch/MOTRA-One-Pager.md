## The Opportunity

Waymo just hit **400,000 rides/week** and is targeting **1 million/week by end of 2026**. They raised **$16 billion** at a **$126 billion valuation**. The robotaxi industry has reached escape velocity.

But here's the hidden cost: **every 100 robotaxis requires 80-120 operations staff at physical depots.** Vehicles return to warehouses just to get wiped down, charged, and inspected. At scale, this is the #1 operational expense — and it doesn't scale.

---

## The Solution

**MOTRA replaces 40-50% of robotaxi depot operations with mobile, gig-powered fleet care.**

We deploy certified mobile technicians directly to vehicles — wherever they are — for turnaround cleaning, preventive maintenance, and emergency response.

## Depot Replacement Economics

**Current depot cost per 100 vehicles:** $4.3-7.4M/year
- Staff (80-120 @ ~$45K avg): $3.6-5.4M
- Facility: $500K-1.5M
- Equipment: $200-500K

**MOTRA replaces 40-50% of depot functions mobile:**
- Staff reduction: 40-55 roles = $1.8-2.5M saved
- Smaller facility needed = $200-600K saved
- Recovered ride revenue (less downtime) = $300-400K
- **Operator saves $2.3-3.5M/year per 100 vehicles**

**MOTRA's cost to operator:** ~$1.5-2M/year
**Net savings:** $800K-1.5M/year per 100 vehicles
