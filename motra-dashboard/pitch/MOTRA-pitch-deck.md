# SLIDE 2: The Problem

## Robotaxi depots are the biggest hidden cost in autonomous vehicles.

**Every 100-vehicle fleet requires 80-120 operations staff at a physical depot.**

The 7 depot roles today:
1. Fleet Operations Manager
2. AV Technicians (sensors, calibration)
3. Fleet Maintenance Technicians (tires, brakes, HVAC)
4. **Vehicle Readiness / Turnaround Team (cleaning, charging, inspections)**
5. Remote Assistance Operators
6. Dispatch & Fleet Monitoring
7. Safety & Incident Response

**The pain:**
- 🏭 **Massive fixed cost** — Industrial real estate + 80-120 staff per 100 vehicles
- ⏱️ **Downtime = lost revenue** — Every minute at depot is a $30-40/hr ride not taken
- 🚗 **Vehicles drive themselves TO the depot** — Just to get wiped down
- 🧹 **Hand wash only** — Can't use car washes (destroys LiDAR and cameras)
- 📈 **Doesn't scale** — More rides = more wear, but depot capacity is fixed

**Waymo hand-washes every vehicle at a depot with 40+ workers. That doesn't scale to tens of thousands of vehicles.**

# APPENDIX: Depot Replacement Economics

## Why AV Operators Will Buy This

**Current depot cost per 100 vehicles:**
- Staff (80-120 @ ~$45K avg): $3.6-5.4M/year
- Facility (industrial real estate): $500K-1.5M/year
- Equipment & supplies: $200-500K/year
- **Total: $4.3-7.4M/year**

**With MOTRA replacing 40-50% of depot functions:**

- Recovered ride revenue (less downtime) = $300K-400K
- **Total savings: $2.3-3.5M/year per 100 vehicles**

**MOTRA's cost to operator for same services:** ~$1.5-2M/year
**Net savings to operator: $800K-1.5M/year per 100 vehicles**
