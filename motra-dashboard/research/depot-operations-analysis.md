# Robotaxi Depot Operations Analysis
**Date:** March 6, 2026
**Purpose:** Map which depot functions MOTRA can replace with mobile services

## The 7 Depot Roles in Robotaxi Fleet Operations

Robotaxi depots (fleet operations centers) employ 80-120 staff per 100 vehicles. These roles mirror airline ground operations.

### 1. Fleet Operations Manager
- Overall depot performance, availability targets, shift scheduling
- **MOTRA Play:** ❌ Internal role at AV company — not a MOTRA service
- **But:** Smaller operators may not need one if MOTRA handles turnaround/maintenance coordination

### 2. Autonomous Vehicle Technicians
- Specialized: LiDAR, cameras, radar, compute hardware, sensor calibration
- Requires precision alignment rigs, clean environments, $200K+ equipment
- **MOTRA Play:** ❌ Depot-locked — too specialized and equipment-intensive
- **Exception:** Basic sensor cleaning (wipe lenses, clear debris) = MOTRA Tier 1 add-on

### 3. Fleet Maintenance Technicians
- Traditional automotive: tires, brakes, suspension, HVAC, battery systems
- **MOTRA Play:** 🟡 PARTIAL
  - ✅ Mobile: Tires, fluid top-offs, HVAC filters, wiper blades, brake inspections
  - ❌ Depot: Brake jobs, suspension work, battery systems (need lifts)
- **MOTRA becomes the triage layer** — handles Tier 1 preventive maintenance mobile, flags Tier 2 issues for depot scheduling, reducing unnecessary depot trips

### 4. Vehicle Readiness / Turnaround Team — 🎯 MOTRA'S CORE
- Interior cleaning, charging/fueling, minor inspections, staging for dispatch
- Similar to aircraft turnaround crews
- **MOTRA Play:** ✅ 100% MOBILE
  - Gig workers in equipped vans doing turnarounds wherever the vehicle is
  - Parking lots, curbside, charging stations — no depot trip needed
  - This IS the MOTRA business model

### 5. Remote Assistance Operators
- Review camera feeds, provide tele-guidance when vehicle encounters unusual situations
- **Not a MOTRA service** — excluded from current scope

### 6. Dispatch & Fleet Monitoring
- Vehicle health monitoring, trip demand routing, maintenance coordination
- **Not a MOTRA service** — excluded from current scope

### 7. Safety & Incident Response Team
- Accident response, passenger issues, law enforcement, safety investigations
- **MOTRA Play:** ❌ Physical + institutional, requires on-site presence and depot data access

**~40-55 out of 80-120 staff functions can go mobile through MOTRA.**

## Staffing Ratios (Industry Estimates)

| Fleet Size | Total Ops Staff | MOTRA-Addressable |
|------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 100 vehicles | 80-120 staff | ~40-55 |
| 500 vehicles | 300-500 staff | ~150-250 |
| 1,000 vehicles | 600-900 staff | ~300-450 |

As autonomy improves, AV tech and remote ops ratios decrease — but turnaround/cleaning/maintenance **stays constant or increases** because more rides = more wear.

## The Key Insight

The big misconception: robotaxis eliminate human labor.
Reality: they shift labor from drivers → fleet operations and technical support.

**Largest cost categories today:**
1. Depot operations
2. Vehicle maintenance
3. Remote operations
4. Charging and cleaning

MOTRA targets #1, #2, and #4 directly.
