## 🎬 BASQUIAT VIDEO WORKFLOW (NEVER FORGET THIS)

**This is THE workflow for AI-generated video ads. Period.**

### The Pipeline
1. **Nano Banana 2** (fal.ai) → Generates high-quality image frames/stills from text prompts
2. **Kling v3 Standard** (fal.ai) → Animates the still image into a 5-second video

### How It Works
- Write a visual prompt → Nano Banana 2 creates the frame
- Feed that frame to Kling v3 with a motion prompt → Get a 5-sec animated video
- Stitch clips together + add text overlays for the final ad

### Can Also Use Real Photos
- Upload a real photo (like a backyard shot TJ takes) → Feed directly to Kling v3
- Kling animates the photo into cinematic motion (camera pan, zoom, etc.)

### Costs
- **Nano Banana 2 image:** ~$0.05-0.10 per image
- **Kling v3 Standard video (5 sec):** ~$0.50 per clip
- **Per finished ad (2-3 clips + frames):** ~$1.50-2.50
- **Platform:** fal.ai (needs credits loaded)

### Key Learnings from Mar 5, 2026
- ❌ Don't make grass change color (looks like landscaping ad)
- ✅ Keep grass SAME — only change is poop gone
- ❌ fal.ai burns credits fast with Kling — budget accordingly
