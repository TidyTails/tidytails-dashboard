## 🎯 Strategy 6: Price Increase for Existing Customers

### When to Raise Prices
**DO IT:** When you hit 15+ customers (late spring/early summer)
**DON'T DO IT:** Right now (8 customers, need to retain every one)

### How to Raise Prices (The Right Way)
1. **Give 30 days notice** — "Starting [date], our weekly service will be $75/mo (from $70)"
2. **Grandfather existing customers for 60 days** — Loyalty reward
3. **Add value first** — Before raising price, add deodorizing or text notifications as "free upgrades"
4. **Frame as industry adjustment** — "With rising fuel and supply costs, we're adjusting our pricing to ensure we can maintain the quality you expect"

### Suggested Timeline
| Milestone | Action | New Price |

| Hit 12 customers | Raise new customer price to $75/mo | New: $75, Existing: $70 |
