## 📋 Program Overview

**Program Name:** "Friends Don't Let Friends Step In It"
**Reward:** Both the referrer AND the new customer get **$10 off their next month**
**Why dual-sided:** People refer more when their friend also gets a deal. Removes the awkwardness of "selling" to a neighbor.

### The Math
- Current pricing: $70/month recurring
- Referral cost: $20 total ($10 each side)
- Customer LTV (6 months avg): $420
- **Cost to acquire via referral: $20 = 4.8% of LTV** ← insanely cheap
- Compare: Facebook ads = $30-80 per lead, maybe 20% close rate = $150-400 per customer
- **Referrals are 10-20x cheaper than paid ads**

### Why Now (Spring 2026)
- Spring = #1 season for new signups (winter thaw guilt)
- 8 recurring customers = 8 potential referral sources
- Neighbors see Jamie's truck → built-in social proof
- New customers who come via referral have **37% higher retention** (industry data)

## 🏷️ Tracking System (Simple — No Software Needed)

### Option A: Name Drop (Simplest)
New customer just says "Sarah referred me" when they text/call.
Jamie logs it in her phone notes or a simple spreadsheet.

### Option B: Google Sheet Tracker
| Date | Referrer | New Customer | Referrer Credit Applied? | New Customer Discount Applied? |
|------|----------|-------------|------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 3/25 | Sarah M. | Mike T.     | ✅ April bill           | ✅ First month                 |

### Tracking Notes
- Apply referrer's $10 credit on their NEXT invoice
- Apply new customer's $10 off their FIRST month
- No limit on referrals (Sarah could refer 5 people = $50 off over 5 months)
- Credits don't stack on single month (1 credit per billing cycle)
- **No expiration** — keep it simple, don't add fine print that kills momentum
