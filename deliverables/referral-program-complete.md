## The Program: "Friends Don't Let Friends Scoop"

### Double-Sided Reward Structure (Both Sides Win)

| Who | What They Get | When |
|-----|---------------|------|
| **Existing Customer** (referrer) | **1 FREE week of service** (~$25 value) | After referred friend's 2nd paid service |
| **New Customer** (referred friend) | **FREE first cleanup** ($25 value) + waived setup | When they sign up and mention referrer |

### Why This Structure Works:
1. **Double-sided = 2x motivation** — both parties benefit, both share the news
2. **Free week (not cash)** — keeps revenue in the business, feels more generous than $25 off
3. **Triggered after 2nd service** — ensures the new customer actually sticks (not a one-and-done)
4. **Free first cleanup for new customer** — removes the #1 barrier to trying a new service
5. **No cap on referrals** — customer refers 4 friends? They get a free MONTH. That's fine — 4 new recurring customers is worth $400+/month in revenue

### The Numbers:
- Cost per referral: ~$50 (one free week for referrer + one free cleanup for new customer)
- Value of one new recurring customer: **$100-300/month** (depending on plan)
- **ROI: 2x-6x return on EVERY successful referral**
- Break-even: New customer pays for the referral cost in their first month

## Common Objections (And How to Handle)

**"What if someone tries to game it?"**
At 4-5 customers, you know everyone by name. This isn't a tech company with fraud problems. Trust your people.

**"What if I can't afford the free weeks?"**
You only give free weeks AFTER getting a new paying customer. You're always net positive. One new weekly customer ($100/month) easily covers 4 free weeks of referral rewards ($100).

**"What if the referred person doesn't sign up?"**
No cost to you. The program only costs money when it works.

**"Should I do cash instead of free service?"**
No. Free service keeps money in the business and feels more generous. $25 cash feels cheap. "A free week of service" ($25 value) feels premium.
