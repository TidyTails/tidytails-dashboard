When a lead comes in (form, text, call, Nextdoor DM, whatever):
1. **Respond within 5 minutes** during business hours (8 AM - 7 PM)
2. **Respond within 1 hour** evenings/weekends
3. **Never let a lead sit overnight** without at least an acknowledgment

If Jamie can't take a call, TEXT BACK IMMEDIATELY. Speed = trust.

Track EVERY lead. Google Sheets works fine. One row per lead.

|------|------|--------|------|------|--------|--------|---------------|-------|
| 3/25 | Sarah M. | Nextdoor | Kirkwood | 2 | $25/wk | Quoted — waiting | 3/26 | Saw orphaned customer post |
| 3/25 | Mike R. | Website form | Chesterfield | 3 | $30/wk | Booked ✅ | — | Starts 3/29 |
| 3/24 | Lisa T. | Facebook group | Webster | 1 | $25/wk | No response | 3/27 (2nd follow-up) | Messaged back once then ghosted |

### Status Categories:
- **New** — Just came in, needs first response
- **Quoted** — Sent pricing, waiting for response
- **Follow-Up 1/2/3** — Number of follow-ups sent
- **Booked ✅** — Signed up!
- **Dead ❌** — Said no or unresponsive after 3 follow-ups
- **Future** — Interested but not ready yet (seasonal, moving, etc.)

## 🔄 THE FOLLOW-UP CADENCE

Every lead gets this exact sequence. No exceptions.

```
Day 0: First response (within 5 min) + quote
Day 1: First follow-up text (if no response)
Day 3: Second follow-up text
Day 7: Final follow-up text with spring special offer
Day 14: Move to "Future" bucket, add to spring email blast list
```

**5 touches minimum.** That's it. After 5 touches and no response, they go in the "future" bucket — not the trash. Re-engage them in 30 days with a seasonal offer.

## 🏆 OBJECTION HANDLERS

The 5 objections you'll hear every time, and how to handle them:

### "That's more than I expected"
> "Totally get it. Think of it this way — it's about $6 a week per visit. Less than a coffee. And you never have to think about the yard again. Most customers tell me it's the best $25 they spend all week."

### "I'll think about it"
> "No rush at all! I'll follow up in a couple days. One thing to keep in mind — spring is our busiest season and slots fill up fast. But no pressure, just don't want you to get stuck waiting."

### "My spouse needs to agree"
> "Makes sense! Here's what I'd suggest — try us for one week. No contract, cancel anytime. If your spouse doesn't see the difference after that first cleanup, no hard feelings. Most of the time they're the ones who don't want to cancel 😄"

### "I could just do it myself"
> "You totally could! The question is do you want to? 😅 Most of our customers CAN do it — they're just over it. Between work, kids, and everything else... $25 to get your weekends back is kind of a no-brainer."

### "Are you insured / bonded?"
> "Good question — yes, fully insured. We treat your property like it's our own. We've got a 5.0 star rating on Google with [X] reviews if you want to check us out."
