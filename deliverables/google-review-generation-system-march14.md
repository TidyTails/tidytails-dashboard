## 🧠 Review Psychology — What Makes People Actually Do It

1. **Make it EASY** — One click, no account creation needed (Google handles this)
2. **Make it PERSONAL** — "Would YOU mind" beats "Please leave a review"
3. **Make it MEANINGFUL** — "It helps other dog owners find us" (they're helping people, not just you)
4. **Make it TIMELY** — Ask right after service when they're happiest
5. **Make it LOW PRESSURE** — "No worries if you can't" removes guilt

**What NOT to do:**
- ❌ Don't offer discounts for reviews (Google prohibits this)
- ❌ Don't review-gate (only asking happy customers)
- ❌ Don't ask more than twice
- ❌ Don't buy fake reviews (Google will nuke your profile)
- ❌ Don't have employees/family leave reviews from the business address
