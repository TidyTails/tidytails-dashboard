## 1️⃣ GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE (10 min) — DO THIS FIRST

This is the single most important thing you can do for the business today. Without this, Google literally doesn't know your site exists.

**URL:** https://search.google.com/search-console/about

### Steps:
1. Go to the URL above
2. Click **"Start Now"**
3. Sign in with your Google account (cartervhomes@gmail.com)
4. Choose **"URL prefix"** (easier than Domain)
5. Enter: `https://tidytailsstl.com`
6. Click **Continue**

### Verification (pick easiest method):
**Option A — HTML Tag (Recommended):**
- Google gives you a meta tag like: `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="xxxxx" />`
- Add it to the `<head>` section of your website
- Click **Verify** in Search Console

**Option B — DNS Record (if you have Cloudflare access):**
- Google gives you a TXT record
- Go to Cloudflare → DNS → Add Record → TXT
- Host: `@`
- Value: paste the TXT record Google gives you
- Click **Verify** in Search Console

### After Verification:
1. Go to **Sitemaps** (left sidebar)
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**
4. Go to **URL Inspection** (left sidebar)
5. Paste: `https://tidytailsstl.com`
6. Click **Request Indexing**
7. Repeat for: `https://tidytailsstl.com/blog`

**Expected result:** Pages start appearing in Google within 2-7 days.
