### The Pitch at the Booth

**Opener (as people walk by):**
> "Hey! Want a treat for your pup?" [offer dog treat]
> [While they stop] "I'm TJ — I run Tidy Tails. We clean up the... well, you know... so you don't have to."
> [Laugh together] "What part of town are you in?"

**If they're in our service area:**
> "Oh nice! We're all over [area]. Weekly cleanup starts at $25/week — and we're running a spring special right now: your first cleanup is free. Want me to text you the details?"

**Closing:**
> [Get their number] "Cool, I'll shoot you a text tomorrow with everything. Your yard's gonna love you for it."
