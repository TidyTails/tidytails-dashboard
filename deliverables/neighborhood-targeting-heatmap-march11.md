## The Framework: Dog Owner Density Score (DODS)

Not all neighborhoods are equal. I scored each STL County suburb using 5 proxy indicators (no one publishes "dogs per zip code," so we use what correlates):

| Factor | Why It Matters | Weight |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| **Homeownership Rate** | 65.6% of dog owners are homeowners (AVMA). Renters can't modify yards. | 25% |
| **Median Household Income** | $60K-$150K sweet spot. Rich enough to pay $70/mo, busy enough to need help. | 25% |
| **Lot Size / Yard Space** | Bigger yards = more dogs = more poop = more need for service | 20% |
| **Pet Infrastructure Density** | Vet clinics, dog parks, groomers, pet stores nearby = more dog owners | 15% |
| **Family Density** | Families with kids overwhelmingly own dogs. Family suburbs = dog suburbs. | 15% |

## 🔥 THE TOP 10 NEIGHBORHOODS TO TARGET

### TIER 1 — GO HERE FIRST (Highest conversion probability)

#### 1. 🏆 KIRKWOOD (63122)
- **DODS:** ★★★★★ (9.5/10)
- **Median Income:** ~$95,000
- **Homeownership:** ~80%
- **Why #1:** Historic suburb with big yards, strong community identity, walkable downtown, VERY active on Nextdoor and Facebook. Home to Kirkwood Farmers Market where TJ could leave flyers. Multiple vet clinics. Family-dense with the kind of pride-in-yard culture that makes people embarrassed about dog poop.
- **Dog Parks Nearby:** Kirkwood Park, Walker Dog Park
- **Vet Clinics:** Kirkwood Animal Hospital, Big Bend Veterinary Clinic
- **FB Groups:** "Kirkwood MO Community," "Kirkwood Parents," "Kirkwood Neighbors"
- **Nextdoor Neighborhoods:** Kirkwood, Downtown Kirkwood, Meacham Park
- **Best Post Angle:** "Serving Kirkwood families — your neighbors already use us"
- **Estimated dog-owning households:** ~5,500

#### 2. WEBSTER GROVES (63119)
- **DODS:** ★★★★★ (9.3/10)
- **Median Income:** ~$100,000
- **Homeownership:** ~78%
- **Why:** Adjacent to Kirkwood with similar vibes but slightly higher income. Very community-oriented — people TALK to neighbors. Sniffspot has private dog parks here. Old Webster has walkable streets where dog owners are visible. Strong "buy local" culture.
- **Dog Parks Nearby:** K9 Corner (Sniffspot), Memorial Park
- **Vet Clinics:** Webster Groves Animal Hospital (2 locations!), Old Webster Animal Hospital
- **FB Groups:** "Webster Groves Community," "Webster-Kirkwood Times Readers"
- **Nextdoor Neighborhoods:** Webster Groves, Old Webster, North Webster
- **Best Post Angle:** "Local, family-owned. Like the rest of Webster."
- **Estimated dog-owning households:** ~4,800

#### 3. BALLWIN (63011)
- **DODS:** ★★★★★ (9.0/10)
- **Median Income:** ~$85,000
- **Homeownership:** ~82%
- **Why:** Consistently ranked #1 suburb for families in STL. Massive parks system. More affordable than Chesterfield = more families in the "will pay $70/mo" sweet spot. HUGE population (30K+). Lots of newer subdivisions with HOAs that care about yard appearance.
- **Dog Parks Nearby:** Vlasis Park, Queeny Park (large off-leash area)
- **Vet Clinics:** Ballwin Veterinary Clinic, Southwick Veterinary Hospital
- **FB Groups:** "Ballwin MO Community," "Ballwin/Manchester/Ellisville Neighbors"
- **Nextdoor Neighborhoods:** Ballwin, Ballwin Meadows, Kehrs Mill
- **Best Post Angle:** "Your Ballwin neighbors trust us with their yards"
- **Estimated dog-owning households:** ~6,200

- **Vet Clinics:** Creve Coeur Animal Hospital, Heritage Veterinary Hospital, My Best Friend Vet

- **Vet Clinics:** Wildwood Veterinary Hospital
- **FB Groups:** "Wildwood MO Community," "West County Neighbors"
