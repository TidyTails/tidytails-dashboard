## 🔴 Platform Deep Dives

### 1. Nextdoor — FREE, HIGHEST PRIORITY
**What it is:** Neighborhood social network where homeowners recommend local services.
**Why it matters:** Pet waste removal is HYPER-LOCAL. Nextdoor is literally organized by neighborhood. Other pet waste companies (OhDoody, etc.) already have Nextdoor pages with recommendations.

**How to claim:**
1. Go to business.nextdoor.com
2. Click "Claim your free Business Page"
3. Enter business name + phone number for verification
4. Add service area (St. Louis County neighborhoods)
5. Add photos, hours, description

**What you get for FREE:**
- Business page visible to all neighbors in your service area
- Ability to post Local Deals (2 free per month)
- Customer recommendations (like reviews)
- Direct messaging from potential customers

**Paid option:** Nextdoor Ads start at ~$2/day. Target specific neighborhoods.

**⚡ Quick Win:** Post a "Spring Cleanup Special" to all West County neighborhoods.

---

### 2. Pooper-Scooper.com — $7/mo, EASY WIN
**What it is:** The OG national directory of pet waste removal companies. Been around forever.
**Why it matters:** The Missouri page has ZERO listings. Not a single one. TJ would literally be the only pet waste removal company listed for the entire state of Missouri.

**How to list:**
1. Go to pooper-scooper.com/getlisted.htm
2. Pay $7/month for a state listing
3. Add business name, phone, website, service area

**SEO benefit:** Backlink from an established domain. Every little bit helps when you have zero backlinks.

**ROI math:** Even ONE customer from this listing = $88/mo in revenue vs $7/mo cost. 12.5x ROI.

---

### 3. Thumbtack — Pay-Per-Lead, PROCEED WITH CAUTION
**What it is:** Pay-per-lead marketplace. 300,000+ active pros, $400M revenue.
**Category:** Pet waste removal IS available as a service category.

**Cost structure:**
- Free to create a profile
- Pay $10-60 per lead (varies by competition in your area)
- Set a weekly budget ($49-150 minimum recommended)
- You pay when a customer contacts you, even if they don't hire you
- No monthly subscription

**Realistic math for STL:**
- Pet waste removal = lower-competition category = probably $10-25/lead in STL
- Convert 1 in 4 leads = $40-100 customer acquisition cost
- Customer lifetime value at $88/mo for 12 months = $1,056
- ROI: ~10-26x if customers stick

**⚠️ Warnings:**
- Lead costs keep climbing (contractors report frustration)
- You get charged for tire-kicker contacts too
- Set a firm budget cap and track conversion rate
- Thumbtack has ChatGPT integration now — leads may come from AI search

**Recommendation:** Start with $50/week budget. Track every lead. If conversion is <15%, cut it.

---

### 4. Yelp — FREE Listing, Paid Optional
**What it is:** Review platform. Competitor Clean Turf STL already has a Yelp listing.
**Why it matters:** When people Google "pet waste removal St. Louis," Yelp results often appear in the top 5.

**Free tier:**
- Business page with photos, hours, description
- Accept customer reviews
- Respond to reviews
- Basic analytics

**Paid tier (Yelp Ads):**
- $150-300/month minimum
- NOT recommended at this stage — too expensive for the return

**How to claim:**
1. Go to biz.yelp.com
2. Search for "Tidy Tails" — if no listing exists, create one
3. Add category: "Pet Waste Removal" (yes, it exists on Yelp)
4. Add photos, description, service area
5. Link to website

**Priority:** Claim the FREE listing now. Don't pay for ads yet.

---

### 5. Facebook/Google Ads — The $200k Blueprint
**What the data says:** Fresh Start Pet Waste Removal hit $200k revenue in Year 1. Their #1 tactic: Facebook + Google Ads generating 2-5 leads DAILY. Their advice: "NEVER pause or stop ad spend."

**Facebook Ads for Tidy Tails:**
- Start at $5-10/day ($150-300/month)
- Target: Homeowners in St. Louis County, 25-55, dog owners
- Ad type: Lead generation (form fills) or Messages
- Creative: Before/after yard photos, "Spring Cleanup" messaging
- Expected CPL (cost per lead): $8-20 for local services

**Google Ads:**
- More expensive but higher intent
- Keywords: "pet waste removal st louis," "pooper scooper service near me"
- Expected CPC: $3-8 for pet waste keywords in STL (low competition)
- Start with $10/day ($300/month)
- Requires landing page (tidytailsstl.com already has this)

**⚠️ BUT:** Google Ads won't work well until the site is indexed by Google. This circles back to the SAME blocker: set up Google Search Console + Google Business Profile first.

### 6. Platforms to SKIP (For Now)

**Google Local Service Ads (LSAs):**
- ❌ Pet waste removal is NOT an eligible category
- LSAs are only for: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, lawn care, pest control, etc.
- Maybe someday, but not today

**Angi (HomeAdvisor):**
- ❌ $288-300/year + $15-85/lead, shared with 3-8 competitors
- Auto-renewing 12-month contracts, 60-day cancellation notice
- Contractors report $1,400+ effective customer acquisition cost
- BBB rating: 1.96/5 stars from contractors
- Too expensive and adversarial for a small operation

**Porch:**
- ❌ Basically an insurance company now. Lead gen is dead.
- Don't waste your time.

**Houzz:**
- ❌ Pivoted to construction management software. Not relevant for pet waste.
