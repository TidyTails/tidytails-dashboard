## ⚡ TL;DR — Franklin's Recommendation

**NOT YET.** Don't spend money on ads until these 3 things are done:
1. ✅ Get 3-5 Google reviews (currently: ZERO)
2. ✅ Get listed on 5+ directories (currently: 2 — GBP + Yelp)
3. ✅ Get the website indexed by Google (currently: NOT INDEXED after 30+ days)

**Why:** Running paid ads to a site Google hasn't indexed, with zero reviews, is like paying to send people to an empty restaurant. They'll Google you, see nothing credible, and bounce. Fix the foundation first (1-2 weeks), THEN consider ads.

**When you're ready:** Start with **Facebook Ads** at **$150-200/month**, not Google Ads.

## 📊 The Numbers: What Ads Actually Cost in Pet Waste Removal

### Facebook/Meta Ads (RECOMMENDED FIRST)

| Metric | Industry Average | What TJ Should Expect |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Cost per lead** | $5-15 | $8-20 (new account, no pixel data) |
| **Monthly budget** | $150-300 starter | $150-200 recommended |
| **Leads per month** | 10-30 at $200/mo | 10-25 leads |
| **Lead-to-customer rate** | 15-30% | 20% (TJ's a natural closer) |
| **New customers/month** | 2-5 | 2-5 |
| **Cost per customer** | $30-100 | $40-100 |

**Why FB First:**
- Pet waste removal has **LOW search volume** on Google — most people don't know the service exists
- FB lets you CREATE demand by showing ads to dog owners who didn't know they could hire someone
- Swoop Scoop (7-figure pet waste company) generated **10,000+ leads** from FB ads over 3 years
- FB ads work with lower budgets ($5-10/day) vs Google ($10-20/day minimum)
- One successful operator started with **$100-200/month** on FB and grew to $525/month revenue in month 1

### Google Ads

| Metric | Home Services Avg | Pet Waste Estimated |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Cost per click** | $4-13 (varies by trade) | $3-8 (lower competition niche) |
| **Monthly budget** | $300-500 minimum | $200-300 minimum to get data |
| **Conversion rate** | 5-15% | 8-12% (high intent searches) |
| **Cost per lead** | $25-75 | $20-50 |
| **Cost per customer** | $50-200 | $80-150 |

**Why Google Second:**
- Higher intent (people actively searching "pooper scooper near me")
- But **LOW search volume** for pet waste in most markets
- More expensive per lead than Facebook
- Requires a well-optimized landing page (TJ's site IS good, just not indexed yet)
- Best used AS A COMPLEMENT to FB ads — people see your FB ad, then Google you later
- **Google Local Service Ads (LSA)** may not be available for pet waste removal category

## 🎯 Facebook Ads: Starter Campaign Blueprint

*For when TJ has 3+ reviews and 5+ directory listings*

### Campaign Structure

**Objective:** Lead Generation (Facebook Lead Form — NOT website traffic)
- Lead forms keep people ON Facebook (no landing page bounce risk)
- Collect: Name, Phone, Email, Zip Code
- Follow up within 5 minutes (speed = close rate)

### Targeting

**Audience 1: Dog Owners (Broad)**
- Location: 15-mile radius from Florissant, MO 63031
- Age: 25-65
- Interests: Dogs, Dog lovers, Pet owners, Dog food brands
- Exclude: Pet waste removal businesses, competitors

**Audience 2: Homeowners with Dogs**
- Location: Same radius
- Demographics: Homeowners
- Interests: Dogs + Home & Garden
- This is your PREMIUM audience (they own the yard)

**Audience 3: Lookalike (After 50+ customers)**
- Upload customer list → Facebook builds lookalike
- Start at 1% lookalike (most similar)
- This is where the MAGIC happens for scaling

### Budget & Schedule

| Phase | Budget | Duration | Goal |
|-------|--------|----------|------|
| **Testing** | $7/day ($210/mo) | 2 weeks | Find winning ad + audience |

- **Track in CRM:** Source = "Facebook Ad", date, converted Y/N

## 🔍 Google Ads: When & How (Phase 2)

### When to Start Google Ads
- AFTER website is indexed by Google ✅
- AFTER you have 5+ Google reviews ✅  
- AFTER Facebook ads are profitable ✅
- Budget: Add $200-300/month ON TOP of Facebook spend

### Keyword Targets (Estimated CPC for STL)

| Keyword | Est. Monthly Searches (STL) | Est. CPC | Competition |
|---------|---------------------------|----------|-------------|
| pet waste removal near me | 50-100 | $3-6 | Low-Med |
| pooper scooper service st louis | 20-50 | $2-5 | Low |
| dog poop cleanup service | 30-70 | $3-7 | Low-Med |
| pet waste removal st louis | 10-30 | $2-5 | Low |
| dog poop removal near me | 50-100 | $4-8 | Medium |
| pooper scooper near me | 40-80 | $3-6 | Low-Med |

**Note:** These are ESTIMATES. Actual CPCs for pet waste in STL are likely on the LOW end because:
- Low competition (most STL competitors don't run Google Ads)
- Niche service with low search volume
- No national brands dominating bids in this market

### Google Ads Negative Keywords (CRITICAL)
Block these to avoid wasting money:
- pooper scooper (product) — people buying the tool, not service
- dog poop bags
- pet waste bags
- pooper scooper amazon
- DIY dog poop
- how to pick up dog poop
- free (anything with "free")
- jobs, hiring, salary, career

## 🏆 What Successful Pet Waste Companies Do

### Swoop Scoop — $400K/month, 2,500+ customers
- **#1 channel:** Facebook Ads (10,000+ leads over 3 years)
- **#2 channel:** Google Ads (captures people who saw FB ad then searched)
- **#3 channel:** SEO (long-term organic growth)
- **Also:** Truck wraps (mobile billboard)
- Started with FB ads, added Google once profitable

### Fresh Start Pet Waste Removal — $200K Year 1
- Runs FB + Google ads for 2-5 leads daily
- "NEVER pause or stop ad spend" — consistency is key
- 175+ Google reviews (trust builder)
- Branded trucks

### Poop Scoops for Noobs — Solo Operator Year 1
- Started with $100-200/month on Facebook ads
- Spring rush was biggest growth period
- Grew from 0 to consistent weekly clients in 6 months
- Total year 1 spend: ~$2,000 on ads
- Total year 1 revenue: ~$6,000+ 
- ROI: 3x on ad spend

### Key Pattern
Every successful pet waste company follows the same path:
1. **Free stuff first** (reviews, directories, Facebook groups, Nextdoor)
2. **Facebook ads** ($150-300/month to create demand)
3. **Google ads** ($200-300/month to capture demand)
4. **Scale** (increase budget as ROI proves out)

## 💡 The Bottom Line

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Should TJ run ads RIGHT NOW? | **No** — fix foundation first (reviews, citations, indexing) |
| When should TJ start? | **After 3+ reviews + 5+ citations** (target: April 2026) |
| Which platform first? | **Facebook** — creates demand, lower cost, better for new businesses |
| Starting budget? | **$150-200/month** ($5-7/day) |
| Expected results? | **2-5 new customers/month** at $40-100 per customer acquisition |
| ROI timeline? | **Month 2-3** (first month is learning/testing phase) |
| Break-even math? | 2 new weekly customers ($200/mo revenue) covers $200 ad spend. Everything after = profit. |

**The math is simple:** If TJ spends $200/month on Facebook ads and gets just 2 new weekly customers ($50/week each = $400/month), that's a 2x return. And those customers keep paying monthly — the LTV of a weekly customer is $1,200+/year.

**But first: Do the free stuff.** Every dollar spent on ads before the foundation is set is money wasted.
