## The Customer Lifecycle (7 Stages)

```
INQUIRY → QUOTE → FIRST CLEAN → WEEK 2 CHECK-IN → MONTH 1 REVIEW ASK → MONTH 2 REFERRAL ASK → ONGOING RETENTION
```

## Stage 1: INQUIRY RESPONSE (Within 15 minutes)

**When:** Someone texts/calls/messages asking about service  
**Goal:** Respond FAST, sound professional but human, get the quote info  
**Speed matters:** 78% of customers go with whoever responds first

### Template A — Text Response (most common)
```
Hey [NAME]! This is TJ with Tidy Tails. Thanks for reaching out! 🐕

Quick question so I can get you an accurate quote:
- How many dogs?
- Roughly how big is your yard?
- How often are you thinking? (Most folks do weekly or biweekly)

I service [THEIR AREA] every [DAY], so I can usually get you started within a week.
```

### Template B — Facebook/Nextdoor Response
```
Hey [NAME]! Thanks for the interest. I'd love to help you out.

Shoot me a text at 314-850-7140 and I'll get you a quick quote. Usually takes about 2 minutes — I just need to know how many dogs and yard size.

Looking forward to it! 🐾
```

### Template C — Voicemail Follow-Up (if they called and you missed it)
```
Hey [NAME], this is TJ with Tidy Tails returning your call. Sorry I missed you — was probably out scooping! 😄

Give me a call back or shoot me a text at 314-850-7140 and I'll get you a quote real quick. Talk soon!
```

**Rules:**
- Respond within 15 minutes during business hours (8 AM - 7 PM)
- After hours: respond by 8 AM next day
- Always move the conversation to TEXT (easiest to manage)
- NEVER leave an inquiry hanging overnight without at least acknowledging it

## Stage 2: THE QUOTE (Same day as inquiry)

**When:** They've told you dogs + yard size  
**Goal:** Give clear pricing, remove friction, make it easy to say yes

### Template — Quote Text
```
Alright [NAME], here's what I'm thinking:

📋 [X] dog(s), [SIZE] yard
💰 $70/month (weekly service) — most popular
   Also available: $45/biweekly | $25/week priority

That covers a full yard sweep every visit. I handle everything — bag it, haul it, done. No contracts, cancel anytime.

I'm in [THEIR AREA] every [DAY]. I could start as early as [NEXT AVAILABLE DATE].

Want me to add you to the schedule? 👍
```

**Why this works:**
- Monthly price first (our competitive advantage — $70 beats everyone)
- "No contracts" removes the biggest objection
- Specific start date creates urgency
- "Want me to add you?" is a soft close — yes/no, no ambiguity

### If They Don't Respond (24-hour follow-up)
```
Hey [NAME]! Just wanted to make sure you got my quote. No pressure at all — happy to answer any questions if you've got 'em. 🐾
```

### If They STILL Don't Respond (72-hour final follow-up)
```
Hey [NAME], just circling back one last time. If the timing isn't right, totally understand. I'll be here whenever you're ready. Have a good one! 👋
```

**Rules:**
- Max 3 touchpoints (quote + 2 follow-ups). After that, stop.
- Never be pushy. Friendly persistence, not desperation.
- Log every quote in your phone notes: Name, Area, Dogs, Date Quoted, Status

## Stage 3: FIRST CLEAN CONFIRMATION (Day of first service)

**When:** Day you're heading to their yard for the first time  
**Goal:** Set expectations, build confidence, start the relationship right

### Template — Morning of First Clean
```
Hey [NAME]! TJ here — heading your way today for the first cleanup. 🐕

Just a heads up: first visit might take a bit longer since I'm doing a full deep clean to get you caught up. After that, weekly visits keep it quick and spotless.

I'll shoot you a text when I'm done. Have a great day!
```

### Template — After First Clean
```
All done at [ADDRESS/AREA]! ✅

Your yard is looking good. Grabbed [X] bags worth — should be WAY less going forward with weekly visits.

A couple things:
- I'll be back next [DAY]
- If your dog has a favorite corner they destroy, let me know and I'll hit it extra hard 😄
- Any questions, just text me anytime

Thanks for trusting me with your yard, [NAME]!
```

**Why this matters:**
- Shows professionalism (most competitors just show up and leave)
- "X bags worth" quantifies the value — they SEE what you did
- The dog personality question builds rapport
- Sets the weekly cadence expectation immediately

## Stage 4: WEEK 2 CHECK-IN (After 2nd service)

**When:** After their second clean  
**Goal:** Catch problems early, make them feel valued, lock in the habit

### Template
```
Hey [NAME]! Just finished visit #2. Yard's looking even better this week — [SPECIFIC DETAIL like "way less in the back corner" or "your pup's been busy by the fence!"].

Quick check — everything looking good on your end? Anything you want me to adjust?

Appreciate you! 🐾
```

**Why week 2:**
- If they're unhappy, this is when they'd ghost you. Get ahead of it.
- A specific detail proves you actually pay attention (not just a faceless service)
- Asking "anything to adjust?" gives them permission to speak up instead of canceling silently

---

## Stage 5: MONTH 1 — REVIEW ASK (After 4th service)

**When:** After ~1 month of service (4 weekly visits)  
**Goal:** Get a Google review while they're still in the "honeymoon phase"

### Template
```
Hey [NAME]! We're about a month in now and your yard's been looking 🔥

Real talk — as a small local business, Google reviews are HUGE for me. Would you mind leaving a quick one? Even just a sentence or two makes a massive difference.

Here's the link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

No worries at all if you're not up for it. Just appreciate you being a customer! 🙏
```

**Why this works:**
- "Real talk" + "small local business" = authentic, not corporate
- "Even just a sentence" lowers the bar (most people think reviews need to be long)
- Direct link = zero friction
- The "no worries" escape valve actually increases compliance (less pressure = more action)

### If They Leave a Review — Thank Them!
```
[NAME]! Just saw your review — seriously made my day. Thank you so much. 🙌

People like you are the reason I love doing this. See you [NEXT SERVICE DAY]!
```

**Also:** Reply to their Google review publicly. Something like:
> "Thanks [NAME]! Always a pleasure taking care of your yard. See you next week! 🐾 — TJ"

## Stage 6: MONTH 2 — REFERRAL ASK (After 8th service)

**When:** After ~2 months (relationship is solid)  
**Goal:** Turn happy customers into a lead generation channel

### Template
```
Hey [NAME]! Random question — know anyone in [THEIR AREA] who's tired of dodging landmines in their yard? 😄

I've got a couple spots opening up on my [DAY] route and figured I'd ask my favorite customers first. If you send someone my way who signs up, I'll knock $20 off your next month as a thank you.

No pressure at all! Just thought I'd ask. 🐾
```

**Why this works:**
- Humor ("dodging landmines") makes it feel casual, not salesy
- "Favorite customers" = flattery that's also true
- "Couple spots opening up" = scarcity (even if you have tons of capacity)
- $20 off = real incentive that costs you almost nothing ($20 to acquire a $840/year customer)
- "On my [DAY] route" = you're already in the neighborhood (convenient for them to recommend)

### Track Referrals
Keep a simple list:
```
Referred By → New Customer → Date → $20 Credit Given?
[NAME]     → [NEW]        → [DATE] → ✅
```

## Stage 7: ONGOING RETENTION (Monthly)

**When:** Every month, rotate through these touchpoints  
**Goal:** Stay top-of-mind, catch churn before it happens, keep them feeling valued

### Month 3+ Rotation:

**Seasonal Check-In (Spring/Fall)**
```
Hey [NAME]! Spring's here and the dogs are probably spending more time outside — which means more presents for me 😄

Just a heads up: if you ever want to bump up to weekly from biweekly (or add a deep clean), just let me know. Otherwise, see you [DAY]!
```

**Holiday Thank You (December)**
```
Hey [NAME]! Just wanted to say thanks for being a Tidy Tails customer this year. You and [DOG NAME(s)] are awesome.

Happy holidays! See you next week. 🎄🐾
```

**"Haven't Heard From You" (If they go quiet / skip payments)**
```
Hey [NAME]! Just checking in — noticed I haven't been out to your place in a bit. Everything good?

If you want to pause or adjust anything, no worries at all. Just let me know what works for you. 👍
```

**Anniversary Text (1 Year)**
```
Hey [NAME]! Fun fact — it's been a year since you joined Tidy Tails! 🎉

Thanks for sticking with me. Your yard's come a long way since that first deep clean. Here's to another year of stepping in NOTHING. 😄🐾
```

## The Tech Stack (Keep It Simple)

You don't need a CRM. You need a system. Here's what works at 5-30 customers:

### Option 1: Phone Notes App (Free, Right Now)
Create a note for each customer:
```
[NAME] — [AREA] — [DAY] — [# DOGS]
Started: [DATE]
Monthly: $70
Status: Active
---
3/12 - First clean ✅ (5 bags)
3/12 - Sent after-clean text ✅
3/19 - Visit #2, sent check-in ✅
4/9 - Asked for review ✅ (left 5 stars!)
5/7 - Asked for referral ✅ (referred neighbor)
```

### Option 2: Google Sheets (Free, Shareable)
Columns: Name | Phone | Area | Day | Dogs | Start Date | Monthly $ | Status | Last Visit | Next Action | Notes

### Option 3: Simple CRM (When you hit 15+ customers)
- **Jobber** ($49/mo) — built for field services, scheduling + invoicing + follow-ups
- **Housecall Pro** ($49/mo) — similar, good mobile app
- **Don't** get a CRM before you need one. Phone notes → Sheets → CRM. In that order.

## Red Flags to Watch (Churn Prevention)

Catch these early = save customers:

| Red Flag | What It Means | What to Do |
|----------|---------------|------------|
| Stops responding to texts | Might be unhappy or shopping around | Send a casual check-in, not a sales pitch |
| Asks to switch to biweekly | Testing if they want to cancel | Accommodate gracefully, don't push back |
| Late payment | Could be financial stress or dissatisfaction | Give it a week, then friendly reminder |
| "We're going to handle it ourselves" | Classic pre-cancel | "Totally get it! If it gets overwhelming, I'm a text away" |
| Dog passed away | Sensitive — they might not need service | Express condolences, offer to pause. Don't pitch. |
