**URL:** https://apaws.org
**Cost:** $85/year (online application)

**What you get:**
- ✅ **Directory listing** — apaws.org/search/ (shows up when people search for pet waste pros in MO)
- ✅ **Insurance discount** — through Pet Sitters LLC plan (exclusively for members)
- ✅ **Professional resources** — sample business forms, promotional materials, press release templates
- ✅ **Workshops & classes** — business growth education from experienced operators
- ✅ **Credibility badge** — "aPaws Certified Member" on your website, truck, business cards
- ✅ **Industry networking** — connect with successful operators across the country

**Requirements:**
- Must provide proof of liability insurance (do you have this?)
- Agree to Code of Conduct
- Pay annual dues ($85 online)

**Why this matters:** Doggie Doodies doesn't advertise any professional association membership. Clean Turf STL doesn't either. Being an aPaws member instantly differentiates you:
- "Tidy Tails — aPaws Certified Professional" on your website header
- Trust badge on your GBP profile
- Insurance discount could save more than the $85 membership fee

### ⚠️ PREREQUISITE: Liability Insurance
aPaws requires proof of liability insurance. Industry standard:
- **General Liability:** $1-2M coverage, ~$500-1,200/year
- **Where to get it:** Pet Sitters LLC (petsitllc.com) — they specialize in pet service businesses
- **Why you need it even without aPaws:** Protects your personal assets if a customer claims property damage, a dog gets injured, etc. Any serious customer (especially commercial) will ask for proof of insurance.
