## 🎯 PLATFORM-SPECIFIC TIPS

### Facebook Groups
- **Join these groups** (if not already): Florissant Community, O'Fallon Community, St. Peters Community, STL Dog Owners, West County Dogs, North County Neighbors
- Post as yourself (TJ), not as a business page
- Respond to EVERY comment — engagement helps visibility
- Don't post more than 1x/week per group (admins will kick you)

### Nextdoor
- **Claim your business** on Nextdoor Business if you haven't
- Posts are visible to your neighborhood + nearby neighborhoods
- "Recommendations" carry HUGE weight — ask happy customers to recommend you
- Keep it casual, like you're talking to a neighbor (you literally are)

### Instagram
- **Hashtag strategy:** Always include #stlouis #stlouismo #stldog #tidytailsstl
- **Photos beat text** — even a simple pic of a clean yard works
- **Stories** are low-effort, high-visibility — post 2-3x/week
- **Reels** get the most reach — even a 15-second talking head works
