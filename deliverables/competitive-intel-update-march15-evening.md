## 🎯 Strategic Takeaways for TJ

### 1. Google Indexing is THE #1 Priority
Every single active competitor is indexed. TJ is not. This is like having a store with no address on the map.

### 2. Doggie Doodies' St. Charles Expansion = Opportunity AND Threat
- **Threat:** They're growing and building momentum
- **Opportunity:** As they spread thinner across 2 counties, service quality may dip. TJ can be the FOCUSED West County specialist while they're stretched.

### 3. Cross-Sell Partnerships are Proven
Dirty Dog Wash charges premium prices for a side service. TJ should approach local groomers, dog washes, and pet stores for referral partnerships. The model works.

### 4. Add-On Services Create Differentiation
Clean Turf STL's "Fresh & Sanitary Finish" is a smart upsell. TJ should consider:
- Yard deodorizer spray ($5-10 add-on per visit)
- Waste station restocking for HOAs
- Seasonal deep cleans (spring cleanup packages)

### 5. Content Marketing Matters
Doggie Doodies is publishing SEO blog posts targeting specific cities. Each post = another way to get found. TJ needs blog content targeting "pet waste removal Ballwin MO", "dog poop cleanup Manchester MO", etc.
