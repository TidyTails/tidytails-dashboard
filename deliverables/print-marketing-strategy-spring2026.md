## Tidy Tails STL — Door-to-Door Guerrilla Marketing

### Why Print Marketing RIGHT NOW

Digital is saturated. TJ isn't indexed on Google. Competitors have years of SEO advantage. But **nobody is doing door hangers** in these neighborhoods. This is a blue ocean.

**The math:**
- 250 door hangers from Staples = ~$75-125
- If 2-3% convert (industry avg for local services) = 5-8 leads
- If 50% of leads close = 2-4 new customers
- 2 weekly customers at $25/wk = $200/mo recurring = **$2,400/yr from $100 spend**
- ROI: **2,400%**

### 📅 Execution Playbook

#### Week 1: Print & Prep
- [ ] Open print-marketing-kit-spring2026.html in browser
- [ ] Print each piece to PDF (scale 100%, no margins)
- [ ] Take PDFs to Staples — order on cardstock:
  - 250 door hangers ($75-125)
  - 250 business cards ($15-25)  
  - 50 referral postcards ($20-30)
- [ ] Order 5 yard signs from Vistaprint ($60-75)

#### Week 2: Near-Customer Blitz
- [ ] After each service visit, drop 5-10 leave-behind cards at neighboring homes
- [ ] Ask current customers: "Mind if I put a yard sign up while I service your yard?"
- [ ] Hand each current customer 3 referral postcards: "Know anyone who needs this?"

#### Week 3: Door Hanger Saturation
- [ ] Pick one Tier 1 neighborhood
- [ ] Saturday morning (9-11 AM): Hang 100-150 door hangers
- [ ] Focus on houses with visible dogs, dog toys, or "beware of dog" signs
- [ ] Avoid houses with "no soliciting" signs

#### Week 4: Expand & Track
- [ ] Hit a Tier 2 neighborhood with remaining door hangers
- [ ] Track which neighborhood generates calls
- [ ] Ask every new lead: "How did you hear about us?"
- [ ] Double down on highest-converting area

### 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Timing matters:** Saturday/Sunday mornings are best. People are home, doing yard work, thinking about their messy yard.

2. **Target dog houses:** Look for dog toys, water bowls, "I ❤️ My Dog" bumper stickers, dog doors. These are your people.

3. **Pair with digital:** After hanging 100+ in a neighborhood, run a hyper-targeted Facebook ad to that same zip code. Double exposure = higher conversion.

4. **The yard sign play:** Ask your best customer if you can leave a sign in their yard for a month. Offer a free week of service. Neighbors see it EVERY DAY.

5. **Season it:** Spring is perfect — people are coming outside, seeing the mess winter left behind. "Spring cleanup" messaging hits hard right now.

6. **Track everything:** Add "NEIGHBOR10" tracking to know referral card ROI. Ask every caller where they heard about you.

7. **Leave-behind after rain:** The day after a big rain, yards are extra messy. Perfect time to drop cards — owners are already annoyed at the mud + poop combo.

### 🚫 What NOT to Do

- **Don't leave in mailboxes** — that's a federal offense (seriously, USPS-only)
- **Don't litter** — secure hangers on doorknobs, don't throw on ground
- **Don't go at night** — creepy and ineffective
- **Don't skip tracking** — if you can't measure it, you can't optimize it
- **Don't do this once and quit** — consistency compounds. Monthly rounds build awareness
