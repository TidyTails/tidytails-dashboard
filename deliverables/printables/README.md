## Print Tips
- **Use Chrome** (best print CSS support)
- **Uncheck "Headers and Footers"** in print dialog
- **Set margins to "Minimum"** or "None"
- **Cardstock** for business cards (any office supply store, ~$8 for 250 sheets)
- **Regular paper** is fine for flyers and sign-up sheets
