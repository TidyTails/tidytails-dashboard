## The Math

- 8 current customers
- If even 4 refer 1 person each → 12 customers
- If all 8 refer 1 each → 16 customers (HALFWAY to goal)
- Cost: $0 in ads. ~$25-50 in discounts per referral
- Referral customers have 4x higher retention than cold leads (they already trust you through their friend)

---

## The Offer

**For the referrer (existing customer):**
- $25 off their next month (basically one free week)

**For the new customer:**
- First cleanup FREE (one-time yard cleanup, normally $79-99)
- Then $25/week if they sign up for weekly service

**Why this works:**
- $25 discount costs TJ ~30 minutes of labor. A new weekly customer at $70/month pays that back in 2 weeks.
- Free first cleanup removes ALL risk for the new customer. They see the work before committing.
- Existing customers feel appreciated, not sold to.
