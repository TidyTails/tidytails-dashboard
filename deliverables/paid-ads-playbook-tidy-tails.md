## OPTION 2: GOOGLE ADS (Add After GBP is Verified)

### Why Google Second?
Lower search volume for "pet waste removal" means fewer clicks to work with. But the clicks you DO get are **high intent** — someone searching "pooper scooper service near me" is READY to buy. Higher conversion rate than Facebook.

### Two Types to Consider:

#### A. Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) — BEST OPTION
- **Pay per lead, not per click** (only charged when someone calls/messages)
- Shows at the VERY top of Google with a "Google Guaranteed" badge
- Cost per lead: $30-80 for home services (pet waste is on the lower end)
- Requires: Google Business Profile + background check + proof of insurance
- Setup: [business.google.com/us/ad-solutions/local-service-ads/](https://business.google.com/us/ad-solutions/local-service-ads/)

**Budget:** Set a weekly budget of $100-150. You only pay for actual leads.

**Why LSAs are perfect for TJ:**
- No ad copy to write (Google auto-generates)
- No keyword research needed
- Pay only for real leads
- "Google Guaranteed" badge = instant trust
- STL pet waste competition for LSAs is likely VERY low

#### B. Standard Google Search Ads
- **Pay per click** (whether they convert or not)
- More control over keywords and ad copy
- Requires more management and optimization

**Keywords to Target:**

## 🔑 CONVERSION TRACKING (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Without tracking, you're throwing money in the dark. Set up:

1. **Google Analytics 4 on tidytailsstl.com** — tracks all website behavior
2. **Meta Pixel** — tracks FB ad conversions on your site  
3. **Google Ads conversion tracking** — tracks calls and form fills from Google Ads
4. **Call tracking number** (optional but powerful) — use CallRail or similar ($45/month) to get a trackable number for ads. Know exactly which ad generated which call.

**Minimum viable tracking:**
- GA4 on site (free)
- Meta Pixel (free)  
- Track form submissions as conversions
- Track phone call clicks as conversions

## 💡 PRO TIPS FROM SUCCESSFUL POOP SCOOP COMPANIES

Based on research from companies doing $100K-$1M+ in this niche:

1. **"NEVER pause ad spend"** — Fresh Start ($200K year 1) says constant visibility is what keeps leads flowing. Even at $5/day, stay visible.

2. **Phone photos > professional photos** on Facebook. They feel more authentic and get more engagement.

3. **Always include a promotion** in your ads. "Free first cleanup" is the industry standard and converts like crazy.

4. **FB ads educate, Google ads capture.** Run both: FB creates awareness ("oh, that service exists?"), then when they Google it later, your Google ad catches them.

5. **Reviews are your moat.** Every competitor with 100+ reviews dominates. TJ needs a review generation machine. Ask every single customer, every time.

6. **Route density matters.** When picking targeting areas, focus ads on neighborhoods where you already have customers. More customers on the same route = more profit per hour.
