### PLAY 3: The "Florissant Loyalty Rate" (Decide This Week)

You're more expensive. Period. DD is $83/month for weekly vs your $108. But slashing prices is a race to the bottom. Here's the play:

**Option A: Grandfather Lock (Recommended)**
Text each customer:
```
Hey [name]! Just wanted to let you know — I'm adjusting some pricing for new customers this spring, but your rate is locked in. You've been with me since the beginning and I want to make sure you always get the best deal. No changes on your end!
```

**Why:** This preempts any price shopping. If they hear DD is cheaper, they'll think "but I'm already locked in at a good rate." It costs you NOTHING — you're not changing their price. You're just framing their current price as a loyalty benefit.

**Option B: Loyalty Discount (If Needed)**
If a customer mentions they're considering switching or you sense hesitation:
```
You know what — let me do $90/month for you. You've been with me since day one and I'd rather keep you happy than nickel and dime a loyal customer. Deal?
```

- Shannon: $140 → $120 (saves her $20/month, you keep $120 vs losing $140)
- Amanda: $120 → $100 (saves her $20/month, you keep $100 vs losing $120)
- Chastity: $90 → $75 (saves her $15/month, you keep $75 vs losing $90)

**Total revenue impact:** -$55/month IF you discount all three. But that's $295/month retained vs $0 if they leave. The math is obvious.

**Option C: Add Value Instead of Cutting Price**
Instead of discounting, add a free monthly "deep yard refresh" (basically your standard visit but you call it something special):
```
Hey [name]! Starting this month, all my weekly customers get a free monthly deep refresh — I hit the whole yard extra thorough, edges, flower beds, everything. Just a thank you for being loyal. You'll notice it on your [first Sunday of month] visit!
```

This costs you maybe 10 extra minutes per customer per month but positions your premium price as "premium service."

**My recommendation:** Start with Option A (free, zero risk). Keep Option B in your back pocket if someone actually gets a DD flyer or mentions them. Option C is great if you want to go the extra mile.
