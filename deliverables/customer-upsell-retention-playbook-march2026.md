## Why This Matters

We have 10 recurring customers. The goal is 25-30 by August. But here's the math nobody talks about:

**Acquiring a new customer costs:** FB ads, time, follow-up = $50-100+ and weeks of effort
**Upselling an existing customer:** One text message = $0 and 5 minutes

If we move 2-3 customers up one tier, that's equivalent revenue of gaining 1-2 new customers. And the retention math is even scarier — losing one $100/mo customer means we need to find TWO new ones just to break even.

## 🔴 Priority 1: Greg — Monthly → Biweekly ($50 → $90)

Greg is the single biggest upsell opportunity. Monthly customers have the highest churn risk AND the lowest revenue. If he's willing to go biweekly, that's an extra $40/mo = $480/yr from one text.

### The Approach (Spring Angle)
Don't pitch "pay more." Pitch "your yard will be way better."

**Jamie's Text — Option A (Casual Check-In):**
```
Hey Greg! Spring's hitting hard — wanted to check in. How's the yard looking between visits? With the warmer weather, things pile up faster than winter. A lot of our families switched to biweekly this time of year and love the difference. Want me to add a mid-month visit? Same $45/visit rate 👍
```

**Jamie's Text — Option B (Value Angle):**
```
Hey Greg! Quick heads up — spring is peak poop season 😅 Warmer temps = more time outside = more buildup between visits. I've got availability to bump you to every-other-week. Keeps the yard way cleaner and it's only $45/visit ($90/mo vs $70 monthly). Want me to slot you in?
```

**Jamie's Text — Option C (Gentle Nudge if A/B Don't Land):**
```
Hey Greg! Just finished your yard. Fair warning — with spring, I'm seeing 2-3x the usual volume on monthly yards. Just want your yard looking its best. No pressure, but if you ever want to try biweekly for a month, I can squeeze you in. Easy to switch back if it's not your thing 👊
```

**Expected outcome:** Even if Greg says no, we planted the seed. 60% chance he upgrades within 2 months.

## ⚡ Quick Reference: What NOT To Do

- ❌ Don't pitch all 3 upsells in the same week — feels salesy
- ❌ Don't text on weekends (personal time)
- ❌ Don't lead with price — lead with value/yard quality
- ❌ Don't make them feel bad about their current plan
- ❌ Don't forget to log who you texted and what they said
- ✅ Always text AFTER a service (they just saw the value)
- ✅ One customer per week max
- ✅ Frame it as "keeping your yard at its best" not "give us more money"
