## 📊 POSTING CHECKLIST (Do This Every Time)

Before you hit "Publish" on any GBP post:

- [ ] **Photo attached** — NEVER post without a photo. Text-only posts get 5x less engagement.
- [ ] **Phone number included** in the text body (not just the CTA button)
- [ ] **CTA button set** — "Call now" for most posts, "Get quote" or "Learn more" for info posts
- [ ] **Location services ON** when taking photos (geotagging helps local ranking)
- [ ] **No stock photos** — Google can detect them and they hurt trust. Use your own.
- [ ] **Under 1,500 characters** — GBP truncates at ~1,500 chars. Keep it tight.
- [ ] **Proofread** — typos on GBP look worse than typos on social media

## 📸 PHOTO BANK — Build This Over Time

Take these photos whenever you're on a job. Store them in a folder on your phone. You'll use them over and over:

1. **Before/After shots** (the #1 most engaging content type)
2. **You working** (scooping, walking yards, at the truck)
3. **Clean yards** (wide angle, good lighting, green grass)
4. **Happy dogs** in clean yards (people LOVE dog photos)
5. **Your equipment** laid out clean and professional
6. **Seasonal shots** (snow melting, spring flowers + clean yard, fall leaves)
7. **Pet waste stations** (for commercial pitches)
8. **Neighborhood shots** (Kirkwood, Webster Groves, your service areas)

**Pro tip:** Take 5 photos at every job. It takes 30 seconds and gives you months of content.
