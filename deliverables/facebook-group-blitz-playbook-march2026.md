1. **Read each group's rules first.** Some don't allow service ads — post in pet/community groups instead.

## STEP 5: THE BEFORE/AFTER PHOTO SYSTEM

This is your **#1 marketing asset.** Erica's entire business exploded from one before/after post.

### How to take them:

1. **BEFORE:** Stand at the gate/entry point. Take a wide shot of the whole yard. Get some poop visible (not zoomed in, just... obvious).
2. **AFTER:** Stand in the EXACT same spot. Take the same shot. Clean, green yard.
3. **Bonus:** Take a "haul" photo — the bags of waste you removed. People love seeing the volume.

### Rules:
- Ask customer permission before posting their yard (most say yes)
- Don't show addresses, house numbers, or identifying features
- Good lighting matters — morning or late afternoon is best
- Take 2-3 sets per week to build a library

## KEY LESSONS FROM SUCCESSFUL FOUNDERS

### What Erica (80+ customers) learned:
1. **Pricing too low kills you.** Started at $55/mo → switched to $14-15/week billed every 4 weeks.
2. **Before/after photos are the #1 marketing tool.** Bar none.
3. **Dropped off donuts at vet offices** with hot pink business cards. The donuts were the key.
4. **Dog park banners work** ($250 investment).
5. **Word of mouth is powerful** but you have to ASK for referrals.

### What the $200k/year guy (Fresh Start) learned:
1. **NEVER stop marketing.** Even when you're busy.
2. **Reviews are everything.** They have 175+ five-star reviews.
3. **Hire people who like dogs AND talking to people.**
4. **Branded trucks = mobile billboards.**

### What Holy Crap Poop Scoop (150 yards) learned:
1. **$85/month for weekly service.** Payment due BEFORE service.
2. **Facebook + door hangers = main channels.**
3. **Spring cleanup season is when you book the most.**
4. **"Clean yards mean happy dogs, safe kids, pest-free spaces."** Use this messaging.

### What the Reddit Pooper Scooper KPI article teaches:
1. **Track your Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).** $300/mo marketing → 9-25 customers → $12-33 per customer.
2. **Customer Lifetime Value matters.** $25/week × 6 months = $650 per customer.
3. **Churn is your enemy.** Quality control = customer retention.
4. **Don't close 100% of quotes** — it means your prices are too low.
