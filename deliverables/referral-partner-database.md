## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Go during slow hours.** Vets: mid-morning Tue-Thu. Groomers: early afternoon. Stores: weekday mornings.
2. **Buy something first** at retail stores. Shows you're a customer, not just a salesperson.
3. **Name-drop neighborhoods.** "I mostly service Kirkwood and Webster Groves" = instant local credibility.
4. **Follow up in 2 weeks.** Swing by, ask if cards need refilling. This is how you turn a drop-off into a relationship.
5. **Start with independents.** Local businesses support local businesses. Chains have corporate policies that slow things down.
6. **Instagram connections.** Follow + engage with these businesses on Instagram. Comment on their posts. Build digital familiarity BEFORE walking in.
