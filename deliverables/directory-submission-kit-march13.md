## 🏆 TIER 1 — Do These FIRST (Highest Impact)

These are the directories that will move the needle most. Each one is high domain authority and directly impacts local search rankings.

### 1. ⭐ Google Search Console (THE #1 PRIORITY)
**URL:** https://search.google.com/search-console
**Why:** Without this, Google literally doesn't know your site exists
**Time:** 15 min
**Steps:**
1. Go to URL above, sign in with Google account
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Choose "URL prefix" → enter `https://tidytailsstl.com`
4. Verify via **HTML tag** method (easiest):
   - Copy the meta tag they give you
   - Add it to your website's `<head>` section
   - Click "Verify"
5. Once verified → go to "Sitemaps" in left menu
6. Enter: `https://tidytailsstl.com/sitemap.xml` → Submit
7. Go to "URL Inspection" → paste `https://tidytailsstl.com` → click "Request Indexing"
8. Repeat for `/blog/`, `/ferguson`, `/florissant`, `/st-charles`

**⚠️ If you can't add meta tags to the site:** Use DNS verification instead:
- Choose "Domain" property type
- Add the TXT record they provide to your Cloudflare DNS
- Takes 5-10 min to propagate

**Status:** ☐ Not started
