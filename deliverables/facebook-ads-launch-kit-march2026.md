## STEP 1: Create a Meta Business Account (if you don't have one)

1. Go to **business.facebook.com**
2. Click "Create Account"
3. Use your Tidy Tails Facebook Page
4. Add your payment method (debit/credit card)
5. Go to **Ads Manager** (adsmanager.facebook.com)

If you already have a Facebook Page for Tidy Tails, you're halfway there.

---

## STEP 2: Install the Meta Pixel on tidytailsstl.com

This tracks who visits your site from ads so Meta can optimize.

1. In Ads Manager → go to **Events Manager** (left sidebar)
2. Click **Connect Data Sources** → **Web** → **Meta Pixel**
3. Name it "Tidy Tails Pixel"
4. Choose **Install code manually**
5. Copy the pixel code snippet
6. Paste it in the `<head>` section of your website (or ask your web host)
7. Use the **Meta Pixel Helper** Chrome extension to verify it's working

**Skip if overwhelmed** — ads still work without the pixel, you just can't retarget or optimize as well. Come back to this later.

## STEP 5: Creative (Images/Video)

### What Works Best (from Swoop Scoop's 10,000+ leads):
1. **Phone-quality photos > professional shots** — they feel more real on Facebook
2. **Real people > stock photos** — always
3. **Before/after yard photos** — super effective
4. **Short video (15-30 sec)** — you talking to camera about the service

### What to Shoot (takes 10 minutes):
- **Photo 1:** You in the yard with your equipment, smiling. Tidy Tails shirt if you have one.
- **Photo 2:** A clean, green backyard (the "after")
- **Photo 3:** Your truck/equipment setup
- **Video:** 15-second selfie video: "Hey St. Louis, I'm TJ with Tidy Tails. I scoop dog poop so you don't have to. First cleanup's free. Link in comments."

### In the meantime:
Use a **bright, clean image** of a happy dog in a green yard. Canva has great free templates. Add text overlay: "First Cleanup FREE 🐕" + "Tidy Tails — St. Louis"

## STEP 7: Lead Follow-Up (CRITICAL — This Is Where Money Is Made or Lost)

**The rule: Text within 5 minutes.** Leads that get contacted within 5 minutes are **21x more likely** to convert than leads contacted after 30 minutes.

### When a Lead Comes In:
1. **Get the Meta notification** on your phone (turn on Ads Manager notifications)
2. **Text them immediately:**

```
Hey [Name]! This is TJ from Tidy Tails. You signed up for a free yard cleanup — awesome! 🐕 What day works best this week for me to swing by? I'm flexible.
```

3. **If no response in 2 hours:**
```
Hey [Name], just following up on the free cleanup. I've got openings [Tuesday] and [Thursday] this week. Want me to pencil you in?
```

4. **If no response in 24 hours:**
```
Last ping! Just want to make sure you don't miss your free cleanup. Text me back when you're ready — no rush. 🐾 -TJ
```

### Convert the Free Cleanup to Recurring:
After the first free visit, text:
```
Hey [Name]! Just finished up — your yard is looking 🔥. If you want to keep it that way, I do weekly visits for $25/week. Same day every week, no contract. Want me to add you to the schedule?
```
