## 🧠 FACEBOOK MARKETPLACE ALGORITHM: HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS

The algorithm decides who sees your listing. Here's what it rewards:

### What Boosts Your Listing:
1. **Fast response time** — Reply to messages within 15 minutes. FB tracks this and rewards fast sellers with more visibility
2. **Complete listing details** — Every field filled out = higher search placement
3. **Fresh/renewed listings** — Relisting or editing bumps you back up in search
4. **Engagement signals** — Saves, shares, and messages tell FB "this listing is good"
5. **Competitive pricing** — FB surfaces listings in "Best Deals" and "Price Drop" filters
6. **Multiple high-quality photos** — Listings with 5+ photos get significantly more engagement
7. **Specific titles with keywords** — "10x12 Storage Shed" beats "Shed for Sale"
8. **Tags** — Up to 3 tags per listing (e.g., #storage_shed, #backyard, #workshop)

### What Kills Your Listing:
- ❌ Slow response time (>1 hour)
- ❌ Generic titles ("Shed" or "Building for Sale")
- ❌ Single photo or blurry photos
- ❌ Empty description
- ❌ Wrong category
- ❌ Duplicate listings that are identical (FB detects and suppresses)

### The Refresh Trick:
**Every 7 days, edit something on your top listings** — change the description slightly, swap the first photo, adjust price by $1. This tells the algorithm the listing is active and pushes it back into feeds. With 200+ listings, rotate through 25-30 per day.

## 📸 PHOTO STRATEGY (This Is Where Most Sellers Fail)

### The 7-Photo Minimum:
1. **Hero shot** — Full shed, straight on, good lighting, clean background
2. **3/4 angle** — Shows depth and two sides
3. **Interior empty** — Shows floor space, ceiling height, natural light
4. **Interior staged** — Same shed with tools/items to show scale and use
5. **Door/entry detail** — Shows accessibility, width, quality
6. **Close-up of materials** — Paint quality, hardware, roofing
7. **Context shot** — Shed in a real backyard setting (customer installs if available)

### Photo Rules:
- **NEVER shoot with the shed door closed as the only photo** — people want to see inside
- **Shoot in morning or late afternoon light** — harsh noon sun washes out colors
- **Leave space around the shed in frame** — listings are viewed on mobile, cramped crops look cheap
- **Clean the area first** — no trash, mud, or random lumber in the background
- **Square or 4:5 aspect ratio** — mobile-first, don't shoot landscape

## 💡 ADVANCED MARKETPLACE HACKS

### 1. The $1 Listing Trick
List one shed at $1 with "See Description for Pricing" in the title. This triggers curiosity clicks and messages. In the description, explain it's custom-priced based on size/options. You'll get more messages than any normal listing.

### 2. The "Just Installed" Photo Play
After every install, take 3-4 photos with the customer's permission. Post it as "Just installed this [size] in [neighborhood]!" Social proof + local targeting = high engagement. Ask "Want one like this? Message me."

### 3. The Relist-as-New Strategy
Instead of renewing old listings, DELETE and create fresh ones every 2 weeks. New listings get priority in the algorithm over renewed ones. Yes, even with the same photos.

### 4. The Cross-Post to Groups
After posting on Marketplace, share the listing to 5-10 local STL buy/sell groups. The listing lives on Marketplace but gets group exposure. Groups to hit:
- St. Louis Buy Sell Trade
- STL Marketplace (all variants)
- Any neighborhood-specific groups in your delivery radius
- Home improvement / DIY groups

### 5. The "Mark as Available" Timer
Set a phone alarm to "Mark as Available" on your top 10 listings every Tuesday and Saturday. This is the easiest algorithm refresh that takes 30 seconds per listing.
