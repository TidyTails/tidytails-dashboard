## ❓ Q&A SECTION (Pre-Seed These Questions)

You can ask AND answer your own questions on GBP. This is a known strategy — seeds your profile with keyword-rich content.

### Q1:
**Question:** How much does pet waste removal cost in St. Louis?
**Answer:** Tidy Tails pricing starts at $15/week for weekly service. Biweekly starts at $40/visit, and monthly at $50/visit. One-time cleanups are priced based on yard size and accumulation. We always provide free estimates — just call or text us!

### Q2:
**Question:** What areas do you serve?
**Answer:** We serve all of St. Louis County including Florissant, Overland, Olivette, University City, Creve Coeur, Maryland Heights, Hazelwood, Ferguson, O'Fallon, Saint Charles, Saint Peters, and more. If you're in the greater STL area, we can probably help!

### Q3:
**Question:** Do I need to be home during the cleanup?
**Answer:** Nope! As long as we have access to the yard (gate code, etc.), we'll come through on your scheduled day whether you're home or not. We'll send a text when we're done so you know it's taken care of.

### Q4:
**Question:** Do you offer one-time cleanups or only recurring service?
**Answer:** Both! We offer one-time deep cleans for spring cleanups, move-in/move-out situations, or anytime you need a reset. We also offer weekly, biweekly, and monthly recurring plans with no contracts.

### Q5:
**Question:** Do you service apartment complexes or commercial properties?
**Answer:** Yes! We offer commercial pet waste removal for apartment complexes, HOAs, dog parks, and any multi-unit property. We can also install and maintain pet waste bag stations. Contact us for a custom quote.
