## 🟢 PLATFORM 2: Nextdoor Ads

### Why Nextdoor
- **Highest trust platform for local services** — people trust neighbor recommendations
- **Hyper-local** — targets specific neighborhoods, not just zip codes
- **Low competition** — most small businesses don't advertise here
- **Perfect for "competitor just died" messaging** — organic posts + paid boost

### Nextdoor Ad Options for Small Business

**Option A: Local Deal (Self-Service)**
- Create a "deal" that appears in neighbors' feeds
- Example: "Spring Special: First Cleanup FREE — Tidy Tails STL"
- Targets neighborhoods within your service area
- Cost: Pay per impression or per redemption
- Setup: nextdoor.com → Business page → Create a Local Deal

**Option B: Neighborhood Sponsorship**
- Your business appears as "Neighborhood Sponsor" in local feeds
- Includes your logo, business name, and a short pitch
- Cost: ~$75-250/mo depending on neighborhoods selected
- Higher visibility than deals, builds ongoing awareness

**Option C: Nextdoor Ads Manager (Bigger Budget)**
- Full self-service ad platform (similar to Facebook Ads)
- Requires contacting Nextdoor sales OR going to business.nextdoor.com
- More targeting options, performance tracking
- Better for $200+/mo budgets

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't boost posts from your Page** — Use Ads Manager for proper targeting. Boost = lazy, expensive.
2. **Don't set and forget** — Check weekly. Pause bad performers. Scale good ones.
3. **Don't target too broad** — St. Louis metro is huge. Target specific zip codes/neighborhoods.
4. **Don't skip the Pixel** — Install BEFORE running ads. Retargeting is the cheapest traffic.
5. **Don't send traffic to homepage** — Send to a specific landing page or use Lead Forms.
6. **Don't ignore leads** — 5-minute response time. Every hour you wait, conversion drops 10x.
7. **Don't run ads without GBP complete** — People Google you after seeing an ad. GBP must be solid. ✅ Done!
