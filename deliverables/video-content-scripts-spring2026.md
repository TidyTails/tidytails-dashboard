## 📱 QUICK TIPS FOR FILMING

1. **Don't overthink it.** Raw, real content outperforms polished stuff on TikTok. Your phone camera is enough.
2. **Film horizontal AND vertical.** Vertical (phone upright) for TikTok/Reels. But grab a few horizontal clips for YouTube later.
3. **Natural light only.** You're outside — this is already handled.
4. **Talk to camera like you're talking to a friend.** Not a news anchor. Your real voice is your brand.
5. **Keep it under 30 seconds** when possible. Shorter = higher completion rate = algorithm pushes it more.
6. **Use text overlays** instead of just talking. Many people watch without sound.
7. **Add captions.** TikTok has auto-captions. Turn them on.
8. **Post consistently.** 3-4x/week beats one "perfect" video per month.
9. **Engage with EVERY comment** in the first hour. Algorithm rewards creator replies.
10. **Save trending sounds** when you see them on your FYP. Use them within 3-5 days for max reach.
