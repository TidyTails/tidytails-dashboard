## 🔑 KEY LESSONS FROM SUCCESSFUL SCOOPERS

### From William Milliken (Swoop Scoop, $2M+/yr):
1. **Track your numbers obsessively** — Customer acquisition cost, churn rate, lifetime value. You can't grow what you don't measure.
2. **10,000 leads in 3 years** came from Facebook Ads + Google Ads. Not organic. Not hope. PAID acquisition with measured ROI.
3. **Commercial contracts are the profit multiplier** — Residential pays the bills, commercial puts money in the bank.
4. **CRM from day one** — He teaches Sweep&Go setup in his course. It's not optional.
5. **Don't provide arrival windows** — Just show up and scoop. Arrival windows create customer service headaches.

### From Erica Krupin (Kroopin's, 200 weekly customers):
1. **Raise prices incrementally** — She went from $55 → $99/mo by charging new customers more. Old customers eventually accept increases too.
2. **376 Google reviews > any ad spend** — Reviews ARE your marketing at local scale.
3. **Vehicle wraps are marketing machines** — Her bright pink fleet generates leads by simply existing on the road. Budget for this when you hit 15+ customers.
4. **One employee can handle 125 houses/week** at $18.50/hr. That's the math for when you're ready to hire.
5. **Nextdoor + Facebook Groups are free lead gen** — Her vehicle-wrap thank-you post = 10 inquiries from ONE post.
6. **Gate photos are non-negotiable** — Protect yourself. Document every closed gate.

### From Both:
- **Spring is your Super Bowl.** The "spring thaw" is when every yard reveals 3+ months of accumulated poop. Market HARD from March through May.
- **Content builds authority.** Erica's YouTube, William's Skool community — being visible makes you the obvious choice.
- **Franchise-proof yourself.** POOP 911 and Scoop Soldiers are expanding into STL. Your advantage is being LOCAL, PERSONAL, and REVIEWED. Lean into that.
