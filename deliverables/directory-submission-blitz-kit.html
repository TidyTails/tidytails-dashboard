🎯 Submission Day Checklist

Before you start: Have these open in browser tabs — email (for verification links), Google Business Profile (to copy-import where supported), and this page (for NAP copy-paste).
Photos ready: Have 3-5 photos on your phone — logo, you working (action shot), clean yard before/after, truck/equipment. Most directories let you add photos.
Hours: Use same hours everywhere. Suggested: Mon-Sat 8AM-6PM, Sun 9AM-4PM (scooping day).
Service areas: List these consistently: St. Louis County, St. Charles County, Florissant, Ferguson, O'Fallon, St. Peters, Maryland Heights, Kirkwood, Webster Groves.
⚠️ FIX SSL FIRST: Renew your SSL certificate in GoDaddy before submitting to directories. Some verify your website URL — if it shows "Not Secure," they may reject it or it looks unprofessional.
After ALL submissions: Submit sitemap to Google Search Console again. The new backlinks + fixed SSL = Google should index within 3-7 days.

🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Inconsistent NAP: "Tidy Tails" vs "Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal" vs "TidyTails" — pick ONE and use it everywhere. Use the full name.
  • Wrong phone: The shed number (636-354-7406) is NOT the Tidy Tails number. Always use 314-850-7140.
  • Skipping verification: Many directories send a postcard or email. VERIFY every listing. Unverified = doesn't count.
  • No photos: Listings with photos get 35% more clicks. Add at least your logo + 1 action shot.
  • No description: Copy the short or long description from above. Empty listings rank lower.
