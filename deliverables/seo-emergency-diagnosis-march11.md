## The Problem
**tidytailsstl.com has ZERO pages indexed by any search engine.** Not Google, not Bing, not Brave. Searching for the exact domain name returns nothing. Searching for "tidy tails pet waste st louis" returns nothing. The site is completely invisible to anyone searching online.

## Root Cause: Brand New Domain + Zero Backlinks

**Domain registered:** February 16, 2026 (23 days ago)

This is the #1 factor. Google is inherently suspicious of brand-new domains because most are spam. Without external signals proving the site is legitimate, Google deprioritizes crawling and indexing. The site needs **backlinks** and **citations** to earn trust.

### What IS Working (The Good News)
The site itself is technically excellent:
- ✅ Server-rendered HTML (not a JS SPA — crawlers can read it)
- ✅ Proper `<title>` tags, meta descriptions, OG tags
- ✅ robots.txt allows all crawling
- ✅ XML sitemap with 17 URLs, submitted to Google Search Console
- ✅ LocalBusiness + FAQ structured data (schema.org)
- ✅ Canonical URLs set correctly
- ✅ No noindex tags anywhere
- ✅ HTTPS via Cloudflare
- ✅ Fast load times
- ✅ 11 unique city landing pages with localized content
- ✅ 4 blog posts with real, useful content
- ✅ Good internal linking between all pages
- ✅ geo.region and geo.placename meta tags

**The site is ready for traffic. It just doesn't exist in Google's eyes yet.**

### What's MISSING (The Actual Problem)
- ❌ **Zero backlinks** — no external site links to tidytailsstl.com
- ❌ **Zero directory listings** — not on Yelp, Google Business Profile, Thumbtack, etc.
- ❌ **Zero social presence linking to site** — no Facebook page, Instagram bio link, etc.
- ❌ **Domain is 23 days old** — Google sandbox period for new domains is typically 2-8 weeks
- ❌ **No Google Business Profile** — the single most important local SEO signal

## 🎯 THIS WEEK: 5 Actions That Actually Matter

These are in priority order. Each one sends a "this is a real business" signal to Google.

### 1. Google Business Profile (30 min) — **HIGHEST PRIORITY**
- Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
- Create listing for "Tidy Tails" as a service-area business
- Category: "Pet waste removal service"
- Add phone (314-850-7140), website (tidytailsstl.com), hours, photos
- Verify via postcard or phone (phone verification is instant if available)
- **Why:** GBP is THE local SEO signal. Competitors like Poopy Paws and DoodyCalls all rank locally because of their GBP, not their websites.

### 2. Yelp Business Page (15 min)
- Go to [biz.yelp.com](https://biz.yelp.com)
- Claim or create "Tidy Tails" listing
- Category: "Pooper Scooper" or "Pet Waste Removal"
- Add all info + link to website
- **Why:** 12+ STL competitors are on Yelp. Tidy Tails is not. Yelp pages rank on Google.

### 3. Submit URLs Individually in GSC (10 min)
- Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
- Use URL Inspection tool for each of these 5 URLs:
  1. `https://tidytailsstl.com/`
  2. `https://tidytailsstl.com/florissant`
  3. `https://tidytailsstl.com/ferguson`
  4. `https://tidytailsstl.com/blog/how-often-pick-up-dog-poop`
  5. `https://tidytailsstl.com/blog/is-pooper-scooper-service-worth-it`
- Click "Request Indexing" for each
- **Why:** Sitemap submission is passive. Individual URL submission is active — it tells Google "crawl THIS page NOW."

### 4. Create Free Directory Listings (45 min total)
Each of these creates a backlink + NAP citation (Name, Address, Phone):

| Directory | URL | Time |
|-----------|-----|------|
| Thumbtack | thumbtack.com | 10 min |

| Angi (Angie's List) | angi.com | 5 min |
| BBB | bbb.org | 5 min |
| Manta | manta.com | 5 min |

**Why:** Each listing = 1 backlink + 1 NAP citation. Google uses citation count to validate local businesses. Right now: 0 citations. Competitors have dozens.

### 5. Social Media Links (15 min)
- Create/update Facebook page → link to tidytailsstl.com
- Instagram bio → link to tidytailsstl.com
- Share 1-2 blog posts on Facebook/Instagram
- **Why:** Social signals don't directly boost rankings, but they DO trigger Google's crawlers to discover URLs. Google discovers new pages by following links from sites it already crawls frequently.
