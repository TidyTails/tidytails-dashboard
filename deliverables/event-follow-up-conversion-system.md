## The Problem This Solves

You're going to collect names on Saturday. Sign-up sheet, business cards, conversations. **That's the easy part.** The hard part — and the part that turns a free event into $2,000+/month in recurring revenue — is what happens AFTER.

Most small businesses collect leads at events and... never follow up. Or they send one text and give up. The conversion happens in the **follow-up sequence**, not at the booth.

**Goal: Convert 20-30% of sign-ups into paying customers within 14 days.**

---

## ⏰ TIMELINE: The 72-Hour Conversion Window

### 🟢 SATURDAY (Day of Event) — During & After

**At the event (Jamie's job):**
- Mark interest level on sign-up sheet: 🔥 Hot / 🟡 Warm / 🔵 Cool
- Hot = "I want to sign up" or asked about pricing
- Warm = interested, asked questions, took a card
- Cool = just signed up for the raffle/freebie

**Saturday evening (within 4 hours of event ending):**

**Text ALL sign-ups — personalized by interest level:**

#### 🔥 HOT leads (asked about pricing/signup):
```
Hey [Name]! This is Jamie from Tidy Tails — great meeting you at the Paw-ty today! 🐾

You mentioned wanting to get your yard cleaned up. I'd love to get you on the schedule!

Here's what I can do:
✅ First cleanup FREE (no strings)
📅 Weekly: $25/wk | Biweekly: $45 | Monthly: $70 flat

When works best for your first cleanup? I've got a few spots open this week!
```

#### 🟡 WARM leads (interested but didn't commit):
```
Hey [Name]! Jamie from Tidy Tails here — loved meeting you (and [dog's name if you caught it]!) at the Paw-ty today 🐾

Just wanted to say thanks for stopping by! If you ever want to try out our service, your first cleanup is on us — totally free, no commitment.

Just text back whenever you're ready and we'll get you scheduled!
```

#### 🔵 COOL leads (just signed the sheet):
```
Hey [Name]! This is Jamie from Tidy Tails — thanks for stopping by our table at the Crestwood Paw-ty today! 🐾

If you ever need your yard cleaned up, we do pet waste removal starting at $25/week. First one's free!

Have a great weekend! 🐕
```

### 🟡 SUNDAY (Day 1) — Social Proof Drop

**Post event recap on Facebook + Instagram:**
- Photos from the event (even phone pics work)
- Tag the vet clinic
- "Amazing turnout at Saturday's Paw-ty! Met so many awesome pet parents. Spring special still going — first cleanup FREE! 🐾"

**NO follow-up texts today.** Let Saturday's texts breathe.

---

### 🟡 MONDAY (Day 2) — The Value Add

**Text ONLY Hot + Warm leads who haven't responded:**

```
Hey [Name]! Quick question — how many dogs do you have? Just want to make sure I give you the right quote. Most of our customers in [their neighborhood if known] pay around $70/month for worry-free yards. 🐕
```

**Why this works:** It's a question, not a pitch. People respond to questions. And it re-opens the conversation naturally.

---

### 🔴 WEDNESDAY (Day 4) — The Urgency Nudge

**Text Hot leads who STILL haven't responded:**

```
Hey [Name]! Heads up — I'm filling up my Monday route for April and wanted to make sure you got a spot if you're interested. No pressure at all! Just didn't want you to miss out. 😊
```

**Why this works:** Scarcity + no pressure. The "filling up" is real — you DO have limited Monday capacity.

---

### 🟠 SATURDAY (Day 7) — The Last Touch

**Text remaining non-responders (ALL levels):**

```
Hey [Name]! Happy Saturday 🐾 Just checking in one last time from the Paw-ty last week. If pet waste removal is ever on your radar, we're here! First cleanup is always free. Have a great weekend!
```

**After this:** Move to monthly nurture list (see below). Don't keep texting — respect the silence.

## 🧠 MINDSET NOTES FOR JAMIE

1. **Follow-up is NOT pushy.** You met these people. They signed up. They WANT to hear from you. The pushy thing would be texting strangers. These people literally wrote down their phone number at your booth.

2. **Speed matters.** The person who follows up within 4 hours gets the customer. The person who waits until Monday loses them. Text Saturday evening.

3. **Personalization wins.** "Hey Jane, loved meeting [dog's name]!" beats "Dear valued lead" every single time. Use whatever you remember from the conversation.

4. **The vet is the long game.** Individual sign-ups are great. But one vet partnership can generate 2-5 new customers per month for YEARS. Invest in that relationship.

5. **Don't take silence personally.** People are busy. They saw your text. They might convert in 2 months when their yard gets bad enough. The seed is planted.
