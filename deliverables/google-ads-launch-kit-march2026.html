🚀 Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Follow these exactly. Total time: 45-60 minutes.

Step 1: Create Google Ads Account (5 min)

  1. Go to ads.google.com
  2. Click "Start Now" — sign in with your Google account
  3. IMPORTANT: When it asks "What's your main advertising goal?" — click "Switch to Expert Mode" at the bottom. Do NOT use Smart Campaigns (they waste money)
  4. Click "Create an account without a campaign"
  5. Confirm your business info and billing country
  6. Add your payment method (debit/credit card)
⚠️ Skip "Smart Campaign" setup. Google will try to get you into their simplified mode. It sounds easier but gives you zero control and wastes budget. Always use Expert Mode.

Step 2: Set Up Conversion Tracking (10 min)

  1. In Google Ads, go to Goals → Conversions → Summary
  2. Click "+ New conversion action"
  3. Select "Phone calls" → "Calls from ads or website"
  4. Enter your phone number: 314-850-7140
  5. Set minimum call duration to 30 seconds (filters out accidental clicks)
  6. Save
  7. If your website has a contact form, also add a "Website" conversion for the thank-you page
Why this matters: Without conversion tracking, you can't tell which keywords are getting you actual calls vs. wasting money. This is the #1 mistake new advertisers make.

Step 3: Create Your Campaign (15 min)

  1. Click "+ New Campaign"
  2. Objective: "Leads"
  3. Campaign type: "Search"
  4. Select your phone call conversion from Step 2
  5. Campaign name: "Tidy Tails - Pet Waste STL"
  6. Networks: UNCHECK "Include Google Search Partners" and "Include Google Display Network" — Search only!
  7. Locations: "Enter another location" → type "Ballwin, Missouri" → set radius to 15 miles
  8. Location options: Select "Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"
  9. Language: English
  10. Budget: $10/day ($300/month)
  11. Bidding: "Maximize conversions" (if you have conversion tracking) or "Maximize clicks" with a $5 max CPC bid cap (if no tracking yet)

Step 4: Create Your Ad Group & Keywords (10 min)

  1. Ad group name: "Pet Waste Removal"
  2. Copy/paste the Phrase Match keywords from the Keywords tab
  3. Make sure each keyword has quotes around it (phrase match)
  4. Go to Keywords → Negative Keywords
  5. Copy/paste ALL the negative keywords from the Keywords tab

Step 5: Create Your Ads (10 min)

  1. Select "Responsive search ad"
  2. Final URL: https://tidytailsstl.com (or .net)
  3. Copy/paste the 15 headlines from the Ad Copy tab
  4. Copy/paste the 4 descriptions from the Ad Copy tab
  5. Add your Call extension: 314-850-7140
  6. Add Sitelink extensions from the Ad Copy tab
  7. Add Callout extensions from the Ad Copy tab
  8. Preview your ad → looks good? Hit "Publish"

Step 6: Ad Schedule (2 min)

  1. Go to "Ad schedule" in your campaign
  2. Set ads to run Monday-Saturday, 7AM to 8PM
  3. This ensures you only pay for clicks when you can actually answer calls

⚡ Speed to Lead Protocol

78% of customers go with whoever responds first.

When a call comes in from Google Ads:

  1. Answer within 3 rings — or call back within 5 minutes
  2. Say: "Hey, this is TJ with Tidy Tails! Thanks for calling."
  3. Ask: How many dogs? How often do they use the yard? What area?
  4. Quote immediately: "$25/week for 1-2 dogs. First cleanup is free."
  5. Book the first service on the call: "I can be there Thursday — does morning or afternoon work better?"
  6. Send a text confirmation right after: name, date, time, address
If you miss the call: Call back within 5 minutes AND text: "Hey, this is TJ from Tidy Tails! Just saw I missed your call — I do pet waste removal in the STL area. When's a good time to chat?"
