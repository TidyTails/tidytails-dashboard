## PART 1: THE REFERRAL PROGRAM

### Structure: "Send a Friend, Save $10"

**How it works:**
- When a current customer refers someone who signs up, **BOTH get $10 off their next service**
- No limit on referrals — refer 3 people, save $30
- Referral must complete their first paid service to trigger the discount

**Why $10 (not free service):**
- $10 is enough to motivate without killing your margins
- Giving away free services devalues what you do
- $10 off a $25 visit = 60% margin retained
- Both sides win = they'll actually tell people

### Referral Tracking (Simple)

You don't need software. Just a note in your phone:

```
REFERRAL LOG
-----------
[Date] - [Who Referred] → [New Customer Name] → [Status: Contacted/Signed Up/Credit Given]

Example:
3/15 - Shannon Z. → Sarah M. → Signed up! Credit given to both
3/18 - Amanda H. → Mike T. → Left VM, following up
```

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- ❌ Don't offer FREE services as referral incentive (devalues your work)
- ❌ Don't spam texts — one review ask, one referral announce, one follow-up MAX
- ❌ Don't post the same thing in multiple FB groups on the same day (looks spammy)
- ❌ Don't fake reviews or ask friends who aren't customers to review
- ❌ Don't discount more than $10 per referral — your margins are already tight at $25/week
