### Why This Matters
1. **SEO cannibalization** — Google sees 6+ "Tidy Tails" pet waste companies and doesn't know which is which
2. **Brand confusion** — potential STL customers googling "Tidy Tails" find Charleston or Atlanta instead
3. **The .net domain hurts** — .com and branded domains rank better (.net signals "generic/cheap")
4. **No local signals** — without GBP, citations, or "St. Louis" in the domain, Google has no reason to show TJ's version

### What TJ Should Do
- **SHORT TERM:** Add "STL" or "St. Louis" to EVERY mention — "Tidy Tails STL Pet Waste Removal"
- **MEDIUM TERM:** Consider tidytailsstl.com (check availability) for a cleaner, localized domain
- **LONG TERM:** GBP + citations will eventually disambiguate, but the brand collision makes it slower
