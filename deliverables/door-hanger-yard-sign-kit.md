## Why This Is the #1 Thing You're Not Doing

**The math is stupid simple:**
- You service ~8 yards per week
- Each yard has 5-10 immediate neighbors
- That's **40-80 households per week** who SEE your truck but get ZERO touchpoint
- A door hanger left on 5 neighbor doors per visit = **40 touchpoints/week**
- Door hanger conversion rate for local services: **1-3%** (industry average)
- **40 touchpoints × 2% = almost 1 new lead per week = 4 per month**
- At your price point ($70/mo avg), that's $280/mo revenue from a $30 print job

**The psychology:**
- "I just cleaned your neighbor's yard" = instant social proof
- They already see you on the street = familiarity breeds trust
- It's NOT cold outreach — it's warm by proximity
- Nobody throws away a door hanger without reading it (unlike flyers)

**Cost:** ~$0.15-0.30 per door hanger (VistaPrint/Canva Print, 250 qty)
**Time per visit:** 3 minutes to hang 5 doors
**ROI:** Land 1 customer from a $45 batch = 18x return in year 1

## PART 3: THE EXECUTION SYSTEM

### The 3-Minute Door Hanger Routine (Do This EVERY Visit)

```
BEFORE cleaning:
1. Park truck (visible from street — it's a billboard)
2. Place yard sign in front yard near sidewalk
3. Start cleaning

AFTER cleaning:
4. Walk 5 houses (2-3 each direction from customer)
5. Hang door hanger on front door handle
6. Pick up yard sign
7. Drive to next job

Total added time: 3 minutes
```

### Which Houses to Target

**Priority 1 (always hang):**
- Immediately adjacent neighbors (left + right)
- House directly across the street
- Any house with visible dog toys, bowls, or fencing

**Priority 2 (if you have extras):**
- Houses 2-3 doors down in each direction
- Corner houses (high visibility)
- Houses with "Beware of Dog" signs (lol, yes, they need you)

### Tracking What Works

Keep a simple count in your phone notes:


**Ask new leads:** "How'd you hear about us?" If they say "saw your door hanger" or "saw you at my neighbor's" — that's the data.

### Recommended First Order

**Start small, test, then scale:**
1. Order **100 of Version A** ("We Were Just Next Door") — ~$25-30
2. Use for 2-3 weeks
3. Track how many leads mention the door hanger
4. If it works (it will), order 500 and add Version B or C to the rotation

### Design Specs for Printing

- **Size:** 4.25" × 11" (standard door hanger)
- **Paper:** 14pt cardstock, gloss or matte (gloss looks more pro)
- **Sides:** 2-sided (front = hook, back = details)
- **Die-cut hole:** Standard 1.5" circle at top (fits door handles)
- **Bleed:** 0.125" on all sides
- **File format:** PDF (300 DPI)

### DIY Option (If You're Broke)

Print on cardstock at home or Staples. Won't look as pro but still works:
1. Use the text above in a Word/Google Doc template
2. Print on 8.5" × 11" cardstock
3. Cut in half (gives you 2 hangers per sheet)
4. Punch hole at top with a hole punch
5. Cost: ~$0.05/hanger + your time

## PART 5: CANVA TEMPLATE LINKS & DESIGN TIPS

### How to Make It in Canva (Free)

1. Go to canva.com → Search "door hanger"
2. Pick any template with the right dimensions (4.25" × 11")
3. Replace text with copy from Version A, B, or C above
4. Use these colors:
   - **Primary:** Forest Green (#2D5F2D) — clean, outdoor, trustworthy
   - **Accent:** White (#FFFFFF) — clean and readable
   - **Pop color:** Gold/Yellow (#FFD700) — for CTAs and pricing
5. Add your logo if you have one
6. Download as PDF Print (300 DPI)
7. Upload to VistaPrint/GotPrint and order

### Design Rules

- **BIG headline** — readable from 3 feet away
- **Phone number HUGE** — biggest thing on the hanger
- **Less is more** — don't cram everything. Front = hook. Back = details.
- **Include a photo** if possible — a clean green yard, your truck, the before/after
- **QR code optional** — some people scan, most won't. Phone number is king.
