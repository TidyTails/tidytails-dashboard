## 🎯 INVESTOR PITCH AMMUNITION

### "Why Now?" Evidence
1. **6 platforms dead/zombie** — Cherry (Founders Fund!), Washe, Wype, NuWash, Squeegy all failed
2. **Spiffy ($90.8M) abandoned consumers** — pivoted entirely to dealer SaaS
3. **MobileWash contracted to 1 city** — was "dominant in SoCal," now NYC-only
4. **Only 2 real consumer marketplaces remain** (Washos + Panda Hub)
5. **$2.6-3.1B market, 11% CAGR** — massive TAM with shrinking competition

### Objection Handling
**"If Spiffy failed with $90M, why will you succeed?"**
- Spiffy owned the labor (W2 → franchise → lawsuits). WAXR is a marketplace — we don't employ detailers, we quality-gate them. Fundamentally different cost structure. Spiffy proved the demand exists; they just chose the wrong fulfillment model.

**"Panda Hub has 12K reviews and Deloitte recognition. Isn't it too late?"**
- Panda Hub lacks AI quality verification, real-time tracking, dynamic pricing, and B2B fleet. They're a booking platform with a checklist. We're a quality-verified marketplace with enterprise. Different tier of product.

**"Why haven't Washos or Panda Hub added these features?"**
- They're built on legacy booking architectures. AI photo verification and dynamic pricing require ground-up engineering. They'd have to rebuild, and their existing detailer base would resist quality gates (lower performers get downranked).

### Key Stat for the Deck
> "Of the 10 mobile detailing platforms launched in the US, 6 are dead, 1 pivoted to B2B, 1 contracted to a single city. Only 2 consumer marketplaces remain — and neither has AI quality verification. The market is $3.1B and growing 11% annually. The timing has never been better."
