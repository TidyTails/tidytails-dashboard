## 📝 Pro Tips

1. **Photos from actual jobs crush stock photos.** Even a quick phone pic of a clean yard beats a perfect stock image.
2. **Respond to every review.** Google weights review responses in ranking.
3. **Post Google Q&As yourself.** Add common questions ("Do you serve Chesterfield?" "How much for 2 dogs?") and answer them. Free SEO.
4. **Check Insights weekly.** GBP shows you how many people saw your profile, what they searched, and what actions they took. This data guides everything.
5. **Photos of YOU build trust.** People want to know who's coming to their yard. Face > logo.
