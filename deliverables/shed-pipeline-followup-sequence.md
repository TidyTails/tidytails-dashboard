## SEQUENCE C: Phone Call Scripts (For High-Value Leads)

### Opening (warm, not scripted-sounding):
> "Hey [Name], it's Darius from TuffShed — you'd reached out about a [shed/garage]. Just wanted to give you a quick call instead of another text. Got about 2 minutes?"

### Key Talking Points:
1. **Ask about their project** — "What are you trying to solve? Garage overflow? Need a workshop?"
2. **Match to product** — "Based on what you're telling me, a [10x12/12x16/etc.] would be perfect for that"
3. **Handle price concern** — "The 0% for 6 months means you're basically getting a free loan. And if you want longer, we've got 10-year financing too"
4. **Create urgency (honest)** — "Lumber costs are going up this year, so current pricing is actually the best it's gonna be. And our install calendar fills fast March through June"
5. **Soft close** — "Want me to put together a quick quote? Takes me about 5 minutes and there's zero obligation"

### Common Objections + Responses:

**"I need to talk to my wife/husband"**
> "Totally get it. What if I put together the quote with a couple options so you have something to show them? Makes the conversation easier than just a number."

**"I'm still shopping around"**
> "Smart move. What are you comparing against? [If big box] — just keep in mind most of those are kit builds. Ours come fully installed, professional grade. But I'm biased 😄"

**"It's more than I expected"**
> "Yeah, I hear that a lot upfront. But break it down monthly — at $150-200/mo with 0% interest, most people find it's less than their streaming subscriptions combined. And this actually adds value to your property."

**"I want to wait for a sale"**
> "I get it. Our last cash discount ended in February. Next one's probably Memorial Day at the earliest. But here's the thing — lumber prices are trending up, so even with a discount later, the base price might be higher. The 0% financing right now is honestly just as good."

**"HOA might not allow it"**
> "Let me help with that. Most HOAs just need a plan drawing and specs — I can get you those. And most of the time it's approved, they just want to see the footprint and materials."
