# Tidy Tails Video Ad Prompts — March 2026

## VIDEO 1: "Get Your Yard Back" (Lifestyle/Freedom)

### Frame 1 — The Shame
A 30-something woman standing at her back sliding glass door, looking out at a neglected suburban backyard scattered with dog waste. A golden retriever sits beside her. Overcast sky, muted cool lighting. St. Louis-style brick ranch house, wooden privacy fence, mature oak trees. Shot from inside the house looking out, shallow depth of field on the woman's face showing guilt and avoidance. Realistic, cinematic, slightly desaturated color palette. Green grass with scattered brown spots. 16:9 aspect ratio.

### Frame 2 — The Relief
Same St. Louis suburban backyard, now bathed in warm golden hour light. A uniformed technician in a branded green polo shirt is mid-cleanup with a professional rake and collection bag. The golden retriever watches curiously from the patio. The yard is half-cleaned — one side still messy, the other pristine green. Camera angle from the patio looking across the yard at a slight low angle. Warm, hopeful lighting. The woman watches from the doorway with visible relief. Realistic cinematic style. 16:9 aspect ratio.

### Frame 3 — The Joy
Same backyard, now fully alive. Golden hour sunlight streaming through mature trees. Two kids running barefoot on pristine green grass, the golden retriever chasing them. A man tends a smoking BBQ grill. String lights hang across the fence. A woman laughs from a patio chair. St. Louis brick ranch house in background. Everything warm, vibrant, saturated. The yard is immaculate — thick green grass, no waste anywhere. Wide shot capturing the full scene of a family reclaiming their outdoor space. Realistic but slightly cinematic warmth. 16:9 aspect ratio.

## VIDEO 2: "The Party Panic" (Urgency/Event)

### Frame 1 — The Panic
Over-the-shoulder shot of a man in his kitchen holding his phone, looking through the window at a messy suburban backyard littered with dog waste. Morning light, slightly harsh. A mixed-breed dog sits on the patio outside. The phone screen shows a calendar notification. The man's body language shows tension and dread. St. Louis suburban home, wooden privacy fence, mature trees, brick exterior. Realistic, slightly anxious mood. Cool morning color palette. 16:9 aspect ratio.

### Frame 2 — The Save
A branded work truck/van (clean, professional, green and white) parked in the driveway of the same St. Louis suburban home. A uniformed technician in green polo and cap walks through the side gate carrying professional pet waste removal equipment. The mixed-breed dog watches from the yard excitedly. Mid-morning light, brightening. The man watches from the kitchen window with hope. Sense of the cavalry arriving. Realistic cinematic style. 16:9 aspect ratio.

### Frame 3 — The Celebration
Same backyard transformed into a lively afternoon party. Pristine green grass, not a single spot of waste. Folding tables with food, coolers, string lights. Eight to ten adults laughing and socializing. Kids and the mixed-breed dog playing on the clean grass. The man from frame 1 is now relaxed, holding a drink, smiling proudly. Late afternoon golden light. Warm, celebratory, vibrant colors. St. Louis suburban vibes — brick ranch, privacy fence, big trees. Wide cinematic shot. 16:9 aspect ratio.
