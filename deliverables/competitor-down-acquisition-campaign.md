## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Don't trash the competition** — "they shut down" is fact, not shade. Keep it classy.
2. **Post at different times** — spread posts across a week, not all at once
3. **Respond to EVERY comment** — even "how much?" deserves a personal reply within 1 hour
4. **Use photos** — attach a before/after yard photo if you have one. Posts with images get 2-3x engagement
5. **Track which platform drives leads** — ask new customers "how'd you find us?"
