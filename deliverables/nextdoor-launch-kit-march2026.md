## 🔑 KEY DIFFERENCES: Nextdoor vs Facebook vs Instagram

| Factor | Nextdoor | Facebook | Instagram |
|--------|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Audience** | Verified neighbors only | Anyone | Anyone |
| **Trust level** | Very high (real names, addresses) | Medium | Low |
| **Best content** | Helpful, community-focused | Entertaining, visual | Visual, aesthetic |
| **Selling style** | Soft, neighborly | Direct ads OK | Aspirational |
| **Competition** | Low (most businesses don't use it) | Saturated | Saturated |
| **Cost** | Free (organic), cheap (deals) | Pay to play | Pay to play |
| **Best for** | Local home services ✅ | Brand awareness | Brand building |

**Bottom line:** Nextdoor is the HIGHEST trust, LOWEST competition platform for exactly what Tidy Tails does. This should be a priority channel alongside Google and Facebook.
