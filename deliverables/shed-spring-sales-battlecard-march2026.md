## 🤝 FINANCING — Your Closing Weapons

### Option 1: 0% Interest for 6 Months (Service Finance)
- **Best for:** Buyers who can pay off quickly
- **Pitch:** *"Zero interest. Six months. If you pay it off in that window, you literally paid nothing extra. It's the best deal in the shed business right now."*
- **Fine print:** Interest accrues during promo but waived if paid in full. 10% down required.

### Option 2: 9.99% Fixed APR for up to 120 Months (Service Finance)
- **Best for:** Buyers who want low monthly payments
- **Monthly payment examples:**
  - $5,000 shed → ~$66/mo for 120 months
  - $7,500 shed → ~$99/mo for 120 months
  - $10,000 shed → ~$132/mo for 120 months
- **Pitch:** *"Less than your phone bill for a building that lasts 20+ years. And it adds value to your property."*
- **Fine print:** Credit check required. Loans up to $100K. 10% down.

### Option 3: RTO National — Variable APR up to 60 Months
- **Best for:** Traditional loan people
- **APR:** As low as 9.99%
- **Pitch:** *"Standard loan, straightforward terms. Up to $20K financed."*
- **Fine print:** Renters OK. 10% down required.

### Option 4: RTO National — Flex Lease (Rent-to-Own)
- **Best for:** Credit-challenged buyers
- **Pitch:** *"No credit check. Low deposit. You rent it, and at the end, it's yours. Perfect if you're not sure about going all-in right away."*
- **⚠️ Reality check:** RTO total cost is significantly higher (can be 2x+ purchase price). Use this as last resort to close.
- **Fine print:** Select shed models only. Up to $20K.
