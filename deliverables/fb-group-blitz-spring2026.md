## Step 4: Posting Rules

1. **Rotate posts** — don't use the same copy in every group. Alternate A, B, C
2. **Space them out** — post to 5-10 groups per sitting, spread over 2-3 days
3. **Check group rules** — some groups don't allow services. If they do, post. If not, skip.
4. **Respond to EVERY comment/DM within 1 hour** — speed kills in lead response
5. **Repost every 2-3 weeks** with fresh photos

1. Reply within 1 hour (seriously, speed matters)

4. After free cleanup: "How'd it look? Want me to come weekly? It's $70/month and I'll keep it clean so you never have to think about it."
