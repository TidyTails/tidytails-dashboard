## Step 1: Get Your Google Review Link (5 minutes)

### Option A: If You Have Google Business Profile
1. Go to https://business.google.com
2. Click your business listing
3. Click "Ask for reviews" or "Get more reviews"
4. Google gives you a direct link — COPY THIS
5. Save it somewhere easy (Notes app, pinned text)

### Option B: Create the Link Manually
Your link format: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID`

To find your Place ID:
1. Go to https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-id
2. Search "Tidy Tails STL" or your business name
3. Copy the Place ID
4. Plug it into the URL above

### Option C: Shortcut
Just search "Tidy Tails pet waste removal St Louis" on Google. If your GBP shows up, there's a "Write a review" button. That URL is your review link.

**⚠️ If you don't have a Google Business Profile yet, STOP and set that up first.** That's the 5 AM task from today — it's the #1 highest-ROI action.

```
If you've been happy with the service, a quick Google review would help me reach more dog owners in [THEIR NEIGHBORHOOD]. No pressure at all: [LINK]
```

### Text Template #3: The Casual Ask (Best for laid-back customers)

```
Hey [NAME]! Random ask — you mind throwing me a Google review when you get a sec? Trying to get the word out this spring and reviews are huge. [LINK]

Appreciate you! 🐕
```

### Pro Tips for Texting:
- **Send between 10 AM - 7 PM** (not too early, not too late)
- **Personalize each one** — mention their dog's name if you know it
- **Don't send all 5 at once** — spread over 2-3 days so reviews look natural to Google
- **Follow up once** if no response after 3-4 days (see templates below)

## What GOOD Reviews Look Like (Coach Your Customers)

You can't write reviews for people, but you CAN suggest what to mention. When asking, add:

```
If you're not sure what to write, just mentioning what neighborhood you're in 
and what you like about the service helps a lot!
```

**Why?** Reviews that mention specific neighborhoods ("Great service in Kirkwood!") help you rank for those local searches. Reviews that mention specific services ("weekly cleanup for our 2 dogs") help Google understand what you offer.

### Dream Review Example:
> "TJ from Tidy Tails has been cleaning our backyard in Webster Groves every week for the past 3 months. With two labs, our yard was a disaster zone before. Now it's always clean when the kids want to play. Super reliable, fair pricing, and way cheaper than the big companies. Highly recommend!" ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

That one review contains: neighborhood name, service frequency, problem solved, dog count, reliability, pricing comparison, and recommendation. SEO gold.
