**Why it works:** Same hook concept that got 47%, compressed to 12 sec, real person on camera

### Ad Concept 2: "The Shoe Test" (8-10 sec)
**Hook (0-2 sec):** Close-up shot of a shoe stepping near poop in grass (NOT in it — keep it clean)
**Body (2-7 sec):** "If you've ever had THAT moment in your yard... we fix that. Every week. $17.50."
**CTA (7-10 sec):** "First visit free. Message us 👇"
**Why it works:** Visual hook = pattern interrupt. Universal relatable moment. Price shown = qualifies leads.

### Ad Concept 3: "Before/After Split" (10 sec)
**Hook (0-2 sec):** Split screen — left side: yard with visible poop. Right side: same yard, clean.
**Body (2-7 sec):** "This is what $17.50 a week looks like. We come, we scoop, you enjoy your yard."
**CTA (7-10 sec):** "First visit's on us. Tap Message 👇"
**Why it works:** Visual proof > words. Demonstrates the transformation. Simple.

### Ad Concept 4: "Do The Math" (12 sec) — Jamie Version
**Hook (0-2 sec):** Jamie smiling to camera: "How much is your time worth?"
**Body (2-9 sec):** "An hour every weekend picking up poop? Or $17.50 and we handle it. I'm Jamie. I come every week, same day, same time. Your yard stays clean."
**CTA (9-12 sec):** "First visit free. Message us!"
**Why it works:** Jamie's face = trust. "Same person every time" is the #1 differentiator vs franchises.

### Ad Concept 5: "The Numbers" (8 sec) — Text-Only Style
**Hook (0-2 sec):** Bold text on screen: "YOUR DOG POOPS 730 TIMES A YEAR"
**Body (2-6 sec):** "That's 730 land mines in your yard. We remove them all. Weekly. $17.50."
**CTA (6-8 sec):** "First visit FREE → Message us"
**Why it works:** The "Quick Math" angle as pure text = ultra cheap to make, no filming needed. Great for testing.

## Filming Tips (For TJ/Jamie)

### Equipment
- **Phone camera** is fine (real > polished)
- **Film HORIZONTAL** (Facebook feed = landscape/square preferred)
- **Natural lighting** (morning or late afternoon, avoid harsh noon sun)
- **NO CapCut watermark** — use free version of InShot or just phone's built-in editor

### The Rules
1. **First 2 seconds = EVERYTHING.** If you don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.
2. **Keep it under 15 seconds.** Your data proves nobody watches past 12.
3. **Real footage > AI footage.** Every time. People can tell.
4. **Show your face.** Trust is everything in a service business.
5. **End with a CLEAR CTA.** "Message us" button > "Text TIDY to..." (less friction)

### What NOT To Do
- ❌ No CapCut templates/watermarks
- ❌ No AI-generated imagery
- ❌ No videos over 15 seconds
- ❌ No "text to a number" CTA (use Facebook's built-in Message button)
- ❌ No music louder than voice
- ❌ No landscape shots without a person
