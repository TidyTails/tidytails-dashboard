# 🐕💩 Tidy Tails Referral Program — Spring 2026

## The Strategy

**Why now:** 8 customers × 2 referrals each = 16 new leads. Even at 50% conversion, that's 8 new customers — putting us at 16 and halfway to goal. Spring is when neighbors are outside noticing each other's yards. Strike now.

**Program name:** "Good Neighbor" Referral Program

**The deal:**
- **Referrer gets:** FREE week of service ($17.50 value) for each successful referral
- **New customer gets:** First cleanup FREE + first month at $60 (normally $70)
- **Both win.** Low cost to TJ (~$27.50 per acquired customer vs. $50+ for paid ads)

**Why this structure beats cash discounts:**
- Free service week costs TJ ~15 min of labor, not cash out of pocket
- New customer gets to try before full commitment
- $60 intro month creates urgency ("lock in this rate")
- After month 1, normal $70/mo pricing kicks in

## Phase 3: Neighborhood Blitz (Optional — Higher Effort)

For each of TJ's current 8 service addresses:
1. Identify 5-10 neighboring houses with dogs (look for fences, dog doors, toys in yard)
2. Leave a door hanger or card at each one
3. Reference the neighbor: "We already service a yard on your street!"

**Door hanger one-liner:**
> "We already keep your neighbor's yard poop-free. Want in? First cleanup's on us. Text TJ: [PHONE]"

This is powerful because social proof is LOCAL. "Someone on MY street uses this" hits different than a Facebook ad.
