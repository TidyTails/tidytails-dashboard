## 🎯 Review Best Practices for Jamie

1. **Timing is everything** — Ask RIGHT AFTER service, same day. Customer satisfaction peaks when the yard is fresh.
2. **Be personal** — Use their name and dog's name. "Hey Sarah! Hope Luna's enjoying the clean yard" hits different than "Please review us."
3. **One at a time** — Don't mass-text all 10 customers in one day. Google flags sudden review spikes. 2-3 per week is perfect.
4. **Never incentivize** — Don't offer discounts for reviews (Google TOS violation). The ask itself is enough.
5. **Respond to EVERY review** — When someone leaves a review, reply within 24h. Shows we're active and appreciative.
6. **If someone says no** — That's fine! Never push. "No worries at all!" and move on.
