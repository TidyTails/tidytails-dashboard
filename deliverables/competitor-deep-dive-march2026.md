## ⚔️ TIDY TAILS COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES (What to Lean Into)

### 1. **Price Transparency**
Most competitors hide pricing. Post yours everywhere. "$15/week" in every ad.

### 2. **Personal Touch**
You're TJ, not "Technician #47 from Authority Brands." Customers remember names. Poopy Paws wins on this — "Charlie" is in every review.

### 3. **No Contracts**
Industry standard but still worth emphasizing. Poop 2 Scoop and POOP 911 both push this.

### 4. **One-Time Cleanups**
Poopy Paws DOESN'T do these. That's a conversion funnel: one-time → recurring. Offer them.

### 5. **Spring Timing**
March in STL = thaw. Every yard that was ignored all winter needs a cleanup NOW. One-time "Spring Cleanup Special" converts to weekly.

### 6. **Modern Marketing**
Yucko's website is from 1998. DoodyCalls is corporate. Poop 2 Scoop hasn't updated since 2024. Fresh social media content + video (from today's earlier scripts) = instant differentiation.

### 7. **Speed**
None of these competitors advertise response time. "Same-week service" or "48-hour first cleanup" would be unique.
