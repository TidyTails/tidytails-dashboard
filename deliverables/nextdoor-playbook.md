## Step 1: Claim Your Business Page (10 minutes)

### Go to: https://nextdoor.com/create-business

**What you need:**
- Personal Nextdoor account first (real name + home address)
- Then claim/create "TJ's Tidy Tails" as a business
- Category: **Pet Waste Removal** or **Pet Services**
- Add: phone (314-850-7140), website (tidytailsstl.com), service area

**Business description (copy-paste this):**
> TJ's Tidy Tails is your local pet waste removal service in St. Louis County. We handle the dirty work so you can enjoy your yard again. Weekly service, flat-rate pricing ($70/month for most yards), no contracts. Locally owned and operated — not a franchise. Call or text 314-850-7140 for a free quote.

**Pro tip:** Your business page won't be visible to neighbors until you get at least 1 recommendation. That's Step 2.

## Step 4: Organic Engagement (Free, Unlimited)

Beyond your 2 monthly posts, you can engage in community conversations FOR FREE:

### Monitor These Topics:
- "Dog walker recommendations"
- "Pet services"
- "Yard cleanup"
- "Moving to the area with pets"
- Anyone complaining about dog poop in parks, sidewalks, neighbor's yards

### How to Engage:
- **Don't sell.** Be helpful first. Answer questions about dogs, yards, local pet resources.
- When someone asks for pet service recs, mention what you do naturally.
- Comment on dog-related posts (lost dogs, dog park discussions, pet events).
- **Goal:** Be the person everyone thinks of when they think "dog + yard."

### Reply Template (when someone asks for recommendations):
> Hey! I run Tidy Tails — local pet waste removal right here in [neighborhood]. Happy to help if you need a hand with yard cleanup. Feel free to text me at 314-850-7140. First visit is free!

## Step 5: Paid Ads (When Ready — Optional)

### Nextdoor Ad Pricing:
- **Neighborhood-only ad:** As low as $1.51 per ad
- **Broader reach:** ~$20 per 1,000 impressions (CPM)
- **CPC (cost per click):** $2-5 for local campaigns
- **Budget:** You control it. Can start at $25-50/month.

### Why Nextdoor Ads Beat Facebook/Google for You:
| Factor | Nextdoor | Facebook | Google |
|--------|----------|----------|--------|
| Audience | Verified homeowners | Anyone | Anyone searching |
| Trust level | Neighbor recommendations | Low (ad blindness) | Medium |
| Cost | $1.51-$5/click | $5-15/click | $10-25/click |
| Setup time | 5 minutes | 30+ minutes | Complex |
| Local targeting | By neighborhood | By zip code | By keyword |

**Recommendation:** Start FREE (Steps 1-4). If you're getting traction after 2-3 weeks, try a $50 paid campaign targeting 3-5 neighborhoods near your current customers.
