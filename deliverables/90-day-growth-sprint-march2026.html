Channel Ranking by ROI
Ranked by expected cost per acquired customer
🥇 #1: Customer Referrals — $0 CAC
Your 8 current customers are your best salespeople. A $25 credit per referral costs way less than any ad. Industry data: referred customers have 37% higher retention.
Action: Text each customer a personal referral ask. Script in Neighborhood Saturation Kit.
🥈 #2: Facebook Ads — ~$20-60 CAC
The proven #1 channel for pet waste removal. Low competition in STL. Educates people that the service exists (most don't know). Start at $5/day.
Best targeting: Dog owners + Advantage+ in STL County, 25-65 age. Use phone photos, not stock images.
🥉 #3: Nextdoor — $0 CAC
Highest-trust local platform. Free business page + neighborhood recommendations. Pet-related posts get 3x engagement.
Action: Create business page, post weekly. Use scripts from Nextdoor Kit.
4. Door Hangers / Yard Signs — ~$5-15 CAC
Drop in neighborhoods where you already service. Near-zero marginal cost per new customer on existing routes.
Focus: Florissant (63031/63033) — 3 customers already there = social proof.
5. Google Ads — ~$40-80 CAC
Captures high-intent searchers. Home services CPC averages $7.85 nationally, but pet waste is a niche with LOWER competition = likely $2-5 CPC in STL. Complements FB (people see FB ad → Google you later).
Top keywords: "pooper scooper st louis", "pet waste removal near me", "dog poop pickup service"
6. Facebook Groups — $0 CAC (time cost)
Good for brand awareness but harder to track. Post in STL pet/neighborhood groups 2-3x/week. Don't spam.
7. Google Business Profile + SEO — $0 (long game)
Critical foundation but takes 3-6 months to rank. Do it NOW so it pays off later. Key: get 10+ Google reviews ASAP — reviews are the #1 local ranking factor.
⚠️ Channels NOT Worth It Right Now
• Instagram Ads — Not enough social proof yet. Build organic first.
• Google Local Services Ads — Pet waste removal isn't a supported category in most markets.
• Direct mail — Too expensive at this scale ($0.50-1.00 per piece, low response rate).
• Commercial leads — Paused per TJ. Revisit at 20+ residential customers.

Sprint Rules — Non-Negotiable
Break these and the sprint fails
Rule #1: No more planning without doing.
We have 50+ kits, guides, and playbooks. The problem isn't knowledge — it's execution. This sprint is about DOING, not reading more strategies.
Rule #2: Fix the SSL cert this week. Period.
It's been expired 26 days. Every day it stays broken is a day Google can't find you. 15 minutes in GoDaddy. Do it today.
Rule #3: Never pause ads once started.
Even $5/day. Consistency beats intensity. Pausing resets the algorithm and wastes the learning period. Set it and let it run for 2+ weeks before judging.
Rule #4: Respond to leads within 5 minutes.
Speed to lead matters more than anything. The first company to respond gets the customer 78% of the time. Turn on phone notifications for FB messages and form submissions.
Rule #5: Track everything in one place.
Keep a simple spreadsheet: Lead name, source (FB/Google/Nextdoor/referral), date, status (new/quoted/closed/lost). Can't improve what you can't measure.
Rule #6: One new customer per week minimum.
17 customers in 12 weeks = ~1.4/week. Round up: if you're not gaining at least 1 per week, something is broken. Diagnose and fix immediately.
Rule #7: Ask for reviews after EVERY completed service.
Google reviews are the #1 local ranking factor. Goal: 1 new review per week. By end of sprint: 12+ reviews. Send the review link by text right after service.
