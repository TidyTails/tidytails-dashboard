## 📞 THE CALL — APARTMENT COMPLEXES

### Opening (10 seconds)
> "Hi, this is TJ with Tidy Tails — we're a local pet waste removal company here in St. Louis. I'm looking for whoever handles your grounds maintenance or vendor services. Is that you, or could you point me to the right person?"

### If Transferred / Right Person:
> "Hey [Name], thanks for taking a minute. I'll be quick — I know you're busy.
>
> I noticed [Property Name] is pet-friendly, which is awesome. We work with apartment communities to handle pet waste in common areas — dog parks, walking paths, green spaces.
>
> Quick question: how do you handle pet waste cleanup right now? Is that your maintenance team, or do you have a service?"

### Listen & Respond:

**If "maintenance team handles it":**
> "That's what most properties do, and it works — but I hear from a lot of property managers that their crew would rather spend that 5-10 hours a week on actual maintenance. We take that completely off their plate. Weekly service, we bag everything and take it off-site. Starts around $150-250/month depending on the property."

**If "we use a service":**
> "Nice, good to know. If you're ever looking to compare or your current vendor falls through, I'd love to be your backup option. We're local, owner-operated, and we don't require contracts. Can I at least drop off a card?"

**If "not really an issue for us":**
> "That's great — sounds like your residents are pretty responsible. Even so, I'd love to do a quick drive-by. A lot of properties don't realize how much accumulates in the corners and edges of dog parks. Totally free, no commitment — just a 15-minute walk and I'll email you what I find. Worst case, I confirm you don't need us."

### The Close (Get the Walkthrough):
> "Here's what I'd suggest — let me swing by for a free walkthrough. Takes about 15 minutes. I'll walk your grounds, identify the problem areas, and put together a custom quote. If it makes sense, great. If not, no hard feelings. What day works this week?"

### If Right Person:
> "Thanks, [Name]. Real quick — pet waste in common areas is one of the most common complaints in HOA communities. Board members hate dealing with it, residents hate stepping in it, and fines don't actually solve the problem.
>
> We're a simple, affordable fix. We come through weekly, clean every common area, and take everything off-site. One flat monthly rate per community. No contracts.
>
> How many of your communities are pet-friendly?"

### Then:

### The Close:
> "Which community would be the best fit for a free walkthrough? I'll put together a proposal and you can present it to the board."

## 🔥 OBJECTION HANDLING

### "What's the cost?"
> "It depends on the property — size, frequency, what areas we're covering. For a ballpark: smaller communities start around $150/month, mid-size around $250, larger properties $400+. But I'd rather give you an exact number after seeing your grounds. Free walkthrough, 15 minutes."

### "We already have someone / don't need it"
> "Totally fair. Can I at least leave a card in case your situation changes? We've had a few competitor companies shut down recently in St. Louis, so we're getting a lot of calls from properties whose old vendor disappeared."

### "I need to check with my manager / the board"
> "Absolutely. Want me to email you a one-page overview you can forward? It's got pricing ranges, what's included, and a before/after from one of our properties. Makes it easy to share."

### "That sounds expensive"
> "I get it. Here's how most managers think about it: your maintenance crew is probably spending 5-10 hours a week on this. At $15-20/hour, that's $300-800/month in labor. We do it better for less, and your crew focuses on what they're actually trained for."

### "We use fines / DNA testing"
> "Some communities try that — but honestly, it costs more per year than professional cleanup, it antagonizes residents, and you still have waste on the ground until someone tests it. We just... remove it. Simpler, cheaper, and nobody gets a fine letter."
