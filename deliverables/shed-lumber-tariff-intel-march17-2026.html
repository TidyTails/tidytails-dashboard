📈 Why Prices Are Rising — The 4 Forces

  • Tariff stack: ~35% Canadian countervailing duties + 10% Section 232 (Oct 2025) = ~45% on imported lumber. This is the highest in modern history.
  • Supply crunch: Mill closures across North America removing 1.3 billion board feet this year. Less wood = higher prices.
  • Housing recovery: Multifamily starts surged 29.1%, total residential up 7.2%. More building = more demand for lumber.
  • Global costs: Rising energy costs from Middle East tensions pushing up transport and shipping for timber worldwide.

🔮 Where Prices Are Heading

  • May 2026 futures: $598/MBF — market betting prices stay high
  • 2027 futures: Even higher — "progressively higher levels" per CME data
  • Analysts: "Shift toward supply-constrained environment" that won't ease even with high mortgage rates
  • Political wild card: Trump admin seeking new tariffs to close $1.6T revenue gap (LA Times, March 15). Lumber already targeted. Could go HIGHER.

🏠 What This Means for Shed Prices

  • Lumber = 40-60% of shed material cost. A 22% lumber surge means shed prices WILL increase.
  • TuffShed/WoodMaster haven't repriced yet — current inventory is still at old lumber costs. This is the window.
  • Competitors running spring sales: French Creek doing 20-30% off through March 31 — they're trying to move inventory before repricing too.
  • No active TuffShed promo since the $1K-off ended Feb 11. Next likely: Memorial Day (May 25).
  • Bottom line: Today's price is genuinely tomorrow's discount. This isn't a sales tactic — it's math.
