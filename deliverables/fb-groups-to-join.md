# 📱 STL Facebook Groups to Post In

**Strategy:** Join these groups, be helpful for a few days, THEN post about Tidy Tails. Don't lead with an ad — lead with value.

---

## High-Priority Groups (Dog Owner Communities)

### 1. St. Louis Dog Owners / STL Dog Parents
**Search:** "St. Louis dogs" on Facebook
**Why:** Direct audience — people who own dogs and talk about dog stuff
**Post angle:** "Hey fellow dog parents — anyone else's yard look like a disaster after winter?"

### 2. Florissant Community / Florissant Neighbors
**Why:** 3 of TJ's 8 customers are in Florissant. Proven market.
**Post angle:** "Local service recommendation" style — let existing customers vouch

### 3. O'Fallon / St. Peters / St. Charles County Groups
**Why:** Growing suburbs, lots of new homeowners with dogs
**Post angle:** "Just launched service in the area" — newness creates curiosity

### 4. STL Recommendations / St. Louis Recommendations
**Why:** Huge groups (50K+) where people ask for local service providers
**Post angle:** Wait for someone to ask about yard services or pet services, then reply

### 5. Maryland Heights / Bridgeton Community Groups
**Why:** Service area, not yet penetrated with customers
**Post angle:** Spring cleanup offer

---

## Posting Rules (Don't Get Banned)
1. **Read group rules first** — some ban business posts
2. **Join and engage for 2-3 days** before posting anything business-related
3. **Reply to others' posts** — "I actually do this!" is better than cold posting
4. **One business post per group per week MAX**
5. **Use personal profile**, not a business page (more trust)
6. **Spring cleanup offer** ($79) is the hook — it's a service, not a pitch

> Hey! I run Tidy Tails — local pet waste removal. I serve [THEIR AREA] and I'm running a spring cleanup special right now ($79 for a full yard reset). Happy to help! Text me at 314-850-7140 or check out tidytailsstl.com 🐕
