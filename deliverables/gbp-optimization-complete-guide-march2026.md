**3-Star or Below Response:**

### Step 4: Ongoing Review Strategy

- **Ask within 2 hours of service** — conversion rate drops 80% after 24 hours
- **Ask via text, not email** — 98% open rate vs 20% for email
- **Don't ask every week** — once per customer, then only if they seem extra happy
- **Never offer incentives for reviews** — Google WILL penalize you
- **Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours** — Google tracks response rate

### Priority Customer List for First Reviews
1. Customer who's been with you longest (most loyalty)
2. Customer who's said something positive recently
3. Any friend/family who's used the service
4. Anyone who responded positively to a recent text
5. One-time deep clean customer who was thrilled
