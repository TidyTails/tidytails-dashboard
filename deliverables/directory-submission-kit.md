## ✅ DIRECTORY CHECKLIST

### 🟢 TIER 1 — Do These First (Highest Impact)

#### 1. Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps)
- **URL:** https://businessconnect.apple.com
- **Why:** Every iPhone user's default maps app. ~55% of US smartphone users.
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign in with Apple ID
  2. Search for your business or click "Add New"
  3. Fill in all fields from copy-paste block above
  4. Upload logo + 3-5 photos
  5. Add services with pricing
  6. Verify (phone or postcard)
- **Status:** ☐ Not started

#### 2. Bing Places for Business
- **URL:** https://www.bingplaces.com
- **Why:** Powers Bing, Cortana, and many voice search results. Can import from Google.
- **Time:** 5 min (import from GBP) or 10 min (manual)
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign in with Microsoft account
  2. Choose "Import from Google Business Profile" (fastest!)
  3. Review imported info, fix any gaps
  4. Verify
- **Status:** ☐ Not started

#### 3. Facebook Business Page
- **URL:** https://www.facebook.com/pages/create
- **Why:** Social proof + shows up in Facebook searches. Enables Marketplace posting too.
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Steps:**
  1. Create page as "Local Business"
  2. Category: "Pet Service"
  3. Fill all fields from copy-paste block
  4. Upload profile pic (logo) + cover photo
  5. Add services with pricing
  6. Fill "About" with long description
  7. Add service area
  8. Enable messaging
- **Note:** If page already exists, skip to optimization
- **Status:** ☐ Not started

#### 4. Nextdoor Business Page
- **URL:** https://business.nextdoor.com
- **Why:** THE platform for local service discovery. Neighbors trust Nextdoor recommendations.
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Steps:**
  1. Create free business page
  2. Category: Pet Services
  3. Fill all fields
  4. Add service area (specific neighborhoods)
  5. Respond to any pet-related posts in your neighborhoods
- **Status:** ☐ Not started

#### 5. Thumbtack
- **URL:** https://www.thumbtack.com/join
- **Why:** Lead gen platform — people actively searching for pet waste removal.
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign up as a pro
  2. Service: "Pet Waste Removal" (may be under Lawn Care or Pet Services)
  3. Set service area
  4. Set pricing
  5. Free profile — you only pay if you respond to leads
- **⚠️ Note:** Thumbtack charges per lead you respond to. Set a weekly budget cap.
- **Status:** ☐ Not started
