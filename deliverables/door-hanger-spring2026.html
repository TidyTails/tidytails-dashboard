🖨️ Tidy Tails Door Hangers — Print Guide

  • Paper: Print on 8.5" × 11" cardstock (110lb recommended, 65lb works)
  • Settings: Landscape NOT needed — prints 2-up on portrait letter
  • Scale: Set to 100% or "Actual Size" — do NOT "Fit to Page"
  • Sheet 1 = FRONT (green side, customer-facing)
  • Sheet 2 = BACK (white side, info/testimonial)
  • Cut: Cut along dashed borders. Cut the door hole at top.
  • QR Code: Replace the placeholder with a real QR → tidytailsstl.com (use qr-code-generator.com)
  • Quantity: 100 hangers = 50 sheets × 2-sided. FedEx/Staples ~$0.30/sheet = ~$15 for 100 hangers.
  • Best neighborhoods: Kirkwood, Webster Groves, Olivette, Florissant, Ballwin
