⚙️ Step 1: Get Your Google Review Link

You need this link before sending ANY review texts. Here's how:

  1. Open business.google.com on your phone
  2. Select "Tidy Tails" from your businesses
  3. Look for "Get more reviews" or "Ask for reviews"
  4. Copy the short URL Google gives you
  5. Paste it below 👇

📋 Paste Your Review Link Here

🔗 Alternative: Manual Review Link Format

If you can't find the "Get more reviews" button, you can create the link manually:

Google Review URL Format
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID

To find your Place ID:

  1. Go to Google Place ID Finder
  2. Search "Tidy Tails St Louis"
  3. Copy the Place ID
  4. Replace YOUR_PLACE_ID in the URL above

📱 Quick Tip: Shorten Your Link

Long Google URLs look sketchy in texts. Use tinyurl.com to shorten it. Even better: if your website is working, create a redirect page at tidytailsstl.com/review that redirects to Google.

5-Star Response Template
