## 📊 Posting Best Practices

**Facebook:**
- Post between 9-11 AM or 6-8 PM (peak engagement)
- Always include a photo — text-only posts get buried
- Reply to EVERY comment within 1 hour
- Join local Facebook groups (STL Dog Owners, Florissant Community, etc.) and be helpful before promoting

**Instagram:**
- Reels get 3-5x more reach than static posts
- Use 8-15 hashtags (mix popular + niche)
- Post Stories daily if possible (even just a quick yard pic)
- Engage with local dog accounts — comment on their posts

**Nextdoor:**
- Questions get the most engagement
- Be a neighbor first, business owner second
- 2 free business posts per month — use them wisely
- Respond to every recommendation request for pet services

**All platforms:**
- Before/after photos are GOLD
- Consistency > perfection — a decent post today beats a perfect post never
- Track which posts get the most calls/messages and do more of those
