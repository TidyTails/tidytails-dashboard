## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Post in Jamie's voice** — warm, approachable, local. Not corporate.
2. **Reply to EVERY comment** on your posts. Even just "Thanks! 🐾" — engagement boosts reach.
3. **Don't delete posts that get low engagement.** The algorithm needs data.
4. **Tag Crestwood Vet's page** in your posts if they have one — they'll likely share it.
5. **Use the event hashtag** if one exists — check Crestwood Vet's social pages.
6. **The Sunday recap is the MOST IMPORTANT post.** It has photos, social proof, and a CTA. Most of your event leads will come from people who see this post, NOT from the event itself.
