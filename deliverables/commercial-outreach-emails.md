## 🎯 OUTREACH PLAYBOOK

### Best Order of Operations:
1. **Call first** (if you have a phone number) — personal touch wins
2. **Email the proposal** within 1 hour of the call
3. **Text** if they asked you to, or if no answer on phone
4. **Follow up** in 3-5 business days (email + text)
5. **Final follow-up** at 10 days — then move to quarterly check-in

### Best Days/Times to Reach Property Managers:
- **Tuesday–Thursday**, 9:00 AM–11:30 AM
- Avoid Mondays (slammed) and Fridays (checked out)
- Don't call during lunch (11:30–1:00)

### What to Say When They Answer:
> "Hey [Name], this is TJ with Tidy Tails. I'm a local pet waste removal service, and I was calling because I noticed [Property Name] is pet-friendly. Do you guys deal with any waste issues in the common areas? ... [Let them talk] ... Yeah, that's exactly what we handle. We come through weekly, clean everything up, restock your stations if you have them, and take all the waste with us. Most properties our size pay around $[X] a month. Would it be worth me doing a quick walkthrough so I can put together an exact number for you?"

### Objection Handlers:
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We handle it in-house" | "Totally get it. What does that cost you in staff time per week? Most properties find we're cheaper than the labor hours — and your team can focus on turns and repairs." |
| "We don't have budget" | "Understood. A lot of our properties actually use this as a pet amenity they can promote in listings. Some even add a small pet rent increase to cover it — residents are happy because the grounds are clean." |
| "Send me info" | "Absolutely — what email should I send it to? I'll include a proposal and some info on what we do. Would it be okay to follow up Thursday?" |
| "Not interested" | "No worries at all. If things change — especially when spring hits and the complaints start — my number's (314) 310-3610. Appreciate your time." |
