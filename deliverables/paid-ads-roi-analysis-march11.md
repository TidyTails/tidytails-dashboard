2. **FB educates and creates demand** — You're showing dog owners a service they didn't know they needed
3. **Visual + emotional** — Photos of clean yards, happy dogs, real people work great
4. **Hyper-local targeting** — Target dog owners within 15 miles of your service area

### How to Set Up (Copy-Paste Ready)

**Campaign Structure:**
1. **Objective:** Leads or Engagement (NOT Awareness or Traffic)
2. **Targeting:** 
   - Location: 15-mile radius around TJ's service area
   - Interest: Dog Lovers, Pet Owners, Dog Parks
   - Age: 25-65
   - Homeowners (if available)
3. **Budget:** $5-10/day to start
4. **Creative tips from Swoop Scoop:**
   - Use real photos taken with your PHONE (not stock images)
   - Open with: "Attention dog owners in [St. Louis County]!"

**Pro Tips:**
- Run "Advantage+" targeting to let Facebook's algorithm find your buyers
- Build a lookalike audience from your existing customer list (even 8 customers works)
- Track conversions properly or you'll burn money

## Channel #2: Google Ads ⚠️ SECOND PRIORITY

### Why Google Is Second (Not First)

**The problem:** People don't search for pooper scooper services in high volume. Google Ads work best for high-intent, high-volume searches. Pet waste removal has:
- Low keyword volume in most markets
- Many searchers looking for DIY tools, not services
- High CPC relative to the niche

**Google's real role:** People see your Facebook ad → later Google your business name → find your website/GBP → sign up. Google is the **conversion point**, not the **discovery point**.

### What It Would Cost

**Industry benchmarks (2025-2026):**
- Home services avg CPC: **$6.55-$7.85**
- Landscaping CPC: **$8.76** (closest comparable)
- Home services avg conversion rate: **10.22%**
- Estimated CPL for pet waste: **$40-80**

**Realistic Tidy Tails budget:**

#### $200/month Google Ads Budget
- ~25-30 clicks at ~$7 CPC
- ~2-3 leads (10% conversion)
- ~1-2 customers
- **Cost per customer: $100-200**
- **12-month ROI: 320%-740%**

Still profitable, but 2-3x more expensive per customer than Facebook.

### Critical Prerequisites (Do NOT Run Google Ads Without These)

1. ❗ **Google Business Profile** — This is NON-NEGOTIABLE. Without GBP, you're invisible in local results. Google Ads + no GBP = burning money.
2. ❗ **Website indexed in Google** — Currently 0 pages indexed. Ads can still work (they bypass organic search), but landing page quality score suffers.
3. ❗ **Conversion tracking** — Set up Google Ads conversion tracking on tidytailsstl.com. Without it, you can't optimize.
4. ❗ **Dedicated landing page** — Don't send ad traffic to your homepage. Create a page specifically for the ad offer.

### Keyword Strategy

**High-intent keywords to bid on:**
- "pet waste removal near me" / "pet waste removal St. Louis"
- "pooper scooper service [city]"
- "dog poop cleanup service St. Louis"
- "yard waste dog St. Louis"

**Keywords to AVOID (waste of money):**
- "pooper scooper" (people looking for the tool, not service)
- "how to clean up dog poop" (DIY intent)
- "dog waste bags" (product, not service)

## Channel #3: Nextdoor Ads ✅ CHEAPEST OPTION

### Why Nextdoor Is Underrated

- **77% of users are homeowners** (your exact customer)
- **Hyper-local by design** — neighbors trust neighbors
- **Dirt cheap:** $1.51/ad for neighborhood-only targeting
- **FREE tier is powerful:** Business page + 2 free posts/month + unlimited comments

### What It Would Cost

- **Free tier:** $0/month — business page, 2 posts, community engagement
- **Paid ads:** $50-100/month gets significant neighborhood reach
- Estimated CPL: **$10-30** (lowest of any paid channel)
- **Cost per customer: $25-75**
- **12-month ROI: 1,020%-3,260%**

### The Catch
- Smaller audience than Facebook
- Can't scale as aggressively
- Best as a supplement, not primary channel

## Channel #4: Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) ❓ MAYBE

### What Are LSAs?
- "Google Guaranteed" badge — builds instant trust
- Pay per LEAD, not per click (much better model)
- Show up at the very TOP of Google results
- Average cost: $15-30 per lead for home services

### The Problem for Pet Waste Removal
- **Pet waste removal is NOT explicitly listed** as an eligible LSA category
- Closest categories: Lawn Care, Pet Services
- TJ would need to apply under "Lawn Care" or similar
- **May require business license/insurance verification**

### If Eligible
- $100-200/month budget
- 3-7 qualified leads per month
- Cost per customer: $50-150
- Worth exploring once GBP is established
