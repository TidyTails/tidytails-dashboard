- [ ] If getting clicks but no bookings, the landing page may need work

### Key Metrics to Watch
| Metric | Good | Okay | Bad |
|--------|------|------|-----|
| Cost per click (CPC) | < $0.80 | $0.80-1.50 | > $1.50 |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | > 2% | 1-2% | < 1% |
| Cost per lead | < $15 | $15-30 | > $30 |
| Messages received/day | 2+ | 1 | 0 |

## 📸 AD CREATIVE IDEAS

You don't need fancy graphics. Real photos convert better for local services.

### Best performing ad images for service businesses:
1. **Before/after split** — dirty yard → clean yard (even just grass)
2. **You working** — TJ in the yard with a scooper, branded shirt if you have one
3. **The truck/equipment** — makes it look professional and real
4. **Dogs being dogs** — cute dog in a clean yard (stock or real)
5. **Text on solid color** — Simple "$100 SPRING DEEP CLEAN" on green background

### Quick Canva templates:
- Go to canva.com → Search "Facebook ad" → Pick 1080x1080
- Green background + white text: "YOUR YARD. CLEAN. $100."
- Add logo if you have a clean PNG version

### Video ad (highest engagement):
- 15-30 second clip of you scooping, time-lapse of yard getting clean
- End screen: "$100 Spring Deep Clean — Text 314-850-7140"
- iPhone quality is fine — authenticity beats production value
