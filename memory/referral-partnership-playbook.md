# 🤝 Referral Partnership Playbook — Florissant & North County
*Created: March 20, 2026 | For Jamie to execute*

## Why This Matters
One vet clinic with a stack of Tidy Tails cards = 3-5 referrals/month passively. ZERO ongoing effort after the initial drop-off. This is the highest-ROI growth channel we haven't touched.

## The Pitch (30 seconds)
> "Hi, I'm Jamie with Tidy Tails — we do pet waste removal right here in Florissant. We'd love to leave some cards for your clients. A lot of dog owners don't even know this service exists, and it's a nice thing to recommend when people ask about yard care. No cost to you — just a stack of cards at the front desk."

## The Offer
- **Leave cards/flyers** at their front desk (we print, they display)
- **Cross-promote on social** — tag each other on Facebook/Instagram
- **10% referral discount** — their clients mention the business name, get $10 off first month
- **Optional:** If they refer 3+ customers, offer their business a free monthly cleanup of their outdoor area
