## The Idea

**MOTRA** = Mobile autonomy maintenance platform
**Tagline:** "Autonomy, Maintained"
**Problem:** Robotaxi depots cost $4-7M/year per 100 vehicles. 80-120 staff sit in warehouses doing work that could be mobile.
**Solution:** Gig economy platform deploying mobile techs to handle turnaround and preventive maintenance on-location — replacing 40-50% of depot operations.

**Positioning (Updated Mar 6):** MOTRA is NOT a cleaning company. It's **depot replacement.**

## Why It's Good

- Waymo: 400K rides/week → 1M/week by end of 2026
- No scaled competitor exists (blue ocean)
- Gig model proven by Uber/DoorDash
- TAM: $547M (2028) → $5.5B (2032)
- AV companies hand-wash at depots — doesn't scale
- Waymo already outsources to Transdev — validates the model
- "Autonomy Infrastructure" = $100B+ investor category forming

## Service Tiers (Focused)

### Tier 1: Vehicle Turnaround (Daily, High Frequency)
- Interior cleaning & sanitization
- Trash removal, quick vacuum, odor neutralization
- Sensor lens cleaning
- Charge level check / charging coordination
- Visual inspection checklist
- **Time:** 5-15 min | **Price:** $12-25

### Tier 2: Mobile Maintenance (Weekly/Monthly)
- Tire service & pressure
- Fluid top-offs
- HVAC filter / wiper blade replacement
- Brake pad inspection (visual)
- Deep interior + full exterior wash
- Detailed sensor cleaning
- **Time:** 30-90 min | **Price:** $45-150

### Emergency Response (On-Demand, Premium)
- Biohazard / spill cleanup: $75-150
- Accident scene support: $100-200

## Depot Operations Breakdown (New Intel, Mar 6)

**7 roles at a robotaxi depot. MOTRA addresses 2 of them mobile:**

| Role | Staff/100 vehicles | MOTRA? |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| Fleet Ops Manager | 2-3 | ❌ Internal |
| AV Technicians | 10-15 | ❌ Depot (sensor calibration rigs) |
| Fleet Maintenance | 15-20 | 🟡 Partial (Tier 1 mobile, Tier 2 depot) |
| **Turnaround Team** | **25-35** | **✅ 100% MOTRA** |
| Remote Assistance | 15-20 | N/A — not in scope |
| Dispatch/Monitoring | 10-15 | N/A — not in scope |
| Safety/Incident | 5-8 | ❌ Depot |

**MOTRA's addressable share: ~40-55 out of 80-120 staff (40-50%)**

### Why Turnaround Grows With Scale
As autonomy tech improves, AV tech and remote ops ratios decrease. But turnaround/cleaning/maintenance **stays constant or increases** — more rides = more wear.

## Economic Value Proposition

**Depot cost per 100 vehicles:** $4.3-7.4M/year
**MOTRA replaces 40-50%:** saves operator $2.3-3.5M/year
**MOTRA's cost to operator:** ~$1.5-2M/year
**Net savings:** $800K-1.5M/year per 100 vehicles

**Plus:** Recovered ride revenue — vehicle earning $30-40/hr spends 2 fewer hours/week at depot = $3,000-4,000/year per vehicle. For 1,000 vehicles = $3-4M.
