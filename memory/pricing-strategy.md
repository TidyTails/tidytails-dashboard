| Competitor | Weekly | Monthly |
|------------|--------|---------|
| **Poop 2 Scoop** | $18 | ~$72 |

| **Tidy Tails** | $25 | **$70** ✅ |

**TJ's weekly is HIGHEST in market. TJ's monthly is LOWEST.**

---

## Pricing Order in All Ads

### ✅ DO THIS:
```
$70/month for a clean yard year-round.
*Also available: $45/biweekly | $25/week priority service*
```

### ❌ NOT THIS:
```
$25/week | $45/biweekly | $70/month
```

---

## Why This Works

1. **First number anchors.** $70/month feels cheap. $25/week feels expensive.
2. **Monthly commitment = stickier customers.** Better LTV.
3. **"Priority service" framing** makes weekly feel premium, not entry-level.
4. **Beats competition** on the metric we lead with.

---

## Pricing Tiers (Full Reference)

| Tier | Price | Positioning |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Monthly | $70 | **Lead with this.** Best value, lowest in market. |
| Biweekly | $45 | Middle option. For smaller yards or budget-conscious. |
| Weekly | $25 | "Priority service" — premium tier for heavy use. |
| One-time | $100 | Deep clean. Good trial conversion path. |

---

## Scripts

**When asked "What's it cost?"**
> "$70 a month for a reliably clean yard. That's less than most streaming subscriptions. We also do biweekly if you have a smaller yard, or weekly priority service if you've got heavy traffic."

**When customer hesitates on $25/week:**
> "The monthly is actually the best deal — $70 for four cleanups. A lot of folks start there."
