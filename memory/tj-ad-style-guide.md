## Voice & Tone

### Core Characteristics
- **Conversational** — talks like a neighbor, not a salesperson
- **Humor** — uses relatable jokes ("gossip at a hair salon", "haven't parked in your garage since '22")
- **No pressure** — almost always includes "No pressure" or similar
- **Problem → Solution** — identifies pain point, then offers the fix
- **Local feel** — personal, reachable, "just neighborly help"

### Emoji Usage
- ✅ or ✔ for benefit checklists
- 💥 for emphasis (FREE delivery)
- 🤑 for financing/money topics
- 🏠 for sheds/buildings
- 💇 for humor
- Uses sparingly — not overdone

## Ad Structures That Work

```
PSA: If you haven't parked in your garage since '22...
...it's time for a shed.
✅ Rent-to-own ✅ Traditional financing ✅ FREE delivery
No judgment—just neighborly help (and more space for your car).
```

## Title Patterns (Marketplace)
- Include size: "10x16 Utility Shed"
- Include monthly payment: "$208/mo"
- Use cases in title: "CUSTOM Workshop, Office, Mancave, Home Gym"

---

## Key Selling Points (Always Mention)
1. **FREE delivery** (& setup)
2. **Financing options** (rent-to-own, traditional, no credit check)
3. **Local/personal** (easy to reach, see in person)
4. **Custom options** (design it your way)
5. **No pressure** (just options, neighborly help)

---

## What NOT to Do
- ❌ Sound corporate or stiff
- ❌ Overload with emojis
- ❌ Be pushy or use hard sells
- ❌ Use jargon
- ❌ Long paragraphs — keep it scannable
