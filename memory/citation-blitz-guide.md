## 🔴 TIER 1 — DO THESE FIRST (Highest Impact, All Free)
*These are the heavy hitters. DA 80+. Do all 7 this week.*

### 1. Apple Business Connect (DA 99) — FREE
- **URL:** https://businessconnect.apple.com
- **Why:** Shows up in Apple Maps, Siri, Safari, Spotlight search on every iPhone
- **Time:** 15 min
- **How:** Sign in with Apple ID → Search for "Tidy Tails" → Claim or Add New → Fill in business details
- **NAP to use:**
  ```
  Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal
  St. Louis, MO (service area business — no physical address)
  (314) 850-7140
  tidytailsstl.com
  ```

### 2. Bing Places for Business (DA 93) — FREE
- **URL:** https://www.bingplaces.com
- **Why:** Bing powers Cortana, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, and many AI assistants
- **Time:** 15 min
- **How:** Sign in with Microsoft account → Import from Google Business Profile (fastest) OR add manually
- **Pro tip:** "Import from Google" auto-fills everything. Takes 5 minutes.

### 3. Facebook Business Page (DA 94) — FREE
- **URL:** https://www.facebook.com/pages/create
- **Why:** 2nd largest search engine for local businesses, feeds data to Meta AI
- **Time:** 20 min
- **How:** Create page → Category: "Pet Service" → Fill in all details, add photos, link website
- **Bonus:** Can run Marketplace ads later from this page

### 4. Nextdoor Business Page (DA 83) — FREE
- **URL:** https://nextdoor.com/create-business
- **Why:** #1 neighborhood app. Pet owners LIVE here. Neighbors recommend services.
- **Time:** 15 min
- **How:** Choose "Professional Business" → Fill details → Get 2 free business posts/month
- **This is HUGE for pet waste.** Neighbors asking "who cleans up dog poop?" = Nextdoor gold.

### 5. Petworks.com — FREE (INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC)
- **URL:** https://www.petworks.com (look for "List Your Business" or "Claim")
- **Why:** Pet industry directory. Poop2Scoop and Dr. Scoops are ALREADY listed. TJ is NOT.
- **Time:** 15 min
- **How:** Create listing → Category: Dog Poop Clean Up Services → Add all service areas
- **This is where pet owners specifically search for poop scoopers.**

### 6. MapQuest (DA ~75) — FREE CLAIM
- **URL:** https://www.mapquest.com/us/missouri/tidy-tails-pet-waste-removal-777064125
- **Why:** Already auto-generated! Just needs claiming + correcting (shows Olivette MO 63132)
- **Time:** 10 min
- **How:** Visit the listing → Click "Claim this business" → Verify → Update address/details

### 7. Foursquare / Swarm (DA 91) — FREE
- **URL:** https://foursquare.com/add-place or https://business.foursquare.com
- **Why:** Feeds data to Apple Maps, Uber, Snapchat, Samsung, 150K+ apps
- **Time:** 10 min
- **How:** Add new place → Fill business details → Will sync to partner apps
