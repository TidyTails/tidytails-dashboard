# WAXR — Business Overview
*Added: March 24, 2026*

## What Is It
**WAXR** (waxr.io) — tech-first mobile detailing marketplace. TJ + his brother. Built on Lovable.dev.
"Book a detailer. Track the job. Love the result."

## The Opportunity
- **$2.6-3.1B** US mobile detailing market, **11% CAGR**
- No dominant consumer marketplace exists
- Spiffy ($90.8M raised) pivoted to dealer SaaS = consumer vacuum NOW
- App-based bookings growing at **24.96% CAGR**
- 79% of US drivers use professional car wash
- Quality inconsistency = #1 complaint (34% of reviews)

## WAXR's Model
- **Quality-gated hybrid labor** — certified detailers earn premium jobs, lower tier handles basics
- **AI quality verification** — before/after photo scoring
- **Dynamic pricing** — demand, vehicle, weather, time-of-day
- **Uber-grade tracking** — real-time GPS
- **B2B fleet contracts** — anchor revenue before consumer launch

## Revenue Streams
- Consumer bookings: $60-299/job (25% take rate)
- Fleet B2B subscriptions: $800-5K/month
- Consumer subscriptions: $49/month unlimited basic
- Ceramic/PPF upsells: $500-2K/job (~60% gross margin)

## The Ask
- **$1.5M seed round**, 18-month runway
- 35% supply-side SaaS + onboarding
- 25% consumer app
- 20% B2B fleet sales
- 15% brand + acquisition
- 5% legal/insurance/ops

## 18-Month Milestones
- 20-30 certified detailers in launch metro
- 3+ fleet B2B contracts ($2K+/mo each)
- Consumer app with Uber-grade tracking live
- NPS > 60, <5% no-show rate
- SOM target: $540K ARR
