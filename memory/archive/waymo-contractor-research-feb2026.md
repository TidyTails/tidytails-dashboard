## 1. TRANSDEV - Waymo's Main Operator Partner

### Contract Details
- **Partnership started:** Summer 2019 (multi-year contract)
- **First-year contract value:** "Tens of millions of dollars" - could reach **nine figures ($100M+)** according to Bloomberg/TT News source with direct knowledge
- **Current scope:** Over **1,000 operators** supporting Waymo across Phoenix, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Austin, and Dallas
- **Services:** Test driving, tech validation, mapping, fleet management, maintenance, customer support, remote vehicle monitoring

### Transdev Financials
- **Transdev North America revenue:** ~$1.3 billion annually (LeadIQ, Dec 2025)
- **Transdev Group global revenue:** €10.05 billion in 2024 (first time over €10B)
- **EBITDA:** €655 million (10% YoY increase)

### Why Waymo Uses Transdev
From the TT News article: The arrangement puts legal space between drivers and Waymo, allowing workers to stay on indefinitely as Transdev employees. Waymo required bidders to:
1. Guarantee hiring drivers as full-time employees
2. Articulate a strategy for career development

## 3. PER-VEHICLE ECONOMICS

| Cost Category | Annual Amount |
|--------------|---------------|

| Charging/depot | $5,500 |

| Insurance + permits + connectivity | $10,000 |
| **TOTAL** | **~$61,500** |

### Cleaning Specifics
- **Daily cleaning cost:** ~$15 per car per day
- **Annual cleaning cost:** ~$5,000 per vehicle
- **Services:** Outside hand wash (to protect AV equipment), basic inside clean
- **Operational days:** ~347 days/year

### Higher Estimates
Other analysts have estimated total per-vehicle costs at:
- **$150,000/year** (current)
- **$50,000/year** (projected for next-gen vehicles)

### Per-Mile Economics
- **Unit cost per revenue mile:** ~$2.00 (per Uber estimate)
- **Revenue per mile:** $3-6 (rider pricing)
- **Revenue-generating miles:** ~50% of total driven miles

## 7. KEY INSIGHTS FOR MOTRA

### The Opportunity
1. **Cleaning is a ~$5K/vehicle/year revenue opportunity** at current pricing
2. **Major players are using multiple contractors** - Waymo intentionally diversifies (Moove, Avis, Lyft, Transdev, Amerit)
3. **Labor is the bottleneck** - Transdev has 1,000+ operators for Waymo alone
4. **Geographic expansion = new contracts** - Nashville (Lyft), Dallas (Avis), Miami (Moove) all got new partners

### Current Market Gaps
- No single company dominates fleet cleaning
- Cleaning often bundled with broader fleet ops contracts
- Specialized AV cleaning (protecting sensors) is skilled work paying $25-30/hr
- Typical car wash doesn't understand AV equipment sensitivity

### Contract Model
- Multi-year partnerships are standard
- First-year values in "tens of millions" scaling to "nine figures"
- Partners expected to build out infrastructure (depots, charging)
- Companies must guarantee full-time employment + career paths

### Wage Competitive Analysis
- **$25-30/hr for AV cleaners** in major markets
- Significantly higher than traditional car wash wages
- Full benefits required by Waymo contracts
