# Jamie's Call Tracking Guide 📞

## Why Track Calls?

Every call is data. When we track what happens, we learn:
- Which types of leads convert best (property managers vs daycares vs vets)
- What times work best for reaching decision makers
- Which objections we need better answers for
- Our actual conversion rate (calls → customers)

**This info helps us focus on what WORKS.**

---

## Quick Reference: Outcome Codes

| Code | What It Means | Next Step |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| **NO_ANSWER** | No pickup | Try again tomorrow, different time |
| **VOICEMAIL** | Left VM | Follow up in 2-3 days |
| **CALLBACK_REQUESTED** | "Call me back at X time" | Set reminder, call back exactly then |
| **SPOKE_DM** | Talked to decision maker! | Note their interest level & objections |
| **SPOKE_OTHER** | Talked to staff | Got DM name/contact? Add to notes |
| **MEETING_SET** | 🎉 Walkthrough scheduled | Confirm date/time, show up early |
| **QUOTE_SENT** | Sent proposal | Follow up in 3-5 days |
| **WON** | 🎉🎉 CLOSED DEAL! | Celebrate, add to customers |
| **NOT_INTERESTED** | Hard no | Move on, note why (price? timing? DIY?) |
| **WRONG_NUMBER** | Bad contact | Research new contact info |
| **OUT_OF_SERVICE** | Business closed | Remove from list |

## Call Tips

### Best Times to Call
- **Property Managers:** 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM (avoid lunch)
- **Daycares:** Before 8 AM or after 6 PM (less chaos)
- **Vet Clinics:** Early morning before appointments start
- **HOAs:** Try to get the community manager's direct line

### When You Get a Decision Maker

1. **Introduce yourself fast:** "Hi, this is Jamie from Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal"
2. **Ask, don't pitch:** "Do you currently have a solution for pet waste on the property?"
3. **Listen for pain:** Long grass? Complaints? Doing it themselves?
4. **Offer the walkthrough:** "I'd love to swing by for 10 minutes, walk the grounds, and give you a no-pressure quote"

### Common Objections & Responses

| They Say | You Say |
|----------|---------|
| "We have maintenance handle it" | "That makes sense. Quick question—how many hours a week does that take them? A lot of our property manager clients found their maintenance team was spending 5-10 hours on waste, time they could use for repairs and turnovers." |
| "What does it cost?" | "It depends on the property size, but most of our commercial clients are in the $200-400/month range. Could I stop by for a quick walkthrough to give you an exact number?" |
| "Not in the budget" | "Totally understand. If you don't mind me asking—what are you spending now on labor for this? Sometimes we actually save money compared to having staff do it." |
| "We're locked into a contract" | "No problem at all. When does that come up for renewal? I'd love to be on your radar for when you're shopping again." |

## Daily Call Goal

**Minimum:** 5 calls per session
**Good:** 10 calls
**Crushing it:** 15+ calls

Remember: It takes about 10-15 calls to get 1 meeting, and about 3 meetings to get 1 client. That's the math. Just keep dialing.

---

## Priority Order (Who to Call First)

1. **CALLBACK_REQUESTED** — They're expecting you!
2. **SPOKE_OTHER** — You have momentum, follow up on that contact
3. **Property Managers** — Multi-property potential = biggest deals
4. **Dog Daycares** — High volume waste = good recurring revenue
5. **Vet Clinics** — Referral partnerships possible
6. **Dog Parks** — Usually government, slower process
