# Task: Decide Pricing Strategy

## Status: 📝 To Do (Needs TJ Decision)

## The Situation
Our main competitor, **Poop2Scoop**, charges **$18/week**.
Tidy Tails currently charges **$25/week**.

That's a **39% price premium** we're charging.

## The Options

### Option 1: Match Their Price ($18/week)
**Pros:**
- Removes price as an objection
- Easier to win on price-sensitive leads
- Faster customer acquisition

**Cons:**
- Less revenue per customer ($72/month vs $100)
- Race to the bottom mentality
- May attract price-shoppers who churn easily

### Option 2: Keep Premium Pricing ($25/week)
**Pros:**
- Higher revenue per customer
- Attracts customers who value quality
- Positions as premium service
- You've never lost a customer to service quality

**Cons:**
- May lose price-sensitive leads
- Harder initial conversion

### Option 3: Reframe the Value (Recommended)
**Keep $25 but tell a better story:**
- Emphasize owner-operated (same face every week)
- Highlight reliability (never missed a pickup)
- Push the "dog person" angle
- Make competitors look corporate/impersonal

## Franklin's Recommendation
**Option 3.** You've never lost a customer due to service issues. That's your competitive advantage, not price. Own the premium.

## What We Need
TJ to decide:

- [ ] Keep current ($25/week) and differentiate
- [ ] Something else (adjust to $20? Offer intro discount?)
