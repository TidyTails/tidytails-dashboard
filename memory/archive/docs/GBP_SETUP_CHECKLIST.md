## After Setup: Get Reviews

**This is crucial.** Reviews = trust = customers.

### Ask Your Current Customers
Send this text/message to your 4-5 existing customers:

```
Hey [Name]! Quick favor — would you mind leaving Tidy Tails a Google review? It really helps a small business like mine get found. Here's the link: [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Totally optional, but I'd really appreciate it! 🙏 — TJ
```

### How to Get Your Review Link
1. Search your business on Google
2. Click "Write a review"
3. Copy that URL
4. Or use: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[YOUR_PLACE_ID]`

---

## Ongoing: Keep It Active

- **Post weekly** — Google likes active profiles. Share tips, before/after photos, seasonal reminders.
- **Respond to reviews** — Always thank people, even for 5-stars.
- **Update photos** — Fresh content = better rankings.
- **Answer questions** — Be the first to answer Q&A on your profile.
