## Competitive Analysis

### Tidy Tails Pricing vs Market
| Service | Tidy Tails | Poop2Scoop | Difference |
|---------|------------|------------|------------|
| Weekly | $25/week | $18/week | +$7 (39% higher) |
| Biweekly | $45/2wks ($22.50/wk) | - | - |
| Monthly | $70/mo (~$17.50/wk) | - | COMPETITIVE |
| One-time | $100 | - | - |

### Pricing Strategy Options
1. **Premium positioning:** Keep prices, emphasize quality/reliability
2. **Competitive pricing:** Drop weekly to $20 to compete
3. **Value bundling:** Push monthly plan (best value at ~$17.50/week)
4. **Commercial pricing:** Develop separate pricing for multi-unit contracts

### Tidy Tails Differentiators
- TJ's personal touch and reliability
- "Genuine dog people" positioning
- Never lost a customer to service issues
- Local owner, not franchise

### Threats
- Poop2Scoop undercutting on price
- Yuckos has 35-year head start
- Franchises (DoodyCalls, POOP 911) have brand recognition

### Opportunities
- Commercial market (property managers, dog daycares)
- Yuckos' website is dated - we can look more professional
- Franchises are impersonal - we can be the "local guy"
- Monthly pricing is competitive - push that angle

## Recommendations
1. **Push monthly pricing** in ads - better value than weekly
2. **Target commercial** - Yuckos does it, so should we
3. **Emphasize local/personal** vs franchises
4. **Get that website up** - look more professional than Yuckos
5. **Collect reviews** - build social proof fast
