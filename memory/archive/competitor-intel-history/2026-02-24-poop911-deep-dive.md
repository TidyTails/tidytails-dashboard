If the St. Charles franchisee does $60K/year revenue:
- **Pays to corporate:** $15,000/year
- **Keeps:** $45,000/year

If TJ does $60K/year revenue:
- **Pays to corporate:** $0
- **Keeps:** $60,000/year

**TJ has a $15,000/year advantage at the same revenue level.**

This franchisee MUST:
- Cut corners on service
- Raise prices quietly
- Add upsells
- Or go out of business

## 🎯 POOP 911 WEAKNESSES (Exploit These)

### 1. **Franchisee Financial Pressure**
25% royalty = they're bleeding money. They CANNOT:
- Offer price-matching
- Provide extra free services
- Spend heavily on marketing

### 2. **Corporate vs Local**
- Different technician each time (rotating staff)
- Customer issues go to corporate, not owner
- No personal relationship
- Cookie-cutter service scripts

### 3. **Legal Concerns**
FDD discloses **lawsuits and/or bankruptcy information**. Indicates:
- Unhappy franchisees
- Service quality issues
- Corporate disputes

### 4. **Hidden Financial Performance**
They don't disclose revenue/earnings in their FDD. Why? Because franchisees aren't making good money after that 25% royalty.

### 5. **No Monthly Pricing Option**
They price weekly only. TJ's $70/month is **unique** — no competitor offers this.
