2. **Budget:** $5/day ($150/mo)
3. **Targeting:**
   - Location: 15-mile radius around Florissant, MO (covers all service areas)
   - Age: 25-65
   - Interests: Dogs, Dog Lovers, Pet Owners, specific breeds

## Google Ads Strategy

### Campaign Setup
1. **Campaign type:** Search (text ads)
2. **Budget:** $3.30/day ($100/mo)
3. **Keywords to target:**
   - "pet waste removal near me"
   - "dog poop cleanup [city name]"
   - "pooper scooper service St Louis"
   - "yard poop removal Florissant"
   - "pet waste removal St Louis"
   - "dog poop pickup service"
4. **Negative keywords:** (prevent wasted spend)
   - "pooper scooper" (alone — people buying equipment)
   - "DIY" "how to" "free" "job" "hiring"
5. **Location:** St. Louis County + St. Charles County
6. **Ad schedule:** 7am-9pm (when people are searching)

### Expected CPC
- Pet waste removal keywords: **$2-$8 per click** (low competition niche)
- At $3.30/day → ~15-50 clicks/month
- At 10-20% conversion rate → 2-5 new customers/month
- **CPC is LOW compared to other home services** (plumbing=$20-50, HVAC=$30-60)

### Quality Score Tips (Lower Your CPC)
- Landing page MUST match ad keywords (tidytailsstl.com already has service pages)
- Include phone number in ad with call extension
- Use location extensions (requires completed GBP — another reason to finish it!)
- Track conversions (phone calls + form submissions)

## Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) — Future Option

LSAs show at the very TOP of Google search with a "Google Guaranteed" badge. **Pay per LEAD, not per click.** However:
- Pet waste removal is NOT currently a standard LSA category
- Closest category: "Pet Care" or may need to check eligibility
- Worth investigating once GBP is fully complete and has 5+ reviews
- Cost: ~$15-30 per qualified lead

**Action: After GBP is 80%+ and has 5+ reviews, TJ should check LSA eligibility at ads.google.com/local-services-ads**
