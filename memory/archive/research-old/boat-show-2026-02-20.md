## 🎯 Shed ↔ Boat Owner Overlap (KEY INSIGHTS)

### Why Boat People Need Sheds:

1. **Boat Storage**
   - Many owners need covered storage for off-season
   - Protects from weather damage
   - Security/theft prevention
   
2. **Gear & Tackle Storage**
   - Rods, reels, nets, coolers
   - Life jackets, safety equipment
   - Electronics & accessories
   
3. **Workshop/Maintenance**
   - Boat maintenance space
   - Motor work, trailer repairs
   - Winterization area

4. **Lake Property Needs**
   - Vacation home additions
   - Dock equipment storage
   - Outdoor entertaining supplies

5. **Outdoor Lifestyle Crossover**
   - Hunting gear
   - ATV/UTV parking
   - Camping equipment
   - Lawn/landscaping tools

### Forum Evidence (Hull Truth, Key West Forums):
- Boat owners actively discuss pole barns, metal sheds, storage buildings
- $6,000-$15,000+ common spend range
- Major concern: protecting $30K-$100K+ boat investment
- Concrete slab vs pole barn construction debates active
