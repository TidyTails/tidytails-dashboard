## Why This Will Work

Google's local ranking algorithm uses 3 factors:
1. **Relevance** — Does your profile match what people search for? (Categories + Services fix this)
2. **Distance** — Are you near the searcher? (Service Areas fix this)
3. **Prominence** — Are you legit? (Reviews + Photos + Posts fix this)

Right now, Tidy Tails scores ~2/10 on all three. This kit fixes relevance and prominence immediately. Reviews are the only piece that takes a few days.

**The bottom line:** You have a verified GBP with almost nothing in it. Your competitors — even the ones closing down — have more complete profiles. 30 minutes of Jamie's time will do more for customer acquisition than any dashboard, playbook, or ad campaign we've built.
