## 🧠 PRO TIPS FROM THE RESEARCH

### What Actually Works (From successful pet waste companies):
1. **Before/after photos are KING** — they stop the scroll and make people go "oh shit, literally"
2. **Buy/sell/trade groups outperform pet groups** for direct sales
3. **Post 2-3x per week per group** — consistent visibility beats one viral post
4. **Respond to EVERY comment** — engagement boosts the algorithm
5. **Be funny** — poop jokes work. Lean into it. People share funny posts.
6. **Spring is peak season** — "spring thaw" posts convert highest March-May
7. **Mom groups = referral machines** — one recommendation → 3-5 signups

### What Doesn't Work:
1. ❌ Posting once and disappearing
2. ❌ Long, corporate-sounding ads
3. ❌ Posting the same thing in every group (looks like spam)
4. ❌ Ignoring group rules (instant ban = burned bridge)
5. ❌ Only posting in pet groups (too narrow)

### Group Rules to Follow:
- **Read pinned rules** in every group before posting
- Most BST groups allow service ads — some limit to 1 per week
- Pet groups usually ban direct ads — engage helpfully instead
- Mom groups vary — some have "Service Saturday" threads
- If your post gets deleted, DON'T repost — message an admin
