# MOTRA Project Kickoff — Feb 23, 2026

## What It Is

**MOTRA** = Mobile autonomy maintenance platform
**Tagline:** "Autonomy, Maintained"
**Model:** Gig economy for servicing autonomous vehicles

## The Core Problem

Who cleans and services Waymos/robotaxis between rides?
- No driver to notice messes
- Depot-based cleaning doesn't scale
- Sensors require specialized care

## Key Research Findings

### Market Size
- Waymo: 2,500+ vehicles, 400K rides/week
- Targeting 1M rides/week by end of 2026
- TAM 2028: $547M | TAM 2032: $5.4B

### How Waymo Handles Cleaning Today
- Hand wash only (can't use auto car wash — sensors)
- Depot-based operations
- Manual charging + cleaning combination
- Honor system for passenger messes

### Competition
- **Direct:** None at scale (blue ocean)
- **Adjacent:** Traditional fleet services, car washes (not AV-specific)
