## 💰 COMMERCIAL PRICING GUIDE (for TJ's calls)

Commercial pricing is different from residential. Here's what competitors charge:

- **DoodyCalls STL** — custom quotes per property, pet waste station maintenance included
- **Doggie Doodies** — has a dedicated commercial page (HOAs, waste stations)
- **Industry standard:** $75-$200/visit depending on property size, 1-3x/week

**Suggested Tidy Tails commercial pricing:**
- Small complex (< 50 units): $150-$200/month (1x/week)
- Medium complex (50-150 units): $250-$400/month (2x/week)
- Large complex (150+ units): $400-$600/month (3x/week)
- HOA common areas: $100-$300/month depending on acreage
- Pet waste station maintenance: $50-$75/month add-on

**TJ's advantage:** Local, personal service. The big franchises (DoodyCalls, POOP 911) have overhead. TJ can undercut them and still make great margins.
