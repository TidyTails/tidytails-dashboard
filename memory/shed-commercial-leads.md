## 🎯 TJ's Target Categories
- **Churches** — facilities director / church administrator / head pastor
- **Schools** — facilities & maintenance director / principal
- **Gas Stations** — independent/franchise owner or district manager (skip corporate chains like QT/Circle K)
- **Liquor Stores** — owner/manager on site (most are independently owned)

**Why these businesses buy sheds:**
- Extra storage for equipment, supplies, seasonal items
- Groundskeeping gear, janitorial supplies
- Overflow inventory (liquor stores especially)
- Secure outdoor storage without adding on to building
- Cost-effective vs. building expansion

**Who signs off:**
- Churches: Facilities Director or Head Pastor (for mid-size), Administrator for large
- Schools: Facilities & Maintenance Dept — Director of Facilities
- Gas stations: If independent franchise → owner is on site daily. If chain → district manager
- Liquor stores: Almost always owner-operated, they're there during business hours

## 🗣️ Universal Pitch Framework (Shed Commercial)

**Opening line:**
> "Hi, I'm Darius with TuffShed out in Saint Charles — we're the shed dealer right off [nearby road]. Do you have a moment? I work with a lot of [churches/schools/business owners] in the area and I've been reaching out to see if outdoor storage is something that's ever come up for you."

**If yes / tell me more:**
> "We carry everything from small utility sheds to full workshop buildings — American-made, custom built. Free delivery and setup, we have financing options including 6 months deferred interest right now, and no credit check options too. Most [churches/schools] I work with are looking at something in the 10x12 to 12x16 range. Takes about a week to deliver. Would it be worth swinging by our lot or having me shoot you a quick quote?"

**Key objections:**
- "We rent" → "That's fine — if the property owner is okay with it, you'd just want to ask. Most commercial landlords allow sheds for tenants."
- "No budget right now" → "Totally understand. We have rent-to-own options too — no credit check, low monthly. Some people start at $139/month."
- "Let me think about it" → "Of course. Can I get your email and send you a few options? No pressure — just so you have pricing when it comes up in a budget meeting."
