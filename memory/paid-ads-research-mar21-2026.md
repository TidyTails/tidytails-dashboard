# Paid Advertising Research — March 21, 2026

## Sources Analyzed
1. **Swoop Scoop (ScoopStart.com)** — 7-figure pet waste biz, 10,000+ FB leads, 500+ reviews
2. **Fresh Start Pet Waste (Reddit)** — $200K year 1 revenue, FB+Google ads, 175+ reviews
3. **LocaliQ 2025 Benchmarks** — 3,200 home services search ad campaigns analyzed
4. **WordStream 2025** — Overall Google Ads industry benchmarks
5. **WebFX 2026** — Home services marketing benchmarks

## Key Findings

### Facebook Ads > Google Ads for Pet Waste
- Low industry awareness = low search volume
- FB "creates demand" by showing ads to dog owners who don't know the service exists
- Swoop Scoop generated 10K+ leads from FB, not Google
- Fresh Start: 2-5 leads/day from FB+Google, $200K year 1

### Cost Benchmarks (2025 Home Services)
- **Average CPC (all home services):** $7.85
- **Cleaning Services CPC:** $8.50
- **Cleaning Services CTR:** 9.01% (above average)
- **Cleaning Services CVR:** 17.65% (BEST in all home services!)
- **Cleaning Services CPL:** $46.99 (2nd best after Pools)
- **Average CPL (all home services):** $90.92

### Pet Waste Specific Estimates
- Lower competition than general home services in STL
- Est. CPC: $4-8 (lower than cleaning avg)
- Est. CVR: 12-18% (similar to cleaning niche)
- Est. CPL: $35-60 (below industry average)
- FB cost per lead: $8-15 (much cheaper than Google)

### Customer Economics
- Average LTV: ~$1,500 (18 months × $85/month)
- Max healthy CAC: $150 (10:1 LTV:CAC)
- Payback period: 30 days (1st month covers ad cost)
- Break even: 2 customers at $70/month > $150/month spend

### Competitor Ad Activity (STL)
- POOP 911: Active Google Ads (national franchise budget)
- DoodyCalls: Active Google Ads (national franchise)
- ALL local indie competitors: NO paid ads detected
- Opportunity: $5/day FB puts Tidy Tails ahead of every local indie immediately

### Critical Success Factors (from 7-figure operators)
1. NEVER pause ad spend — consistency is everything
2. Phone photos > professional images on FB
3. Call out city name in ad: "Attention Dog Owners in [CITY]!"
4. Include promo: Free first cleanup or $20 off
5. Run 2-3 ad variations, kill losers after 7 days
6. Ask every lead "How did you hear about us?"
7. Reviews accelerate EVERYTHING (SEO, ad conversion, trust)

### The Strategy
**Phase 1 (NOW):** Facebook Ads, $5/day, Lead Generation, Dog Lovers targeting, 15mi radius
**Phase 2 (10+ reviews):** Add Google Ads $10/day, city keyword targeting, match to landing pages  
**Phase 3 (20+ customers):** Scale to $20-30/day combined, expand geo, add retargeting + video
