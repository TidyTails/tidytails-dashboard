## ⚠️ Group Rules Reality

Most community groups allow:
- ✅ "Recommendation" style posts ("anyone know a good...")
- ✅ Soft pitches with value ("tips for keeping your yard clean this spring")
- ❌ Hard sales ("HIRE ME NOW")

**Best approach:** Post as a helpful neighbor first, service provider second.
