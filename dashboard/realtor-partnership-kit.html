Why Realtors Need Us
📸 Listing Photos
Yard must be clean before photographer arrives. One pile in a listing photo = embarrassment + lower offers. Realtors will pay for peace of mind.
🏡 Open Houses
Buyers tour the backyard. Nothing kills a showing faster than a smelly yard. Realtors who serve dog owners need a go-to cleanup service.
📦 New Owner Gift
"One free cleanup as a closing gift from your realtor" — turns a $70 gesture into a potential $840/yr recurring customer.
🐕 Dog-Owner Clients
~50% of St. Louis homebuyers with kids have a dog. Realtors who work with families are constantly serving dog households.
The Offer to Realtors

🤝 Preferred Realtor Partner Program

Give realtors a referral card or digital link. When they send a client:

  • Client gets: First cleanup FREE (no obligation, no contract)
  • Realtor gets: $25 credit per converted recurring client (or just goodwill — most don't want money, they want a reliable partner)
  • Pre-listing special: Same/next-day cleanup before photos — priority scheduling for partners
Best Realtor Profiles to Target
1. Dog-friendly realtors — look for agents who post about their own pets on social. They GET it immediately.
2. High-volume suburban agents — St. Louis County, South County, North County. Volume = more dog-owner clients.
3. Realtors at open houses — walk up, introduce, leave a card. They're standing there, bored, with nothing but time.
4. RE/MAX, Coldwell Banker, Keller Williams offices — present at team meetings or leave materials with office manager.
5. Facebook "St. Louis Real Estate" group members — post value first, DM realtors who engage.

"My clients don't really have dogs" → "Totally get it — the ones who do are the ones who actually need this most, so even 1-2 a year makes it worth having the contact saved. I'll leave one card just in case."
"I already have a service" → "Perfect, always good to have a backup. We do same-day which a lot of services can't, so if you ever need a last-minute before photos I'm your call."
"I'll keep your card but I don't really send referrals" → "No worries — honestly you don't even have to call it a referral. Just pass the card when it's relevant. No reporting, no formal anything."
"How much does it cost?" → "For their first visit, it's free. After that $70/month flat — one price no matter the dog count. We try to make it a no-brainer."
