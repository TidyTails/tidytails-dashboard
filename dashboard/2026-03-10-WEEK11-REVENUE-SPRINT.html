Speed > Perfection — A mediocre door hanger distributed beats a perfect one in your printer

03
Ask for the sale — Don't just inform. "Can I get you on the schedule for this week?"
04
Track everything — If you don't measure it, you can't improve it
05
Spring is NOW — Every day you wait, competitors are grabbing customers waking up to gross yards
