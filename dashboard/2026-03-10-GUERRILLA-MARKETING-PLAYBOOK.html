Quick tip for fellow dog parents: 🐾 Winter waste that's been sitting under snow can actually harbor parasites like roundworm and hookworm that survive in cold temps. If you've got a backyard that hasn't been cleaned since fall, it's worth doing a thorough spring cleanup before your pup (or kids!) play out there. If you need a hand, that's what we do at Tidy Tails! But even if you DIY, please do get it done before the warm weather really kicks in. Your yard (and your pup's paws) will thank you. 🌱
