💡 Content Creation Tips

  • Take photos EVERY service visit. Before/after, dogs you meet, clean yards. Build a photo library. You'll never run out of content.
  • 15-second Reels > 60-second Reels. Short and punchy wins. Film quick POV clips while working.
  • Reply to EVERY comment. Comments boost the algorithm. Even "thanks!" counts. But try to ask a question back to keep the thread going.
  • Post to Stories daily. Even just a selfie with "heading out to clean yards!" Stories keep you top-of-mind without cluttering the feed.
  • Cross-post strategically. What works on Instagram might flop on Nextdoor. Adjust tone: IG = fun/visual, Nextdoor = helpful/neighborly, FB = conversational/community.
