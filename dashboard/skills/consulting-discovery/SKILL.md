# Consulting Discovery Protocol

**Purpose:** Find small business owners who will PAY for AI help — not just say "that's cool."

**Based on:** Eisenmann's false positive research, The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick), Steve Blank's Customer Development

---

## The Problem This Solves

Most "interested" prospects are just being polite. They'll:
- Say "I'd definitely be interested in that"
- Ask for a proposal they never read
- Ghost after the discovery call

**Real buyers** show commitment signals BEFORE you pitch.

---

## The Core Rules

### 1. Talk About Their Business, Not Your Services
```
❌ "I offer AI automation for small businesses..."
✅ "What's eating up most of your time right now?"
```

### 2. Ask About Past Behavior, Not Future Intentions
```
❌ "Would you pay for help with that?"
✅ "Have you tried to solve this before? What happened?"
```

### 3. Look for Commitment, Not Compliments
```
❌ "That sounds really cool"
✅ "Can we schedule 30 minutes this week to dig into that?"
```

---

## Discovery Call Script

### Opening (2 min)
> "Thanks for chatting. I'm not here to pitch you anything — I'm trying to understand what's actually hard about running [their business type]. Mind if I ask some questions?"

### Problem Discovery (10 min)
1. "Walk me through a typical day. Where do you lose the most time?"
2. "What tasks do you hate but can't delegate?"
3. "Last time you tried to fix that, what happened?"
4. "How much is that costing you — in hours or dollars?"

### Solution Awareness (5 min)
5. "Have you looked into automation or AI tools?"
6. "What stopped you from doing it yourself?"
7. "What would solving this be worth to you?"

### Commitment Test (3 min)
8. "If I could show you exactly how to fix [problem], would you have 30 minutes next week to look at it?"
9. "Who else would need to approve something like this?"

---

## Scoring Responses

### Strong Buy Signals (+3 each)
- [ ] Quantified their pain ("costs me 10 hours/week")
- [ ] Already tried to solve it (spent money or time)
- [ ] Asked about pricing unprompted
- [ ] Scheduled follow-up on the call
- [ ] Introduced you to decision-maker

### Moderate Signals (+1 each)
- [ ] Described problem in detail
- [ ] Asked clarifying questions
- [ ] Said "we've talked about this internally"
- [ ] Gave specific examples

### False Positive Flags (-2 each)
- [ ] Only said "sounds interesting"
- [ ] Talked about "someday" or "eventually"
- [ ] Couldn't quantify the problem
- [ ] No decision-making authority
- [ ] Asked for free advice without commitment

### Score Interpretation
| Score | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 8+ | Hot prospect | Send proposal within 24h |
| 4-7 | Warm lead | One more touchpoint, then decide |
| 0-3 | Tire-kicker | Politely move on |
| Negative | False positive | Don't waste time |

## Qualifying Questions by Industry

### For Service Businesses (Cleaners, Contractors, Salons)
- "How do you handle scheduling right now?"
- "What happens when you miss a follow-up?"
- "How much time do you spend on quotes?"

### For Retail/E-commerce
- "How do you decide what to restock?"
- "What's your process for responding to customer questions?"
- "How do you handle returns/exchanges?"

### For Professional Services (Lawyers, Accountants, Consultants)
- "How do you track billable time?"
- "What's your intake process for new clients?"
- "How much time goes into document prep?"

---

## Red Flags — Walk Away

1. **"We're not really looking to spend money right now"**
   - Translation: Tire-kicker
   
2. **"Can you just give me some ideas I could try myself?"**
   - Translation: Wants free consulting
   
3. **"Let me run this by my partner/boss and get back to you"** (then ghosts)
   - Translation: No authority or interest
   
4. **"We'd need to see it working first"**
   - Translation: Wants free pilot, won't commit

---

## The 5-Interview Rule

Before refining your packages or building any custom solutions:

1. **Interview 5 real prospects** (not friends, not other AI people)
2. **Use this script** — talk about THEIR problems, not your services
3. **Score each one** — look for patterns
4. **Identify the hot pain** — what 3+ people mentioned unprompted?
5. **Build packages around THAT** — not what you think is cool
