💡 How to Calculate Your Bid

Step 1: Walk the property. Time yourself. Note hot spots.

Step 2: Count pet waste stations (if any).

Step 3: Estimate visits needed (most HOAs need 2x/week).

Step 4: Calculate: (time per visit × $75/hr) × visits per month = monthly price

Step 5: Add station service if applicable: stations × $20/station/week × 4.3 weeks

Example: 45-min walk × 2x/week = 1.5 hrs/week × $75/hr = $112.50/week = $487/month. Add 4 stations at $20/week each = $347/month. Total package: $834/month 🔥

🏷️ Pricing vs. Competitors (Commercial)

• DoodyCalls (franchise): Custom quotes, typically $200-600/mo for HOAs. National brand premium.

• POOP 911 (franchise): Focuses on residential. Limited commercial presence in STL.

• Sgt. Poopers: $16.95/wk for removal + $16.95/wk per station — ~$147/mo for basic service

• TJ's advantage: No franchise fee markup. Owner shows up. Can undercut franchises by 15-20% and still make great margins.

Positioning: Price between franchises and DIY. Emphasize: "You get the owner, not a random tech. Same face every visit."
