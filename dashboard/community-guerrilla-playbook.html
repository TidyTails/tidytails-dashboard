⚡ Rules of Engagement

The difference between "local business hero" and "that spammer who got banned." Follow these.

1

Read the Group Rules FIRST

Every group has rules. Some allow business posts freely. Some only on certain days (e.g., "Self-Promo Saturdays"). Some never. Getting banned = permanently losing access to that audience.

2

Be a Member Before Being a Business

Join → Comment on 5-10 posts → Share helpful info → THEN post about Tidy Tails after a few days. Groups can see your join date. Don't be the person who joins and immediately spams.

3

80/20 Rule: 80% Value, 20% Promotion

For every promotional post, share 4 helpful posts: dog care tips, spring yard advice, funny dog content. Build reputation first, sales follow naturally.

4

NEVER Spam Multiple Groups Simultaneously

Facebook's algorithm detects cross-posting. Space posts out: 1-2 groups per day MAX. Vary the wording each time. Different opening line, different closing.

5

Answer Recommendation Requests FAST

When someone posts "does anyone know a good dog poop service?" — be the FIRST reply. Set up Facebook notifications for groups with keywords: "dog poop," "yard cleanup," "pet waste," "pooper scooper."

6

Post From Jamie's Personal Profile

People trust people, not business pages. Post as Jamie (personal) with a mention of the business. Feels like a neighbor sharing their service, not an ad.

7

Track Every Lead

When someone comments or DMs, log it immediately. Name, where they came from, what they asked. Use the CRM or just a note — but TRACK IT. Ask: "How'd you hear about us?"

💡 The Best Time to Post:
• Facebook groups: Tuesday-Thursday, 7-9 PM (people browsing after dinner)
• Nextdoor: Saturday-Sunday morning (people thinking about home stuff)
• Buy/Sell groups: Weekend mornings (highest browse traffic)
• Avoid: Monday mornings, Friday nights, anything after 10 PM
