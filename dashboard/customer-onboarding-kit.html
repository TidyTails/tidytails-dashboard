Quick recap: ✅ Service: [Weekly/Bi-Weekly] cleanup ✅ Day: [DAY]s ✅ Start date: [DATE] ✅ Rate: $[PRICE]/[week/visit] I'll text you before my first visit. 💡 This is the "churn prevention" text. Most cancellations happen in the first month because of unspoken issues. This catches them. It also sets up the review ask perfectly.

Appreciate you telling me that! I'll [fix/adjust/focus on that] starting this [DAY]. You shouldn't have to worry about it — that's literally why you hired me 😄 I'll check in again after my next visit to make sure it's dialed in. 👍
⚡ Never be defensive about feedback. Thank them, fix it, follow up. This is how you create customers who never leave.

🛡️ Churn Prevention: Red Flags & Saves

🚩 Red Flag: "I want to cancel"
Hey [NAME], totally understand — no contracts means no pressure! Before you go, can I ask what's not working? If it's something I can fix, I'd love the chance. And if not, no hard feelings at all. I appreciate you giving Tidy Tails a shot! 🐕
⚡ 40% of cancellation requests are saveable. The key: ask WHY, then offer a specific fix. "I'll focus more on [area]" or "Let me switch you to bi-weekly at $45" beats "sorry to see you go."
🚩 Red Flag: No Response to Check-In
Hey [NAME]! Haven't heard from you in a bit — just making sure everything's good with the service! Still cleaning up every [DAY] as usual. If you ever need to adjust anything, I'm just a text away. 👍🐕
Silent customers are at-risk customers. No response to 2 consecutive check-ins = schedule a call.
💡 Save: Price Objection
Totally get it — I want to make sure this makes sense for you! A couple options: 1️⃣ Switch to bi-weekly ($45/visit) — still keeps things manageable 2️⃣ I can do monthly ($70) if that fits better What sounds right? I'd rather adjust than lose you! 🐕
📊 Downgrading is ALWAYS better than cancellation. A bi-weekly customer paying $90/mo beats a lost customer paying $0.
💡 Save: "I'll just do it myself"
Ha, I've heard that one before! 😄 Tell you what — take a few weeks off and see how it goes. If you find yourself dreading it (everyone does), I'll be right here. No restart fees, no hassle. In the meantime, enjoy the yard! 🐕
🎯 Don't fight this one. Let them try. 70% come back within 6 weeks. The "no restart fees" plants the seed for easy return.
