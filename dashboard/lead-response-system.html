⚡ The 5 Golden Rules

  • 1. Respond in under 5 minutes. The first company to respond wins 78% of the time. Set text notifications for quote requests.
  • 2. Always give a price. Don't say "it depends." Give a range: "Most yards like yours are $60-80/month." People hate uncertainty.
  • 3. Offer a free first visit. "Let me do your first cleanup free so you can see the difference." Zero risk = easy yes.
  • 4. Follow up 7 times. 80% of sales happen after the 5th contact. Most people quit after 1. Be the one who doesn't.
  • 5. Always book the next step. Never end a conversation without scheduling something: a quote visit, a first cleanup, a callback time.

CALL Inbound Phone Script 
ANSWER: "Hey, this is TJ with Tidy Tails! Thanks for calling."

LISTEN: Let them tell you what they need. Don't interrupt.

QUALIFY: "Cool! So how many pups do you have? And roughly how big is the yard?"

PRICE: "For [X] dogs on a weekly schedule, you're looking at about $[PRICE] a month. That covers everything — I come by every week, clean up the whole yard, and haul everything away."

CLOSE: "Tell you what — let me come do your first visit for free. You can see how it works, make sure you're happy, and then we'll get you on the schedule. What day works this week?"

IF THEY HESITATE: "No pressure at all! The free visit is zero commitment. I just want you to see the difference a clean yard makes. Most people text me after the first visit saying they wish they'd done it sooner 😄"

BOOK IT: "Perfect! I'll put you down for [DAY]. I'll text you the morning of to confirm. Sound good?"
Key moves: (1) Give a specific price, not "it depends" — (2) Free first visit removes ALL risk — (3) Always book a specific day, not "sometime this week" — (4) Follow up with a confirmation text immediately after hanging up.
