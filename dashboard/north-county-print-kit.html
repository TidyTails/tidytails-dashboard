🎯 Why Door Hangers + When to Use Them

North County (Florissant, Hazelwood, Ferguson, Bridgeton) has ZERO active competitors — Dog Doody Defenders is DNS dead. There are an estimated 75-150 households that had pet waste service and lost their provider. These people are already sold on the concept — they just need someone to show up.

Door hangers reach these people directly when the FB group posts might miss them. Take 30-50 hangers when driving to/from a Monday scoop and drop them on homes that have dog toys, dog signs, fenced yards, or "Beware of Dog" signs.

🟢 Hanger A — Standard (Use for most neighborhoods)

Best for: General Florissant/Hazelwood neighborhoods. Homeowners who may or may not have had prior service. Front: "Your Yard Deserves Better" — Back: pricing, how it works, full feature comparison.

🟣 Hanger B — Orphaned Customer (Use near Dog Doody Defenders territory)

Best for: Florissant/Hazelwood homes that may have had Dog Doody Defenders service (the defunct competitor). Front: "Did Your Poop Scoop Service Disappear?" — direct, empathetic, immediately converts existing buyers. High conversion when targeted correctly.

💳 Business Cards — Leave with vet offices, dog daycares, neighbors

Print 100-200 on cardstock. Leave with: Baronwood AH, Cross Keys AH, Gateway City AH (Monday vet blitz), plus at any North County dog-related business. Fits in mailboxes too (more subtle than a hanger).

⚡ Targeting tip: Look for these as you drive: dog toys in yard, fenced backyard, "Beware of Dog" signs, dog door, multiple dog bowls on porch, chain/dog run in yard. Hit those houses first — 90% probability of dog owner. Skip apartments with no yard access.

🟢 Hanger A — Standard ("Your Yard Deserves Better")

2 hangers per sheet. Print on 8.5×11 cardstock, cut vertically down the center. Fold top tab over door handle. Print 25 sheets = 50 hangers.

💡 Print tip: Print ALL fronts first. Let dry 30 sec. Re-feed same paper, flip horizontally (NOT vertically) to print backs. Test with 1 sheet first to confirm alignment.

🟣 Hanger B — Orphaned Customer ("Did Your Poop Scoop Service Disappear?")

Same print process as Hanger A. Print 10-15 sheets = 20-30 orphan-targeted hangers. Deploy in areas where Dog Doody Defenders was most active (central Florissant, Hazelwood near Hwy 270).

📍 Where to drop orphan hangers: Florissant zip 63031 (Dog Doody Defenders was most active here per their old Google listing). Look for older, established neighborhoods where residents are more likely to have had recurring service before.

💳 Business Cards (10 cards per sheet)

Print on cardstock. 1 sheet = 10 cards. Print 10-20 sheets = 100-200 cards. Leave with vet offices, daycares, dog parks, and in Paw-ty follow-up envelopes. QR code links to tidytailsstl.com.
