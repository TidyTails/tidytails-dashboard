  • Target: Houses with dogs visible (toys in yard, dog door, "beware of dog" sign, barking when you walk by)
  • Best time: Saturday/Sunday 10 AM - 2 PM
  • Hit neighborhoods you already service — you're already driving there
  • Leave on door handle, NOT in mailbox (mailbox = federal offense, seriously)
  • Priority neighborhoods: Ballwin subdivisions near current customers, Manchester near parks, Ellisville near dog parks

  • Track which streets you hit — note it in your phone so you don't overlap
