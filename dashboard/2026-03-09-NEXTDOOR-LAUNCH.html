⚠️ Nextdoor Rules — Don't Get Flagged ▼

Business pages: 2 free posts/month to nearby neighborhoods

Use these strategically — your intro post and your best promotional post. Everything else goes from your personal account.

Personal posts: Be helpful, not spammy

Nextdoor neighbors will flag purely promotional posts from personal accounts. Always lead with value (education, humor, story) and keep the CTA soft. "Text me if interested" beats "CALL NOW FOR 20% OFF!!!"

Don't post more than 2-3x per week

More than that and you'll annoy neighbors and risk getting flagged. Quality over quantity. One great post beats five mediocre ones.

Engage authentically in between posts

Comment on other people's posts. Help with non-business questions. Be a neighbor. This builds goodwill that makes your promotional posts feel natural instead of intrusive.

Never ask for recommendations in exchange for discounts

This violates Nextdoor's terms. You can ASK for recommendations, but don't offer anything in return. The referral program (Post #10) is for referrals, not recommendations. Keep these separate.
