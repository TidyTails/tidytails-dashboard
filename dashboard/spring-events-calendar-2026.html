🔁 Weekly Recurring Opportunities

🌿 Tower Grove Farmers Market
Every Saturday, 8:00 AM - 12:30 PM (April → November)
📍 Tower Grove Park, St. Louis
FREE DOG-FRIENDLY ✅ WEEKLY

Why Farmers Markets Work

  • Dog-friendly — tons of people bring their dogs
  • 37K Facebook followers = massive community
  • Casual atmosphere, easy to chat
  • Same people come every week = build recognition
  • Live music, food, browsing = people are relaxed and open

The Farmers Market Strategy

  • Wear your Tidy Tails shirt every time
  • Bring cards in your pocket
  • When you see someone with a dog: compliment the dog, then mention what you do
  • Don't hard-sell — just be visible and friendly
  • After 3-4 weeks, regulars will know you as "the poop guy"

⚠️ NOT Soulard Market

Soulard Farmers Market does NOT allow dogs. Skip it. Tower Grove and St. Charles are the dog-friendly ones.
