🗣️ Battle Scripts (Copy These)

When They Say: "DoodyCalls quoted me $X"

"Yeah DoodyCalls is a big national franchise — they're fine. But here's the thing: you'll get a different person every time, you'll call an 800 number if there's a problem, and their pricing gets complicated fast with extra dogs. With us it's $70 a month flat, same person every week, and you text me directly. Simple."

When They Say: "I found someone cheaper"

"Totally fair — there are cheaper options out there. The ones charging $13-15 a visit are usually franchises where you're a number in a system. I'm the one actually showing up to your yard every week. Same person, same quality. And at $70/mo flat, you're not doing per-visit math wondering what the bill's gonna be. If price is the only factor, I get it. But if reliability matters, give us a shot — first visit's free."

When They Say: "I have 3 dogs, that's gotta be expensive"

"Actually that's where we really shine. Most companies charge extra per dog — you'd be looking at $90-120 a month elsewhere. We do $100/mo flat regardless of how many dogs. Three dogs, five dogs, same price. And the first visit is free so you can see the difference before you commit to anything."

When They Mention POOP 911

"Oh yeah, POOP 911 — I'm not sure they're still active in St. Louis honestly. Their local page went down recently. They're a national franchise so it depends on whether the local operator is still running. We're local, we're not going anywhere. Text me anytime."

When They Mention Scoop Soldiers

"Scoop Soldiers just started in St. Louis — they're a franchise out of Texas. Brand new here, no local reviews yet. We've been serving St. Louis families for over a year. You'll get the same person every visit, not whoever the franchise sends that week. And they don't even post their prices — you gotta call for a quote. Ours is simple: $70/mo."

The Quick Elevator Pitch

"We're Tidy Tails — local pet waste removal. $70 a month flat, same person every week, text us anytime. First visit's free. No contracts. That's it."
