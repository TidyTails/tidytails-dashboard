📱 #1 — After First Service

Same Day
Hey [Name]! TJ from Tidy Tails 🐾 Hope the yard's looking good! Quick favor — if you're happy with the cleanup, would you mind dropping a quick Google review? It's huge for a small business like mine. Takes like 30 seconds: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] No pressure at all — thanks for giving us a shot! 🙏
Why it works:

Casual, grateful, low-pressure. "30 seconds" removes the mental barrier. Same-day means the clean yard is fresh in their mind.

💪 #2 — After 3rd Service (Loyalty)

Visit #3
Hey [Name]! Just wrapped up visit #3 — your yard is staying sharp 💪 Real talk, your review on Google would mean the world. Most people check reviews before hiring someone and we're just getting started building ours up. [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] Even just a sentence or two helps. Thanks for being one of our first customers — that means a lot 🐕
Why it works:

By visit 3 they're committed. "One of our first customers" creates ownership — they want to help you succeed.

🔥 #3 — After a Compliment (Strike While Hot)

Immediately
Ha, that means a lot! Hey since you're already feeling good about us — any chance you'd throw that on Google as a review? 😄 Even just what you texted me. It really helps people find us. [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] Seriously appreciate it 🙏
Why it works:

They JUST said something nice. "Just copy what you said" is the lowest friction ask possible. Feels natural, not salesy.

👑 #4 — Existing Recurring Customers (The OG Ask)

[GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] Doesn't have to be long — even just "great service" helps. You've been awesome to work with and I really appreciate it 🐾
Why it works:

Honest and direct. "Random ask" feels casual. "Doesn't have to be long" removes perfectionism paralysis. USE THIS ONE FOR ALL CURRENT CUSTOMERS THIS WEEK.

✨ #5 — After Deep Clean

Same Day

Deep cleans have dramatic before/after results. "Small local business" triggers the support-local instinct.

🔄 #6 — Quarterly Gentle Nudge

Every 90 Days

Why it works:

Light touch, no guilt. Some people need 2-3 asks — that's totally normal. "No rush" keeps it pressure-free.

📊 Best Times to Send Review Requests

Best days: Tuesday – Thursday
Best times: 10 AM – 2 PM or 5 – 7 PM
Avoid: Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, weekends
Pro move: Ask in person during service, then text the link after

📱

Text beats email — always

98% open rate for texts vs 20% for email. Text is the only channel for review requests.

🗣️

Ask in person first, text the link after

"Hey, mind leaving a review? I'll text you the link." Verbal commitment = 3x more likely to follow through.

🚫

Never offer incentives for reviews

Violates Google's policy. Reviews can get removed and your profile flagged. Just ask honestly.

⚡

Respond to every review in 24 hours

Google rewards engaged businesses. Even "Thank you!" signals that you're active and care.

🎯

5 reviews = star rating in search

Google doesn't show your star rating until you hit 5 reviews. That's the first milestone.

⚠️

Never ask for "5 stars"

Just ask for "a review." Google flags businesses that solicit specific ratings. Let quality speak.

📈

Space them out — 2 per day max

Google's algorithm flags sudden review spikes as suspicious. Steady trickle looks organic and safe.
