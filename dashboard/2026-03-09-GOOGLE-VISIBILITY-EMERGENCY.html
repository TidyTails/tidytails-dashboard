Step 1: Google Search Console CRITICAL ⏱ 15 min

This is how you tell Google your site exists and submit all your pages for crawling.

  1. Go to Google Search Console
  2. Click "Add Property" → choose "URL prefix"
  3. Enter: https://tidytailsstl.com
  4. Verify ownership (DNS or HTML file method — your hosting provider can help)
  5. Once verified, go to Sitemaps → submit: https://tidytailsstl.com/sitemap.xml
  6. Go to URL Inspection → enter your homepage URL → click "Request Indexing"
  7. Repeat URL Inspection for your top 5 location pages:

Bing powers search for many voice assistants (Alexa, Siri sometimes, Cortana). Free backlink too.

  2. Sign in with Microsoft account
  3. Add site: https://tidytailsstl.com

Step 4: Fix www Redirect MEDIUM ⏱ 5 min

Both www.tidytailsstl.com and tidytailsstl.com serve the same content. Google sees these as duplicates. Pick one and redirect.

  1. In your hosting/Cloudflare dashboard, set up a 301 redirect from www.tidytailsstl.com → tidytailsstl.com (since your canonical already uses the non-www version)
  2. In Cloudflare: Go to Rules → Page Rules → add rule:
    • URL: www.tidytailsstl.com/*
    • Setting: Forwarding URL (301)
    • Destination: https://tidytailsstl.com/$1

Step 5: Add Social Profile Links MEDIUM ⏱ 5 min

Your structured data has an empty "sameAs" array. Google uses this to connect your website to your social profiles.

  1. In your website code, find the "sameAs": [] in the LocalBusiness schema
  2. Add your social profiles:
