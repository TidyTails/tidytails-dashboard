💬 Scripts — What to Say

Walk in casual. Smile. Ask for the office manager or practice manager. You're not selling — you're offering their clients a perk.

At the Front Desk
🚪 Opening — Walk Up to Receptionist
"Hey! My name's [Jamie/TJ] — I run Tidy Tails, we're a local pet waste removal service. I'm not here to sell anything to you guys — I actually wanted to talk to whoever handles community partnerships or the practice manager. We're offering local vet clinics a really easy way to add value for their dog-owner clients. Is that person available for just 2-3 minutes?"
If they say the manager is busy → "No worries! Can I leave some materials and your name so I can follow up?" Then get the manager's first name and best time to call.
With the Practice / Office Manager
🎯 The Core Pitch (30-45 seconds)
"So we scoop yards — weekly or biweekly, $35-70 a month depending on how many dogs. We're local, no contracts, and honestly most people who try it never quit. What I'm offering vet clinics is this: if you recommend us to your clients — even just put our flyers in your waiting room — we'll give you one free month of service for your staff member who has a dog, or a free initial cleanup for whoever needs it. Zero cost to the clinic, easy win for your clients. We're working with a handful of other vets in the area doing this. Your clients are already coming in asking how to care for their dogs better — this is one more easy answer."
Key: don't rush. Pause after "no contracts" — they expect a catch. When it doesn't come, it builds trust.
❓ Objection: "We don't usually promote outside businesses"
"Totally understand — and I'm not asking you to endorse us or put up a banner. Even just a stack of cards at the checkout counter works. Your clients will pick it up if they need it, and it costs you nothing. If it helps one family, it was worth it, right?"
❓ Objection: "We already have someone for that" or "We've heard of you"
"Oh perfect — then you know it's a real service people use. We're just making sure clients in THIS neighborhood know we're available, because honestly a lot of them don't even know this exists. Mind if we leave some cards? If even two or three clients sign up, they'll feel good about the recommendation."
✅ The Close — When They Say Yes
"That's awesome — thank you! Here's our leave-behind sheet with all the details, and a stack of cards clients can grab. And just to confirm — is there a dog owner on staff who'd want that free month? I want to make sure you guys actually get the perk, not just the paperwork. I'll follow up in a week or two to see if you're getting any calls from this. What's the best way to reach you?"
Get their name, a phone or email, and the staff member's name. This turns a flyer drop into a real relationship.

📞 3-5 Day Follow-Up Call
