"Hi [name], this is Jamie with Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal. We're local, based right here in Florissant. Got a minute?"

IF YES — Hook (10 sec)

"So I noticed your property is pet-friendly, which is great. A few of the pet waste services that used to cover North County have closed down recently — POOP 911, DoodyCalls — so there's kind of a gap right now. I wanted to see if waste in common areas is something you deal with."

THEIR RESPONSE — Listen, then pivot:

If "yes, it's a problem": "That's exactly why I called. We can set up weekly or biweekly cleanups — common areas, dog parks, walking paths. Most properties our size pay $75-200/month depending on frequency. We'd do the first cleanup free so you can see the difference."

If "we handle it internally": "Totally get it. How much time does your team spend on it? Most property managers tell me it's 3-5 hours a week for their maintenance crew. At $75-100/month, we free up that time for actual maintenance work."

If "not interested": "No problem at all. Would it be okay if I sent you our info in case it comes up at a board meeting or the situation changes? What's the best email?"

CLOSE

"Great — let me set up that free cleanup. What day works best for a quick walkthrough? I can come by, show you exactly what we'd cover, and leave you a quote. Takes about 10 minutes."
