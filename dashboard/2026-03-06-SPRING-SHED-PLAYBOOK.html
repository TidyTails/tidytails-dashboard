🛡️ Spring-Specific Objection Handling

❌ "I'm gonna wait until summer"
✅ Response: "Totally get it — but heads up, summer is our busiest time. Delivery goes from 2-3 weeks to 6-8 weeks. And prices typically go up. If you buy now, you'll have it set up and done before everyone else is still waiting in line. Plus the 0% interest deal might not be around then."
❌ "That's more than I expected"
✅ Response: "I get that. But let me run the math real quick — a storage unit is $150/month. That's $1,800 a year for a space you don't own. A TuffShed at $99/month? After 3 years you OWN it. It's actually saving you money long-term. Plus it's in your backyard, not across town."
❌ "I can get a cheaper one at Home Depot / Lowe's"
✅ Response: "You absolutely can — and if budget is the only factor, that might work. But here's what you're comparing: those are thin-walled kits you have to build yourself. No windows, no workbench, no real floor. A TuffShed is a permanent structure — pro-installed, 2x4 construction, rated for wind and snow. It's the difference between a toy and a tool. Plus we deliver and set it up for free."
❌ "I need to talk to my wife/husband"
✅ Response: "100%. Why don't you both come check them out in person this weekend? Way easier to get on the same page when you can walk inside one. I'm here Saturday, no appointment needed. I'll have the pricing and financing options all laid out — zero pressure."
❌ "My HOA might not allow it"
✅ Response: "Good thinking — that's actually more common than you'd think, and it's usually fine. Most HOAs just want it to match the house (color, placement, etc.). We do custom colors and can adjust the design. I can even give you a spec sheet to send to your HOA board. Most get approved in a week."
❌ "What if I move?"
✅ Response: "Great question. Two options: we can relocate it to your new place (yes, they move). Or — and this is the smart part — a quality shed adds $3-5K to your home value. Some of my customers have actually made money on their shed when they sold. It's not a cost, it's an asset."
