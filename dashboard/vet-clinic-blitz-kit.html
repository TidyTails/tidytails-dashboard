Walk-In Script (30 seconds)

Ask to speak with the office manager or front desk lead. Keep it quick.
"Hey! I'm TJ — I run Tidy Tails, a local pet waste removal service right here in [Florissant/North County]. We clean up dog poop from yards — weekly service. We're building our route and I wanted to introduce ourselves. A lot of your clients have dogs and yards — would you be okay leaving some of our cards at your desk? We'd give your clients 10% off their first month. Local business helping local dogs."
💡 Tips: Smile, be quick, don't oversell. Leave 10-20 cards + 1-2 flyers. If they say yes, ask for the office manager's name and if there's an email to follow up. If they're busy, just leave the materials and your business card. A YES takes 2 minutes.
🎁 Offer: Tell them their referred clients get 10% off first month. Low bar. Easy yes. If the manager seems interested personally, mention you could handle waste removal for their outdoor/kennel areas too.

📋 What to Bring to Each Clinic

Business cards (10-20 per clinic) — front and back, phone number prominent
1-page flyer with: service description, pricing ($X/week), phone number, website, "10% off first month for new clients"
QR code on flyer linking to tidytailsstl.com — easy for clients to scan and sign up
Your own business card to leave with the manager in case they want to refer you themselves
Optional: Small branded zip bag with waste bags + your card inside. Easy gift, memorable, on-brand
Notes from this app: jot down the manager's name and any reactions after each visit
