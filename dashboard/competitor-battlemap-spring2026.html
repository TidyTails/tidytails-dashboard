🎯 Price Positioning Strategy

Where to play:

  • Don't compete on price with franchises — they have scale advantages. Compete on personality, reliability, and local trust.
  • Multi-dog discount opportunity — Poop 2 Scoop charges $4/extra dog. We should consider a flat rate for 2+ dogs (like Poopy Paws does) to steal multi-dog households.
  • Spring deep clean upsell — Nobody is advertising spring one-time cleanups prominently. $99 deep clean → $70/mo recurring is a killer conversion path.
  • Add deodorizing — Poop 2 Scoop charges $4/visit for this. Easy upsell that adds $16-17/mo per customer.
🔥 Hot Take

At $70/mo, our margins are healthy. The real play isn't lowering price — it's increasing perceived value. Before/after photos, text notifications when service is complete, holiday cards... things that make customers feel like they're getting a premium experience at a mid-tier price.
