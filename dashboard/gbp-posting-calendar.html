📊 Why GBP Posts Are a Cheat Code

  • Google rewards activity. Businesses that post weekly rank higher in Maps. Period.
  • Posts show in your listing. When someone searches "pooper scooper near me," your latest post appears right in the search results.
  • Posts expire after 6 months, but the ranking boost from consistent activity is permanent.
  • Competitors ARE posting. POOP 911, Doggie Doodies, and DoodyCalls all post regularly. Zero posts = falling behind.
  • It's 100% free and takes 2 minutes. There is literally no reason not to.

📸 Photo Tips (Huge Boost)

  • Always add a photo — posts with photos get 35% more clicks
  • Real photos ONLY. No stock images. Google can detect them.
  • Best performers: before/after yards, dogs, Jamie working, equipment
  • Phone camera is totally fine — authenticity beats perfection
  • Snap a quick pic at each customer's yard — build a library for posts
  • Name files descriptively: "pet-waste-removal-florissant-mo.jpg" (minor SEO boost)

⏰ Best Times to Post

  • Weekday mornings (7-9 AM) — people checking phones before work
  • Weekday evenings (5-7 PM) — people browsing after work
  • Avoid late night and early morning
  • Consistency matters more than exact timing — just pick a routine

🔄 After These 4 Weeks

  • Recycle! Rewrite slightly and repost — Google doesn't penalize this
  • Add seasonal content: summer BBQ season, fall leaf cleanup, winter persistence
  • Share customer wins (with permission): "Just finished a deep clean in O'Fallon — 3 dogs, zero traces left!"
  • Announce service area expansions, new pricing, or referral promos
  • Respond to every Q&A on your listing (another free ranking signal)

🎯 GBP Post Types That Work

  • Updates — general business info, service area announcements
  • Offers — spring specials, referral deals, first-time discounts
  • Tips — pet health, yard care, environmental facts (builds authority)
  • Social Proof — customer results, transformation stories, testimonials
  • Mix them up! This calendar alternates between all four types.
