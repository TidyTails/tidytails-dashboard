🧠 Why This Exact Order — The Strategy Behind the Sequence

1
Reviews + GBP before anything else. Every other channel (ads, directories, Google search) converts better when you have social proof. Running ads with 0 reviews = wasting money. People Google you before they call — make sure what they find looks legit.
2
Free channels before paid. FB groups, Nextdoor, and referrals cost $0 and prove the messaging works. If you can't get customers for free, paying won't fix it — it'll just burn money faster. Prove the pitch, then amplify with ads.
3
Facebook Ads before Google Ads. Most people don't know pooper scooper services exist — they don't search for it. FB puts the idea in front of them. Google captures the small % who DO search. FB creates demand; Google captures it. Create first, capture second.
4
Residential before commercial. Residential customers come faster (1-week close), build your review count, and create the social proof that commercial accounts want to see. A property manager checking out a company with 0 reviews won't call back.
5
Expansion after establishing a base. St. Charles and West County are opportunities, but spreading thin too early means worse service, longer routes, and no density advantage. Dominate Florissant/North County first, then expand from a position of strength.
6
159 deliverables → 42 tasks. Everything else Franklin built is reference material. You don't need to read 159 pages — you need to DO 42 things in the right order. This roadmap links to the relevant deliverable for each task. Open it when you need it, not before.
