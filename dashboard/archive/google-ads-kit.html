💡 Pro Tips (Don't Skip These)

1. Call Tracking Is Everything

Most pet waste removal leads CALL, they don't fill out forms. Set up Google forwarding number so you can track which keywords generate calls. Go to Tools → Conversions → + → Phone calls.

2. Ad Schedule — Don't Burn Money at 3 AM

Set ads to run 7 AM - 9 PM only. Nobody's searching for poop cleanup at midnight. This stretches your budget to reach people when they'll actually call.

3. Location Bid Adjustments

After 2 weeks, check which zip codes convert best. Increase bids +25% for Florissant (63031, 63033) and your best-performing areas. Decrease -25% for areas that click but don't convert.

4. Google Guarantee Badge (Future)

Once you have 5+ Google reviews, apply for Google Local Services Ads. These show ABOVE regular search ads with a "Google Guaranteed" badge. You only pay per lead (not per click). This is the endgame for local service businesses.

5. The $150 Google Ads Credit

New Google Ads accounts often get a "Spend $150, get $150 free" promo. Check Google Ads Offers before creating your account. That's a free month of ads.
