💬 Text Response Templates

FIRST RESPONSE — THE GOLDEN TEXT
Hey [Name]! 👋 This is TJ from Tidy Tails. Thanks for reaching out! I'd love to help get your yard cleaned up. Quick question — how many dogs do you have and what area are you in? I can get you a quote in about 2 minutes 🐕
Why this works: It's warm, personal, asks an easy question (they WANT to talk about their dogs), and sets the expectation of a fast quote. The emoji keeps it casual — you're a neighbor helping out, not a corporation.
AFTER THEY REPLY WITH DETAILS
Awesome! [Dog count] dog[s] in [area] — I'm actually in that area already for other clients which keeps the price down. For [frequency] service I can do $[price]/visit. That includes the full yard, front and back, and I haul everything away. Want me to come by this [day] for a first cleanup? 🙌
AFTER-HOURS AUTO REPLY
Hey! Thanks for reaching out to Tidy Tails 🐾 I'm away from my phone right now but I'll get back to you first thing in the morning with a quote. Talk soon!
QUOTE FOLLOW-UP (IF NO REPLY IN 2 HRS)
Hey [Name], just wanted to make sure my quote came through! $[price]/visit for [frequency] service. No contracts or commitments — try it once and see what you think. I've got an opening this [day] if you're interested! 👍
FIRST-TIME SERVICE CONFIRMATION
Hey [Name]! You're all set for [day/date]. I'll be there between [time window]. No need to be home — I'll let myself through the gate and send you a photo when I'm done. Looking forward to it! 🐕💩✨
AFTER FIRST SERVICE
All done at your place! ✅ Yard is looking fresh. [Optional: specific note like "Found a lot built up by the back fence — got it all"]. Same time next [day]? Let me know if you want to adjust anything!
REFERRAL ASK (AFTER 3RD SERVICE)
Hey [Name]! Hope you're loving the clean yard 🐕 Quick favor — if you know anyone else who could use this service, I'd really appreciate a referral. I'll throw in a free cleanup for both of you! Just have them mention your name 🙌
REVIEW REQUEST (AFTER 4TH SERVICE)
Hey [Name]! Quick ask — would you mind leaving a quick Google review? It helps a ton as a small local business. Here's the link: [Google review link]. Even just a sentence or two means the world! 🙏
SEASONAL RE-ENGAGEMENT (SPRING)
Hey [Name]! Spring's here and yards are starting to thaw out 😅 A lot of my customers are booking spring cleanups to clear out the winter buildup. Want me to swing by this week? I've got a few spots open!

📞 Phone Scripts

Not word-for-word — just hit these beats in order. Be conversational.

Inbound Call (They Called You)

OPENING (10 sec)
"Hey, this is TJ with Tidy Tails! Thanks for calling. What can I help you with?"

→ Let them talk. They'll tell you everything you need.
DISCOVERY (30 sec)
Ask these 3 questions:
  1. "How many dogs do you have?" (pricing)
  2. "What part of town are you in?" (route fit)
  3. "How often were you thinking — weekly or every other week?" (frames the choice)
QUOTE (15 sec)
"For [X] dogs on a [frequency] schedule, I'd be at $[price] per visit. That covers the whole yard — front, back, side — and I haul everything away so you never have to think about it."
THE CLOSE (10 sec)
"I've actually got an opening this [day]. Want me to put you down for that?"

→ Always suggest a specific day. Don't say "whenever works." Make it easy.
IF THEY HESITATE
"Tell you what — try the first one and see how you like it. No contracts, no commitments. If you love it, we'll set up a regular schedule. If not, no hard feelings."

Outbound Call (Following Up on a Lead)

OPENING
"Hey [Name], this is TJ from Tidy Tails — you reached out about yard cleanup? Just wanted to follow up and see if you still need help with that!"

→ Keep it casual. You're a neighbor, not a telemarketer.
📞 Phone Tips:
  • Smile when you talk — they can hear it
  • Stand up or walk around — it adds energy to your voice
  • Use their name 2-3 times in the conversation
  • Match their energy — if they're chill, be chill
  • Always end with a specific next step, never "let me know"

Key Phrases That Close

✅ Say This
  • "I'm already in your area"
  • "No contracts ever"
  • "Try it once, see what you think"
  • "I'll send a photo when done"
  • "Most of my clients say..."
❌ Don't Say This
  • "Let me know what you think"
  • "Whenever works for you"
  • "Our services include..."
  • "I can send you more info"
  • "We're a pet waste company"

💡 Pricing Psychology Tips

Always quote per-visit, not monthly. "$20 per visit" sounds like lunch money. "$80/month" sounds like a bill. Same price, different psychology.
Anchor with the one-time price. "A one-time cleanup for that yard would be $75, but on weekly service it's just $20/visit — way better deal." Makes weekly feel like a steal.
Bundle the value. Never just say the price. Say: "$20 per visit — that covers the full yard, front and back, and I haul everything away." They're not buying poop pickup, they're buying a clean yard they never think about.

🛡️ Objection Handlers

Click any objection to see the response. Practice these until they're automatic.

"That's too expensive" / "Let me think about it" ▼
RESPOND WITH
"Totally understand — what does your time look like right now though? Most people tell me they spend 30-45 minutes a week on it, and nobody enjoys it. For $20, that's buying back your Saturday morning. Plus no contracts — try it once, and if it's not worth it to you, no hard feelings."
The move: Reframe from cost → time saved. $20 for 30-45 min of the worst chore? That's cheaper than a pizza delivery. Then remove risk with "try it once."
"I can just do it myself" ▼
RESPOND WITH
"Oh for sure, you definitely can! Most of my customers could too — they just got tired of being the one who always had to do it. It's one of those things where once someone else handles it, you wonder why you waited so long. Want to try one cleanup and see?"
The move: Never argue or make them feel dumb. Validate, then use social proof ("most of my customers felt the same way"). End with the low-commitment try.
"I need to talk to my spouse" ▼
RESPOND WITH
"Absolutely! Here's an easy way to frame it — it's about $5 a week per dog. Less than a coffee. And the first cleanup is basically a trial run, no commitment. Want me to text you the quote details so you can show them?"
The move: Give them ammunition for the conversation. Make it easy for them to sell it to their partner. The text follow-up keeps the door open.
"I only have one dog, it's not that bad" ▼
RESPOND WITH
"Ha, that's actually most of my clients! One dog can still put down 10+ piles a week. It adds up fast, especially in spring. And at $15-18 per visit for one dog, it's basically nothing. Want to try it for a month and see if it's worth it?"
The move: Quantify the poop. People don't realize how much even one dog produces. Then hit them with the low single-dog price.
"What if my dog is in the yard?" ▼
RESPOND WITH
"Great question! I love dogs, so that's usually not an issue. Most dogs are curious for the first visit and then don't even care after that. If your dog is aggressive or you'd rather them be inside, I can text you 5 minutes before I arrive so you can bring them in. Whatever works best!"
"Do you have insurance?" ▼
RESPOND WITH
"Yep, fully insured! It's one of those things that separates a legit operation from some random person on Craigslist. You can rest easy knowing your property is covered."
Note: If you don't have insurance yet — GET IT. General liability for pet waste is ~$30-50/month and it's a dealbreaker for commercial clients and cautious homeowners. It's also a competitive advantage.
"I'll call you back" / Ghost after quote ▼
FOLLOW UP SEQUENCE
Day 1 (2 hours after quote):
"Hey [Name], just checking — did you have any questions about the quote? Happy to adjust anything!"

Day 3:
"Hey [Name]! Just following up — I've got a few openings this week if you want to try that first cleanup. No pressure either way! 👍"

Day 7:
"Last ping, I promise! 😄 If the timing isn't right, no worries at all. I'll be here whenever you're ready. Have a great week!"
The move: 3 follow-ups, then stop. You're persistent, not desperate. The "last ping" one works surprisingly well — people feel bad and often respond.
"Another company is cheaper" ▼
RESPOND WITH
"For sure, there are cheaper options out there. What I can tell you is I'm local, I'm the one who shows up every time (not a random employee), and I actually care about doing it right. My customers stick around because the service is consistent. But hey, if price is the main factor, I totally get it — no hard feelings."
The move: Don't compete on price — compete on trust, consistency, and being local. The "no hard feelings" is genuine and paradoxically makes them more likely to choose you. Nobody wants to feel sold.

📅 Follow-Up Sequences

The fortune is in the follow-up. Most leads need 5-7 touches before buying.

🔥 Hot Lead (Just Inquired)

Within 5 min
Send first response text (see Texts tab). Ask about dogs + area.
Within 10 min of their reply
Send the quote. Always include a specific day: "I've got Thursday open."
2 hours after quote (if no reply)
"Hey, did you get the quote? Any questions?"
Day 2
"Just following up — I have openings this week if you want to try it out!"
Day 5
"Last check-in! No pressure either way. I'll be here when you're ready 👍"

🌱 New Customer (Just Signed Up)

Day of signup
Confirmation text with date, time window, instructions (gate code, dog info)
After 1st service
Completion text + photo of clean yard. Ask for any adjustments.
After 3rd service
Referral ask: "Know anyone who needs this? Free cleanup for both of you!"
After 4th-5th service
Google review request with direct link.
Monthly (ongoing)
Quick check-in: "Everything going well? Anything I should adjust?"

❄️ Cold Lead (Inquired 30+ Days Ago, Didn't Sign Up)

Seasonal triggers
"Hey [Name]! Spring's here — want to knock out a cleanup before it gets crazy?"
Special offers
"Running a spring special — first cleanup 50% off. Thought of you!"
Social proof
"Just hit [X] 5-star reviews! If you're still thinking about it, we'd love to add you to the schedule."
🏆 The Referral Loop: Every new customer should generate at least 1 referral ask. Your best marketing channel is happy customers telling their neighbors. Referral customers close at 4x the rate of cold leads and cost $0 to acquire.
