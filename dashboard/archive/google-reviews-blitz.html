💡 Review-Getting Pro Tips

🕐 Timing is Everything
Best time to ask: Right after service, while the yard is fresh. Monday evening texts after scooping = peak conversion. Worst time: Random Tuesday 3 weeks after service.
📱 Text > Email > Social
Text messages: 90% read within 3 min, ~35% response rate. Email: 20% open rate, ~10% response. Always text first.
🗣️ In-Person + Text = Magic Combo
Asking face-to-face makes it personal (hard to say no). Texting the link immediately after makes it easy. Together = 70%+ conversion. This is why Monday scooping day is your secret weapon.
💬 Don't Script Their Review
Never tell customers what to write. Never offer incentives (Google will ban you). Just make it easy and genuine. The link does the work.
⭐ Why Reviews Matter THIS Much
  • Local SEO: Reviews are #2 ranking factor for Google Maps (after GBP optimization)
  • Trust: 93% of consumers say reviews influence their buying decisions
  • Conversion: Businesses with 5+ reviews get 270% more clicks than those with 0
  • Ads: Google Ads show star ratings once you have 100+ reviews — but even 5 reviews make your listing WAY more clickable
  • Competitors: Dog Doody Defenders has 6 reviews. Even 5 puts you in the game.
⚠️ What NOT To Do
  • ❌ Don't offer discounts/gifts for reviews (Google Terms violation)
  • ❌ Don't buy fake reviews (Google WILL catch you and nuke your listing)
  • ❌ Don't ask friends/family who aren't customers
  • ❌ Don't ask more than twice per customer
  • ❌ Don't ask people to say specific things
  • ✅ DO respond to every review — even "Great service!" deserves a thank-you
