🤔 Should TJ Lower Prices?

Short answer: NO. Here's why:

1. TJ's $70/month offering is unique — nobody else offers monthly service. This attracts budget-conscious customers who wouldn't pay $60-100/month for weekly.

2. At 8 customers, TJ can't compete on volume. Scoop Soldiers is a national franchise that can afford $15/visit because they run routes of 30+ stops. TJ's advantage is personal service, reliability, and local trust.

3. Racing to the bottom kills small businesses. At $15/visit, TJ would need 4x the customers to make the same money.

Better strategy: Compete on VALUE, not price. Personalized texts, gate photos, dog treats, first-name basis, local trust. Things a franchise can't replicate.
