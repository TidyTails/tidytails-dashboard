💡 Posting Rules That Get Results

✅ DO

  • ✅ Read group rules BEFORE posting (some don't allow services)
  • ✅ Post in 2-3 different groups per day (max)
  • ✅ Vary your copy — don't paste the same thing everywhere
  • ✅ Engage with comments quickly (within 1 hour if possible)
  • ✅ Like and comment on other posts in the group (build presence first)
  • ✅ Include a photo when possible (you working, a clean yard, your truck)
  • ✅ Use the spring angle — it's timely and relatable
  • ✅ Post your personal profile post on Friday — friends share weekend content
  • ✅ Ask existing customers to comment on your posts ("Love Tidy Tails!")
  • ✅ Reply to every DM/text within 5 minutes during business hours

❌ DON'T

  • ❌ Post in 10 groups in one hour (looks like spam, may get banned)
  • ❌ Use the exact same copy in every group (FB detects duplicates)
  • ❌ Post in rescue groups with a hard sell (be helpful first)
  • ❌ Ignore comments or questions on your posts
  • ❌ Post more than once per week in the same group
  • ❌ Get into arguments in comments (kill with kindness)
  • ❌ Forget to include your phone number
  • ❌ Post late at night (looks unprofessional)

🔥 Pro Tips

  • 🔥 Photos convert 2-3x better than text-only posts. Snap a quick before/after.
  • 🔥 Video converts 5x better. Even a 15-second clip of you scooping and a clean yard.
  • 🔥 Ask customers to leave a Google review. Text them: "Hey! Would you mind leaving a quick Google review? It really helps a small business like mine. Here's the link: [your GMB review link]"
  • 🔥 Cross-post to Instagram Stories. Screenshot your FB post and share to Stories. More eyeballs.
  • 🔥 Track what works. When someone texts, ask "How'd you hear about us?" Write it down.
  • 🔥 Respond to texts in under 2 minutes and you'll close 50%+ of inquiries. Speed = trust.
  • 🔥 The referral play: Text current customers: "Hey! If you know anyone who needs yard cleanup, I'll give you a free extra visit if they sign up. Just have them mention your name!"
