💡 Why This Matters RIGHT NOW

The Competition Gap

Every active competitor in STL has a referral program: Doggie Doodies, POOP 911, DoodyCalls, Pet Butler. TJ doesn't. That means competitors are systematically turning every happy customer into a salesperson, and we're leaving money on the table.

The Math

Average Tidy Tails customer = ~$100/month × 12 months = $1,200/year lifetime value. A $25 referral credit costs you 2% of that value. Even if only 1 in 4 customers refers someone, that's a new customer for $25 instead of the $50-100+ it costs through ads.

Key Principle

Don't wait for the perfect system. A referral program you run via text messages is 100x better than a fancy automated one you never launch. Start simple, upgrade later.

🏗️ Program Structure

The Offer

🎁

Refer a Friend

When your referral signs up for weekly service, BOTH of you get a credit

$25 off
each person

Why $25 + Both Sides?

  • $25 is meaningful — it's basically a free week of service. Enough to motivate without killing margins.
  • Both sides win — the new customer gets a discount to try, the referrer feels rewarded. Swoop Scoop does $25 and it works.
  • Simple to explain — "Tell a friend, you both save $25." That's it. No tiers, no points, no confusion.
  • Easy to track — just ask new customers "who referred you?" and apply credits manually.

Rules (Keep It Simple)

  • Referral must sign up for weekly or bi-weekly recurring service (not one-time cleanups)
  • Credit applied to the next bill after referred customer's first paid service
  • No limit on referrals — refer 10 friends, get $250 in credits
  • New customer gets $25 off their first month
  • Can be combined with seasonal promos (spring cleanup + referral discount = great hook)

Future Upgrades (Not Now)

  • 🔮 3-referral bonus: Free month of service
  • 🔮 Referral leaderboard (gamification)
  • 🔮 Unique referral codes per customer
  • 🔮 Automated email/text when credit is applied

💬 How to Ask for Referrals

The #1 reason service businesses don't get referrals: they never ask. These scripts make it natural. Tap to copy any of them.

🔥 After a Great Service (Best Timing)

Hey [Name]! Just finished up your yard — looking good! 🐕 Quick thing: I just launched a referral deal. If you know anyone who could use a cleaner yard, you BOTH get $25 off. No pressure at all, just wanted you to know it's there. Thanks for being a great customer! 🙌

📱 Casual Text to Existing Customers

Hey [Name]! Hope you and [dog name] are doing well. I'm growing Tidy Tails this spring and offering $25 off for referrals — both you AND your friend get the discount. If anyone in your neighborhood ever complains about their yard, send 'em my way! 😄 No pressure, just appreciate you spreading the word. 🐾

📞 Phone Script (If You Call)

"Hey [Name], it's TJ from Tidy Tails. Just calling to check in — everything going well with the service? [let them respond] Awesome, glad to hear it! Hey, real quick — I'm running a referral thing this spring. If you know anyone who needs their yard cleaned up, I'll give you both $25 off. No catch, just trying to grow through word of mouth instead of ads. I can text you the details if you want to share it?"

📋 Follow-Up (If They Said They'd Think About It)

Hey [Name]! Just a heads up — that referral deal ($25 off for you + your friend) is still good. Spring's the perfect time since everyone's starting to notice their yards again 😅 If anyone comes to mind, just have them text me at [your number] and mention your name!

🏆 After They Refer Someone

Hey [Name]! Just wanted to say THANK YOU — [referred person] signed up! You're awesome. 🙌 I'm applying your $25 credit to your next bill. And hey, if you know anyone else... the offer never expires 😄🐾

⏰ Best Times to Ask

Timing Matters
  • Right after service — They just saw their clean yard. Peak satisfaction.
  • After a compliment — If they text "yard looks great!" → that's your opening.
  • Spring (NOW) — Everyone's noticing their yard after winter. Natural conversation starter.
  • After a special request — If you went above and beyond, they feel grateful.
Don't Ask When

Right after a complaint or issue. Fix the problem first, wait a week, then check in normally. Referral ask can come later once they're happy again.
