🎯 Market Gaps TJ Can Exploit

The #1 Yelp-ranked STL pet waste removal company has a dead website. Their potential customers are searching right now and finding nothing. This is the single biggest opportunity — be where they can't be.

Only POOP 911 (national franchise) offers a free first cleanup. Zero local competitors offer free trials. This is the #1 conversion tactic in the industry — first-time scoop free, then 35% convert to weekly.

→ Launch "Free First Scoop" offer on Nextdoor + FB groups

🔥 Gap #3: Terrible Digital Presence Across the Board

Yucko's has a website from 1998. Poopy Paws is down. Dog Doody Defenders has a basic Wix site. Poop 2 Scoop is the only one with a decent site. Nobody is doing consistent social media. The bar is on the floor.

Deck/patio deodorizing is a $20-30 add-on per visit. Only DoodyCalls mentions it. Dog Doody Defenders lists "yard deodorizing" but no pricing. This is easy upsell revenue.

→ Add deodorizing as $25 add-on service

Dog Doody Defenders = Florissant (North). Poopy Paws = city-area. Yucko's = general STL. Nobody owns Ballwin/Chesterfield/Manchester/Ellisville specifically. These are the highest-income suburbs with the most dogs.

→ Market as "West County's Pet Waste Removal Service"

💡 Gap #6: Nobody Has Reviews (except franchises)

DoodyCalls STL: 4 Yelp reviews. Most locals: 0-6 reviews. Even 10 Google reviews would put TJ ahead of every local competitor. The Spring Blitz playbook's review strategy is critical.

→ Target 10 Google reviews by April 30
