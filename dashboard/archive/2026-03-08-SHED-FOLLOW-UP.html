TEXT Day 1 — The Recap Same day
Hey [NAME]! Thanks for stopping by today. Just wanted to make sure you got everything you need on that [SIZE/MODEL]. Your quote is good for 30 days, and right now spring install slots are running about 2-3 weeks out. Let me know if any questions come up! — TJ
Professional, sets the 30-day clock, plants urgency about install times.
TEXT Day 3 — The Value Add 2 days later
Hey [NAME] — quick thought on your [SIZE] shed. A lot of folks don't realize we can add windows, shelving, or a workbench during the build for way less than adding it after. If you're thinking about using it for [workshop/storage/hobby], might be worth a second look at the options. Happy to send you some ideas.
Positions you as helpful, not pushy. Opens upsell conversation naturally.
TEXT Day 7 — Spring Urgency 1 week
Hey [NAME]! Just a heads up — we're getting into spring booking season and install wait times are starting to stretch. Right now it's still 2-3 weeks, but by mid-April it's usually 6+. No pressure, but wanted you to know so you can plan accordingly. Your quote's still locked in. 👍

— OR if a promo is running —

Hey [NAME] — TuffShed just dropped a [PROMO DETAILS]. Your [SIZE] quote from last week would qualify. Want me to run the numbers with the discount applied? Could save you $[AMOUNT].
Creates urgency without being salesy. "No pressure" is disarming.
TEXT Day 14 — Social Proof 2 weeks
Hey [NAME]! Random — just finished an install in [NEARBY AREA] yesterday. The customer did a [SIZE] with [FEATURE] and it came out awesome. Reminded me of what you were looking at. If you want, I can send you a pic of how it turned out. Sometimes seeing a finished build helps make the decision easier.
Real proof, local relevance, low-pressure. People love seeing finished projects.
TEXT Day 21 — The Honest Check 3 weeks
Hey [NAME] — I know you've been thinking about the shed for a few weeks. Totally get it — it's a big purchase. Just wanted to ask honestly: is it still on your radar, or did something change? Either way is totally fine. If timing's the issue, I can hold your quote and you can pull the trigger when you're ready. No weirdness either way. 👊
Gives permission to say no — reduces pressure and often gets a YES.

#1 — PERSONALIZE EVERYTHING
"[NAME], I remember you mentioned wanting space for your woodworking tools..." beats generic every time. Reference specific details from your conversation.
#2 — PHOTOS CLOSE DEALS
Keep a folder of finished installs on your phone. Send them in follow-ups. Visual proof > words. Take a photo of EVERY completed install.
#3 — SPRING URGENCY IS REAL
Install times genuinely stretch from 2-3 weeks to 6-8 weeks. Use it because it's TRUE, not because it's a tactic. Honest urgency converts.
#4 — LOG EVERY TOUCH
If you don't track it in Salesforce, you'll forget who's at what stage and double-text people. Instant credibility killer.
#5 — THE BREAKUP TEXT WORKS
Seriously. It's the highest-response follow-up in sales. Use it on EVERY lead that goes quiet after 4 touches. The guilt factor is real.
#6 — NEVER "JUST CHECKING IN"
Every follow-up must add new value — a photo, a promo, a timing update, a neighbor's build. "Just checking in" = "I have nothing to offer but I want your money."
#7 — USE THE COMPETITIVE CHEAT SHEET
When someone says "I can get a portable for less," you have the exact rebuttal ready. Check the Shed Competitive Pricing Cheat Sheet on the dashboard.
