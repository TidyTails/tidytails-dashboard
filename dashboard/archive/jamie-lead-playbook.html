📊 Why Monthly Beats Per-Visit

When they ask about per-visit pricing, reframe it:

"Most of our customers go with the monthly plan because it works out way cheaper — $70/month comes out to just $17.50 per visit for weekly. And you don't have to think about scheduling, we just show up every [DAY]. No surprises on the bill either."

If they insist on one-time: $25/visit. But push monthly — it's better for them AND us.

🛡️ Top Objections & Comebacks

Tap any objection to see the response. Don't memorize — just pull up this page.
"That's too expensive" ›
Jamie says: "I totally get it — let me break it down though. $70/month is $17.50 per visit. I'm out there for 15-20 minutes each time, bagging everything, hauling it away. Most people spend more than that on a fast food run. 😄

And compared to other services in the area, we're actually one of the more affordable monthly options. Doggie Doodies charges $83/month, Poopy Paws is $80. We're $70 flat."

If they're still hesitant: "We also do biweekly for $45/month if that fits your budget better. Most people upgrade to weekly after a couple weeks because the yard stays SO much cleaner."
"I can just do it myself" ›
Jamie says: "Oh totally, you can! The question is do you want to? 😄 Most of our customers COULD do it themselves — they'd just rather spend that 30 minutes a week doing literally anything else.

Plus we do a really thorough sweep — we find stuff in corners and along fences that's easy to miss. After a couple weeks most people wonder why they didn't do this sooner."

Key: Never make them feel bad for doing it themselves. Just paint the picture of NOT having to.
"I need to think about it" ›
Jamie says: "Absolutely, take your time! No rush at all. Just know there's no contract — you can try it for a month and if it's not your thing, cancel anytime. Zero risk.

I'll follow up in a day or two to see if you have any questions. Sound good?"

Key: "No contract" + "cancel anytime" eliminates the risk of deciding. Follow up in 24h.
"Do you do [specific area]?" ›
Check the Areas tab for our full service map. If they're in our zone:

"Yep! I'm in [AREA] every [DAY]. I could get you on the schedule as early as next week."

If they're outside our zone: "I don't currently cover that area, but I'm expanding routes this spring. Mind if I take your info and reach out when I add your neighborhood?"

Key: Never say "no" flat out. Collect info for future routes.
"What if you miss spots?" ›
Jamie says: "Great question! I do a really thorough grid-pattern sweep every visit — fences, corners, under bushes, the works. But if you ever notice something I missed, just text me. I'll come back and make it right, no charge.

I take photos sometimes too so you can see the before/after. Your yard, your standards — I want you 100% happy."

Key: The "I'll come back" guarantee removes all risk.
"How do I pay?" ›
Jamie says: "Super easy — I send a simple invoice at the beginning of the month. You can pay by Venmo, Zelle, or cash, whatever's easiest for you. Most people do Venmo since it takes about 5 seconds."

Key: Keep it simple. No complicated payment portals.
"Do I need to be home?" ›
Jamie says: "Nope! That's the beauty of it. As long as I can access the yard (gate code, side gate, etc.), I come, clean, and go. You come home to a clean yard without lifting a finger.

I'll text you when I'm done each time so you know it's taken care of."

Key: The "text when done" detail = professionalism that competitors don't offer.
"I only have one dog, is it worth it?" ›
Jamie says: "You'd be surprised! Even one dog can leave 15-20 piles a week. Multiply that over a month when it's been raining... it adds up fast. 😅

Most of our one-dog customers say the same thing after the first clean — 'I had no idea there was that much out there.' It's one of those things you don't realize until someone handles it for you."
