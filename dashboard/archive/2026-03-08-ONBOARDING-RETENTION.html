🎉 New Customer Welcome Sequence

First impressions set the tone for the entire relationship. This 7-day sequence turns a new signup into a confident, committed customer. Every message is timed for maximum impact.

💡 Why this matters: 68% of customers leave because they feel the business doesn't care about them. A structured welcome flow signals professionalism and builds trust before you even pick up the first pile.
IMMEDIATELY — Within 5 minutes of signup
🔥 Confirmation + Excitement Text
Set expectations, build excitement, confirm the details. Speed = professionalism.
Hey [NAME]! 🎉 This is TJ from Tidy Tails — welcome aboard!

Here's what we've got set up for you:
📍 Address: [ADDRESS]
🐕 Dogs: [#]
📅 Service day: [DAY]
💰 Rate: $[X]/visit

I'll be out for your first service on [DATE]. You don't need to do anything — just leave the gate unlocked if the yard is fenced, and I'll handle the rest.

Any questions before then, just text me here! Looking forward to keeping your yard spotless 🐾
DAY BEFORE — First service eve
📋 Reminder + Access Check
Reduce no-access situations and show you're organized.

Just want to confirm:
✅ Gate will be unlocked? Or should I use a code?
✅ Any areas to avoid or focus on?
✅ Dog(s) will be [inside/outside]?

See you tomorrow! 🐕
AFTER FIRST SERVICE — Same day
✨ Service Complete + Photo (Optional)
Confirm you were there, show the result, invite feedback. This is the "wow" moment.

Shows you care about quality, catches problems early.

✅ First Service Checklist

The first service is your audition. Nail it and you've got a customer for years. Rush it and they'll cancel within a month. Print this or screenshot it.

🚗 Before You Leave (5 min)

  • Check customer detailsAddress, gate code, # of dogs, special instructions
  • Equipment loadedBags, scooper, rake, sanitizer spray, gloves
  • Clean truck/uniformFirst impression matters — look professional
  • Business cards in pocketLeave one at the door, have extras for neighbor questions
  • Door hanger readyFor 2-3 neighbors on each side — free advertising while you're there

🏠 On Arrival (2 min)

  • Text "On my way" or "Here!"Customers love the heads-up. Makes you feel like a legit service.
  • Walk the full yard firstIdentify all waste areas, note yard layout, check for hazards
  • Meet the dog(s) if possibleLearn their names. Use names in future messages. Customers LOVE this.
  • Note any existing issuesHeavy accumulation? Muddy areas? Landscaping obstacles? Mention proactively.

🧹 During Service (15-25 min)

  • Be thorough — ESPECIALLY first timeGo over every inch. First service should be the most thorough service they ever get.
  • Get the edgesAlong fences, under bushes, near the house — places they never clean themselves
  • Double-check before leavingWalk the yard one more time. Miss one pile on the first visit and trust drops 50%.
  • Take a "clean yard" photoOptional but powerful — shows proof of work. Send with completion text.

👋 After Service (5 min)

  • Send completion textUse the template from Welcome Flow tab — confirm you were there
  • Leave business card at doorEven if they're home — professional touch
  • Drop door hangers on 2-3 neighborsYou're already there! "Your neighbor uses Tidy Tails" is the best ad copy ever
  • Log service in CRMDate, time, notes, any issues — build the history
⚠️ First Service Red Flags to Watch: Gate locked (no code given), aggressive dog with no warning, yard is a biohazard (needs spring cleanup quote instead), customer expects you to also do landscaping. Address immediately — don't let problems fester.

🐕 Customer Dog Profile Card

💡 Power move: "How's [DOG NAME] doing?" in your texts instead of "How's your dog?" — tiny detail, huge relationship impact. Customers tell their friends about this stuff.

🚨 Churn Prevention Triggers

Watch for these signals and respond IMMEDIATELY. Catching churn early = saving a customer.

⚠️ Yellow Flag: Skipping a service

Customer asks to skip a week. Normal occasionally, but 2+ skips = danger zone.

No problem, [NAME]! I'll skip this [DAY] and see you next week. Let me know if anything changes! 🐾
🟠 Orange Flag: Payment delay

Invoice unpaid after 5+ days. Don't let it pile up — address with kindness.

Hey [NAME]! Just a friendly reminder on the [MONTH] invoice ($[X]). No rush — just wanted to make sure it didn't slip through the cracks. 

Venmo: @TidyTails | Zelle: cartervhomes@gmail.com

Thanks! 🐕
🔴 Red Flag: "Thinking about cancelling"

This is your save-the-customer moment. Don't argue — listen, empathize, offer solutions.

Hey [NAME], totally understand! Mind if I ask — is there anything I could do differently? I'd hate to lose you as a customer.

If it's a budget thing, I could switch you to every-other-week ($[X]/visit) — still keeps the yard manageable and cuts the cost in half. 

Whatever works best for you! 🐾
🔴 Red Flag: Unresponsive to messages

No reply to 2+ texts. They might be ghosting. One more try, then respect the silence.

Hey [NAME]! Haven't heard from you in a bit — hope everything's okay with you and [DOG NAME]! 

Just want to confirm we're still on for [DAY]? If your schedule's changed, no worries at all — just let me know and we'll figure it out. 👍
