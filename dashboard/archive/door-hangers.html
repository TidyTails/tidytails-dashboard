🚪 Door Hangers — Print & Drop

Print at home, cut in half, hang on neighbor doors every Monday while you're scooping.
Zero cost. Highest conversion rate of any marketing channel for local services.

📋 How to Use

  1. Pick a style below (or print all 3)
  2. Click 🖨️ Print Door Hangers
  3. Print on cardstock for best results (regular paper works too)
  4. Cut along the dashed line — 2 hangers per page
  5. Every Monday: drop 5-10 on doors near the house you're scooping
  6. Focus on houses with visible dog stuff (toys, bowls, leash hooks, fence signs)
💡 Pro tip: Neighbors who just watched you clean the yard next door convert at 3-5x the rate of cold marketing. They already trust you.
