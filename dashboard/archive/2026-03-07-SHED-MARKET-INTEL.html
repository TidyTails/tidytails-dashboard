🗺️ STL Shed Market Landscape

The St. Louis shed market is fragmented — lots of small players, no single dominant brand outside TuffShed's franchise. Here's the breakdown:

  • National franchise: TuffShed (2 locations — Fenton + Alton, IL)
  • Regional dealers: Cardinal Buildings, Beyond The Backyard, America's Classic Buildings, Marten Portable, STL SHEDZ
  • Online/Lot: ShedMafia, A-1 Outdoor Products, Barrett Buildings, 5 Star Buildings
  • Solo operators: The STL Shed Guy (rent-to-own focus), All About Shedz, Rent Sheds
  • Custom builders: HomeAdvisor lists 19+ shed builders (mostly handyman-level)

📈 Spring 2026 Market Trends

🏷️ Pricing Tactics Used by Competitors

  • Rent-to-Own (RTO): STL Shed Guy, TuffShed, some portables — "Just 1st month down!" (total cost usually 30-50% MORE than cash)
  • Clearance/Display: Barrett has suspicious $2,499 for a 12x20 — likely display model or price error (great to watch/match)
  • Free delivery: Most dealers within ~50 miles, some charge $2-3/mile after
  • Spring sales events: French Creek Designs running "Save up to 30%" — seasonal discounting is standard
  • 3D Configurator: TuffShed's online tool lets customers design → price → save quotes (sets expectations)
  • Veteran/Military discount: STL Shed Guy plays the veteran card prominently

🎯 Backyard Buildings Pricing Strategy
