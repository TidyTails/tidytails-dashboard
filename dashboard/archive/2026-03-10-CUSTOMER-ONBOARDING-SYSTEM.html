📋 New Customer Onboarding Timeline

Every new customer goes through this flow. No exceptions. This is how you go from 5 to 30.

🟢 Day 0 — Signup

Service agreement signed (or verbal via text) • Gate access confirmed • Service day confirmed • Pricing agreed • Added to customer tracker • Welcome text sent • Address added to route

🔵 Day 1 — First Visit

Extra thorough cleanup • Check for hazards • Note yard size & waste level • Send "first visit complete" text • Drop business cards at 2-3 neighbor houses

🟡 Week 3 — Review Request

Send Google review request • Check for any issues • Confirm customer is happy with day/time

🟣 Month 1 — First Invoice

Send invoice with payment instructions • Include referral offer ($10 off per referral) • Update tracker • Confirm payment received

📅 Ongoing — Monthly

Invoice by the 1st • Follow up late payments by the 5th • Quarterly seasonal check-in text

🔥 Ready-Made Scripts for Common Situations

Click to expand. Copy the text. Handle any situation like a pro.

💸 Late Payment — Day 5 (Gentle) ▼
Hey [NAME]! Quick reminder — your monthly payment of $[AMOUNT] was due on the 1st. No worries if it slipped through! You can send via Venmo (@TidyTailsSTL) or Zelle (cartervhomes@gmail.com). Thanks! 🙏
💸 Late Payment — Day 10 (Firm) ▼
Hey [NAME], just following up on the payment for this month. It's $[AMOUNT] — let me know if there's an issue or if you need to set up a different payment method. Happy to work with you! 👍
🚪 Customer Wants to Cancel ▼
Hey [NAME], no problem at all. I appreciate you being a customer! If it's a budget thing, I could do biweekly visits at half the cost — keeps your yard manageable without breaking the bank. Either way, no hard feelings. 🙏
😤 Complaint — Missed Spot ▼
Hey [NAME], I'm sorry about that! I'll swing back by today and take care of it — no extra charge. I want to make sure you're 100% happy. Thanks for letting me know. 👍
🌧️ Weather Delay ▼
Hey [NAME]! With the weather today, I'm pushing your service to tomorrow. Everything else stays the same. Stay safe out there! ❄️
🐶 Adding a Dog ▼
Congrats on the new pup! 🐶 With the extra dog, your rate would go to $[NEW_RATE]/week. I'll update starting next visit. What's the new one's name?

🗓️ Quarterly Customer Check-In Templates

Send once per quarter to every customer. Keeps you top of mind and catches cancellations before they happen.
