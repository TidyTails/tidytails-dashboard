💡 Pro Tips

1
Hit new customers first. They just experienced the "wow" moment. Ask for referrals within the first 2 weeks while they're most excited.
2
Make rewards immediate. Don't say "next month." Say "your next visit is free." Speed of reward = speed of next referral.
3
Name names (with permission). "Sarah from Ballwin just earned a free week!" Social proof drives action from other customers.
4
Spring is your golden window. Everyone's yard is gross right now. The next 6 weeks are prime referral season. Push hard.
5
Stack with reviews. Customer refers someone AND leaves a Google review? Bonus free week. Lock in that loyalty and build online presence simultaneously.
