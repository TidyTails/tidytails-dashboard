Objection Handling — When Prospects Mention Competitors

🗣️ "I'm already using POOP 911 / DoodyCalls"

Customer has a franchise service

"Oh nice, they're a solid company. The main thing people tell me when they switch is they got tired of seeing a different person in their yard every week. With me, I'm the guy — same person, same day, every week. Your dog actually gets to know me. Plus you can text me directly instead of calling a 1-800 number. If you're ever not happy with them, I'll match their price and throw in the first week free."

🗣️ "I found someone cheaper" (Poopy Paws / Critter Sitters)

Price-sensitive prospect

"Totally get it — price matters. What I'd ask is: is it the same person every time? Do they text you when they're done? Do they actually check your dog's waste for anything weird? I do all of that at $17.50 a week. That's less than a Chipotle run. And if you refer a neighbor, I'll drop it to $15. Nobody does that."

🗣️ "Yucko's has been doing it for 30+ years"

Customer loyal to legacy provider

"Man, 36 years — that's impressive. You know what's funny though, have you tried to book with them online? It's phone-only, Monday through Friday 8 to 3. If you need to reach me, you text me at midnight and I'll get back to you in the morning. I respect what they've built, but I think you deserve someone who communicates the way you actually want to."

🗣️ "I'll just pick it up myself"

Not yet convinced they need a service

"Hey, I respect that. Most people say that until they realize they're spending 20-30 minutes every weekend doing it, and they've still got spots they missed. For less than $18 a week — that's like two coffees — you get your whole weekend back. I come on the same day every week, rain or shine. Most of my customers tell me the first month they're like 'why didn't I do this sooner.' No contract — try it for a month. If you don't love it, you're out nothing."

🗣️ "How do I know you'll actually show up?"

Trust / reliability concern

"Fair question. I text you a photo when I'm done every single visit. Same day, same time, every week. I've been doing this for over a year now and haven't missed a single service day. Google me — Tidy Tails. Check the reviews. And if I ever don't show? That week's free. I don't play games with people's trust."

🗣️ "Your website says you're in St. Charles but I'm in [other area]"

Service area question

"I cover a pretty big area — Florissant, Hazelwood, St. Charles, O'Fallon, St. Peters, Maryland Heights, all through North County. If you're within about 30 minutes of Florissant, I can get to you. Where exactly are you? Let me see if I can fit you on one of my existing route days — that way you get the best rate."
