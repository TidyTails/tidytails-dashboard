🔧 Step-by-Step Campaign Setup

Follow these steps to get your first Facebook ad live. Total setup time: ~45 minutes.

1

Create a Facebook Business Page (if not done)

Go to facebook.com/pages/create. Choose "Local Business." Use "Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal" as the name. Add your logo, cover photo, and fill in ALL business details (address, phone, hours, website).

Already have a page? Skip to step 2. Make sure all info is current and your page has at least 1 post.
2

Set Up Meta Business Suite

Go to business.facebook.com. Create a Business Account if you don't have one. Connect your Facebook Page. This is where you'll manage all ads.

3

Install Facebook Pixel on Your Website

In Business Suite → Events Manager → Create a Pixel. Add the pixel code to tidytailsstl.com (in the <head> tag). This tracks who visits your site from ads — critical for retargeting and optimization.

⚠️ If your site is on a builder like Squarespace/Wix/WordPress, search "[platform] + Facebook pixel" for specific instructions. Usually just paste the Pixel ID in settings.
4

Create Your First Campaign

Go to Ads Manager → Create Campaign → Choose objective:

✅ RECOMMENDED: "Leads" Objective

Meta optimizes to find people most likely to submit their info. Use Facebook's built-in Lead Form (no website visit needed — lower friction).

🔄 ALTERNATIVE: "Engagement" Objective

Gets more comments/shares/reactions. Great for brand awareness + social proof. People comment "interested" and you DM them.

⚠️ Do NOT start with "Traffic" or "Awareness" objectives — they optimize for clicks/views, not actual customers.
5

Set Your Budget

Start with $10/day ($300/month). This is enough to generate data and leads while learning. You can always scale up once you know your cost per lead.

💡 Swoop Scoop's #1 rule: NEVER pause ad spend. Consistency > bursts. Even $5/day is better than $20/day for 1 week then nothing.
6

Set Up Lead Form (for Leads objective)

Create an "Instant Form" in the ad. Keep it SHORT:

  • Intro: "Get a FREE yard cleanup quote!"
  • Fields: Full Name, Phone Number, Email (that's it — 3 fields max)
  • Thank You: "Thanks! We'll call within 24 hours to schedule your free cleanup."
🔥 Fewer form fields = more leads. Name + Phone is enough. You can get details when you call.
7

Respond to Leads FAST

Download the Meta Business Suite app on your phone. Turn on notifications for lead forms. Call or text every lead within 5 minutes.

⚠️ Lead response time is EVERYTHING. A lead contacted within 5 minutes is 21x more likely to convert than one contacted after 30 minutes. This is the #1 factor in Facebook ads ROI.

🖼️ Creative & Image Guidelines

Your ad creative is 80% of the battle. Follow these rules from the top performers.

📸 Photo Rules

✅ DO: Phone Photos Beat Professional

Swoop Scoop's #1 creative tip: Phone photos outperform professional shots on Facebook. They feel authentic and blend into the newsfeed naturally.

📷 Best Photo Ideas for TJ:
  • Before/After: Messy yard → clean yard (BIGGEST winner)
  • You + a dog: TJ petting a customer's dog after cleanup
  • Action shot: You scooping (from behind, artistic angle)
  • Clean yard hero shot: Beautiful green lawn, dog playing
  • Your truck/equipment: Shows professionalism
  • Funny signage: "Tidy Tails was here" type shots
🚫 DON'T:
  • Stock photos (people scroll right past them)
  • Photos of actual dog poop (obviously 😅)
  • Dark or blurry images
  • Too much text on images (Meta limits text to <20% of image)
  • Competitor logos or names

🎬 Video Ideas (Highest Engagement)

15-30 Second Videos Win
  • "Day in the life" — Quick timelapse of you doing a cleanup
  • "Spring thaw reveal" — Show a messy winter yard → transformation
  • "Meet your scooper" — Quick intro of TJ, casual and friendly
  • "Customer testimonial" — Ask a happy customer for a 10-second clip
  • "Satisfying cleanup" — Oddly satisfying before/after reel
💡 Videos get 2-3x more engagement than static images on Facebook. Even a simple phone video outperforms a polished stock photo.

✍️ Copy Formatting Rules

The Swoop Scoop Formula
  1. Call out your audience in the first line ("Hey St. Louis dog owners")
  2. Pain point — what sucks about their current situation
  3. Solution — what you do (keep it simple)
  4. Offer — free cleanup, discount, limited time deal
  5. CTA — "Click below," "Comment CLEAN," etc.
