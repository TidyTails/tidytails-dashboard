⚠️ The #1 Problem: Price + Density

TJ's $17.50/wk is 20-37% below market rate. Industry standard for 1 dog weekly: $22-$28/wk. At $25/wk, the same 5 customers would generate $1,083/mo instead of $750 — a 44% increase with zero new customers. Combine that with scattered routes (Florissant to O'Fallon = 25+ min drive) and you're burning time and gas.
