🎯 The Fix — Step by Step

P0 This Week — Get Indexed (30-60 min total)

  • 1. Set Up Google Search Console
    1. Go to search.google.com/search-console
    2. Click "Add Property" → enter https://tidytailsstl.com
    3. Verify ownership via DNS TXT record (Cloudflare dashboard → DNS → Add Record → TXT)
    4. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" → submit https://tidytailsstl.com/sitemap.xml
    5. Use "URL Inspection" → paste homepage URL → click "Request Indexing"
    6. Repeat for each city page (Ferguson, Florissant, etc.)
    ⏱ 15-20 minutes
  • 2. Create Google Business Profile
    1. Go to business.google.com
    2. Click "Manage now" → "Add your business"
    3. Business name: Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal
    4. Category: Pet Waste Removal Service
    5. Service area: St. Louis County, St. Charles County, + each city you serve
    6. Phone: 314-850-7140 | Website: tidytailsstl.com
    7. Add photos, business hours (M-Su 8am-6pm), description
    8. Verify via postcard or phone
    ⏱ 15-20 minutes
  • 3. Set Up Bing Webmaster Tools
    Go to bing.com/webmasters → Import from GSC (one-click after GSC is set up). This also covers DuckDuckGo and Yahoo results.
    ⏱ 5 minutes

P1 This Month — Build Authority

  • 4. Create Free Directory Listings
    Each listing = a backlink + citation. Use EXACT same info everywhere (NAP consistency):

    Phone: 314-850-7140
    Website: https://tidytailsstl.com
    • Yelp — Create business listing (free)

    • Thumbtack — Lead gen + backlink
    • BBB — Credibility + strong backlink
    • Facebook Business Page — Link to website
    • Apple Maps Connect — For iPhone users
    ⏱ 1-2 hours total (spread over a week)
  • 5. Ask Current Customers for Google Reviews
    Once GBP is live, text your 4-5 current customers: "Hey [name], would you mind leaving a quick Google review for Tidy Tails? It'd really help me out. Here's the link: [GBP review link]"

    Even 3-5 reviews puts you ahead of most local competitors for a new listing.
    ⏱ 10 minutes to send texts
  • 6. Set Up Google Analytics (GA4)

    ⏱ 15 minutes
