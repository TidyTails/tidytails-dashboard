📖 Complete Printing Guide

Exactly what to do at Staples, VistaPrint, or at home

🏪 Option 1: Staples Same-Day Print

  • Door Hangers: Print on 110lb cardstock, 8.5x11, cut in half = 2 per sheet. ~$0.15/hanger. Ask for their paper cutter.
  • Flyers: Standard color copies on 28lb bright white paper. ~$0.50/page. Or self-serve for $0.25.
  • Leave-Behinds: Print 4-up on 8.5x11 cardstock, cut to size. ~$0.08/card.
  • Total for 100 door hangers + 50 flyers + 100 leave-behinds: ~$45

🏠 Option 2: Print at Home

  • Use a color inkjet or laser printer
  • Buy cardstock from Dollar Tree ($1/pack of 25 sheets)
  • Print door hangers at actual size, cut with scissors or paper cutter
  • Hole punch the top for the door handle
  • Cost: ~$0.07/hanger (just ink + paper)

🌐 Option 3: VistaPrint (Best Quality, 3-5 days)

  • Door Hangers: 250 for $40 ($0.16/each) — professional die-cut with hole
  • Business Cards: 500 for $20 — premium matte
  • Yard Signs: 18x24 coroplast, $12 each — includes metal stake
  • Upload designs as PDF or use their editor
  • Tip: VistaPrint almost always has 40-60% off coupons. Google "VistaPrint promo code" before ordering.

🚶 Distribution Strategy — Saturday Blitz

  • 2 hours = 200 doors. That's the target.
  • Start with streets where you already have customers
  • Look for yards with dog toys, bowls, leash hooks, or dog doors
  • Skip houses with "No Soliciting" signs
  • Best time: Saturday 10am-12pm (people are home, doing yard work)
  • Wear a branded shirt or hat if you have one
  • If someone comes to the door: "Hey, I'm TJ — I clean yards in the neighborhood. First week's free if you want to try it." Keep it casual.
