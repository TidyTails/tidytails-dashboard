📍 The 5-Step Close
1
Qualify Fast (First 2 msgs)
Ask one question: size, use case, or timeline. Get them talking. Never pitch everything upfront — you need to know what they actually want before you quote.
"What size are you thinking?" — the one question that opens everything.
2
Send a Specific Quote (Not "it depends")
Give them 2 options — good/better. Always show the monthly payment alongside the total. Include what's included (delivery, install, warranty). Make it concrete.
Concrete beats vague. A price range = indecision. Two options = a choice.
3
Handle the Real Objection
Price, timeline, spouse, permit — find out what's actually blocking them and address it directly. Most stalls are info problems, not true objections.
"Is it the price, the timing, or something else?" — let them tell you what's really going on.
4
Create Real Urgency
Use honest urgency signals: spring lead times filling up, tariff pricing locked in, deposit holds the date. Never fake it — one "gotcha" and you lose the sale and your reputation.
Truth-based urgency closes. Fake urgency destroys trust.
5
Make It Easy to Say Yes
Offer the financing link. Offer to come to their place. Offer to meet at the lot. Remove every "I need to" friction point. The easier you make the next step, the more people take it.
"I can send you the financing link right now — takes 2 minutes, no commitment to check."
