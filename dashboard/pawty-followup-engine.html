📋 7-Day Follow-Up Sequence

Proven sequence — each step customized with lead's name and details

Day 0 — Same Day (Saturday) 📱 Text

Send within 2 hours of meeting. Be personal!

Hey {name}! It was great meeting you at the Paw-ty today 🐾 This is TJ from Tidy Tails. I'd love to get your yard looking spotless — want me to set up a time for your first cleanup?
Day 1 — Sunday 📱 Text

Quick value-add follow up. Don't be pushy.

Hey {name}! Hope you and {dog_name} are enjoying the weekend 🐶 Just wanted to let you know — if you sign up this week, your first cleanup is on us! No strings. Just want to show you how clean your yard can be.
Day 3 — Tuesday 📞 Call

Quick 60-second call. If voicemail, leave a friendly one and follow up with text.

CALL SCRIPT: "Hey {name}, it's TJ from Tidy Tails — we met at the Crestwood Vet Paw-ty on Saturday. Just wanted to check in and see if you had any questions about our service. We're running a special this week — first cleanup free if you start a weekly or biweekly plan. Any interest?" IF VOICEMAIL → send text: Hey {name}! Tried to give you a quick call — just following up from the Paw-ty. First cleanup is free this week if you want to try us out! Let me know 🙂
Day 5 — Thursday 📱 Text

Social proof + urgency. Last soft push.

Hey {name}! Quick update — we picked up 3 new customers from the Paw-ty this week 🎉 Your neighbors are already loving the clean yards. We've still got a couple spots open in your area this week. Want me to add you to the schedule?
Day 7 — Saturday 📱 Text

Final attempt. Respectful close — leave the door open.

Hey {name}! Just wanted to follow up one last time — we're wrapping up our Paw-ty special this weekend. If the timing isn't right, totally get it! But if you ever need your yard cleaned up, you've got my number 🐾 Have a great weekend!
🧠 Pro Tips
  • Hot leads: Text AND call on Day 0. Don't wait.
  • New adopter special: If they just adopted, mention "We know the first weeks are crazy — one less thing to worry about!"
  • Multiple dogs: Reference their dog count — "With {dog_count} dogs, that yard fills up fast!"
  • Referred by vet: "Dr. [Name] recommended us — we service a lot of their clients!"
  • If they say 'not right now': Set a 30-day reminder. People's situations change.
  • ALWAYS personalize. Use their dog's name. Mention something specific from your conversation.
