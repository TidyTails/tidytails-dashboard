## The Idea

**MOTRA** = Mobile autonomy maintenance platform
**Tagline:** "Autonomy, Maintained"
**Problem:** Who cleans and services Waymos/robotaxis between rides?
**Solution:** Gig economy platform deploying mobile techs to service AVs on-location

## Why It's Good

- Waymo: 400K rides/week → 1M/week by end of 2026
- No scaled competitor exists (blue ocean)
- Gig model proven by Uber/DoorDash
- TAM: $547M (2028) → $5.5B (2032)
- AV companies hand-wash at depots — doesn't scale
