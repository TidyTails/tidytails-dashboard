Geotag Your Photos (Free Ranking Boost)
Geotagging tells Google WHERE you took the photo, reinforcing your service area.

Easiest method: Just make sure Location Services is ON in your phone camera settings before taking photos. iPhone/Android automatically embeds GPS coordinates.

For existing photos: Use GeoImgr.com (free) to add coordinates to any photo. Set them to addresses in your service areas.
