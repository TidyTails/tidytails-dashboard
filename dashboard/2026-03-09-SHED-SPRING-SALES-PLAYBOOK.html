🛡️ Top 8 Shed Objections (And How to Crush Them)

Every objection is a buying signal in disguise. They're not saying "no" — they're saying "convince me."

❌ "It's too expensive"

▶

Reframe to monthly: "I totally get it — it's a real investment. But have you looked at the monthly payment option? With our rent-to-own, you could be looking at $150-200/month with no credit check. That's less than a storage unit — and you OWN it at the end."

Compare to alternatives: "What are you paying for off-site storage right now? $100-200/month? Over 5 years that's $6,000-12,000 and you own nothing. A shed is right in your backyard and it's yours forever."

Value stack: "Think about it this way — a quality shed actually increases your home value. Real estate agents say a good shed adds $5,000-15,000 to resale value. So you're not spending money, you're moving it."

❌ "I need to talk to my spouse"

▶

Include them: "Absolutely — this is a big decision you should make together. Want me to set up a quick 10-minute call with both of you? That way I can answer any questions they have too, and you don't have to play middleman."

Arm them: "Makes total sense. Let me put together a one-page summary with the pricing, financing options, and some photos of that style — makes it easier to show them exactly what you're thinking."

❌ "I'm going to shop around / compare prices"

▶

Encourage it (then win): "You should! Smart move. Just do me a favor — when you're comparing, make sure you're looking at the same specs. A lot of the cheaper options use thinner walls, basic roofing, and no warranty worth mentioning. TuffShed has been doing this since 1981 — we've built over a million buildings. Ask the other guys how many they've built."

Plant the seed: "When you're comparing, check three things: warranty length, what's included in the install price, and whether they handle permits. A lot of 'cheaper' quotes don't include site prep, delivery, or engineering — and those add up fast."

❌ "I want to wait until summer / fall"

▶

Urgency play: "I hear that a lot — and here's what happens every year. People wait until summer, call us in June, and our install calendar is booked into August or September. If you get your order in now, you pick your date. Wait until summer and summer picks for you."

Logic play: "Makes sense — but here's the thing: every month you wait is another month of paying for that storage unit / cramming stuff in your garage / that project not getting done. What if we lock in a summer install date now and get the financing started? No rush, but you'll be glad when June comes and you don't have to wait."

❌ "I could just build one myself"

▶

Reality check: "Totally — some people do. But when you add up materials, your time, permits, and getting the foundation right... most guys tell me they spent about the same or more and it took them 3 weekends instead of 3 hours. Our crew drops it in one day, fully assembled, with a real warranty. Your weekends are worth something, right?"

Quality angle: "The engineering that goes into a TuffShed is 40 years of R&D. These things are built to handle 100+ mph winds and serious snow loads. A DIY build? I'd be worried about the roof in 5 years. Our warranty covers you for way longer than that."

❌ "I'm not sure what size I need"

▶

Help them figure it out: "That's actually the fun part — let's figure it out together. What are you planning to store? [Listen]. OK, based on that, I'd say you're looking at a [size]. But here's my rule of thumb: go one size bigger than you think. Everyone wishes they'd gone bigger. Nobody ever says 'I wish my shed was smaller.'"

❌ "The HOA might not allow it"

▶

Remove the barrier: "Good thinking — you definitely want to check that first. Most HOAs allow sheds as long as they meet certain guidelines on size, placement, and colors. Here's what I'd suggest: get me your HOA's building guidelines, and I'll make sure we spec a building that's compliant. I do this all the time."

❌ "I saw cheaper ones at Home Depot / Lowe's"

▶

Educate: "Yep — those are our Sundance line at Home Depot, actually! TuffShed makes those too. They're our economy tier — great for basic storage. What I sell is the Premier and Premier Pro lines, which are a completely different animal. Thicker walls, better doors, way more customization, and serious weather ratings. It's like comparing a Corolla to a Camry — same brand, different league."

💡 Pro Tips From Top Shed Sellers

  • Photos sell sheds. Take a photo of EVERY install you do. Before/after of the yard. Happy customer standing next to it. These are worth more than any ad copy.
  • Video walkthroughs crush it on Marketplace. 30-second walk-around of a finished build gets 5-10x more engagement than photos alone.
  • "Go one size bigger" is the easiest upsell in the business. Nobody ever regrets getting the bigger shed.
  • The spouse is always the decision maker. Always try to get both parties on a call or at the showroom. One person says "let me ask my wife" = 50% chance you never hear from them again.
  • Follow up speed matters more than follow up quality. The first person to respond to a Marketplace inquiry wins the deal 78% of the time. Set up notifications.
  • Monday morning is your power hour. People think about home projects all weekend. Monday morning they're ready to act. That's when you make your calls.
  • Financing closes deals that price kills. Always present the monthly payment FIRST, total price second. "$175/month" hits different than "$8,500."
