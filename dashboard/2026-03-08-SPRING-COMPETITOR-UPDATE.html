⚠️ Pricing Reality Check

TJ's $25/wk ($100/mo) puts him mid-to-high range for the STL market. POOP 911 undercuts everyone at $13.95/visit. Poopy Paws offers unlimited dogs at $20/wk flat. Dirty Dog Wash is $95/mo for weekly.

BUT: The franchises (DoodyCalls, Scoop Soldiers) hide pricing behind quotes — likely $25-40/wk. TJ's transparent pricing is a competitive advantage over them. Price-shoppers comparing TJ vs franchises see real numbers vs "call for quote."

✅ TJ's Sweet Spot

Don't race to the bottom. POOP 911's $13.95 is a franchise loss-leader strategy — TJ can't (and shouldn't) match it. Instead, lean into: transparent pricing, owner-operated trust, local knowledge, and personal service. The customers who pay $20-25/wk and stay are worth more than bargain hunters who churn.
