🚀 Strategic Opportunities

1. GBP Completion = Instant Visibility

Tidy Tails doesn't appear ANYWHERE in Google search results. Not organic, not Maps, not AI Overview. POOP 911 dominates with 180 reviews. The guide is built (dashboard) — TJ just needs to spend 25 minutes completing it. This is the single highest-ROI action possible.

2. DDD Customer Rescue (Time-Sensitive!)

Every day without action = Scoop Soldiers or POOP 911 scoops these customers. The DDD Rescue Blitz is ready. Post on Nextdoor Florissant TODAY. These are warm leads who already pay $18-25/wk for this exact service.

3. Steal Ideas from Doggie Doodies

Their playbook is smart: gate photos, background checks, "Paw Points" rewards, e-gift cards, referral program. TJ should implement at least 3 of these. The customer onboarding kit already covers most — but the rewards program and e-gift cards are low-hanging fruit worth copying.

4. "Free First Cleanup" is Industry Standard

POOP 911, Doggie Doodies, Scoop Soldiers, and No. 2 Crew all offer free first service. TJ currently doesn't (or isn't advertising it). This is table stakes — not offering it puts TJ at a disadvantage in every comparison. Consider adding it to the website and all ads.

5. Price Position: Don't Race to Bottom

At $25/wk, TJ is $5-7 above cheapest. But the franchises (POOP 911, DoodyCalls) probably charge similar or more. The play: keep $25 but add visible value — gate photos, text notifications, "Jamie" personality, local owner answering texts personally. Make the $25 feel like a steal, not a premium.

6. Deodorizing = Untapped Upsell

Little Stinkers, POOP 911, Poop 2 Scoop, and DoodyCalls all offer deodorizing. It's an easy upsell ($4-20/visit extra). Requires minimal extra supplies. TJ should consider adding this — even as a seasonal spring offering.
