Nextdoor Algo Rules (What Actually Works)
✅ DO
  • • Post as yourself, first person ("I'm TJ...")
  • • Mention the specific neighborhood in the post
  • • Use a conversational opening, not "Introducing!"
  • • Include a real phone number (not just a website)
  • • Reply to every comment within 2 hours
  • • Post in Neighborhood > For Sale/Free section OR General
  • • Space posts 5–7 days apart per neighborhood
  • • Start with a question or relatable scenario
❌ DON'T
  • • Don't post the same copy to multiple neighborhoods
  • • Don't use "Introducing my business!" openers
  • • Don't post more than 1x/week per community
  • • Don't ignore comments (kills visibility)
  • • Don't use all caps or excessive emojis
  • • Don't post and ghost — check for replies
  • • Don't lead with price in the first sentence
  • • Don't make it sound like an ad — sound like a neighbor
