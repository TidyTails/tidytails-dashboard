🧠 Route Intelligence Tips
📍
Cluster within zip codes. Each mile you save = $0.21 back in your pocket + 3-4 min of time. Target: 3 stops per zip before moving on.
📅
Day-of-week optimization. Mon/Tue = North County. Wed = South City. Thu/Fri = West County. Never criss-cross the map in one day.
📦
Batch at 15 customers. That's when you hit the route wall. Solution: dedicated Jamie day, zone split, or biweekly-only policy for outliers.
💰
Outliers cost more than they earn. A customer 20 min from anyone else costs ~$8/visit in extra fuel + drive time. Price them at $75+/mo or politely redirect.
🗓️
Offer two specific days, not open-ended. "I service your area Mondays and Thursdays — which works?" closes faster and builds route density.

🏆 What Moves the Needle Most?
1️⃣
Referrals first, always. CAC ~$15. Conversion rate ~60% (they already trust you). Fastest path to next 3-5 customers with zero ad spend.
2️⃣
Google reviews compound for free. Going from 6 → 20 reviews could triple organic monthly signups. That's $0 CAC, forever.
3️⃣
Upsell before acquiring. One $45/mo biweekly customer upgrading to $70/mo weekly = same revenue as a whole new customer. No new route stops.
4️⃣
Route density drives margin. Each stop added within an existing zone costs ~$2 in fuel vs ~$8 for a new zone. Fill your zones before opening new ones.
5️⃣
Churn kills compounding. Losing 1 customer/month = 12 customers/year you have to replace just to stay flat. Winning on retention > acquisition.
