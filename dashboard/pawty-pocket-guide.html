Lead with the DOG, not the pitch. Pet the dog. Ask its name. Let them talk. THEN mention Tidy Tails naturally.

"Hey, what a beautiful [breed]! Just adopted today? Congrats!"

Let them gush about their dog for 30 sec...

"I'm Jamie from Tidy Tails — we do pet waste cleanup right here in Crestwood. Just wanted to say hey and give you my card in case you ever need help keeping the yard clean. Most of my customers are people who just got tired of doing it themselves."
"Yeah, so basically I come by once a week — or every other week — and clean up everything. Most yards take about 10 minutes. You never have to think about it again."

"Oh totally, most people do! I did too before I started this. But the folks who switch to a service always say the same thing: 'Why didn't I do this sooner?' It's like mowing — you CAN do it, but it's way nicer when someone else handles it."
"Totally get it. Most customers pay about $60-70 a month — less than a gym membership or a couple takeout dinners. And your yard stays clean every single week without you thinking about it."

Anchor to: Netflix ($15), gym ($40), meal kit ($60), lawn care ($120+). We're cheaper than all of those.

"You'd be surprised — I have customers with just one dog. It still adds up faster than you think, especially in spring when it rains and you can't get out there. And honestly, the small-dog yards are my fastest jobs."
