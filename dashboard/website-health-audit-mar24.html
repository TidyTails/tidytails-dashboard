🚨 Critical Issues

Fix Now

🔴 4 City Pages Are Homepage Clones

The pages /crestwood, /maplewood, /brentwood, and /shrewsbury all serve identical homepage content with the generic title "Tidy Tails | Pet Waste Removal St. Louis". Their canonical tags point to the homepage, which means Google treats them as duplicates and won't index them separately.

Impact: These 4 cities along I-44 get ZERO organic search presence. Anyone searching "pet waste removal Crestwood MO" won't find Tidy Tails. That's 4 missed local keyword opportunities.
Fix: Each page needs unique content like the Florissant page has — local neighborhoods, a city-specific title tag ("Pet Waste Removal in Crestwood, MO"), a matching canonical tag (href="https://tidytailsstl.com/crestwood"), and localized copy. The Florissant page is the template. Takes ~30 min per page in the site builder.

🔴 4 City Pages Missing from Sitemap

Crestwood, Maplewood, Brentwood, and Shrewsbury exist as live pages but are NOT in sitemap.xml. Google has no map to find them.

Impact: Even if the pages had unique content, Google wouldn't know to crawl them. Double penalty: no unique content AND not in the sitemap.
Fix: Add these 4 URLs to sitemap.xml after building out unique content. Also add to Google Search Console for forced indexing. 5 min fix once content is ready.

🔴 /spring-clean Is a Dead Page

The /spring-clean landing page serves the exact same content as the homepage. Title tag is generic, canonical points to homepage. This was supposed to be a Spring 2026 campaign landing page but was never built out.

Impact: If this URL was shared in ads or posts, visitors land on a generic homepage instead of a spring-focused conversion page. It's spring RIGHT NOW — this is prime time for this page.
Fix: Either build it out as a spring-specific landing page (like /free-cleanup which IS properly built) or remove it from the sitemap and redirect to /free-cleanup. The /free-cleanup page already does this job well.

🟠 Free Cleanup Page Shows "Only 7 Spots Left" — Is It Real?

The /free-cleanup page has urgency copy: "Only 7 spots left" and "Limited spots this month!" If this is static text (not dynamic), returning visitors see the same number forever, which kills credibility.

Impact: Fake urgency backfires. If someone sees "7 spots left" today and "7 spots left" next month, trust drops. But if it's real and not updated, you could be leaving money on the table.
Fix: Either (a) make it dynamic based on actual capacity, (b) remove the specific number and keep general urgency ("Spring spots filling up fast"), or (c) update it manually each week. Option B is the quickest and most honest.

🟠 No Google Analytics / Tracking Detected

Crawl didn't find GA4, Google Tag Manager, or Facebook Pixel scripts on the homepage. (Note: they could be loaded via a tag manager not visible in initial HTML — verify in browser.)

Impact: Without analytics, you can't measure which pages convert, which blog posts drive traffic, or whether ads are working. Flying blind.
Fix: Add GA4 tracking code to the site. Google Search Console is more urgent (for indexing control), but GA4 shows what visitors actually DO on the site.

🟠 Duplicate Blog Topics

Two blog posts cover overlapping topics:
• /blog/dog-poop-laws-st-louis-missouri
• /blog/dog-poop-laws-missouri-st-louis
These have nearly identical titles and compete with each other for the same keywords (keyword cannibalization).

Impact: Google splits ranking signals between two similar pages instead of consolidating them into one strong page. Neither ranks as well as a single, comprehensive page would.
Fix: Merge into one comprehensive post and 301 redirect the other. Or differentiate them clearly — one for city-specific laws, one for county/state laws. 30-minute content task.
