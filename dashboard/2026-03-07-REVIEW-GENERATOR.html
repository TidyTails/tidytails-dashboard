💡 Pro Tips

✅ DO

• Ask after a great service visit
• Send the direct review link
• Ask for "honest feedback"
• Respond to every review
• Text between 10 AM - 2 PM
• Follow up once (after 3-5 days)

❌ DON'T

• Offer money/discounts for reviews
• Ask for "5-star reviews" specifically
• Bug people more than twice
• Review your own business
• Copy-paste identical responses
• Ignore negative reviews

🎯 Best Timing

• Right after service (2-4 hrs)
• After a compliment via text
• At the 1-month milestone
• Spring (motivation is high)
• After fixing an issue (recovery)

📈 Impact

• 5+ reviews = visible in Google Maps
• 10+ = competitive with big players
• 4.5+ stars = trust threshold
• Each review = ~2-5% more clicks
• Responding boosts ranking too
