## Risk Mitigation

**If leads are slow:**
- Increase ad spend
- Post more in Facebook groups
- Door-to-door in target neighborhoods (despite past results)
- Partner promotions with pet businesses

**If close rate is low:**
- Review pricing (consider matching Poop2Scoop at $18)
- Improve follow-up speed
- Add "first cleanup free" promotion

**If capacity maxes out:**
- Raise prices slightly
- Hire part-time helper
- Focus on higher-value weekly customers
