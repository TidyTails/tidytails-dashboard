# Facebook Ads Strategy for Tidy Tails

*Getting started with paid advertising*

---

## TL;DR

Facebook ads can work for local service businesses, but **don't start here**. Get Google Business Profile set up first — it's free and captures people already searching for your service. Facebook is for building awareness, Google is for capturing demand.

**Budget recommendation:** Start with $5-10/day once GBP is live and you have a basic website.

---

## Why Facebook Ads (Eventually)

- 2.9 billion active users
- 1.6 billion connected to small businesses
- Hyper-local targeting (zip codes, radius)
- Visual format great for before/after content
- Pet content performs well on social

---

## Targeting Strategy for STL

### Geographic Targeting
- Primary: 10-15 mile radius from your base
- Target specific zip codes in your service areas:
  - St. Louis County
  - Ferguson, Florissant
  - Maryland Heights, St. Ann
  - O'Fallon, St. Peters
  - St. Charles

### Interest Targeting
Layer these interests to find dog owners:

**Pet-Related:**
- Dog owners (behavior)
- Dogs (interest)
- Pets (interest)
- PetSmart, Petco (retail interest)
- Rover, Wag (service interest)
- ASPCA, local shelters

**Home-Related:**
- Homeowners (important — renters often can't hire yard services)
- Lawn & garden
- Home improvement

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28-65 (homeowners with disposable income)
- Household income: Middle to upper-middle class
- Family status: Parents (often busiest, most likely to outsource)

### Lookalike Audiences (Later)
Once you have customers, upload their emails to create a "lookalike audience" — Facebook finds similar people.

## Ad Creative Ideas

### Format: Video > Carousel > Image
Video performs best on Facebook. Keep it short (15-30 seconds).

### Content Ideas:

**Before/After:**
- Split screen: messy yard → clean yard
- "This is what 2 weeks of 'I'll do it tomorrow' looks like"

**Day-in-the-Life:**
- Quick montage of you scooping
- Show the dogs, the clean yard, the happy homeowner

**Problem/Solution:**
- "Tired of dodging landmines in your own backyard?"
- "Life's too short to spend it picking up poop"

**Testimonial:**
- Quick clip of a happy customer
- Or screenshot of a great review with voiceover

**Humor:**
- Lean into the poop jokes
- "We give a shit so you don't have to"
- "Your dog's business is our business"

### Copy Framework:

**Hook:** Address the pain point  
**Body:** Present the solution  
**CTA:** Clear next step

Example:
```
🐕 Your backyard shouldn't be a minefield.

Tidy Tails takes care of your dog's business so you can actually enjoy your yard again.

✅ No contracts
✅ Local owner-operated
✅ 100% satisfaction guaranteed

St. Louis's friendliest pooper scooper service.

👉 Get a free quote today
```

## Campaign Structure

### Campaign 1: Awareness (Top of Funnel)
- Objective: Video views or reach
- Audience: Broad local dog owners
- Creative: Funny/engaging video
- Goal: Get on people's radar
- Budget: $3-5/day

### Campaign 2: Consideration (Middle of Funnel)
- Objective: Traffic or lead generation
- Audience: Engaged from Campaign 1 + interests
- Creative: Before/after, testimonials
- Goal: Get them to your website/landing page
- Budget: $5-7/day

### Campaign 3: Conversion (Bottom of Funnel)
- Objective: Leads or conversions
- Audience: Website visitors, engaged users
- Creative: Strong offer, clear CTA
- Goal: Get them to request a quote
- Budget: Variable based on results

**Start simple:** Run Campaign 2 only until you have enough data.

---

## Budget Recommendations

### Starting Out ($200/month)
- $6.50/day average
- Run one campaign to test creative and targeting
- Focus on lead generation objective
- Test 2-3 different ads to see what works

### Scaling Up ($400/month)
- $13/day
- Run awareness + conversion campaigns
- Retarget website visitors
- Create lookalike audiences

### Measuring Success
Track these metrics:
- **CPM** (cost per 1,000 impressions): $10-20 is normal for local
- **CTR** (click-through rate): 1-2% is good
- **CPC** (cost per click): $1-3 is reasonable for local services
- **Cost per lead:** Track how many quote requests you get

## Action Items (In Order)

1. **Wait** — Don't start ads until GBP is live + basic website exists
2. Collect before/after photos (need for ads)
3. Get 1-2 video testimonials from current customers
4. Set up Facebook Business Manager and Pixel
5. Create landing page for ad traffic
6. Start with $5/day test campaign
7. Run for 2 weeks, analyze, adjust

## Facebook Ads vs. Other Options

| Channel | Cost | Effort | Best For |
|---------|------|--------|----------|
| Google Business Profile | Free | Low | Capturing search demand |
| Facebook Ads | $$ | Medium | Building awareness |
| Nextdoor | Free/$ | Low | Hyper-local community |
| Facebook Groups | Free | Medium | Organic engagement |
| Google Ads | $$$ | Medium | Search intent capture |
| Door hangers | $ | High | Didn't work well (0.2% response) |

**Priority order:** GBP → Website → Facebook Groups → Nextdoor → Facebook Ads → Google Ads
