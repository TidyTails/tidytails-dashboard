📸 What to Shoot at the Event

  • Table setup with flyers and signage — before anyone arrives (clean, intentional shot)
  • Jamie with a dog — any dog, any size. Pure engagement gold.
  • Wide shot of event crowd — shows energy, creates FOMO
  • Close-up of hands showing flyer or "First Visit FREE" text
  • Any adopted dog with new family — heartwarming, shareable, on-brand
  • The yard bag / tools if you brought them — shows what you do
  • Any funny/cute dog moment — always goes viral if you caption it right
  • Quick 15-sec video of Jamie talking to camera ("We're live at the Paw-ty!")

❌ What NOT to Shoot

  • Close-ups of strangers' faces without asking permission first
  • Kids — never without explicit parent permission
  • Anything messy or off-brand (empty tables, downtime, etc.)
  • Your phone screen or other screens (looks amateur)

🎥 Reels / Video Content to Create

  • "Meet one of our newest customers!" — if someone consents, short intro
  • Boomerang: Jamie fanning out flyers or signing someone up
  • Time-lapse of table setup if you arrive early

  • Offer (1 line): First visit FREE, $35/mo promo
