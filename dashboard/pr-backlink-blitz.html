St. Louis Pet Waste Removal Company Tidy Tails Expands Service Area Amid Competitor Closures

ST. LOUIS, MO — March 20, 2026

Tidy Tails, a locally owned pet waste removal service based in St. Louis County, today announced expanded service coverage across North County, St. Charles, and the I-44 Corridor as spring 2026 kicks off — filling a gap left by multiple competitors who have exited the St. Louis market in recent months.

Founded by Jamie Carter, Tidy Tails provides weekly, biweekly, and monthly yard cleanup services for dog owners who want a clean yard without the hassle. The company now serves over 20 communities across St. Louis County, St. Charles County, Florissant, Ferguson, Maryland Heights, O'Fallon, St. Peters, Kirkwood, and Webster Groves.

"We started this because we're dog people first," said Carter. "Families are busy. Nobody wants to spend their Saturday afternoon dodging landmines in the backyard. We show up same day, same time, every week — and we text you when it's done."

The St. Louis pet waste removal market has seen significant consolidation, with several national franchise operators and local companies recently closing or suspending operations. Tidy Tails is positioned as one of the few remaining locally owned options in North St. Louis County, where competitor coverage has dropped to near zero.

The company offers simple, contract-free pricing starting at $25 per week, with a popular $70 monthly flat-rate plan. A 100% satisfaction guarantee backs every service visit.

"We're not a franchise. There's no call center. When you call Tidy Tails, you get the same person who's going to be in your yard," Carter added. "That consistency is what families want."

With spring cleanup season beginning, Tidy Tails is offering free quotes to new customers across its expanded service area.

Tidy Tails is a locally owned and operated pet waste removal company serving St. Louis County, St. Charles County, and surrounding communities. The company provides reliable, affordable pooper scooper services with no contracts, simple pricing, and a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Learn more at tidytailsstl.com.
