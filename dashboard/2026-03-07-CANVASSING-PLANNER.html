🎯 The Strategy

Not all neighborhoods are equal. This plan targets streets where dog owners with yards live — the exact profile of a Tidy Tails customer. Ranked by:

  • Yard size — bigger yards = more poop = more need
  • Dog park proximity — near Vlasis/Queeny/Conway = confirmed dog owners
  • Family density — families with kids + dogs = "too busy to scoop" demographic
  • Competitor gap — areas where Doggie Doodies and others DON'T advertise
  • Route efficiency — clustered streets you can hit in one pass

📈 Expected Results

Industry data on door-to-door flyers for pet waste removal:

  • Response rate: 1-3% for generic flyers, 3-5% for targeted (dog-owner streets)
  • 200 flyers × 3% response = 6 inquiries
  • 50% close rate = 3 new customers
  • 3 × $95/mo avg = $285/mo recurring ($3,420/year)
  • Cost: ~$30 for 200 flyers at FedEx/Staples + 3 hours of time
  • ROI: 114x first year

⏰ When to Canvas

Best times (Saturday/Sunday):

  • 🌅 9-11 AM — People are home, walking dogs, doing yard work. You'll SEE the dogs.
  • 🌆 4-6 PM — Second dog walk of the day. Yards are visible.
  • ❌ Avoid 12-2 PM (nobody answers doors at lunch)
  • 💡 Pro tip: Look for houses with dog waste in the yard as you walk. Those are your hottest leads — they literally need you RIGHT NOW.

🚗 Optimized Saturday Morning Route

Start at 9 AM, done by noon. Hits all Tier 1 neighborhoods in one efficient loop. Print 200 flyers before you start.

1

☕ 8:30 AM — Print & Prep

Print 200 door hangers at FedEx Office (14856 Manchester Rd, Ballwin). Cost: ~$30 for B&W on cardstock, $50 for color. Grab a coffee. Load the car.

2

🐕 9:00 AM — Queeny Park Dog Park

Tails & Trails, 550 Weidman Rd. Spend 20 min chatting with dog owners. Leave flyers on the bulletin board (if allowed). Hand them out personally — "Hey, I'm TJ, I run Tidy Tails..." This is your #1 guerrilla marketing spot.

3

🏘️ 9:30 AM — Woodsmill Subdivision

Start at Woodsmill Dr entrance. Work your way through all connecting courts. Look for dog signs: Invisible Fence signs, "Beware of Dog" stickers, dog toys, poop bags on porches, visible waste in yards. Mark those houses for follow-up text if they don't respond to flyer.

4

🏘️ 10:15 AM — Wild Wood Subdivision

Drive to Wildwood Dr. Hit the main road first (highest visibility), then work cul-de-sacs. You'll cross near Parkway West HS. Focus on homes with visible backyards. Skip apartments/condos.

5

🏘️ 11:00 AM — Castle Pines

Drive to Castle Pines Dr. This is a newer subdivision so the streets are shorter. Work efficiently through the cul-de-sacs. HOA-managed = these people CARE about clean yards. Your flyer should hit hard here.

6

🐕 11:30 AM — Vlasis Park Quick Stop

Quick pass through Vlasis Park dog area. Leave flyers with anyone walking dogs. This catches the late-morning crowd. 10 min max.

7

🏁 11:45 AM — Done!

Head home. Should have distributed 150-200 flyers across 3 neighborhoods + 2 dog parks. Track responses in the Tracker tab. Repeat with Tier 2 neighborhoods next weekend.

💡 Pro Tips While Canvassing

  • Wear a Tidy Tails shirt/hat if you have one. If not, just look presentable.
  • Bring business cards for face-to-face conversations at dog parks.
  • Take photos of dog waste you spot in yards (for your own reference, not to shame people). Houses with visible waste = hot leads.
  • If someone answers the door: "Hey! I'm TJ, I run Tidy Tails — we scoop dog poop so you don't have to. Just leaving a flyer. Got dogs?" 15-second pitch, max.
  • Note any "For Sale" signs — realtors recommend yard cleanup before showings. Potential one-time job leads.
  • If you see someone walking a dog: that's a better lead than any flyer. Chat them up.

🐕 Dog Park Guerrilla Marketing

Dog parks are concentrated clusters of your exact target customer. Every person there has a dog AND a yard (apartment dwellers use sidewalks, not dog parks). Here's your attack plan.

🏆 Tails & Trails at Queeny Park (PRIMARY)

📍 550 Weidman Rd, Ballwin, MO 63011

Why It's #1:

  • 5-acre membership dog park = committed dog owners who SPEND money on their pets
  • Separate areas for large & small dogs = high capacity, lots of people
  • Has waste stations already = people are aware of the poop problem
  • Agility course, skill stations = engaged pet parents (premium customers)

Best Times to Visit:

  • Saturday 9-10 AM — Peak morning crowd
  • Saturday 4-5 PM — Evening rush
  • Sunday 10-11 AM — Relaxed crowd, longer conversations

Tactics:

  • Stand near the entrance/exit — everyone passes through
  • Lead with their dog: "Great looking [breed]! How old?" → then transition to your pitch
  • Ask the park staff if you can leave a small stack of flyers or cards at the entrance
  • Offer a dog park special: "Mention Queeny Park for $20 off your first month"

🥈 Vlasis Park Dog Area

📍 Vlasis Park, Ballwin, MO 63011

Why It Matters:

  • Fenced off-leash area — draws from surrounding Ballwin neighborhoods
  • More casual atmosphere = easier to start conversations
  • Adjacent to residential streets (Fox Creek, nearby subdivisions)

Best Times:

  • Weekday evenings 5-7 PM — After-work crowd
  • Weekend mornings 9-11 AM

🥉 Conway Park (Chesterfield)

📍 Conway Park, Chesterfield, MO

Why It Matters:

  • Chesterfield = higher income = premium pricing tier
  • Direct competition with Doggie Doodies' territory — steal their potential customers
  • Newer park, growing community

Strategy:

  • Position as the local alternative: "We're not a franchise, we're your neighbor"
  • Chesterfield dog owners can support the $95-135/mo tiers

🎯 Dog Park Conversation Script

[While petting their dog or watching dogs play] "Beautiful [breed]! How old is [he/she]?" [Let them talk — people LOVE talking about their dogs] "I bet [he/she] keeps you busy at home too, huh?" [If they mention yard/cleanup/mess — THAT'S your opening] "Ha, I actually started a business for exactly that — I'm TJ, I run Tidy Tails. We come once a week and scoop everything so you never have to think about it. Here's my card." [If they seem interested:] "We're running a spring special right now — $20 off your first month if you mention Queeny Park. And the first cleanup is free so you can see how it works." [If they're lukewarm:] "No pressure at all — just keep the card. When spring hits and that winter buildup thaws... you'll know who to call." [smile]
