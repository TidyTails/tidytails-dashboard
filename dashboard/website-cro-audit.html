🚨 #1 — No Analytics = Flying Blind

CRITICAL
Zero tracking on the website
No Google Analytics. No Facebook Pixel. No conversion tracking. We're spending time and money driving traffic to a site and have zero idea what's working. How many people visit? Where do they come from? How many click "Get Quote"? How many submit the form? We don't know any of this.
🔧 Fix (15 minutes)
1. Create a Google Analytics 4 property at analytics.google.com
2. Add the GA4 script tag to the <head> of the site
3. Set up a "form_submit" conversion event
4. Optional: Add Facebook Pixel for future ad retargeting
5. Set up a Google Search Console property (verify via DNS or HTML tag)
💰 Impact: Without this, every other optimization is a guess. This is step zero.

🚨 #2 — No Reviews or Social Proof on the Page

CRITICAL
Zero customer testimonials, ratings, or review counts
The site mentions "reviews" in passing but doesn't display a single customer quote, star rating, Google review count, or before/after photo. For a service where someone is trusting a stranger in their backyard, social proof is everything. Competitors like Scoop Soldiers have Google reviews front and center.

❌ Current Site

"Just a local St. Louis guy who knows your neighborhood" — no proof this is true. No customer voices. Visitor has to take Jamie's word for it.

✅ What Converts

"⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 stars from 23 happy families" + 3-4 real customer quotes with names + neighborhoods. "Our yard has never looked better!" — Sarah M., Ballwin

🔧 Fix (30 minutes)
1. Add a "What Our Customers Say" section between pricing and FAQ
2. Include 3-4 real testimonials (ask current customers for a quick text)
3. Add Google review count + star rating near the top (even "5.0 ⭐ on Google" is powerful)
4. Consider embedding Google Reviews widget (free, auto-updates)
5. Add "XX+ yards cleaned" counter if you can estimate it
💰 Impact: Social proof increases conversion by 15-30% on service pages. This is the #1 trust gap.

⚠️ #3 — No Social Share Image (og:image)

HIGH
Sharing on Facebook/Nextdoor shows no image preview
When someone shares the Tidy Tails link on Facebook, Nextdoor, or in a text message, there's no preview image. It shows a blank gray box or a tiny logo. This kills click-through rates from social sharing — the #1 way local service businesses get word-of-mouth referrals online. Every Nextdoor post, every Facebook share, every iMessage link preview is an opportunity wasted.
🔧 Fix (10 minutes)
1. Create a 1200×630px image with the Tidy Tails logo, tagline, and a clean yard photo
2. Add <meta property="og:image" content="https://tidytailsstl.com/og-image.jpg">
3. Add <meta property="og:image:width" content="1200"> and height 630
4. Test at Facebook Sharing Debugger
💰 Impact: Proper og:image increases social CTR by 2-3x. Critical for Nextdoor/Facebook strategy.

⚠️ #4 — Only 2 Images on the Entire Site (Both Are the Logo)

HIGH
No photos of Jamie, clean yards, dogs, or the service in action
The entire website has exactly 2 images — both are the Tidy Tails logo. No photo of Jamie. No before/after yard shots. No cute dog photos. No action shots of the service. This makes the site feel like a template, not a real local business. People buy from people, not from text walls.

❌ Current "Meet Jamie" Section

Text-only paragraph about Jamie. No photo. Visitor doesn't know what Jamie looks like. "Trust this stranger in your yard" is a hard sell with no face.

✅ What Converts

Photo of Jamie smiling with a dog, wearing a Tidy Tails shirt. Maybe action shot of service. Immediately builds trust — "oh, he seems like a good dude."

🔧 Fix (1 hour)
1. Take 5-6 photos: Jamie headshot, Jamie with a dog, clean yard shot, equipment, before/after
2. Add Jamie photo next to the "Meet Jamie" section
3. Add 2-3 photos throughout the page (hero area, service steps, about)
4. Compress images (TinyPNG) before uploading — keep page fast
5. Add proper alt text for SEO ("Jamie from Tidy Tails cleaning a yard in Ballwin MO")
💰 Impact: Pages with relevant images get 94% more views. A face photo alone can increase trust by 35%.

⚠️ #5 — Quote Form Lacks Urgency & Incentive

MEDIUM
No reason to fill out the form RIGHT NOW
The form says "Get Your Free Quote" — fine, but generic. No urgency. No incentive. No mention of how fast they'll hear back. Visitors who are "just browsing" will leave and forget. The form also requires email (which many mobile users skip) and has no indication of what happens after submission.
🔧 Fix (15 minutes)
1. Add urgency: "🌸 Spring Special: First cleanup FREE with weekly service signup"
2. Add speed promise: "We respond within 1 hour during business hours"
3. Add post-submit message: "Thanks! Jamie will text you within the hour."
4. Make email optional (phone is more important for local service)
5. Add trust badges near button: "No contracts • Cancel anytime • Satisfaction guaranteed"
6. Consider adding SMS opt-in checkbox for easy follow-up
💰 Impact: Adding urgency/incentive to forms typically increases submissions by 20-40%.

🟣 #6 — No Before/After Proof

OPPORTUNITY
Missing the most powerful visual proof for this industry
Before/after photos are the most compelling content type for cleaning services. They're visceral, shareable, and impossible to argue with. The site has zero. Even one good before/after slider would massively increase perceived value. Competitors who show nasty yards → clean yards close way more leads.
🔧 Fix (ongoing)
1. Start photographing yards BEFORE cleaning (even phone photos work)
2. Photo the same spot AFTER cleaning
3. Add a before/after image slider to the homepage (free JS libraries available)
4. Post before/afters on Instagram/Facebook weekly (they go viral in local groups)
5. Add a simple gallery page: "/results" or "/our-work"
💰 Impact: Before/after content has 3-5x engagement vs standard posts. Huge for social proof + social media.
