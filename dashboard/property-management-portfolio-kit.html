Why the Portfolio Play Changes the Math

🎯

1 Call = Multiple Contracts

Cold-calling individual properties = 18 calls for 18 leads. Calling Millsap = 1 call for potentially 7 contracts. The effort/reward ratio is 7× better on the portfolio approach.

🔒

Portfolio Contracts Are Sticky

A management company that signs a portfolio agreement doesn't cancel casually. Switching costs are high — they'd have to re-evaluate vendors across all properties. Churn risk is dramatically lower than individual accounts.

📣

Complaints Are Their Pain

Property managers receive pet waste complaints constantly. You're not selling a cleaning service — you're selling a complaint elimination service. Frame the ROI as saved staff time and protected reviews.

🌐

Referrals Compound

One management company recommends you to other PM companies they know. The commercial referral network is tight in STL. One portfolio relationship can cascade into 3-4 companies over a year.
