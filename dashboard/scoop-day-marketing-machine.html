📱 Photo Tips (30 seconds)

  • Before: Wide shot of yard BEFORE scooping (shows the mess)
  • After: Same angle AFTER (shows the magic)
  • Dogs: If the dog is out, snap a pic. Dog content = gold on social
  • Permission: Ask "Mind if I snap a photo of the yard for our page?" Most say yes.
  • Lighting: Face the sun. Morning light is the best light.
  • Never: Don't show poop close-up (gross). Show the CLEAN result.
