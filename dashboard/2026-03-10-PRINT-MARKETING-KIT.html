🎯 The Neighborhood Blitz Strategy

TJ has 3 customers in Florissant and 2 in Saint Louis. That's density. Here's the play:

  1. Sunday scooping day: While servicing each yard, place the yard sign in the front lawn
  2. Before leaving the neighborhood: Hit 20-30 doors around each customer with door hangers
  3. Direct neighbors: Use the special "Neighbor Card" on houses immediately adjacent to customers
  4. Leave business cards at every vet office, pet store, and groomer in those ZIP codes
  5. Truck magnet = passive advertising while driving between jobs
💡 Math: 8 customers × 20 hangers per stop = 160 hangers/week. At 3% response, that's ~5 leads/week. At 50% close, that's 2-3 new customers/week. Do this for 4 weeks = 8-12 new customers for under $100.

Why Magnets > Vinyl Wrap

  • Removable: Take off when not working (personal use, other jobs)
  • Cheap: ~$25-30 for a pair vs $500+ for a wrap
  • Replaceable: Update design anytime without commitment
  • Still effective: Every stoplight, parking lot, and neighborhood = impressions

Magnet Pro Tips

  • Get two magnets — one for each side of the truck
  • Remove and clean weekly — moisture under magnets can trap dirt
  • Don't put on curved surfaces — they'll fly off at highway speed
  • Park facing the street at customer homes for max visibility
🖨️ How to print: VistaPrint Car Magnets → 12"×24" → Pair ≈ $30-40. Or Amazon "custom car magnets" for cheaper options.

📋 Week 2+ Distribution Checklist (Every Sunday)

  • Load 30 door hangers + 10 neighbor cards in truck
  • Place yard signs at customer homes before scooping
  • Put magnets on truck before leaving
  • At each stop: hang 20-30 hangers on surrounding doors
  • At each stop: hand-deliver neighbor cards to adjacent houses
  • Pick up yard signs after scooping (or leave if customer okays)
  • Drop business cards at 1-2 locations (vet, store, groomer)
  • Track: how many hangers distributed, any calls received

📊 Tracking Results

You need to know what's working. Simple system:

  • Ask every lead: "How'd you hear about us?" — track in CRM
  • Different phone greeting: "Thanks for calling Tidy Tails!" → ask source
  • Neighbor cards = easiest to track: "Mention this card for free first cleanup"
  • Count hangers distributed per area — see which neighborhoods respond best
  • Test headlines: Print 100 of Version A, 100 of Version B, see which gets more calls
