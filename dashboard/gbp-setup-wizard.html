✅ Verification

Google will ask you to verify. Methods vary — you might get:

Postcard verification — Google mails a postcard with a code (5-14 days) Most common for new service-area businesses
Phone or email verification — Instant if available Not always offered, but check
Video verification — Record a short video of your business Show signage, equipment, or work vehicle
⚠️ Don't change your business name, address, or primary category while waiting for verification. Any significant edits can restart the process.
