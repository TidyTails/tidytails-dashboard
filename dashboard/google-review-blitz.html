🎯 Review Velocity Strategy

Google cares about recency and consistency, not just total count:

  • Goal after blitz: 1 new review per month minimum
  • Ask every new customer after their 3rd service
  • Seasonal asks: Send a "thank you" text in spring/fall with review link
  • After deep cleans: Perfect time to ask — they see dramatic results
  • Don't batch: 1-2 reviews per week looks more natural than 8 in one day
⚠️ Spread it out: Don't have all 8 customers review on the same day — Google's spam filter may flag sudden review spikes. The 14-day timeline is designed to look natural. Stagger the asks over Week 1-2.
