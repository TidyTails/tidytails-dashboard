• Speed wins: Respond within 15 minutes if possible. Lead response time = #1 conversion factor
• Day 2 nudge: If no reply after first response, follow up next day: "Hey, just checking in — still interested?"
• Day 5 last shot: "No worries if the timing isn't right. I'll be around whenever you need me 👍"
• Don't ghost your leads — even a "no" is better than silence. 3 follow-ups is the sweet spot.
