⚡ Two Paths — Pick One

Option A: Google Local Services Ads (LSA)

$0 setup + pay per lead

🏆 THE MOVE for Tidy Tails right now

  • Pay per LEAD, not per click — only pay when someone actually calls/messages
  • Shows the "Google Guaranteed" badge = instant trust
  • Appears ABOVE regular Google Ads (top of page)
  • Shows your star rating + review count (6 reviews, 5.0★ = strong)
  • $15-30 per lead (better than regular ads for service businesses)
  • Set a weekly budget cap — never spend more than you want
  • Can dispute junk leads and get refunds
  • Perfect for home service businesses

⚠️ Requirement: Background Check

Google requires a background check for LSA (through Pinkerton). Takes 2-5 business days. No cost. They verify you're a real, legit business. This is actually a competitive advantage — filters out scammers.

Option B: Standard Google Search Ads

$100-150 /month to start
  • Full control over ad copy, keywords, targeting
  • Appears below LSA but above organic results
  • Pay per click ($3-6 per click)
  • Can target specific zip codes and neighborhoods
  • Better for commercial targeting (property managers searching)
  • Can run immediately — no background check needed
  • More complex to manage (or hire someone)

🎯 Franklin's Recommendation

Do BOTH, sequentially:

  • Week 1: Apply for LSA + complete GBP profile (free, 25 min)
  • Week 2-3: While waiting for LSA approval, launch a small Google Ads campaign ($5/day = $150/mo)
  • Week 3+: Once LSA is live, run both and compare cost-per-customer
  • Month 2: Double down on whichever channel converts better

⚠️ Mistakes That Burn Money

1. Running ads without a complete GBP

People click your ad → Google you → find an incomplete profile → bounce to a competitor. Complete GBP FIRST or you're paying for competitor leads.

2. Using "Broad Match" keywords

Broad match on "dog poop" will show your ad for "dog poop bags" and "how to clean dog poop" — DIY searchers who'll never hire you. Use Phrase Match or Exact Match only.

3. Not checking the Search Terms Report

Google shows what people ACTUALLY typed. Check it daily for the first week. Add garbage terms as negatives. This alone can cut waste by 30-40%.

4. Targeting "People interested in your location"

Google defaults to showing ads to people "interested in" St. Louis — including tourists and people browsing from other states. Change to "People IN your targeted locations" only.

5. Not answering the phone

60% of Google Ads leads call directly. If TJ misses a call, that $5-6 click is wasted AND the customer calls competitor #2. Set up a missed call auto-text: "Hey! Sorry I missed your call. I'm TJ from Tidy Tails — I'll call you back within 30 minutes!"
