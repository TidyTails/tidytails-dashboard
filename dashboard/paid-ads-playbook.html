🎯 Recommended Campaign Setup

Campaign Type: Lead Generation (Engagement)

  • Objective: Leads or Engagement (NOT Traffic or Awareness)
  • Budget: $5/day ($150/month)
  • Duration: Run continuously — NEVER pause (Swoop Scoop's #1 rule)
  • Geo-target: 15-mile radius around Florissant, MO 63031

Targeting (Test These 3 Audiences):

  • Audience A — Dog Lovers: Interest in "Dogs," "Dog walking," "Pet supplies," "Chewy," "BarkBox" + Age 25-65 + Homeowners
  • Audience B — Advantage+: Let Meta's algorithm find your people (works surprisingly well)
  • Audience C — Lookalike: Upload your 8 customer emails → Meta finds similar people (need 100+ for best results, save for later)

Ad Creative Rules (From a 7-Figure Business):

  • ✅ Phone photos outperform professional shots — more relatable
  • ✅ Start with a callout: "Attention Dog Owners in Florissant! 🐕"
  • ✅ Include a promo: FREE first cleanup, or $20 off first month
  • ✅ Show real people — Jamie smiling, a clean yard, happy dogs
  • ❌ No stock photos, no AI-generated images, no text-heavy graphics

🚀 5-Minute Quick Start — Launch Your First FB Ad

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and log in
  2. Click "Create Ad" → Choose "Leads" as your objective
  3. Set budget: $5/day → Run continuously
  4. Targeting: Location = 15 miles from Florissant, MO → Interests = "Dogs" or "Pets" → Age 25-65 → Homeowners (optional)
  5. Ad creative: Copy one of the 3 templates above → Take a phone photo of a clean yard with a dog → Upload
  6. CTA button: "Call Now" or "Send Message" → Link to phone (314) 850-7140
  7. Review & publish. That's it. Check results in 48 hours.
Pro tip from Swoop Scoop: Run 2-3 ad variations and let Meta show the winner. Kill the loser after 7 days, create a new variation, repeat. This is how you improve over time.
