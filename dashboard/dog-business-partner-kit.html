📞 Script 1 — Cold Call Opener (Universal)

▼
"Hi, this is TJ with Tidy Tails — we're a dog waste removal company right here in [St. Louis / Chesterfield / etc]. I know you're busy so I'll be quick. I wanted to reach out because we serve a lot of the same dog owners you do — people who care enough to invest in their dogs. I had two ideas for working together: First, we could provide weekly cleanup of your outdoor play areas and runs. Takes it off your staff's plate completely, keeps the facility clean, and is something your clients actually notice. Second, your boarding and daycare clients all have dogs at home with yards. We could set up a simple referral arrangement — they get a first cleanup free, you get to offer a useful perk to your clients at zero cost to you. Would you have 5 minutes to chat about either of those?"

🐾 Script 2 — Outdoor Area Cleaning Pitch

▼
"With [X] dogs coming through your facility every week, your outdoor runs and play areas have significant waste volume — that's just the nature of the business. We do weekly professional outdoor area cleanup for pet facilities. We come in, do a thorough grid sweep of all outdoor areas, bag and remove everything. Your staff doesn't have to touch it, it's consistent, and your clients notice the difference. Pricing depends on the size of your outdoor areas — typical pet facility contracts run $150-400/month. For a facility like yours I'd want to walk the outdoor areas and give you a real quote. Would you be open to a quick walkthrough this week?"

🤝 Script 3 — Referral Partnership Pitch

▼
"The simplest version of working together doesn't require any contract or payment from you. Here's how it works: We give you a small stack of referral cards that say 'First cleanup free at Tidy Tails — compliments of [Business Name].' You put them at your front desk. Your clients pick them up when they're waiting. That's it. Your clients get a free first visit from a reliable local service. You get to offer a useful perk to your members without spending anything. And we get warm leads from people who are already proven dog-care investors. If it generates a few customers, we could formalize it — a standing recommendation in your new client welcome packet, mention in your newsletter, something like that. But the cards at the desk require zero commitment from either of us. Can we try that?"
