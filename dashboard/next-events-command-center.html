🗺️ The Arch Bark Situation

Arch Bark is a smaller, more curated dog event than Bark in the Park — typically 200-600 attendees. The Gateway Arch backdrop makes it photo-friendly and social-media-ready. Attendees skew toward urban/near-south-city dog owners. Different zip code from your current customer base (Florissant cluster) — so every lead here is genuinely additive, not overlap.

🎯 What Makes This Event Different

  • Outdoor festival vibe — people are relaxed, lingering, open to conversation
  • Dog-centric crowd — everyone there loves dogs and probably hates picking up poop
  • Not a competitor event — you're a local business, not a vendor; more authentic
  • Social content goldmine — Arch backdrop = instant shareable photos for your feed
  • Smaller = better conversations — higher depth per lead vs. massive events

📍 Territory Angle

Arch Bark draws from south city, downtown, Midtown, Soulard, Maplewood, etc. These aren't your current North County customers — they're NEW territory. Use this language:

"We just launched service in this area! I'm TJ — trying to grow my route in south city so I can stop driving 45 minutes between stops. If you're in [neighborhood], I'll actually be nearby every week."

The "local route density" angle makes your pitch feel genuinely personal to their neighborhood.

👫 2-Person Strategy

Bring Jamie. Split the crowd. One works the 5K/running crowd (active people = responsible dog owners). One works the festival area near vendors. Double your card distribution. Reconnect every 30 min to share what's working.

🎁 Raffle / Giveaway Strategy

Run a simple raffle: "Put your card in the bowl to win a FREE month of service ($70 value)." This does three things:

  • Creates a reason to stop at your booth (curiosity/free stuff)
  • Gives you everyone's business card = phone number + email
  • Makes everyone feel like they might win something from you
  • Draw the winner live at 1 PM — announce on IG Stories for content
  • Even "losers" get: "No worries — here's a free first cleanup card anyway." Everyone wins something.
