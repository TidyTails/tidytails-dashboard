📈 Why Premium Wins Long-Term

1. Budget customers churn. The #1 reason people cancel pet waste service is inconsistency — missed visits, poor quality, can't reach anyone. That's exactly what budget services produce. You don't have that problem.


2. Premium customers refer. People who value quality talk about it. Your best marketing channel is word-of-mouth from happy customers who feel they're getting a premium experience.


3. The math works. At $25/week you need 25 customers for ~$2,500/month. At $12/week (POOP 911 price) you'd need 52 customers for the same revenue. Half the customers = half the drive time, half the wear on your truck, and way more time to deliver great service.


4. Owner-operated is the moat. POOP 911 and Doggie Doodies literally cannot offer what you offer — one person, every time, who cares. As they scale, they get LESS personal. As you scale (up to your capacity), you maintain it.

🤝 The 5-Step Premium Close

Use this framework for every prospect conversation. It naturally guides them from curiosity to commitment without ever feeling pushy.

1
Connect — Learn About Their Dogs
Start with the dogs, not the service. Names, breeds, how many, how long they've had them. This builds rapport AND gives you info for the quote.
"Tell me about your pups! How many do you have, and are they mostly using the backyard?"
2
Discover — Find Their Pain Point
Everyone has a specific trigger that made them look you up. Find it. It's your closing ammo.
"What made you start looking into this? Was it just getting behind on it, or did something specific happen?" [Common answers: stepped in it, kids can't play outside, having a party, just tired of doing it]
3
Differentiate — Plant the Owner-Operated Seed
Don't trash competitors. Just casually mention what makes you different. Let them connect the dots.
"So here's how I work — I'm the owner and I do every single cleanup myself. No crews, no rotation. I'll be in your yard every [DAY] around the same time. When I'm done I text you a photo so you know it's handled. Most people tell me that consistency is what they were missing."
4
Quote — Price with Confidence
State the price clearly, then immediately reframe it. Never pause awkwardly after saying the number.
"For [X] dogs on a weekly schedule, it's $[PRICE] per visit. That works out to about $[DAILY] a day — less than a coffee — and you literally never think about poop again. I also do a free first cleanup so you can see exactly what you're getting."
5
Close — Free First Cleanup
The free first cleanup is your secret weapon. It removes ALL risk. Use it every single time.
"Here's what I'd suggest — let me come out and do your first cleanup free. You see the quality, you see how I communicate, and if you love it we get you on the schedule. If not, your yard's clean and you owe me nothing. Sound good? What day works best?"
🔑 Key insight: "What day works best?" is an assumptive close — you're not asking IF, you're asking WHEN. This works because the free cleanup already removed the risk. There's literally no reason to say no.
