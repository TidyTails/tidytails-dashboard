💡 Key Insights

🏘️ The HOA Goldmine

HOA communities (especially Ballwin) can mandate waste cleanup. One sale to the board = 10-50 customers at once. Recurring revenue on autopilot. Focus on Ballwin's subdivisions first.

🌱 Spring = Your Season

Winter thaw creates visible, urgent problems. People spending more time outdoors, noticing the mess. Spring cleaning mindset + new dog adoptions = perfect storm. Run "Spring Cleanup Special" NOW.

🏠 Local > Franchise

POOP 911 dominates online but they're a franchise. Your edge: "I'm your neighbor, not a 1-800 number." Personal service, flexibility, and community involvement beat corporate every time.

📍 Density = Profit

A scattered customer base kills margins. 5 customers in Kirkwood = more profit than 8 customers across 5 suburbs. Build density in one area before expanding. The triangle strategy does this.
