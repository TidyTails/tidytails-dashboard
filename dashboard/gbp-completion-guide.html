4 Photo tips:
  • Landscape orientation works best

  • Before/after is GOLD for this business — shows value instantly

8. Reply to All 6 Reviews Critical
⏱️ 5 minutes • From phone
▼
🚨 Replying to reviews is a RANKING FACTOR. Google literally says so. Unreplied reviews hurt your visibility. You have 6 reviews — reply to all of them.
1 In your GBP → tap "Reviews"
2 Reply to each one. Keep it personal — mention their name, something specific. Here are templates:
Template 1 (for long-time customers):

Thanks so much, [NAME]! It's been awesome taking care of your yard — we appreciate you trusting us. Looking forward to keeping things spotless for you! 🐕
Template 2 (for customers who mention service quality):

Really appreciate the kind words, [NAME]! We take pride in being reliable and communicating well — glad that comes through. Thank you for being a valued customer! 🙌
Template 3 (general):

Thank you, [NAME]! Reviews like this mean the world to a small local business. We love what we do and it shows in every yard. Thanks for choosing Tidy Tails! 💚
💡 Personalize each reply — don't copy-paste the exact same thing to all 6. Google (and customers) notice. Swap a sentence, mention their dog's name if you know it, reference their neighborhood. 30 seconds of personalization makes each reply genuine.
