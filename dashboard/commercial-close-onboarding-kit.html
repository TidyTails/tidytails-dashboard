📋 Property Walk — What to Document
Before the first service day, walk the property with the manager or solo (with permission). Document:

1. All service areas: Dog run, designated pet zones, common area patios, any area residents currently use.
2. Gate & access: Codes, key boxes, who to call if locked out. Test every gate.
3. Waste station locations: Where are bag dispensers and bins? Are they stocked? Offer to flag if they're empty.
4. Problem zones: Areas with high accumulation, poor drainage, near A/C units or walkways = priority spots.
5. Off-limits areas: Any areas NOT to enter (maintenance yards, private patios, mechanical rooms).
6. Photo map: Take 5–10 photos on your phone. Label them in notes. Reference for every future visit.
📱 Communication Setup
Who gets the completion text? Confirm manager's cell number. Test by texting before first service.

Billing contact: Often different from the property manager. Confirm email for invoices.

Emergency contact: If something unusual happens on property (fence down, gate broken, equipment issue), who do you call?

Monthly check-in: After the first 30 days, send a quick text: "How's everything looking? Anything I should adjust?" — this one message cuts commercial churn nearly in half.
🔄 First 30 Days — Retention Protocol
Day 1:Complete first service. Text manager on arrival + on completion. Follow up same day: "First service done at [PROPERTY] — let me know if anything looks different than expected." Day 7:Second service. Note any problem areas that are accumulating faster than expected. Report back if relevant. Day 14:Text manager: "Two weeks in — any feedback from residents or anything you want me to focus on?" — This keeps the account alive. Day 30:Send month 1 invoice. Include a brief note: "First month complete. [# visits], all areas serviced. Let me know if you want to expand scope or adjust anything going forward." Day 45:Check in on whether residents have noticed. Ask for a Google review or property manager referral to their management company's other properties.

💡 Pricing Tips
Go in 15–20% high on first quote. Leaves room to negotiate without leaving money on the table.
Never quote per-visit for apartments — monthly flat is always easier to get approved in a budget.
Offer the trial to hesitant properties — it converts at 60%+ because once they see it done right, they don't want to go back.
Dog daycare pricing: Multiple short visits/week beats one long visit for facilities — they want consistent cleanliness, not a weekly reset.
Multi-property discount: If a management company has 3+ properties, offer 10% off all if they sign all three. Locks in MRR and cuts the sales cycle.
