# 🚀 Tidy Tails Growth Roadmap

**Goal:** 4-5 → 25-30 recurring customers by August 2025  
**Timeline:** ~7 months (started late Jan 2025)  
**Budget:** $200/mo marketing (scalable)

---

## The Math

- **Current:** 5 customers × $100/mo = $500/mo
- **Target:** 30 customers × $100/mo = $3,000/mo
- **Growth needed:** 25 new customers in 7 months
- **Rate:** ~3-4 new customers per month

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2) ← WE ARE HERE

### Goals
- [ ] Google Business Profile live and optimized
- [ ] Simple landing page / website
- [ ] Reviews strategy in place

### Actions
1. **Google Business Profile**
   - Verify/create listing
   - Add photos, services, service area
   - Set up for reviews

2. **Website (Simple)**
   - One-page site with:
     - What we do
     - Pricing
     - Service areas
     - Contact/Quote form
   - SEO basics (keywords, meta tags)

3. **Reviews Foundation**
   - Ask existing customers for Google reviews
   - Create review request template
   - Set up review monitoring

### Success Metrics
- GBP showing in local search
- Website live and indexed
- 3-5 reviews collected

---

## Phase 2: Visibility (Weeks 3-6)

### Goals
- Organic leads coming in
- Social presence established
- Local awareness building

### Actions
1. **Local SEO**
   - Optimize for "pet waste removal St. Louis"
   - Get listed in local directories
   - Blog content (if time)

2. **Social Media**
   - Consistent posting schedule
   - Before/after photos
   - Customer testimonials
   - Local community engagement

3. **Community Marketing**
   - Nextdoor presence
   - Local Facebook groups (strategic, not spammy)
   - Neighborhood-specific posts

### Success Metrics
- 5+ organic leads per month
- 10+ Google reviews
- Social following growing

---

## Phase 3: Acceleration (Weeks 7-16)

### Goals
- Paid acquisition dialed in
- Referral engine running
- Scaling operations

### Actions
1. **Paid Ads**
   - Facebook/Instagram ads
   - Google Local Service Ads
   - Target: $200/mo budget
   - A/B test messaging

2. **Referral Program**
   - $X off for referrals
   - Easy sharing mechanism
   - Track referral sources

3. **Operations Prep**
   - Can TJ handle 20+ yards/week?
   - When to consider help?
   - Route optimization

### Success Metrics
- 15+ recurring customers
- Cost per acquisition < $50
- Referrals generating 20%+ of new business

---

## Phase 4: Scale (Weeks 17-28)

### Goals
- Hit 25-30 customer target
- Sustainable systems
- Profit optimization

### Actions
1. **Double down on what works**
   - Kill underperforming channels
   - Increase budget on winners

2. **Retention focus**
   - Customer satisfaction checks
   - Seasonal promotions
   - Loyalty program

3. **Efficiency**
   - Route optimization
   - Time tracking
   - Margin analysis

### Success Metrics
- 25-30 recurring customers ✓
- Healthy profit margins
- Predictable growth trajectory

---

## Key Assumptions

- TJ can service up to 15/week solo (comfortable)
- Average customer stays 12+ months
- Close rate on leads: TBD (need data)
- Market has room for growth (fragmented competition)

---

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low close rate | Improve sales process, offer trial |
| Seasonal slowdown | Prepaid packages, winter promotions |
| Capacity constraints | Plan hiring trigger points |
| Competition reacts | Focus on service quality, reviews |
