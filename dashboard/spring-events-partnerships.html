The Hook — If They Seem Interested
"How many dogs do you have? [React] Oh nice! So that's probably — what, couple times a day each? That adds up fast. We come out once a week and handle all of it. Most people say it's the best $70 they spend. Want me to text you more info?"

"Spring's here and I know what that means — everything that piled up over winter is thawing out! We're running a spring deep clean special right now — $99, we come clear out the whole yard. Most people add weekly service after and it's just $70 a month. Here's my card!"
