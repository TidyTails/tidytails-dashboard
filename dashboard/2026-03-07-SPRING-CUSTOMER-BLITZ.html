🔥 Proven Growth Tactics (Ranked by ROI)

1. Free Trial → Review Pipeline

💰 HIGHEST ROI — DO THIS FIRST
  • Offer 2-3 free cleanups to anyone with a dog in service area
  • After 2nd visit, ask for Google review
  • After final free visit, send conversion text with pricing
  • Even non-converts give you a review = future leads
  • Target: 5 new trials per week

2. Facebook Group Saturation

💰 HIGH ROI — FREE
  • Join every buy/sell/trade group in your service cities
  • Post 2-3x per week per group (vary the content!)
  • Funny posts get shared more — use humor
  • Many dog owners don't know this service exists — educate
  • Reply to EVERY comment within 30 minutes

3. Google Business Profile Domination

💰 HIGH ROI — FREE
  • Post updates weekly (offers, photos, tips)
  • Upload photos after every service (clean yard shots)
  • Respond to every review (boosts ranking)
  • Use keywords: "pet waste removal [city]" in description
  • Category: "Waste Management" (no pet waste option yet)
  • Goal: 50+ reviews to dominate the map pack

4. Nextdoor — Your Secret Weapon

💰 HIGH ROI — FREE
  • Create business page, post as the business
  • Offer "neighborhood discounts" exclusive to Nextdoor
  • Be helpful first — answer pet questions, give tips
  • Post spring cleanup specials
  • Neighbors trust Nextdoor recommendations over any ad

5. Referral Program

💰 HIGH ROI — NEARLY FREE
  • Offer 1 free month for every referral that signs up
  • Text all current customers the referral offer
  • Mention it at the end of every service
  • Cost: 1 month of service ($80) to acquire a customer worth $960/year
  • Best ROI: your existing customers already sell for you

6. Door Hangers in Target Neighborhoods

🟡 MEDIUM ROI — LOW COST
  • Focus on Tier 1 neighborhoods (Ballwin, Chesterfield, Manchester)
  • Saturday mornings: walk 2-3 streets, hang 30-50
  • Include spring special + Google review count
  • QR code to your Google profile or website
  • Expected response: 1-2% (1-2 leads per 100 hangers)
