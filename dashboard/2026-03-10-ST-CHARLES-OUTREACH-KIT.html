🗣️ Pitch Scripts

Copy-paste or memorize. Each script is under 30 seconds. Tap any script to copy.

🩺 For Vet Clinic Front Desk

"Hey! I'm TJ from Tidy Tails — we do pet waste removal here in St. Charles County. I know your clients love their pets, and a clean yard actually helps prevent parasites and keeps dogs healthier.

Would it be okay if I left some cards or flyers here? A lot of vet offices partner with us because it's a win-win — your clients get a cleaner yard, and you get to recommend a trusted local service.

We're not a franchise — just a local guy who shows up on time and does a great job."

🐕 For Dog Daycare Manager

"Hi, I'm TJ with Tidy Tails. We do weekly pet waste removal — basically, we keep people's yards clean so they can actually enjoy them.

Your daycare clients are my exact target customer — busy dog parents who love their pets but don't have time for everything. Would you be open to a referral partnership? I'll recommend your daycare to my clients, and you could pass along our cards.

I can leave a stack of flyers — no cost, no commitment. Just helping dog parents out."

🏞️ For Dog Park Conversations

"Hey! Great looking dog. [Compliment their dog specifically.]

Quick question — do you scoop your own yard, or does someone help you with that? [Wait for answer.]

If they scoop themselves: 'Yeah, it's a pain, right? I actually run a service that handles that — Tidy Tails. We come by weekly and take care of it. Most people say it's the best $20 they spend all week. Here's my card if you ever get tired of it.'

If they have someone: 'Oh nice, who do you use? [Gather intel.] Well, here's my card in case you ever want to try someone different.'

If they say no / not interested: 'No worries! If you know anyone who'd want help, pass my card along. Enjoy your day!'

📞 Cold Call (Before Visit)

"Hi, this is TJ with Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal. I'm going to be in the [St. Peters / O'Fallon / St. Charles] area this week and wanted to swing by to drop off some flyers.

We're a local pet waste cleanup service, and a lot of vet offices and daycares partner with us to give their clients a convenient referral.

Would it be okay if I stopped by [Wednesday/Thursday]? I'll just be quick — drop off flyers and say hi. Thanks!"

🎯 Key phrases that convert:
• "Local, not a franchise" — builds trust
• "Your clients" — makes it about THEM, not you
• "No cost, no commitment" — low friction
• "Parasites / health" — vet offices care about this
• "Best $20 they spend all week" — anchors price casually
