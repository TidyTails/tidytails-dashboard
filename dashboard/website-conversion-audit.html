🚨 5 Critical Conversion Killers

1. No Phone Number Visible Anywhere

CRITICAL

The #1 conversion killer. A potential customer lands on the site, wants to call, and has to hunt for a phone number. It's not in the header, not in the hero, not floating on mobile. Every single competitor has their phone number front and center:

• Yuckos: 314-291-7667 — HUGE, impossible to miss
• Poop2Scoop: 314-449-9447 — top of page, click-to-call
• DoodyCalls: (314) 230-8082 — header + multiple CTAs
• POOP 911: Phone number on every page
• Tidy Tails: 🦗 Nothing. "Call, text, or fill out our form" with no number.

⚡ Fix (5 minutes)

Add 314-850-7140 to: (1) top header bar, sticky on scroll, (2) hero section as a big click-to-call button, (3) floating phone icon on mobile, (4) footer. Format: <a href="tel:3148507140"> for instant tap-to-call.

2. No Customer Reviews or Testimonials

CRITICAL

Zero social proof on the site. No Google reviews, no testimonials, no star ratings, no customer count. Jamie's personal bio is great but it's the business vouching for itself. Competitors use reviews heavily:

• DoodyCalls: Testimonial carousel + review page
• Poop2Scoop: Testimonials section on homepage
• POOP 911: Review badges on every page
• People check reviews before buying local services — it's non-negotiable.

⚡ Fix (30 minutes)

Text your 4-5 current customers: "Hey! Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? Here's the link: [review link]". Add their quotes to the site immediately — even 3 testimonials changes the game. Also add: "Trusted by X families in STL County" even if X = 15 (total served, not just current).

3. No Instant Quote / Lead Capture Form on Homepage

CRITICAL

The pricing section shows prices but there's no way to immediately sign up or get a quote. No inline form, no "Get Started" button that goes to a form, no zip code checker. The CTA says "Get Your Free Quote" in the how-it-works section but doesn't link anywhere obvious.

• DoodyCalls: Big orange "Get A Free Quote Today" button → instant form
• Scoop Soldiers: "Get Instant Pricing" → zip code → instant quote
• Poop2Scoop: Contact form right on homepage + "Order Service" button
• Every second without a clear next step = lost lead.

⚡ Fix (20 minutes)

Add a simple form in the hero: Name, Phone, Zip Code, # of Dogs → "Get My Free Quote" button. Can use Google Forms, Typeform, or Jotform if custom code is hard. Also add a sticky "Get a Free Quote" button that follows the user as they scroll. Each pricing card should have its own "Sign Up" or "Get Started" button.

4. Zero Search Engine Visibility (STILL)

CRITICAL

Confirmed again today: site:tidytailsstl.com returns ZERO results on Google, Bing, and Brave Search. The site literally doesn't exist to anyone searching for pet waste removal in St. Louis. All that content — 18 city pages, 12 blog posts — is invisible.

This has been the #1 finding in multiple audits. Google Search Console has NEVER been set up. This is like having a billboard in a closet.

⚡ Fix (10 minutes to start, 48hrs to index)

Go to search.google.com/search-console, add tidytailsstl.com, verify via DNS TXT record (Cloudflare makes this easy). Submit sitemap. Request indexing of homepage + top city pages. This is a one-time 10-minute task that unlocks ALL organic traffic forever.

5. No Text/SMS Option Despite Mentioning It

HIGH

The site says "Call, text, or fill out our form" but provides no text number and no form. For the target demographic (busy families, millennials with dogs), texting IS the preferred contact method. 90% of leads prefer text over phone calls.

⚡ Fix (5 minutes)

Add a "Text Us" button with <a href="sms:3148507140">Text Us 📱</a>. On mobile, this opens the messaging app pre-filled with the number. Pair with the phone number in the header: "📞 Call or 💬 Text: 314-850-7140"

6. No Urgency or Scarcity Triggers

MEDIUM

The site reads like a brochure — informative but passive. No seasonal offers, no limited-time pricing, no "spots filling up" messaging. Compare to competitors who use urgency constantly.

⚡ Fix (10 minutes)

Add a banner: "🌸 Spring Special: First Week FREE — Limited spots in [Ballwin/Chesterfield/your area]" or "Book this week, get your first cleanup free." Even if it's always available, framing it as limited drives action.

7. Contact Page = Homepage (Broken)

MEDIUM

/contact loads the exact same content as the homepage. There's no dedicated contact page with a form, map, service area details, or business hours. Users who click "Contact" expect a contact experience.

⚡ Fix (30 minutes)

Create a real /contact page with: contact form (name, phone, email, address, # dogs, how you heard about us), phone number, text number, email, service area map, business hours, response time promise ("We respond within 2 hours").
