Why This Offer Works
🧠 The Psychology
  • Pain point is REAL right now: Yards have 3-5 months of accumulated winter poop. Snow melted. It's disgusting. Homeowners are dreading going out there.
  • "Spring Deep Clean" sounds premium: Not "pooper scooper" — it's a DEEP CLEAN. Reframes the service as professional yard care.
  • One-time entry point is low-risk: $99 for a one-time cleanup feels easy. No commitment. But once they see a clean yard...
  • The 50% off first month is the hook: After the deep clean, they'll pay $35 to keep it clean for ONE month. Then $70/mo feels like nothing because they never want to go back.
  • Scarcity is real: TJ actually has limited spring schedule spots. This isn't fake urgency.
💰 The Math
  • Deep Clean revenue: $99 per yard (takes ~45-60 min for heavy yards)
  • Conversion to recurring: Industry avg is 40-60% of deep clean customers sign up for weekly
  • If 10 deep cleans → 5 recurring: $990 one-time + $350/mo month 1 + $350/mo ongoing = $4,190 first year from 10 customers
  • Customer lifetime value: Avg retention is 14 months × $70 = $980 per customer
  • Break-even on the discount: Giving up $35 in month 1, gaining $980+ lifetime. That's 28x ROI.
