12-Step Campaign Setup

Steps 1–4 Create the Campaign
▶
  • 1

    Go to Ads Manager

    Navigate to business.facebook.com/adsmanager → Click the green + Create button

  • 2

    Choose Objective: Leads

    In the "Choose a goal" screen → select Leads → Continue. (Not Awareness, not Traffic. Leads. Facebook will optimize for people likely to fill out a form.)

  • 3

    Name your Campaign

    Campaign name: Tidy Tails - Spring Leads - Mar 2026

  • 4

    Budget: $7/day

    Select "Campaign Budget Optimization" → Set daily budget = $7.00 → Toggle CBO ON. Don't touch "Advantage Campaign Budget" — leave it off for now.

Steps 5–8 Build the Ad Set (Targeting)
▶
  • 5

    Ad Set Name

    South County - Dog Owners - $7/day

  • 6

    Lead Method: Instant Form

    Under "Conversion" → choose Instant Form. This keeps people on Facebook (better for mobile). You'll create the form in Step 11. Skip Website for now — test with Instant Form first.

  • 7

    Audience / Targeting

    Location: Zip code list — paste these one at a time:
    63126, 63119, 63122, 63125, 63128, 63010, 63021, 63011
    (That's Crestwood, Webster, Kirkwood, Mehlville, Arnold, Ballwin)

    Age: 25–65+
    Gender: All

    Detailed Targeting → Add Interests: type each one:
    • Dog owners ✅
    • Pet owner ✅
    • Dog (pet) ✅
    Leave Advantage+ audience toggle OFF for first campaign.

  • 8

    Placements: Advantage+ (automatic)

    Leave on Advantage+ Placements — let Facebook decide. Manual placements limit reach unnecessarily for a $7/day budget. Click Next.

If Your Ad Gets Rejected

REJECTED? Common reasons + fixes
▶
Rejection ReasonFix
"Prohibited content" — poop/waste wordingReplace "poop" with "pet waste" or "yard waste." Remove "poop" from headline. Resubmit.
"Personal attributes" — targeting by sensitivityRemove any interest that implies health/family status. Keep only "dog owners" / "pet owner."
Image policy violationDon't use before/after medical comparisons. Yard photos are fine. Logo is fine.
Landing page doesn't match adIf using website URL, make sure tidytailsstl.com/spring is live and loads correctly.
New account under reviewFirst-time advertisers sometimes have a 24-48hr manual review. Just wait. Don't re-submit — it creates duplicates.

The 3-Minute Clinic Script

SCRIPT Walk-in opener
▶
At the reception desk
Hey, I don't have an appointment — I just wanted to pop in for 2 seconds. I run Tidy Tails, a local pet waste removal service here in North County. Do you guys have a bulletin board or a community area where I could leave a few cards and a flyer? [If yes] Perfect, thank you so much — here's a little stack. We do a free first cleanup for new customers, so if any of your clients mention needing the service, please feel free to point them our way. [If no] No worries at all — would it be okay if I left just a couple of business cards at the front desk? A lot of clients ask about this stuff, and I just want to make it easy for whoever might need it. Thanks so much — have a great day!
💡 Tips • Dress like a professional, not like you're about to scoop a yard
• Bring 5 flyers + 10 business cards per clinic
• Don't try to pitch the vet staff on becoming a customer
• If a vet tech seems genuinely interested, offer them the free first cleanup personally
• Get the manager's first name if you can — use it in the follow-up call on Day 5
