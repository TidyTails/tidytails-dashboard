💡 Review Ask Best Practices

  • Text > Email. 90%+ open rate vs 20% for email. Always text.
  • Ask right after service. The yard is clean, they're happy — that's the moment.
  • Make it personal. Use their name, mention their dog's name if you know it.
  • One tap = done. The review link goes straight to the review form. No searching.
  • Don't ask twice in one week. One ask + one follow-up (5-7 days later) max.
  • Thank them! When someone leaves a review, text a genuine thank you.
  • Respond to every review on Google within 24 hours — shows you care.
  • Don't offer incentives for reviews — violates Google's policy and can get reviews removed.
