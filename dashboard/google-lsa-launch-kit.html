Complete the Background Check (Pinkerton)

Google uses Pinkerton (a background check company) to verify all LSA providers. This is free and takes 2–5 business days. You'll receive an email from Pinkerton to submit your info.

  • Check email for a message from Pinkerton (check spam too)
💡 After applying, watch for an email from noreply@backgroundchecks.com or similar Pinkerton address within 24 hours. Complete it same-day to keep the timeline fast.
⚠️ If you've operated under a business license in Missouri, keep that info handy. Some business checks may ask for EIN or DBA registration details.

Day-to-Day LSA Management — 10 Min/Week

🔑 The #1 Rule: Respond FAST

LSA tracks your response rate and response time. Fast responders get more leads and lower CPL. Set up Google LSA notifications to ping your phone the instant a lead comes in. Target: reply within 5–10 minutes during business hours.

Responding to LSA Leads

When a lead comes in via LSA, you get a call or a message. Here's the response protocol:

  • Phone call: Answer and give your standard intro ("Hey this is TJ with Tidy Tails!") then qualify (zip code, dogs, frequency)
  • Message: Reply within 5 min — LSA tracks this and punishes slow responders with higher CPL
  • Missed call: Call back within 30 min. If no answer, text immediately: "Hey this is TJ with Tidy Tails — saw your missed call, can I help you out?"

How to Dispute Junk Leads (Free Refunds)

LSA lets you dispute leads you shouldn't have been charged for. Google refunds valid disputes. Dispute within 30 days of the lead.

  • Dispute these leads:
  • Out-of-area caller (outside your service zip codes)
  • Spam / robo call / no real person
  • Wrong service type (e.g., someone calling about dog grooming)
  • Duplicate lead from same person same day
  • Technical error or accidental call
  • Don't dispute: Someone who was rude, didn't hire you, or chose a competitor — that's a real lead even if it didn't convert

To dispute: Open LSA dashboard → Leads tab → Find the lead → "Dispute this charge" → Select reason → Submit. Usually resolved in 3–5 business days.

Weekly 10-Minute LSA Check-In

Every Monday, spend 10 minutes in your LSA dashboard:

  • Review leads received that week (calls + messages)
  • Mark each lead status (booked, not a fit, no response, etc.)
  • Dispute any junk leads (see above)
  • Check your average cost per lead
  • Adjust weekly budget if CPL is too high or too low
  • Check your response rate — should be above 90%

How to Increase Your LSA Quality Score (Lower CPL)

Google rewards LSA advertisers who provide a good customer experience with more leads at lower cost:

  • Reviews: More 5★ reviews = lower CPL + more impressions. Every new Google review you get moves the needle.
  • Fast response: Under 15 minutes response during business hours = LSA rewards you
  • Booking rate: When leads come in, close them. Higher booking % = better position
  • Low dispute rate: Only dispute legit junk — over-disputing flags your account
  • Accurate service area: Tight service area = more relevant leads = higher close rate
💡 Each new Google review you get while LSA is running directly reduces your CPL. 10 reviews at 5.0★ is significantly better than 6. Text your customers for reviews NOW — before LSA goes live — so the quality score is maxed out on day 1.
What happens if I get too many leads to handle? ▶
Pause your LSA campaign. Go to the LSA dashboard → Settings → Status → Pause. Your listing disappears from Google until you resume. No charges while paused. This is a great problem to have — it means you've hit capacity and can raise prices or hire help before reactivating.
Can I run LSA and FB Ads at the same time? ▶
Yes, and you should. LSA captures high-intent searchers ("dog poop pickup near me" = ready to buy). FB Ads build awareness/discovery among people who didn't know they needed the service. These are different parts of the funnel — they compound each other, not cannibalize.
What if I don't have a business license or insurance? ▶
The background check is primarily criminal, not business credential verification for "pet services" category. However, Google does ask about insurance during the verification process for some categories. If you don't have liability insurance, it's worth getting a basic policy ($40–80/mo) — it also protects you if a dog bites someone or you damage property. If the category requires insurance and you don't have it, the application may not complete until you upload proof.
What if "pet waste removal" isn't a category? ▶
Try these in order: (1) "Pet Services" — broad pet care category that should include waste removal; (2) "Lawn Care" — pet waste removal often falls under grounds maintenance; (3) "Landscaping Services." If none are available in your area, Google may expand categories — check back in 30 days or sign up for the waitlist in your LSA dashboard.
How long until I see leads? ▶
Once your account is approved and active, leads can start within hours. Spring is peak season — April/May should produce solid volume. Expect the first week to be slower (Google is learning), then steady state by Week 2–3. Don't increase budget in Week 1 — let Google optimize first.
