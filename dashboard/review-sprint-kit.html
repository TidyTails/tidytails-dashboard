📏 The Timing Rules

  • BEST: 2–4 hours after service — they just saw clean yard, satisfaction is peak. Don't ask IN the "all done" text — wait 2 hrs.
  • GOOD: Day after service — still fresh, still in their head when they let the dogs out
  • OK: Weekend mornings — people are relaxed, check phones casually, more likely to tap a link
  • BAD: Monday mornings, Friday afternoons — work mode, phone buried, ignored
  • NEVER: Same text as service update — feels robotic. Separate text, 2+ hours later

✍️ What Makes a Review Ask That Actually Works

  • Use their name — "Hey Shannon" beats "Dear Valued Customer" infinitely
  • Make it specific — "We're trying to grow in Florissant" feels real, not a mass blast
  • Give them permission to say no — "No pressure" lowers resistance and paradoxically increases conversion
  • Explain the WHY — "It's how dog owners in your neighborhood find us" = they feel like they're helping, not doing you a favor
  • One click to the link — the link goes directly to the review form. Zero friction.
  • Don't ask via email — text has 98% open rate vs 22% email. Always text.

🔄 Building It Into the Routine

  • After every 2nd service for new customers — they've experienced the value twice, trust is established
  • After every referral — if they sent someone, they love you. 90%+ conversion rate.
  • After a compliment via text — "Oh they're great!" → "That means so much! Would you put that in a Google review?"
  • After seasonal upsell conversation — warm moment after discussing extra service
  • Track in the Customers tab above — who's been asked, who reviewed, who declined. Don't ask twice.

🚫 What NOT to Do

  • ❌ Don't offer discounts or gifts for reviews — Google ToS violation, can get reviews removed
  • ❌ Don't ask multiple times to the same person — one ask, one follow-up max
  • ❌ Don't copy-paste the exact same text to everyone — Google and customers notice
  • ❌ Don't ask in person at the event before they've experienced the service — reviews need to be genuine
  • ❌ Don't ignore reviews when they come in — respond within 24 hours every time
