📝 Week 1: Establish Presence (7 Posts)

Post from your personal profile unless marked as business post. Best times: Tuesday–Thursday mornings (8-10 AM) when homeowners are planning their week. Weekend posts get buried under garage sales and lost pets.

🚫 Critical: Don't Get Flagged

Nextdoor will remove overtly promotional posts from personal profiles. The trick: be a NEIGHBOR who happens to run a business. Share value, show your work, be helpful. Never hard sell from your personal profile.

⚠️ Rules, Pitfalls & Platform Hacks

🚨 Ways to Get Banned/Flagged

1. Promoting from personal profile. Business promotion should come from your business page. Personal profile posts should be helpful, not salesy. The line is thin — mentioning what you do casually is fine; "CALL NOW FOR 20% OFF" is not.

2. Posting too frequently. Don't post from your business page more than 2x/month. Personal is more flexible but daily posting looks spammy. 2-3x/week is the sweet spot.

3. Copy-pasting the same post. Nextdoor flags duplicate content. Every post should be unique — even if the topic is similar, change the wording.

4. Responding to recommendations with canned pitches. When someone asks for recommendations, be genuine. "Hey, I do this locally — happy to chat" beats "Tidy Tails offers premium pet waste removal services starting at..."

5. Creating fake accounts/reviews. Nextdoor verifies addresses. Don't even think about it.

✅ Do This

  • Post from personal profile 2-3x/week
  • Engage with OTHER people's posts daily
  • Watermark ALL photos with your logo
  • Respond to every comment on your posts
  • Share genuine customer stories
  • Use humor — pet waste is inherently funny
  • Be a neighbor first, business owner second
  • Ask questions to drive engagement
  • Update your local deal monthly
  • Turn on notifications for keywords

❌ Don't Do This

  • Hard sell from personal profile
  • Post more than 2x/month from business page
  • Copy-paste identical posts
  • Ignore comments or messages
  • Post only about your business
  • Use corporate-speak ("We're committed to excellence...")
  • Spam recommendation threads
  • Post late at night (gets buried)
  • Argue with anyone publicly
  • Fake reviews or engagement

🏆 The Ultimate Nextdoor Hack

Get your existing customers to recommend you unprompted. When neighbors see "3 people in your neighborhood recommend Tidy Tails" — that's more powerful than any ad. After every job, text: "Thanks [name]! If you're on Nextdoor, I'd really appreciate a quick recommendation. It helps other neighbors find me." Aim for 1 new recommendation per week.
