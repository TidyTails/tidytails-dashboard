⚠️ Onboarding Mistakes That Lose Customers

❌ Slow Response Time

If someone requests a quote and you text back 6 hours later, they've already called POOP 911. Respond in under 5 minutes. Period.

❌ No Confirmation Text

Customer doesn't know if you showed up. They wonder "did he come?" for hours. Always text when done + send a photo.

❌ Forgetting Gate Codes

Nothing says "amateur" like texting the customer every week asking for the gate code. Write it down. Save it in your CRM.

❌ Not Leaving a Gate Secure

This will get you fired instantly. If a dog gets out because you left a gate open, you lose the customer AND get bad word-of-mouth. Triple-check.

🛡️ Customer Retention — Keep Every Customer You Earn

Getting a customer costs $30-75. Keeping one costs $0. Losing one costs $1,680 in lifetime revenue. Retention isn't optional — it's your most profitable activity.

🚨 Early Warning Signs — A Customer Might Cancel

🔴 RED FLAG: Skip Requests Increasing

One skip = normal. Two skips in a row = they're reconsidering. Three = they're about to cancel. After the 2nd skip, send a check-in text.

🔴 RED FLAG: Slow Payment

If payment is late for the first time, it's life. If it's late twice, they may be weighing the cost. Check in casually — "Hey, just making sure everything's good!"

🟡 WATCH: No Response to Texts

If they used to reply "Thanks!" and now silence — something shifted. Don't panic, but do pay attention and provide extra value on next visit.

🟡 WATCH: Seasonal Transition

Fall→Winter and Summer→Fall are the highest churn periods. "Do I really need this when it's cold?" Proactively address this BEFORE they think it.

🟡 WATCH: Life Changes

Moving, new baby, financial stress, pet passing away. Be empathetic. Offer to pause instead of cancel. "We'll be here when you're ready."
