📍 Phase 1: Pre-Service ›

Morning of service day — set expectations before you arrive

Day-Before Reminder (new customers, first 3 visits)
Hey [NAME]! 👋 Just a heads up — Tidy Tails is scheduled for your yard tomorrow. Anything I should know? (gate code changes, new dog, etc.) See you then! - TJ
Catches gate code changes, shows professionalism, builds trust early
On-My-Way Text Stolen from Doggy Doods
🐕 On my way to your place! Should be there in about 15 min.
Customers LOVE this. Lets them put dogs inside, shows reliability, creates a touchpoint
On-My-Way Short (for regulars)
🐕 Headed your way! ~15 min
📸 Phase 2: During Service ›

While you're on-site — document and observe

📸 Gate Photo Protocol Stolen from Doggy Doods
AFTER photo: Take EVERY time (5 seconds, clean yard shot) BEFORE photo: Only if yard is notably messy (marketing content with permission) Rules: • No identifiable personal items visible • Focus on the grass looking clean • Natural daylight, quick snap • This is your receipt + social proof
👀 Quick Yard Scan — Things to Notice
While scooping, look for: • Grass getting long? → mention casually • New dog toys? → "Cute toy, [DOG] must love it" • Fence damage? → "Noticed a loose board" • Holiday decorations? → "Love the setup!" • Anything different → shows you pay attention
Customers are amazed when providers notice things. Shows you care about their property, not just scooping.
✅ Phase 3: Post-Service ›

Within 5 minutes of finishing — this is the MOST important text you send

Standard Complete (first month)
✅ All clean! Your yard's looking great today. [DOG NAME] was [watching from the window/hanging out with me/being adorable]. See you next [DAY]! 🐕
Short Complete (regulars)
✅ Done! Yard's clean. See you [DAY]! 🐕
Complete + Observation
✅ All clean! Quick heads up — noticed [observation]. Not a big deal but wanted to mention it. See you next [DAY]!
Complete + Photo (occasional)
✅ All done! Here's how it looks 👇 See you [DAY]! 🐕
Attach the after-photo. Don't do this every time — once every 2-3 weeks keeps it special.
🔄 Phase 4: Follow-Up Sequences ›

Strategic touchpoints that prevent cancellations and generate growth

3-Visit Check-In (after 3rd service)
Hey [NAME]! Just checking in — how's everything going with the service? Anything I can do differently? I want to make sure [DOG NAME]'s yard stays perfect. 🐕
This catches problems BEFORE they become cancellations. If they give ANY feedback, fix it immediately.
1-Month Review Ask (after ~4 visits)
Hey [NAME]! Been a month of clean yards — hope [DOG NAME] is enjoying it! 😄 Quick favor — Google reviews are HUGE for small businesses like mine. If you've been happy with the service, would you mind leaving a quick one? Takes 30 seconds: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] Totally no pressure — I appreciate your business either way! 🙏
⚠️ Only ask ONCE. If they don't do it, don't ask again for 3+ months. Nothing kills goodwill faster than nagging.
2-Month Referral Seed (after ~8 visits)
By the way — if you know anyone in [THEIR NEIGHBORHOOD] who could use the service, I'm running a deal where you both get $10 off your next month. Just have them mention your name! 🐕
Referrals convert 3-5x better than cold leads. Mentioning their specific neighborhood creates urgency.
❤️ Phase 5: Retention & Delight ›

Seasonal touchpoints + anniversary moments that make cancellation feel impossible

🌱 Spring Touchpoint
Hey [NAME]! Spring's here and [DOG NAME]'s yard is about to get a LOT more use. Want me to do a deep clean to kick off the season? I'll throw it in free for monthly customers. 🌱
☀️ Summer Touchpoint
Hey [NAME]! Summer's heating up — just a heads up I'll be out a little earlier on [DAY]s to beat the heat. [DOG NAME] and I will both appreciate it! ☀️
🍂 Fall Touchpoint
Hey [NAME]! Fall cleanup season — leaves + dog waste = not fun. I'll make sure to get everything even under the leaf piles. Your yard's in good hands! 🍂
❄️ Winter Touchpoint
Hey [NAME]! Just a note — I'll still be out every [DAY] rain, snow, or shine. [DOG NAME]'s yard doesn't take holidays! Stay warm out there. ❄️
🎉 1-Year Anniversary
Hey [NAME]! Fun fact — today marks 1 YEAR of clean yards for [DOG NAME]! 🎉 That's roughly 52 visits and probably a LOT of... you know what. 😂 Thanks for sticking with Tidy Tails — you're one of my OG customers and I genuinely appreciate it. Here's to year 2! 🐕🎉
The Zappos/Chewy move. Nobody in this industry does this. Costs nothing, creates an emotional bond.
