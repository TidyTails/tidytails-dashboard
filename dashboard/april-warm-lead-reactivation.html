🧠 The Psychology of Warm Lead Reactivation

When someone texts "how much?" and doesn't sign up, they're not a NO — they're a "not yet." 80% of sales happen after 5 follow-up touches. Most small businesses follow up zero times after the first reply. TJ's warm leads from March are sitting there with no one who ever sent a second message. That's the gap this kit closes.

📅 Best Re-Engagement Windows

Week 1–3 after first contact: 40–50% close
Week 4–6: 25–35% close
Week 7+: 15–20% close
Most March leads are still in the 40% window — especially ones from early-to-mid March.

🐣 The Easter Hook Is Real

"Easter egg hunt this Sunday — thought of you" is a legitimate, non-pushy reason to reach out. It's not sales-y because the context is real. The yard IS relevant right now. Warm leads respond at 2–3× the rate when there's a natural seasonal trigger behind the message.

💰 Warm > Cold (Always)

A warm lead who already knows your price, already asked about service, already expressed interest closes at 4–8× the rate of a cold Facebook group post reply. You've already done the hard work of getting them interested. This is the follow-up layer that turns that work into revenue.

💬 When They Reply — Handle Every Objection

Most objections are just information requests disguised as hesitation. These responses close the conversation without pressure.

🤔 "Still not sure if I need weekly service…"
"Totally fair — that's exactly why the first cleanup is free. You try it, see the difference, and then decide. Most people who try it once realize how much time they've been spending on Sundays. No card, no commitment. Want to schedule one this week?"
💰 "It's just a little too expensive for right now"
"I hear you. Quick math — $70/month is $2.30/day. That's less than a gas station coffee. And it buys back 2–3 hours of Sundays every month. Plus the first one is free so there's literally no risk. Want to try one cleanup before you decide?"
📅 "Let me check with my spouse / I'll think about it"
"For sure — totally makes sense. Quick note: I do have limited spots for April and Easter weekend is coming up, which tends to fill the last few. If you want to lock in before I'm full, just text me. No pressure either way — the spot will hold for a few days."
🐕 "We only have one small dog — probably not worth it"
"Honestly, one dog is still 300 deposits a year. Small dogs are surprisingly productive 😄 The $70/month is the same whether you have 1 or 3 dogs — it's a flat rate based on weekly visits, not dog count. So for one dog it's actually the best value. First cleanup free if you want to see."
⏳ "I've been meaning to try it but just keep putting it off"
"Ha — that's actually the most common thing I hear. Here's the thing: every week you wait is another week of spring accumulation. Easter weekend is literally perfect timing to start — get the winter buildup cleared out and start fresh. First cleanup free. Takes 30 minutes. Want me to schedule it?"
🧹 "I just clean it myself on weekends"
"That's what most people say before they try us — and then they never go back. Not because they can't do it, but because once you're not spending 45 minutes of your Saturday on it, you realize how much that time was worth. $70/month to buy back your Sundays. But try the free first one and see."
📍 "Do you actually come to [their specific area/street]?"
"Yes — I cover all of [their area/zip code]. Easy way to confirm: text me your address and I'll tell you your weekly day and confirm the route. We're in your neighborhood every week already."
🚫 "Just decided it's not for us right now"
"Totally understand — no worries at all. Can I ask one thing: any particular reason? Just want to make sure I haven't missed something on my end. And if your situation changes in the spring or you want to try before summer cookout season, just reach out — same offer stands."
⭐ The "Buying Signal" — Close Immediately

If they ask: "What day would you come?" / "Is Friday available?" / "How soon could you start?"

That's a buying signal. Stop selling. Close:

"Great — what's your address? I'll confirm your day and we'll get you on the schedule. First cleanup is free, and I'll text you 'On My Way' before every visit so you always know I've been there."

Get the address. That's how it's done.
