🐕 Tidy Tails — The Gift Angle
Why Mom? Why Now?
🐶
Mom got the family dog. Mom also picks up most of the dog poop. A gift that eliminates her least-favorite chore = the most thoughtful gift of the year. Whoever buys this (husband, kids, adult children) gets credit for "actually thinking about what she hates doing."
🪴
She can't enjoy her backyard. The yard she planted flowers in, where she reads on the porch, where she hosts people — she avoids the grass because of the dogs. A clean yard = she gets her outdoor space back.
🎁
Gift subscriptions convert gift-buyers to recurring customers. The spouse/adult kid who signs up their mom as a "gift" often keeps the subscription running because mom loves it. $75 one-time gift → $840/yr LTV.
👩‍👧
Adult children buying for mom is underserved. Most Tidy Tails messaging targets the household directly. The "gift this to your mom" angle opens a new buyer audience — adult kids in their 20s-40s who want a practical gift.
