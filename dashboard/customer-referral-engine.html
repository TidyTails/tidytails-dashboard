💡 Pro Tip: Personalize ONE Detail
Add one personal detail to any script — their dog's name, their neighborhood, the specific cleanup you did. "Hope the yard looks good after the Easter cleanup" lands 3x better than "Hope the service has been great." Customers notice when you actually know who they are.
