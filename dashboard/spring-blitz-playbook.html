⚡ Spring Blitz Rules
1️⃣
Speed kills. Respond to every inquiry within 15 minutes. First responder wins 78% of the time.
2️⃣
One-time → recurring. Every deep clean customer gets offered a recurring plan before you leave. "Most people add weekly once they see the difference."
3️⃣
Scoop Soldiers is the enemy. They're charging $80/mo (national franchise, impersonal). TJ charges $70/mo (local, reliable, personal). Lean into the local angle HARD.
4️⃣
Cluster neighborhoods. Don't chase leads across the metro. Dense clusters = efficient routes = more profit per hour.
5️⃣
Follow up or die. 80% of sales happen after the 5th contact. Set reminders. Don't let leads go cold.
6️⃣
Track everything. Every door hanger, every call, every quote. Can't optimize what you don't measure. Update CRM same day.
