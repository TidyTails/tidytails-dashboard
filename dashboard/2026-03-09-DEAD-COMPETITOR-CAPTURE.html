💡 Pro Tips for Capturing Dead Competitor Customers

1. Speed wins. These customers are looking RIGHT NOW. The first service that reaches them gets them. Every day you wait, Doggie Doodies or POOP 911 might grab them.
2. Don't trash-talk dead competitors. Never name them directly in posts. Say "some services have closed" — let customers connect the dots. Looks classier and avoids drama.
3. Lead with "first cleanup free." These customers already know the value of the service. They don't need to be sold on the concept — they just need to trust YOU. A free first cleanup removes all risk.
4. Emphasize reliability. Their old service literally disappeared. "Local, not a franchise, not going anywhere" hits different when someone just got ghosted by their provider.
5. Check dead competitor reviews. Google reviews for dead companies still show up. Those reviewers are CONFIRMED pet waste removal buyers in your area. Some have full names you could find on Facebook.
6. Poopy Paws was cheaper. At $20/wk (~$80/mo), their customers might balk at paying more. TJ at $70/mo is actually LESS — lead with that price comparison when appropriate.
