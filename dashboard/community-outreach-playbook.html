⚠️ Posting Rules — Don't Get Banned

  • Never hard-sell. Lead with value ("spring tip: dog waste takes 12 months to decompose") then mention your service casually.
  • Read group rules FIRST. Some groups ban business posts. If so, engage in comments only — answer questions, be helpful.
  • Post from Jamie's personal account, not a business page. Personal posts get 5-10x more reach.
  • Space it out. One group per day, max. Posting in 5 groups the same day = spam vibes.
  • Engage first, post later. Join the group, comment on 3-4 posts over a week, THEN share your intro post.
  • Include a photo. Photo of Jamie scooping, a clean yard, or the truck. Posts with photos get 3x engagement.
  • Local angle always. Mention specific neighborhoods: "Just finished a cleanup in Kirkwood!" not "We serve the St. Louis area."

💡 The 3-Post Formula That Works

Post 1 (Week 1): "Hey neighbors! Just wanted to introduce myself…" — personal story, why you started, offer a freebie.
Post 2 (Week 3): Value post — "Fun fact: 1 dog produces 274 lbs of waste per year" or "Spring cleanup tip…"
Post 3 (Week 5): Social proof — "Just hit my 10th weekly customer! Here's what I've learned…" or customer testimonial.
Then: Answer questions in the group whenever someone asks for pet service recommendations.
