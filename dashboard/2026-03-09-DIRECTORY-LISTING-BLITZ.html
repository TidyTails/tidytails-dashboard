Google Business Profile CRITICAL FREE 15 min

The #1 most important listing. Period. Controls what shows in Google Maps and local search.
Why: When someone searches "pet waste removal near me," Google shows the Map Pack first. You're not in it. POOP 911 IS. This single listing is worth more than every other directory combined.

  8. Write first GBP post (spring cleanup promotion)

🟠 High Priority — Do These Day 2

▼

Google Local Service Ads (LSA) HIGH PAY-PER-LEAD 30 min

Shows at the VERY TOP of Google — above regular ads, above the Map Pack. Pay only for actual leads.
Why: In 2026, Google LSAs are the #1 paid lead channel for local services. They show a "Google Guaranteed" badge which builds instant trust. You pay per lead ($15-40 estimated for pet waste), not per click. DoodyCalls is already running these.
  1. Go to Google LSA signup
  2. Select your business category (may need "Pet Care" or "Cleaning")
  3. Submit business license, insurance info
  4. Pass background check (required for "Google Guaranteed" badge)
  5. Set weekly budget (start at $50-100/week, adjust based on lead quality)
  6. ⚠️ Note: May take 2-4 weeks for approval. Start the process NOW.

Thumbtack HIGH PAY-PER-LEAD 20 min

$400M revenue platform. Partnership with ChatGPT means AI assistants recommend Thumbtack pros.
Why: Thumbtack has a ChatGPT partnership — when people ask ChatGPT to find local services, it recommends Thumbtack pros. That's a brand new lead channel most competitors aren't on yet. Plus the listing itself is a backlink.
  1. Go to thumbtack.com/pro
  2. Create pro account → category "Pet Waste Removal" or "Yard Cleanup"
  3. Set service area (St. Louis County zip codes)
  4. Add photos, description, pricing
  5. Set lead preferences and budget (start small: $5-10/lead)
  6. Enable "Instant Book" for higher ranking in results

Angi (formerly Angie's List / HomeAdvisor) HIGH FREE LISTING 15 min

Free listing with option for paid leads. POOP 911 and DoodyCalls are both listed.
Why: Free basic listing creates a backlink and makes you discoverable. Don't pay for "Angi Leads" yet — the free listing alone has value. Angi pages rank in Google search results.
  1. Go to angi.com/pro
  2. Create free business profile
  3. Category: Pet Waste Removal / Yard Cleanup
  4. Add business details, photos, service descriptions
  5. ⚠️ DO NOT sign up for paid "Angi Leads" yet — contracts auto-renew and are hard to cancel
  6. Just claim the free listing for the backlink + discoverability

Apple Maps (Apple Business Connect) HIGH FREE 10 min

iPhone users searching "pet waste removal near me" use Apple Maps by default.
Why: 60%+ of US smartphone users have iPhones. When they ask Siri "find pet waste removal near me," Apple Maps is what shows up. Free, fast, and nobody thinks about it.
  1. Go to businessconnect.apple.com
  2. Sign in with Apple ID
  3. Search for or add your business
  4. Verify ownership, add photos, hours, description

Bing Places HIGH FREE 10 min

Bing powers Alexa, Copilot, and has ~10% search market share. Free backlink.
Why: Often overlooked, but Bing Places feeds data to Microsoft Copilot (AI assistant), Amazon Alexa, and Yahoo Search. That's 3 platforms from 1 listing. Takes 10 minutes.
  1. Go to bingplaces.com

  3. Add business details, verify via phone/postcard
