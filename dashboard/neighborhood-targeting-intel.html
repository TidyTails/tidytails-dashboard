💬 Door-to-Door Scripts

Use these when you're finishing a customer's yard and knock on a few neighbor doors. Keep it casual — you're a neighbor, not a salesman.

🔥 The "I'm Already Here" Script (Best One)

Use when you're literally on their street after finishing a customer's yard.

Hey! I'm TJ — I actually just finished cleaning up [your neighbor's / a yard] right down the street. I do pet waste removal in this area every Sunday. Figured I'd introduce myself since I'm already here. If you've got dogs, I can take care of the yard so you don't have to deal with it. Most folks around here do the $70 a month flat rate — I come out weekly and handle everything. No pressure at all. Here's my card if you ever want to give it a try.
💡 Don't say the customer's name for privacy. "Your neighbor" or "a yard down the street" works great.

😊 The "Spring Cleanup" Opener

Use during March-April when yards are thawing and poop is accumulating from winter.

Hey there! I'm TJ with Tidy Tails — I do pet waste removal around here. Spring's hit and I know a lot of yards got pretty rough over the winter. I'm running a spring cleanup special — one-time deep clean for $100, or if you want to start regular service it's just $70 a month for weekly cleanups. I'm already out here every Sunday servicing a few homes on this street. Figured I'd see if anyone else could use the help!

📝 The "Nobody Home" Door Hanger Note

Quick handwritten note on the door hanger when no one answers.

Hey! I'm TJ — I clean up dog waste for homes in this neighborhood every Sunday. Your neighbor [down the street / on this block] has been using me for a while. Spring special: First cleanup FREE with any monthly plan. Text me anytime → 314-850-7140 tidytailsstl.com 🐕💩 Tidy Tails

🤝 Neighbor Referral Program

Your best salespeople are your current customers. They already trust you, their neighbors see your truck, and word-of-mouth is THE #1 acquisition channel for local services.

The math: If each of your 8 customers refers just ONE neighbor...
8 new customers × $70/mo average = $560/month in new recurring revenue
Cost: $200 in referral credits = 2.8x return in month ONE alone

💰 The Offer

For the referrer: $25 off their next month's service

For the new customer: First cleanup FREE (spring deep clean value: $100)

Why this works: The referrer feels appreciated. The new customer has zero risk. You get a recurring customer for the cost of one free cleanup.

💡 $25 credit is better than cash because: (1) it keeps them as a customer another month, (2) it feels like MORE than $25 because it's off an existing bill, (3) it costs you less than cash since you're already on the route.

🪧 Yard Sign Strategy

Yard signs in customers' yards are the most powerful neighborhood marketing tool. Every neighbor driving by sees it, every dog walker notices it, and it screams social proof.

📋 The Ask (Text to Customers)

Ask your top customers if you can put a sign in their yard. Offer something in return.

Hey [Name]! Quick favor — would you mind if I put a small yard sign in your front yard? Just a little "Yard Cleaned by Tidy Tails" sign with my number. I'll knock $10 off your next month as a thank you. It helps me get the word out to your neighbors and I'll keep it small and tasteful. Totally cool if not!

Size: 18" × 24" corrugated plastic (standard yard sign)

Color: Green background, white text (on brand, visible from road)
