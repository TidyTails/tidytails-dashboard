⚠️ WARNING: Duplicate Blog Content (Keyword Cannibalization)

Two blog posts target essentially the same keyword: "dog poop laws st louis missouri"

PostTitleSize
/blog/dog-poop-laws-st-louis-missouri Dog Poop Laws in St. Louis, Missouri: What Every Dog Owner Needs to Know 35KB
/blog/dog-poop-laws-missouri-st-louis Dog Poop Laws in Missouri & St. Louis: Fines, Rules & What You Need to Know (2026) 30KB

Why This Hurts:

  • Google doesn't know which one to rank → both rank lower than one combined post would
  • Link equity splits between two pages instead of concentrating on one
  • This is called "keyword cannibalization" — your own pages compete against each other
📋 RECOMMENDED FIX
Option A (Best): Merge content into the newer post (dog-poop-laws-missouri-st-louis) and add a 301 redirect from the old URL.
Option B (Quick): Add a rel="canonical" tag on the older post pointing to the newer one.
Option C (Minimal): Differentiate the older post to target a different keyword entirely (e.g., "dog waste ordinances by STL municipality").
⏱️ 15-30 minutes
