The Neighbor Blitz System

Every service visit is a marketing event. Here's the playbook.

How It Works

  • 🏠Step 1: Jamie finishes servicing a yard
  • 👀Step 2: Look for houses with dog stuff visible — dog door, toys in yard, "Beware of Dog" sign, poop in the yard, fence for a dog run, food bowls on porch
  • 🚪Step 3: Hang a door hanger on 5-10 houses on the same street and neighboring streets
  • 📝Step 4: Log the street and count in the tracker (takes 10 seconds)
  • 🔁Step 5: Repeat every week — same houses, different hanger design
⏱️ Time per stop: 5-7 minutes extra. That's it. Jamie's already there. Walking 5 houses takes less time than scrolling Instagram.

Dog House Spotting — Signs They Have a Dog

  • 🐕Dog door installed (screen door or wall)
  • 🦴Dog toys, bones, or balls in the yard
  • 🚧Fenced yard (especially privacy fence or chain link)
  • 💩Visible poop in the yard (JACKPOT — perfect customer)
  • 🥣Water/food bowls on porch or patio
  • ⚠️"Beware of Dog" or dog-related signs
  • 🐾Dog stickers on car bumper or window
  • 🏃Dog walking to/from the house as Jamie arrives
  • 🐶Dog barking when you approach (they're home!)

💬 What To Say If Someone Opens the Door

Most won't. But when they do — here's what Jamie says.

🧠 Rule #1: Don't be salesy. Be neighborly. Jamie isn't selling — he's letting them know he's around. Big difference.
IF THEY OPEN THE DOOR (30 seconds max)
"Hey! I'm Jamie with Tidy Tails — I actually just finished cleaning up [your neighbor's / the yard down the street]. I noticed you might have a dog too. We do weekly poop scooping, and since I'm already on this street, figured I'd leave you some info. No pressure at all — just wanted to say hey!"
IF THEY ASK "HOW MUCH?"
"It's $15 a visit for most yards — I come by once a week and get everything. Since I'm already servicing this street, there's no extra trip charge or anything. First visit's on us so you can see if you like it."
IF THEY SAY "I DO IT MYSELF"
"Totally get it! Most people do. I just like giving people the option — saves about 30 minutes a week. If you ever get busy or go on vacation and need someone to keep up with it, just give us a call. Have a great day!"
IF THEY SEEM INTERESTED
"Awesome! I can actually start as soon as this week if you want. Want me to take a quick look at the yard and give you an exact price? Or I can just text you the details — what's easiest?"
IF THEY SAY "NOT INTERESTED"
"No worries at all! Have a good one. 👋" (Don't push. Don't be weird. Just smile and leave. They know where to find you — your truck's on their street every week.)

Jamie's Quick Cheat Sheet

  • ✅DO: Smile, be casual, mention you were just next door
  • ✅DO: Wear Tidy Tails shirt/hat (recognizable, professional)
  • ✅DO: Have business cards handy as backup
  • ✅DO: Keep it under 30 seconds unless they want to talk
  • ❌DON'T: Knock aggressively or ring twice
  • ❌DON'T: Go to houses with "No Soliciting" signs
  • ❌DON'T: Push after a "no" — you're a neighbor, not a salesman
  • ❌DON'T: Bad-mouth competitors. Just focus on Tidy Tails.
