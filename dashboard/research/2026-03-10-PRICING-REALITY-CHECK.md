## 3 Options for TJ to Consider

### Option A: Lower Weekly Price to $20/week ($87/mo)
- Puts TJ between Doggie Doodies ($83) and Dirty Dog Wash ($95)
- Still premium vs Poop2Scoop ($78) and Critter Sitters ($60)
- Revenue per customer drops ~$87/mo from ~$108/mo (20% cut)
- **Pro:** More competitive, easier to close leads
- **Con:** Cuts into slim margins for a solo operator

### Option B: Keep Price, Add Premium Justification
- Add gate photos/text completion alerts (Doggie Doodies has this)
- Add "satisfaction guaranteed or service free" (already on site)
- Emphasize the personal touch: "Same person every time, not a random tech"
- Add a first-visit-free promotion to remove friction
- **Pro:** Protects revenue per customer
- **Con:** Hard to justify premium when nobody can find you on Google

### Option C: Restructure to Monthly Billing ($95-100/mo flat for weekly)
- Looks cheaper than $25/week even though it's similar
- Monthly billing = less churn, predictable revenue
- Matches how competitors present pricing
- **Pro:** Psychological advantage, competitive positioning
- **Con:** May confuse existing customers

### Recommended: Option C + first-visit-free promotion
Monthly billing ($95/mo for weekly, 1 dog) makes TJ look competitive while maintaining revenue. A free first visit removes the biggest barrier to new signups. Combine with getting on Google Business Profile ASAP.
