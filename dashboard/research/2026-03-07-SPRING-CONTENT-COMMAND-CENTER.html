📖 Platform Playbook

📘 Facebook Groups — Where the Customers Are

  • Target groups: Ballwin Community, Chesterfield Neighbors, West County Moms, STL Dog Owners, Manchester/Ellisville Community
  • Post 2-3x per week max per group (don't get flagged as spam)
  • Rotate posts — never post the same thing in 2 groups same day
  • Engage in comments on OTHER people's posts first (build presence)
  • Best times: 7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, 7-9 PM
  • Reply to every comment within 2 hours

🏡 Nextdoor — The Secret Weapon

  • Why it works: Neighbors trust neighbor recommendations. Nextdoor is built for exactly this.
  • Use "Recommendations" category when asking for business
  • Post in "For Sale & Free" for promotions
  • Comment on pet-related posts organically
  • Ask existing customers to recommend you on Nextdoor
  • Keep tone neighborly — less salesy than Facebook
  • Respond to "looking for pet services" posts FAST

📸 Instagram — Brand Building

  • Post Reels for reach (algorithm pushes video)
  • Before/after yard photos work great
  • Use Stories for day-of-service content
  • Tag location on EVERY post (Ballwin MO, Chesterfield MO, etc.)
  • Engage with local pet accounts and groomers
  • Post consistently — 3-4x per week minimum

  • The Thaw: Snow melted, poop revealed — spring cleanup urgency

  • Time Value: Spring = busy. Yard chores = last priority
