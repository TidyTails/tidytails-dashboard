After Easter cleanup, customers are in their "honeymoon phase" — the yard is clean, they're relieved, they remember exactly how bad it was. Day 3–5 is peak conversion moment. After Day 7, they rationalize: "I can handle it myself now that it's caught up." After Day 14, they've already started DIY again. You have ONE week.
