⚠️ The Real Blockers

What could prevent hitting 25 by August
🔴 Blocker #1: Execution Velocity
Every tool is built. The content is written. The leads are identified. The ONLY thing between 10 and 25 customers is TJ/Jamie actually doing the tasks. Referral texts, event prep, FB posts — these take 20 min each. The plan is not the problem. Fix: Block 15 min each morning, 30 min each evening. That's the whole game.
🔴 Blocker #2: Deployment Gap
The /spring, /calculator, /commercial pages on tidytailsstl.com all return the homepage. Potential customers clicking from ads see nothing. This is a silent conversion killer. Fix: TJ pushes these to Cloudflare. 10 minutes. Should be Priority #1 the next time he's at a computer.
🟡 Blocker #3: Route Efficiency Wall
Currently scooping Mondays. At 15 customers, one day may not be enough — especially if customers are scattered. At 20+ customers, TJ needs either: (a) a second scoop day, (b) geographic clustering so more yards per day, or (c) hiring. Fix: As new customers sign up, prioritize routes that cluster with existing stops. Florissant cluster is already forming (Shannon + Amanda + Chastity).
🟡 Blocker #4: Churn from Inattention
10 customers, zero churn mentioned — great. But growth brings risk: miss a yard, bad weather, customer moves, dog dies. Each churn costs a month of acquisition effort. Fix: Text customers after every visit ("All done! 🐾"). Ask for feedback after first month. Proactively reschedule when you know you'll miss. One touchpoint prevents most churn.
🟡 Blocker #5: Facebook Ads Learning Curve
If ads don't work immediately, TJ might pull the plug too soon. FB ads need 7-14 days of data before making decisions. $7/day × 14 days = $98 before you know if it's working. Fix: Commit to 14 days minimum, judge on leads generated (not just clicks). If zero leads in 14 days, change the creative — not the budget.
🟢 Accelerator: The Viral Nextdoor Post
One post in a neighborhood group by a real customer (not the business) can generate 5-10 leads in 24 hours. This is the black swan upside. It can't be forced, but it can be seeded: ask Shannon (Florissant) and Amanda (Florissant) to each post their own testimonial this week.
🟢 Accelerator: One Commercial Client
Millsap Properties or any HOA signing a $300-800/month contract would be equivalent to adding 3-8 residential customers overnight — with no scooping route changes needed. One deal could jump us from 18 to 25 customers (equivalent) in a month.
