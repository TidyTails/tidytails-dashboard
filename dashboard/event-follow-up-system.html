⏰ The 72-Hour Conversion Window

Speed kills. The business that follows up within 4 hours wins the customer.

Saturday Evening (Within 4 Hours!)

Text ALL Sign-Ups — Personalized by Interest

At the event, mark each sign-up: 🔥 Hot 🟡 Warm 🔵 Cool

🔥 HOT — Asked about pricing Hey [Name]! This is Jamie from Tidy Tails — great meeting you at the Paw-ty today! 🐾 You mentioned wanting to get your yard cleaned up. I'd love to get you on the schedule! ✅ First cleanup FREE (no strings) 📅 Weekly: $25/wk | Biweekly: $45 | Monthly: $70 flat When works best for your first cleanup? I've got spots open this week!
🟡 WARM — Interested but no commit Hey [Name]! Jamie from Tidy Tails here — loved meeting you (and [dog's name]!) at the Paw-ty today 🐾 Just wanted to say thanks for stopping by! If you ever want to try our service, your first cleanup is on us — totally free, no commitment. Just text back whenever you're ready!
🔵 COOL — Just signed the sheet Hey [Name]! This is Jamie from Tidy Tails — thanks for stopping by our table at the Paw-ty today! 🐾 If you ever need your yard cleaned up, we do pet waste removal starting at $25/week. First one's free! Have a great weekend! 🐕
Sunday (Day 1)

Social Proof — Post Event Recap

Post photos from the event on Facebook + Instagram. Tag the vet clinic. NO follow-up texts today. Let Saturday's texts breathe.

Monday (Day 2)

The Value Add — Re-Open Conversations

Text Hot + Warm non-responders only:

Hey [Name]! Quick question — how many dogs do you have? Just want to make sure I give you the right quote. Most of our customers in [neighborhood] pay around $70/month for worry-free yards. 🐕

It's a question, not a pitch. People respond to questions.

Wednesday (Day 4)

The Urgency Nudge — Hot Leads Only

Hey [Name]! Heads up — I'm filling up my Monday route for April and wanted to make sure you got a spot if interested. No pressure at all! 😊

Scarcity + no pressure. The "filling up" is real.

Saturday (Day 7)

The Last Touch — Everyone

Hey [Name]! Happy Saturday 🐾 Just checking in one last time from the Paw-ty. If pet waste removal is ever on your radar, we're here! First cleanup is always free. Have a great weekend!

After this: move to monthly nurture. Don't keep texting.

Drop off a $10 coffee gift card for front desk staff + 20 more business cards. Front desk staff are the ones who recommend services. Make them like you. Highest-ROI $10 you'll ever spend.

Monthly — Stay Top of Mind

Stop by once/month with fresh cards. Ask "has anyone asked about yard cleanup?" Simple. Consistent. Compounding.
