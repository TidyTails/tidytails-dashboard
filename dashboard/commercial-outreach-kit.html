🗣️ Phone Scripts — Ready to Read

These are conversational, not robotic. Adjust to your style. The key phrases in green are the ones that land.

Script A — Apartment Complex / Property Manager

Jamie: "Hi, I'm trying to reach whoever handles your grounds maintenance or vendor services — is that you, or could you point me to the right person?"

[Wait for redirect or confirmation]

Jamie: "Hey [Name], this is Jamie with Tidy Tails — we're a local pet waste removal company here in [Florissant / North County / St. Charles]. I noticed your community has a dog park [or is pet-friendly], and I wanted to see if professional pet waste cleanup is something you've looked into."

[Let them respond — they'll either say yes/no/tell me more]

If interested: "Great — what we do is pretty straightforward. We come out once or twice a week, clean all common areas, dog parks, walking paths — everything your maintenance crew doesn't want to deal with. It's a flat monthly rate, no surprises. Most complexes our size pay between $150-$400 a month depending on how many visits you need."

Close: "I'd love to swing by and walk the property so I can give you an exact quote. What day works this week?"

Script B — HOA Management Company

Jamie: "Hi, I'm reaching out to see if you work with outside vendors for pet waste management at any of your communities. This is Jamie with Tidy Tails — we're a local pet waste removal service."

[Wait for response]

Jamie: "I know pet waste in common areas is one of the top complaints boards deal with. We handle it so they don't have to — weekly cleanup of common areas, dog parks, walking paths. One vendor, one invoice, and the complaints go away."

If they manage multiple: "We can service multiple communities on the same contract. Volume pricing — the more communities, the better the rate."

Close: "Would it make sense to set up a quick 15-minute call with whoever handles vendor relationships? I can send over our one-pager in the meantime."

Script C — Dog Business (Daycare / Boarding / Training)

Jamie: "Hey, this is Jamie with Tidy Tails — we're a pet waste removal company right here in [area]. I had a quick question and a quick idea for you."

Jamie: "First — do you have outdoor play areas that need regular cleanup? We can handle that on a weekly basis, take it completely off your crew's plate."

Jamie: "Second — I bet your clients' yards at home are a mess, right? We could do a referral partnership. You recommend us, your clients get 10% off their first month, and we send people your way too. Win-win."

Close: "Can I drop off some cards and a flyer? I'm in the area [day]."

Script D — Voicemail (30 seconds max)

Jamie: "Hi, this is Jamie with Tidy Tails — we're a local pet waste removal service. I noticed your community is pet-friendly and wanted to see if professional weekly cleanup is something you'd be open to. We handle common areas, dog parks, walking paths — flat monthly rate, no contract. Give me a call back at [YOUR NUMBER]. Again, Jamie with Tidy Tails. Thanks!"

[Under 30 seconds. Fast, confident, done.]

⚠️ Key rules: Never say "pooper scooper" — say "pet waste removal" or "professional cleanup." Sound like a business, not a side gig. Always ask to WALK THE PROPERTY before quoting — it shows professionalism and gets you face-to-face.

💪 Objection Handling — Word for Word

Every objection Jamie will hear, with the exact response that works.

❓ "We already have our maintenance crew handle that."
"Totally understand — and I bet they hate it. Here's the thing: your maintenance team has work orders, HVAC, plumbing, paint — pet waste is always last on the list. We take it completely off their plate so they can focus on what actually matters. Most of our apartment clients tell us their maintenance crew thanked them when they brought us on."
❓ "What does it cost?"
"It depends on the size of the property and how often you want us out. For a community your size, it's usually between $150 and $400 a month. The best way to give you an exact number is for me to swing by and walk the property — takes 15 minutes, no obligation. Can I come by this week?"
❓ "We're not in the budget right now."
"I hear that a lot, and here's what I usually see: the cost of NOT handling it is worse. Dirty common areas = more tenant complaints, lower renewal rates, and you're spending maintenance hours on it anyway. Most of our clients find we actually save them money when you factor in the labor they were using. Can I at least send you a quick proposal so you have numbers for next quarter's budget?"
❓ "Send me some information."
"Absolutely — what's the best email? I'll send our one-pager over today. And just so I send you the right info, how many units are you managing? And do you have dog parks or just common areas?" [This turns a brush-off into a qualifying conversation]
❓ "We have pet waste stations — that's enough."
"Waste stations are great for responsible dog owners. The problem is not everyone uses them. We clean everything the stations miss — the common areas, the walking paths, the spots where dogs go off the beaten path. Think of us as the backup plan for the stations. Most communities with stations still end up needing professional cleanup."
❓ "I need to talk to the owner / board / regional manager."
"Of course — totally understand. Can I send you a one-pager and a quick proposal to share with them? That way you have everything you need to make the case. What's the best way for me to follow up — should I call you back in a week, or email?"
❓ "We tried a service before and it didn't work out."
"Can I ask what happened? [Listen.] Gotcha. Here's how we're different: we're local, owner-operated, and I'm the one doing the work. No subcontractors, no call centers. If something's not right, you call me directly. We're building a reputation here — every property matters to us."
❓ "How are you different from [competitor]?"
"Good question. We're locally owned and operated — I'm the one on your property every week, not some rotating crew. We're also the most affordable in St. Louis right now at $70/month residential. For commercial, we do custom pricing based on your actual needs, not some franchise formula. And no contracts — you can cancel anytime. We earn your business every week."
❓ "Our residents should pick up after their own dogs."
"They should. And some do. But we both know not everyone does. That's why you end up with complaints. We're not replacing resident responsibility — we're the safety net. Clean common areas mean fewer complaints, happier residents, and higher retention. It pays for itself."
💡 The golden rule: Never argue. Agree, then redirect. "I totally understand" → "Here's what we see..." → "Can I come by and show you?"

🔄 Follow-Up Sequences

Most sales happen on the 3rd-5th contact. Don't stop at one call.

📅 The 5-Touch Sequence

  • Day 1 (Monday): Call. If voicemail → leave the 30-sec script. Send follow-up email within 1 hour.
  • Day 4 (Thursday): Second call. Different time of day. If voicemail → "Hey, just following up on my call Monday. Quick question about your grounds maintenance — call me back at [number]." No email.
  • Day 8 (Next Monday): Email only. Subject: "Quick follow-up — [PROPERTY NAME]." Short: "Just checking if you had a chance to think about pet waste service. Happy to answer any questions or stop by for a free property walk."
  • Day 15: Final call. "Last time I'll bug you — just wanted to make sure I wasn't missing you. If now isn't the right time, no worries at all. I'll check back in a couple months."
  • Day 60: Re-engage email. "Hi [Name], touching base — we've added several apartment communities to our route this spring. If pet waste cleanup is something you'd want to explore, I'd love to chat."
⚠️ Never more than 5 touches. After that, you're annoying. Move on and circle back in 60 days.
