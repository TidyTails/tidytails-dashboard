🏢 Apartment Complex Script

Opener (10 sec)
"Hi, I'm [Jamie/TJ] with Tidy Tails — we're a local pet waste removal company. We service apartment communities in the [area] area."
Hook (15 sec)
"I noticed you're pet-friendly [and have a dog park], which is great. With that many residents and dogs, common area cleanup can be a real headache for your maintenance team."
Pitch (20 sec)
"We do weekly professional cleanup of dog parks, walking paths, and common grounds. One flat monthly rate, no contract required. Takes it completely off your plate."
Close (10 sec)
"Could I swing by this week and walk the property with your manager? I'll put together a quick quote on the spot — takes about 15 minutes. What day works?"
💡 Pro Tip
If the leasing office answers, they usually can't make vendor decisions. Ask: "Who handles vendor relationships or grounds maintenance decisions?" Get that person's name and direct line.

🏘️ HOA Management Company Script

Opener
"Hi, I'm [Jamie/TJ] with Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal. We service HOA communities across the St. Louis metro area."
Hook
"I know pet waste in common areas is one of the most common complaints HOA boards receive. We solve that problem completely."
Pitch
"We do weekly cleanup of common areas, dog parks, and walking paths. One flat monthly rate per community. We can also maintain pet waste stations — refill bags, clean receptacles."
The Ask
"I'd love to get on your approved vendor list for pet waste services. Who handles vendor onboarding? And are there any communities currently dealing with pet waste issues I could help with right away?"
💡 Magic Words for HOA Companies
"Approved vendor list" — This is their language. They maintain lists of pre-approved vendors for their communities. Getting on this list = passive lead generation forever.

🏛️ Municipal / Dog Park Script

Opener
"Hi, I'm calling about your dog park at [location]. I'm with Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal."

"We provide professional pet waste removal for public dog parks. Weekly service — we clean the entire park, maintain waste stations, refill bags. It's cheaper than staff labor and the park stays clean for residents."
The Ask

💡 Municipal Sales Are Slow But Sticky
Expect 2-4 weeks minimum. You'll likely need to submit a formal proposal. But once you're in, these contracts renew year after year. Worth the patience.

🛡️ Common Objections & Responses

"We already handle it in-house."
"That makes sense — most properties start that way. The question is whether your maintenance team's time is better spent on cleanup or on repairs, turns, and things that actually need skilled labor. Our flat rate is usually less than what staff labor costs for the same work. Want me to run the numbers for your property?"
"How much does it cost?"
"It depends on the property size and how often you want service. Most apartment communities are $150-$400/month for weekly service. I'd need to walk the property to give an exact number — but no obligation, and I can usually quote on the spot."
"We're not interested."
"Totally understand. Mind if I ask — is that because you don't have a pet waste issue, or because the timing's not right? [If timing:] No worries at all. Can I send you my info so you have it when the time comes?"
"Send me some information."
"Absolutely! What's the best email? [Get it.] I'll send a one-page overview with pricing. And just so I send relevant info — how many units is your property, and do you have a dog park? [Qualify while getting email.]"
"We need to check with [someone else]."
"Of course! Is that your property manager or the ownership group? Would it help if I put together a one-page proposal they can review? I can make it easy for you to forward."
"We've tried pet waste services before and it didn't work out."
"I hear that sometimes — usually it's because of inconsistent service or poor communication. We're owner-operated, local, and I personally check every property. If we miss something, you text me directly and it's fixed the same day. What went wrong with the last company? I want to make sure we don't repeat that."
"That seems expensive for picking up dog poop."
"Ha — I get that! But think about it this way: how much time does your maintenance team spend on it each week? At $15-20/hour, that adds up fast. Plus resident complaints about dirty grounds cost you in renewals. We're usually cheaper than the labor alternative, and way more thorough."
"We just make residents clean up after their dogs."
"Absolutely — and that's the right policy. But we both know not everyone follows it. The common areas still end up with waste, and that affects everyone's experience. We're the safety net that catches what residents miss."
"Do you have insurance?"
"Yes — we carry general liability insurance. I can provide a certificate of insurance with your property listed as additionally insured. Happy to send that over."
"Can you give me references?"
"Absolutely. We service residential clients in [Florissant/St. Peters/area] and they love us — we have [X] Google reviews. For commercial, we're building our portfolio and offering introductory rates to our first apartment partners. You'd be getting our best pricing."
