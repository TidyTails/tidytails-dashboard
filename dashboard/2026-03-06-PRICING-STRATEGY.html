🧠 Pricing Psychology Playbook

1. Anchor High, Close Middle

Always show Premium ($135) first. Standard ($95) looks like a deal by comparison. This is textbook price anchoring — Poop2Scoop and POOP 911 don't do this.

2. "Most Popular" Badge on Standard

Social proof drives choice. Label Standard as "Most Popular" even before it is. Customers follow the herd.

3. The Free First Cleanup (Recurring Only)

POOP 911 uses this and it works. "Free first cleanup when you sign up for monthly." Cost: 15-30 min. ROI: $840+/year per conversion.

4. No Per-Dog Pricing in Ads

Advertise "$70/month" — clean, simple. Per-dog surcharges during quoting, not marketing. Poop2Scoop's "+$4/dog" creates friction before they even call.

5. Plan Annual 8-10% Increases

Year 2: $70 → $77. Year 3: $77 → $85. Still competitive. Grandfather early adopters for 6 months as loyalty. Announce 30 days out, blame fuel + insurance.
