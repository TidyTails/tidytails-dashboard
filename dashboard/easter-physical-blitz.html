🐕 Dog Identification Guide

Only hang where you're 80%+ confident a dog lives. This boosts response rate 3–4x vs. random drops.

✅ Strong dog signals — HANG HERE
Fenced backyard visible · Dog run or kennel · "Beware of Dog" sign · Dog toys visible · Dog water bowl on porch · Dog door · Multiple dog bowls · Poop bags on fence
❌ Skip these
No fence at all · Apartment / condo · No yard access · "No Soliciting" sign · HOA-managed common entry · Already has a service truck out front · Newly vacant / for sale

🚗 Efficient Drop Route Tips

  • Cluster by block, not by address. Walk both sides of a block before moving the car. 30–40 hangers per hour this way.
  • Afternoon drops outperform morning. People are home after 3 PM — they see it immediately and text same-day.
  • Fenced corner lots = jackpot. Big yards visible from two directions. If there's a fence, there's probably a dog.
  • Park once per block. Walk the whole block, hang 4–8 hangers, back to car. Don't idle/creep — looks suspicious.
  • Don't put in mailboxes. That's a federal offense. Door handles, gate handles, fence posts, front door handles only.
  • Photo log optional. Snap a street sign at the start of each block you finish — easy record if anyone asks what streets you covered.
