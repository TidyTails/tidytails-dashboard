✅ Rules of the Game

✅ DO

  • Ask every customer for a review
  • Send the link within 2 hours of service
  • Respond to every review (good or bad)
  • Mention "St. Louis" in your responses (SEO)
  • Keep asks personal and casual
  • Follow up exactly once (5 days later)

❌ DON'T

  • Offer discounts/freebies for reviews
  • Only ask happy customers (review-gating)
  • Ask more than twice per customer
  • Buy fake reviews (Google will nuke you)
  • Have family review from your home address
  • Argue with negative reviews publicly
