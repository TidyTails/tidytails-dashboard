Universal 30-Second Cold Opener

"Hi, this is [YOUR NAME] with Tidy Tails. We're a local pet waste removal service for apartment communities in the St. Louis area. I'm reaching out to [PROPERTY NAME] because you're a pet-friendly property and we've been expanding in [AREA] this spring. Do you have 2 minutes for the property manager?" [If asked "what's this about?" before you reach the manager]: "We service the outdoor pet waste at apartment communities — common areas, dog parks, grounds. It's a service a lot of property managers add once they see the resident review impact. I'd love 2 minutes with whoever handles vendor decisions."

Dog Park Pitch (Highest-Converting)

"The challenge with dog parks in apartment communities is they concentrate the waste in one high-traffic area. Even residents who pick up after their own dogs end up walking through other people's mess. We service the dog park on a regular schedule so it stays functional rather than becoming a liability. Most property managers tell us after the first month that resident complaints about the dog park dropped significantly — and sometimes that alone moves the needle on Google reviews. We charge $[150–300]/mo depending on property size and schedule. Would you be open to a quick walkthrough to get you a quote?"

Portfolio Multi-Property Play

"I'm reaching out because [MANAGEMENT COMPANY] manages several properties in the area, and we offer a portfolio package when we service more than one community. The economics get significantly better — instead of $[200]/mo per property, a two-property package runs closer to $[160]/mo per location. Is there someone at the regional or portfolio level who handles vendor relationships across properties? I'd rather start that conversation at the right level so we don't have to re-pitch for each individual community."

Handling "We Already Have Someone"

"Totally understand — a lot of properties we talk to have tried a service before. Can I ask, is the current service working well for you? We hear two common things from managers: either the service is inconsistent and they're getting calls about missed areas, or residents are still complaining even though service is technically running. If either of those sounds familiar, it might be worth a quick comparison. We're local, flat monthly rate, no contracts, and we send a summary after every visit. Happy to do one free service so you can compare directly. No commitment."

Price/Quote Script

"Our commercial pricing is based on property size and service frequency: Small complex (under 50 units): $150–200/mo — weekly service, all common areas + dog park if applicable Mid-size (50–150 units): $250–350/mo — weekly service, full grounds coverage Large complex (150+ units): $400–600+/mo — based on scope + multi-day coverage That includes: weekly scheduled service, before/after area checks, waste disposal, and a brief visit log sent to management. No contracts — month to month. We send a quote after a quick walkthrough or can give a preliminary estimate based on your unit count. What's your rough size?"
