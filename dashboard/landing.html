Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need to be home during service?
Nope! Most of our customers aren't home. We just need gate access to your yard. We'll work out the details when you sign up.
What happens to the waste?
We bag everything up and take it with us. You'll never see it again. Your trash cans stay clean.
Do you work in winter?
Yes! Dogs don't stop pooping in winter, and neither do we stop scooping. We work year-round, rain or shine (or snow).
What if I have more than 3 dogs?
No problem! We serve households with any number of dogs. For 4+ dogs, we'll provide a custom quote based on your specific situation.
How do I pay?
We keep it simple — monthly invoicing via email. Pay by card, Venmo, Zelle, or cash. Whatever's easiest for you.
Can I pause or cancel service?
Absolutely. No contracts means no cancellation fees. Going on vacation? We can pause and restart whenever you need. Just give us a heads up.
Do you service commercial properties?
Yes! We work with apartment complexes, HOAs, dog parks, vet clinics, and more. Contact us for a custom commercial quote.
