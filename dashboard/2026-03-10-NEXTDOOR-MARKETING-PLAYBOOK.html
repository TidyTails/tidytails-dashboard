PSA for dog parents: Did you know pet waste can contain harmful bacteria (E. coli, salmonella, parasites) that can survive in your soil for YEARS? 🦠

It's not just gross — it's actually a health concern, especially if you have kids playing in the yard. Rain doesn't wash it away; it actually washes the bacteria into the soil and eventually into storm drains.

Three quick tips:
1. Pick up waste at least once a week (or hire someone 😉)
2. Don't compost dog waste — it doesn't get hot enough to kill parasites
3. Keep dogs current on deworming medication
