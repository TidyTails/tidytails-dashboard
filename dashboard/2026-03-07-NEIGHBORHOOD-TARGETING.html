🎯 The 3-Ring Strategy

Grow outward from your base in concentric rings. Don't skip rings.

  • Ring 1 (NOW): Ballwin + Chesterfield + Manchester/Ellisville — your backyard. Every marketing dollar here has maximum ROI because you're already driving through these areas.
  • Ring 2 (At 10 customers): Add Kirkwood + Webster Groves + Wildwood — natural expansion that keeps routes tight.
  • Ring 3 (At 20 customers): Add Fenton/South County + Creve Coeur/Maryland Heights — requires dedicated route days but the market is big enough.

💰 Pricing by Neighborhood

Not every neighborhood gets the same price. Match pricing to what the market will bear:

  • $25/week (Standard): Ballwin, Manchester, Ellisville, Fenton, Florissant — price-sensitive families, value positioning
  • $30/week (Mid): Chesterfield, Kirkwood, Webster, Creve Coeur — can afford a bit more, position as reliable professional service
  • $35-40/week (Premium): Wildwood, Des Peres, Frontenac, Ladue, Town & Country — wealthy homeowners expect premium pricing (too cheap = suspicious)

📣 Platform Strategy by Neighborhood

Different neighborhoods respond to different marketing channels:

  • Nextdoor: Kirkwood, Webster, Ballwin, Chesterfield — community-oriented suburbs where neighbor recommendations carry weight
  • Facebook Marketplace: Manchester, Eureka, Fenton, Florissant — deal-seekers who browse Marketplace regularly
  • Craigslist: ALL areas — still the #1 source for service discovery in STL. Post weekly with location-specific titles
  • Door-to-door flyers: Ballwin (Tier 1 subdivisions), Manchester, Ellisville — walkable subdivisions where you can hit 50+ doors per hour
  • Dog parks: Vlasis Park (Ballwin), Creve Coeur Lake, Kirkwood Park, Queeny Park (west county) — talk to dog owners in person
  • Facebook Groups: Kirkwood Community (15K), Ballwin Neighbors, Chesterfield Life, Wildwood Community — post helpful tips + soft-sell
