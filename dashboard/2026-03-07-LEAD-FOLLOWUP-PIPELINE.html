  • 🔴 Reply within 5 minutes — Not 5 hours. Five. Minutes. Set text notifications to loud.

📅 The 7-Touch Follow-Up Sequence

After the initial response, if they go quiet, use this sequence. Space it out — persistent, not pestering.

🎯 Key Principle: Each follow-up adds NEW value. Never send "just checking in" without giving them a reason to respond. Seasonal hooks, neighbor activity, limited slots — give them something.
Touch 1: Same Day (2-4 hours later)
Hey [Name] — just wanted to make sure my earlier text came through! Let me know if you have any questions about pricing or how it works. No rush 🙂

Why: Texts get buried. A simple bump shows you're responsive without being pushy.

Touch 2: Day 2 — The Value Add
Hey [Name]! Just a heads up — I'm doing cleanups in [their area] this [day]. If you want, I could swing by and do a free first cleanup so you can see how it looks. Takes about 20-30 min and you don't even need to be home. Just let me know 🐕

Why: Offering a free cleanup removes all friction. They don't even need to decide — just say yes to a freebie.

Touch 3: Day 5 — The Social Proof
Hey [Name] — just wrapped up a cleanup in [nearby neighborhood] and the yard looks night and day. These spring cleanups have been crazy lol — winter leaves a LOT behind 😅 Still happy to do yours whenever you're ready. No pressure at all.

Why: Shows you're busy (social proof), references their area (relevance), and keeps it casual.

Touch 4: Day 10 — The Limited Availability
Hey [Name]! Quick update — my [day of week] route in [their area] is almost full. I've got room for 1-2 more yards. If you're still thinking about it, I can hold a spot for you this week. No worries if the timing isn't right — just didn't want you to miss out if you were interested 👍

Why: Scarcity is real (you DO have limited capacity). Creates urgency without being fake.

Touch 5: Day 17 — The Helpful Pivot
Hey [Name] — totally understand if the timing isn't right for regular service. Just wanted you to know I also do one-time cleanups if you ever need a yard reset before a party, showing, or just because lol. $99 for a standard one-time. Either way, offer's always open 🐕

Why: Lowers the ask. They might not want monthly service but a one-time is easier to say yes to. Foot-in-the-door technique.

Touch 6: Day 24 — The Neighbor Hook
Hey [Name]! Random thought — I just signed up someone in [their neighborhood/nearby area]. If you and a neighbor both sign up, I can knock $10/month off for both of you since I'm already in the area anyway. Just throwing it out there! Hope you're having a good week 🙂

Why: Neighbor discount is a real thing (route efficiency saves you time). Makes them think of someone to pair with.

Touch 7: Day 30 — The Graceful Close
Hey [Name] — I don't want to keep bugging you, so this'll be my last text about it 😄 If you ever need a yard cleanup (one-time or ongoing), just shoot me a text. I'm not going anywhere and the offer for a free first cleanup is always good. Have a great [day of week]! 🐕

Why: Shows respect, leaves the door open, and often triggers a response ("sorry, been busy, let's do it!").

🛡️ Objection Handling — What They Say vs. What to Text Back

These are the 8 most common objections in pet waste removal. Each has a copy-paste response that addresses the real concern.

💬 "That's too expensive" / "I can't afford that"
I totally get it — it sounds like a lot at first. But here's how I think about it: $95/month works out to like $22/week, which is about what you'd pay someone on Taskrabbit for one cleanup. I come every week, rain or shine, and you literally never have to think about poop again. Most of my customers say it's one of those things where once you have it, you can't go back lol. If budget's tight, I also do biweekly at $60/month — still way better than doing it yourself 🐕
💬 "I'll just do it myself"
Ha, I hear that a lot! And honestly, some people do great with it. But here's what usually happens — you do it for a couple weeks, then life gets busy, then it piles up, then it becomes a whole project again. I've got customers who "did it themselves" for years before calling me, and every single one has said "why didn't I do this sooner?" But hey, no pressure. If you change your mind, I'm always here 🙂
💬 "I need to think about it" / "Let me talk to my spouse"
Absolutely, take your time! No rush at all. One thing that might help the decision — I do a free first cleanup with no commitment. So you (and your spouse) can see the results before spending a dime. Want me to swing by [day] just to show you how it works? Worst case, you get a free yard cleanup out of it 😄
💬 "I don't want a stranger in my yard"
Totally understand — I actually get that a lot. A few things that might help: I'm a one-man operation. It's always me, never a random employee. Same face every week. I text when I'm on the way and when I'm done. You'll always know when I'm there. And I'm happy to do a quick meet-and-greet on the first visit so you can put a face to the name and see how your dogs react to me (they usually love me lol). Your comfort level matters — if you'd rather be home the first couple times, I can schedule around that.
💬 "I only have one small dog, it's not that much poop"
Ha, you'd be surprised! One dog doing their thing 2-3 times a day = 14-21 deposits a week. That adds up fast, especially when the grass grows in and hides them 😅 But I hear you — for a small yard with one dog, you'd be on the lower end around $70/month. That's like $16/week to never step in it again or spend your weekend hunting for landmines. Free first cleanup is always on the table if you want to try it out!
💬 "Do you have reviews/references?"
Great question! I've been doing this for over a year and haven't lost a customer yet — they all stick around because I actually care about doing a good job, not just a quick scoop. I can put you in touch with a couple customers in [their area] if you'd like to hear from them directly. And I'm building up reviews on Google — [link if available]. The free first cleanup is basically a zero-risk trial too. You can see the quality before you commit to anything.
💬 "What if my dog is aggressive?"
Good looking out — I appreciate you telling me! I've worked with all kinds of dogs. A few options: 1. If the dog can be inside during cleanups, that's easiest 2. If they're an outside dog, I can work around their routine 3. I'm also cool with meet-and-greets — most dogs warm up after seeing me once or twice Just let me know the situation and we'll figure out what works. I've never met a yard I couldn't handle 🐕
💬 "I found someone cheaper" / "Your competitor charges less"
No worries at all! Just a couple things to consider: With me, you're getting the owner every single time. Not an employee who's trying to rush through 30 yards. I take my time and actually get everything. I also do the free first cleanup — so you can compare quality side by side without risking anything. But honestly, if someone else is a better fit, I respect that. My door's always open if things don't work out 🙂
