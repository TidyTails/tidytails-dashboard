📱 Marketplace Posting Tips

  • Post 3-5 listings per day max — Marketplace throttles bulk posters
  • Use REAL PHOTOS from your lot, not stock. Buyers want to see the actual unit
  • Re-list every 7 days — bumps you back to the top of search results
  • Price it $1 less than round numbers ($5,898 vs $5,899) — looks like a deal
  • Reply to DMs within 5 minutes — first response wins 78% of the time
  • Set location to your lot (5520 Westwood Dr, St Charles) so buyers can visit
  • "IN STOCK" and "READY TODAY" are the two most powerful phrases on Marketplace
  • Cross-post to Craigslist and OfferUp — same copy works everywhere
