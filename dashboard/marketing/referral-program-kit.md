## 💰 The Offer

### "Refer a Friend" Deal
- **Referrer gets:** 1 FREE week of service ($25 value)
- **New customer gets:** First cleanup FREE (removes risk)
- **Both win.** Total cost to TJ: ~$50 per successful referral (2 free cleanups)
- **ROI:** If new customer stays 3 months = $300 revenue. That's 6x return on the $50.

### Rules (Keep It Simple)
- Referral counts when the new customer signs up for recurring service
- Free week applied to referrer's next billing cycle
- No limit on referrals (if someone sends you 5 customers, they get 5 free weeks — that's a GREAT problem)
- New customer must be in the service area

## 🎯 Amplifiers (Extra Credit)

### 1. Ask for Reviews Too
While you're reaching out about referrals, ask for Google reviews:
```
Oh and one more thing — if you have 2 minutes, a Google review would mean the WORLD to me as a small business. Here's the link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
```
*(This also helps with the Google indexing problem — reviews signal legitimacy)*

### 2. Nextdoor Post from Customers
Even better than a referral text — ask a happy customer to post about you on Nextdoor:
```
Hey [NAME], random ask — would you mind posting something on Nextdoor about your experience? Even just "I use Tidy Tails for yard cleanup, highly recommend" would be huge. Nextdoor is where people look for local services.
```

### 3. Yard Sign (Low Cost, High ROI)
- Small "Yard Serviced by Tidy Tails" sign left after each cleanup
- Neighbors see it every week → brand awareness
- Cost: ~$3-5 per sign from VistaPrint
- Ask permission first: "Hey, would you mind if I left a small sign in your yard? Totally optional!"

### 4. Spring Door Hangers
- Print 50-100 door hangers for neighborhoods where you already service
- "Your neighbor uses Tidy Tails! First cleanup FREE" 
- Target houses with visible dog toys/fences
- Cost: ~$30-40 for 100 from VistaPrint
