## 📊 NAP Consistency (CRITICAL for SEO)

**NAP = Name, Address, Phone**

Your business name, address, and phone number MUST be **identical** across every directory. Even small differences (like "St. Louis" vs "Saint Louis") hurt your Google ranking.

**Use exactly:**
- **Name:** Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal
- **City:** St. Louis, MO (not Saint Louis, not STL)
- **Phone:** [use same number everywhere]
- **Website:** https://tidytailsstl.com (with https, no trailing slash)
