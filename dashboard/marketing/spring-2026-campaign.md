## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR TJ

1. **Post from personal account, not business page** — Facebook algorithm buries business page posts in groups. Personal = more reach.

2. **Respond to EVERY comment** — even "lol" gets a reply. Engagement = visibility.

3. **Don't post in more than 2-3 groups per day** — Facebook flags rapid multi-group posting as spam.

4. **Take a "before" photo** of a messy yard (with permission) for Instagram. Real > stock photos.

5. **The $70/month framing is your weapon** — competitors lead with per-visit pricing that SOUNDS cheaper but ISN'T. Always lead with monthly.

6. **"First spring cleanup free" is your hook** — it costs you one hour of work but removes the biggest barrier to signing up. Every single person thinking about it is worried about the winter backlog.

7. **St. Charles is wide open** — Little Stinkers died, Scoop Soldiers hasn't targeted it yet. Lean HARD into St. Charles in your posting.
