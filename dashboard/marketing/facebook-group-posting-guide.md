## 📋 POSTING RULES & STRATEGY

### ⚠️ Rules That Will Get You Banned
1. **Don't post the same thing in multiple groups the same day** — FB flags this as spam
2. **Don't post more than once per week in any single group** — you'll get removed
3. **Read group rules before posting** — some ban business posts entirely
4. **Never comment on someone else's post just to plug your business** — be helpful first
5. **Vary your copy** — don't use the exact same text in every group

### ✅ Best Practices That Get Customers
1. **Post between 7-9 AM or 5-7 PM** — peak engagement times
2. **Include a photo** — posts with images get 2.3x more engagement
3. **Ask a question in your post** — drives comments, which drives visibility
4. **Reply to EVERY comment** within 1 hour
5. **Be a community member first** — comment on other posts, be helpful, THEN post your service
6. **Use humor** — "How bad is your yard after winter?" > "Professional pet waste removal"

### 📊 Weekly Posting Schedule

| Day | Groups to Post In | Post Type |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | 2 community groups | Testimonial/result post |
| Tuesday | 1 dog group + 1 BST group | Seasonal/weather tie-in |
| Wednesday | 2 new neighborhood groups | Question-based engagement post |
| Thursday | 1 mom group + 1 community group | "Quick tip" + soft pitch |
| Friday | 1 BST group + 1 business group | Weekend special / availability |
| Saturday | 2 community groups | "Weekend warrior" casual post |
| Sunday | 1 dog group | "Back to work Monday" post |

**That's 12-14 group posts per week across different groups = maximum reach with zero spam risk.**

## 🏠 NEXTDOOR — The Secret Weapon

Nextdoor is MASSIVE for local services and often overlooked.

### How to Set Up
1. Go to nextdoor.com and verify your address
2. Create a FREE Business Page for Tidy Tails
3. You get 2 free business posts per month
4. Neighbors can recommend you (social proof gold)

### Why Nextdoor Wins
- People actively search for local services
- Recommendations carry massive weight
- Less competition than Facebook
- Hyper-local targeting (your actual neighbors)
- Dog owners ask for service recs constantly

### Nextdoor Strategy
1. Claim your free business page
2. Post 2x/month (free limit)
3. Ask satisfied customers to recommend you on Nextdoor
4. Reply to any "looking for pet waste removal" posts
5. Consider paid Local Deals ($2-5/day, hyper-targeted)
