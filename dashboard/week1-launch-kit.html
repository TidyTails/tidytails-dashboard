📞 Jamie's Call Script — Top 20 Leads

Call Script

🏢 Commercial Properties (Apartments/HOAs)

Opening: "Hi, I'm calling from Tidy Tails — we're a local pet waste removal service. I'm reaching out because spring is here and a lot of property managers are dealing with the winter buildup in their dog areas. Do you have a minute?"
Pitch: "We handle all the pet waste cleanup for apartment communities and HOAs — the dog stations, common areas, walking paths, all of it. Takes about 10 minutes for your maintenance team to show us the spots, and we handle everything from there."
Offer: "We're running a spring special — free demo clean of your worst area so you can see the difference before committing to anything."
❌ "We handle it in-house" → "Totally get that. Most of our commercial clients said the same thing before they realized how much maintenance time it was eating up. The demo is free — worst case, your dog area looks great for a day."
✅ "Interested" → "Great! I can have TJ out there this week. What day works — Tuesday or Thursday?"
Close: "Can I grab your email to send over our pricing sheet? It's straightforward — monthly service starts at [quote based on property size]."

🏠 Residential Leads (I-44 Corridor)

Opening: "Hey! I'm Jamie with Tidy Tails, a local pet waste removal service here in [Kirkwood/Webster Groves/area]. Have you heard of us?"
Pitch: "We come out weekly, clean up everything your dog leaves behind, bag it, and take it with us. You literally never have to think about it. Most customers say it's the best $70 a month they spend."
Offer: "We've got a spring deep clean special going — $99 for a full yard reset, plus $20 off your first month if you sign up for recurring. Figured I'd call the neighborhood since we're already servicing the area."
❌ "Too expensive" → "I hear you. Think of it this way — that's about $2.30 per day to never step in it again. And the spring clean alone is $50 off right now."
❌ "I do it myself" → "A lot of our customers said that too! The thing is, life gets busy and it piles up. We had one customer who said she was spending her entire Saturday morning on it. Now she gets that time back."
✅ "Interested" → "Awesome! Can I book a spring deep clean for this week? What's a good day — I've got openings Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday."
