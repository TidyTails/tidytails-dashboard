⚡ Franklin's Pricing Recommendation

The brutal truth: At $25/week, Tidy Tails is the most expensive residential option in STL by $5-7/week. That's a 28-39% premium over the competition.

BUT — the market just changed dramatically. Three competitors gone means fewer options for customers. The remaining competitors (Poop 2 Scoop, No. 2 Crew, Doggie Doodies) are all small operations with limited territory coverage. Nobody has the North County market.

My recommendation: DON'T lower the base price. Instead, weaponize promotions.

🎯 Recommended Pricing Strategies

Strategy 1: Free First Cleanup (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

Doggie Doodies offers this. POOP 911 used to. It's industry standard and TJ doesn't offer it. This removes the #1 objection ("what if I don't like the service?"). Cost: one free visit (~20 min of labor). Expected conversion: 60-70% become recurring. At $25/wk × 52 weeks = $1,300/year, that 20-minute investment pays for itself 50x over.

Implementation: Add to website pricing page, all ads, GBP posts, and Jamie's pitch scripts. "Your first cleanup is on us — no obligation."

Strategy 2: Multi-Dog Discount

Every competitor charges more for multiple dogs. No. 2 Crew: $19 (1 dog) → $24 (2-3 dogs). Doggie Doodies: $83/mo (1) → $88/mo (2). TJ's flat $25/wk for any dog count is actually a DEAL for 2+ dog households. Flip this into marketing: "Same price whether you have 1 dog or 4. We don't charge per poop."

Strategy 3: DDD/POOP 911/DoodyCalls Rescue Pricing

For orphaned customers from dead competitors: "Switching from another service? Get your first MONTH at $20/week." That's competitive with Poop 2 Scoop's $18/wk but only for month 1. After that, they're at full price — and by then, they love Jamie and won't switch again.

Strategy 4: Annual Commitment Discount

Offer $22/wk for customers who commit to 6+ months (prepay or auto-pay agreement). That's $1,144/year vs $1,300 at full price. TJ locks in revenue, customer gets closer to competitor pricing. Win-win.

Strategy 5: Spring 2026 Promo (Time-Limited)

"Spring Clean Special: $20/week + free first cleanup. April only." Creates urgency. Gets customers in the door at a competitive price. After April, new customers start at $25/wk. Existing spring customers keep $20/wk as loyalty pricing — they'll never leave.

From Doggie Doodies (Best Ops in STL)

From Poop 2 Scoop (Service Depth)

  • Overage charge transparency — "+$5 per extra scooper pan" for initial visits. Clear expectations.
  • "No contracts, just firm handshakes" — Great copy. Casual, trustworthy.
