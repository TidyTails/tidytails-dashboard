# 🌷 Spring 2026 Neighborhood Blitz Campaign
*Created: March 6, 2026 by Franklin*

## Overview
**Goal:** Generate 5-10 new leads this weekend through physical + digital neighborhood marketing
**Materials:** Print flyer kit + ready-to-post social content for Nextdoor, FB groups, and neighborhood apps

---

## 📄 PRINT MATERIALS

### Flyer Kit PDF: `dashboard/pdfs/SPRING-FLYER-KIT-2026.pdf`

**Page 1 — Door Hangers (2-up)**
- Print on cardstock, cut in half vertically
- Cut doorknob hole at top
- Hang on doors in target neighborhoods (homes with fenced yards = dog owners)
- Cost: ~$0.05/hanger at home printer or ~$0.15 at Staples

**Page 2 — Full-Page Flyer**
- Post on community bulletin boards (libraries, pet stores, vet offices, dog parks)
- Hand to neighbors when doing existing service calls
- Leave at laundromats, coffee shops with permission

**Page 3 — Leave-Behind Cards (4-up)**
- Print on cardstock, cut into quarters
- Hand out at dog parks, pet events
- Leave in mailboxes (check local rules) or on porches
- Tuck under windshield wipers at PetSmart/Petco parking lots

### 🎯 Target Neighborhoods (Where TJ Already Services)
Focus on neighborhoods NEAR existing customers — reduces drive time for new pickups:
1. **Ferguson / Florissant** — existing customer base
2. **Maryland Heights / St. Ann** — near existing routes
3. **Chesterfield / Ballwin** — higher-income dog owners
4. **O'Fallon / St. Peters** — suburban families

### Printing Tips
- **Home printer:** Use heavy paper/cardstock (65lb+), color
- **Staples/FedEx:** Upload PDF, color cardstock prints, ~$0.50/page
- **Recommended qty:** 50 door hangers (25 sheets), 20 flyers, 50 leave-behind cards (13 sheets)
- **Total cost estimate:** $15-25 at Staples, $5-10 at home
