## Printing Tips
- **Vistaprint:** 250 door hangers for ~$25-35 (cheapest option)
- **Canva:** Free templates, design it yourself, print at home or Staples
- **Staples/Office Depot:** Walk in, same-day print on cardstock
- **DIY:** Half-sheet flyers on colored paper (bright yellow or green stands out)

## Distribution Strategy
- Leave on doors in neighborhoods where TJ already has customers (social proof)
- Hit 20-30 doors each Sunday while out scooping
- Focus on streets adjacent to current customers
- Skip houses with no dogs (no dog toys, no fence, no dog door = skip)
