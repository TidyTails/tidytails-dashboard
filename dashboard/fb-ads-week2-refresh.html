⏱️ THE LEARNING PHASE WINDOW
Facebook's algorithm learns from your first 50 conversions or 7-14 days, whichever comes first. At $7/day, you'll exit based on time (~Day 7-10). After that, the algorithm locks in its targeting — which means it's also when performance becomes stable and readable. Do NOT change the campaign during Days 1-6. Wait. Read this kit instead.

Step 2 — Match Your Numbers to a Scenario

✅ SCENARIO A — SCALE IT (CPL < $25, CTR 2%+, 1+ leads)
The learning phase worked. Don't change the ad yet — keep the winner running. Do:
  • Bump budget from $7/day → $10/day (max 20% increase at a time to avoid re-triggering learning)
  • Duplicate the winning ad set → set to $7/day as a test of new creative only (Ad B-1 from this kit)
  • Launch the North County expansion ad set at $5/day (see NC tab)
  • DO NOT touch the original ad. Leave it alone and let it compound.
⚠️ SCENARIO B — TWEAK IT (CPL $25–$50, CTR 1–2%)
Getting results but bleeding. This is a creative problem, not a targeting problem.
  • Keep current budget ($7/day) — don't cut yet
  • Duplicate the ad set. In the duplicate: swap in ONE new ad from this kit (try B-2: "Spring Roulette")
  • Run both for 5 more days. Whichever has better CTR after Day 12 = winner, pause the other
  • Check audience overlap — if frequency is 2+, widen the radius by 5 miles or add 1-2 interests
🚨 SCENARIO C — HARD RESET (CPL $50+, 0 leads after 7 days)
The creative isn't landing or the audience is wrong. Don't throw money at it.
  • Pause the current ad set. Don't delete — keep the data.
  • Start a brand new campaign with Ad B-3 (Mother's Day gift hook — totally different angle)
  • Widen targeting: bump radius from 15mi → 20mi, add "homeowners" behavioral interest
  • Consider switching objective from Leads → Messages (lower friction on mobile)
  • Budget stays at $7/day until Day 14. No scaling until you have CPL data.

Most likely cause of 0 leads at Day 7: The ad text is getting ignored (bad first line), or the landing form has too many fields. Check the form completion rate in Ads Manager → Leads Center.

📱 How to Launch Video Ads in Ads Manager
Quick 5-step guide. Videos are in dashboard/videos/.

Step 1 — Upload to Meta Business Suite

Go to business.facebook.com → Content → Videos → Upload. Wait for processing (3-5 min).

Step 2 — In Ads Manager: Duplicate Your Best Ad Set

Take your best-performing ad set → Duplicate → Change the ad creative from Image to Video. Upload the processed video.

Step 3 — Add Captions

In the video creative tab → Auto-generate captions → Review and paste from the caption scripts in video-ads-mar28.html. CRITICAL: Facebook's silent autoplay means captions = the hook. Don't skip.

Step 4 — Budget

Start the video ad set at $5/day alongside your $7/day static ad set. If video CPL < static CPL after 5 days → flip the budget (video gets more).

Step 5 — Monitor Thumb-Stop Rate

Thumb-stop rate = 3-second video plays ÷ impressions. Good: 30%+. Warning: 15-30%. Poor: <15%. A low thumb-stop rate means the first 3 seconds aren't grabbing. Fix the caption on the opening frame.
