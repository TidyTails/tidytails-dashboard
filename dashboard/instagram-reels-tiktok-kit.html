How to Film — No Experience Needed
Everything TJ needs to make a good Reel with just an iPhone. No editing skills, no equipment.
📱 The Only Setup You Need
iPhone propped against a fence post or leaned on the truck. Selfie camera for face-to-camera. Rear camera for yard shots. That's it. Don't overthink it. The most-viewed cleaning/service Reels on TikTok look like they were filmed in 3 minutes — because they were. Authenticity > production value for local service businesses.
🌤️ Lighting = Golden Hour or Overcast
Film early morning (7–9 AM) or late afternoon (4–6 PM). Midday harsh sunlight makes faces look rough. Overcast days are actually perfect — even, soft light everywhere. If it looks good on your phone screen when you press record, it's fine. Don't over-filter.
🎵 Music Strategy
Use trending audio on IG/TikTok — the algorithm boosts content using trending sounds. In TikTok: tap the "+" sound icon before recording and choose "Trending." In IG: select "Add Audio" and pick from Reels suggestions. For face-to-camera scripts, record first then add music underneath at 20% volume so voice is audible. Don't skip music — it's an algorithmic signal.
✂️ Editing in CapCut (Free App)
1. Install CapCut (free, iOS/Android)
2. Import your clips
3. Trim to the good parts
4. Add text overlays: tap "Text" → type → move to where you want
5. Add captions: tap "Captions" → auto-generates them
6. Export at 1080p

Total time for a simple Before/After edit: 10–15 minutes. The "On My Way" text reveal or face-to-camera scripts don't even need editing — just post the raw clip.
📐 Format: Vertical 9:16
Always film vertically. Reels and TikTok are full-screen vertical. If you film horizontal (landscape), it'll have black bars on the sides and look low-effort. Just hold the phone vertically. IG lets you trim to Reel format if you accidentally film horizontal, but vertical from the start is better.
📞 Always End With the Phone Number On Screen
Text overlay at the end of every Reel: "(314) 850-7140" + "First Cleanup FREE." People screenshot the number. People pause and write it down. If the number isn't on screen, you're losing leads who watched the whole video but won't hunt down the bio link. Make it easy. Every. Time.
⏱️ Optimal Length by Type
Before/After reveal: 15–25 seconds
Face-to-camera script: 20–30 seconds
Day in the Life POV: 30–60 seconds
Educational/stat video: 20–35 seconds

Shorter = better for retention. IG shows "watch time %" as a metric — if people watch 90%+ of your Reel, the algorithm rewards it. A 20-second Reel with 100% watch time beats a 60-second Reel with 40% watch time every time.
🗺️ Location Tags + Geotag
On IG: Add a location tag when posting (tap "Add Location" → "St. Louis, Missouri" or the specific suburb). On TikTok: mention the city in the first 3 seconds of spoken content ("We're in St. Louis...") — the algorithm reads this. Location signals matter massively for local reach. Always do it.
4-Week Posting Schedule
1 Reel per week minimum. 2 per week = fast growth. Don't try to do all 10 at once — consistency beats volume.

🎯 The Rule: One Good Reel Per Week Beats Nothing Every Day

Consistency matters more than frequency for local service accounts. One video per week for 10 weeks = a library that compounds forever. Two per week gets there faster. Three per week burns out most solo operators. Start with one and build the habit.
