### FAQ Page

**Q: Do I need to be home when you come?**  
A: Nope! Most of our customers aren't. Just make sure we can access the yard, and we'll take care of the rest.

**Q: What do you do with the waste?**  
A: We bag it and take it with us. You'll never have to deal with it.

**Q: Is there a contract?**  
A: Never. You can start, stop, or change your service anytime with no penalties.

**Q: What about winter?**  
A: We scoop year-round! Snow, rain, or shine — your dogs don't stop pooping, and neither do we.

**Q: Do you service apartments or commercial properties?**  
A: We're focused on residential right now, but reach out if you have a commercial need and we can chat.

**Q: How do I pay?**  
A: We invoice monthly. Easy online payment, no hassle.

**Q: What if my dog is in the yard?**  
A: No problem! I'm great with dogs. Just let me know if there's anything I should know about your pup.

## Design Notes for Web Developer

- **Vibe:** Clean, friendly, approachable. Not corporate.
- **Colors:** Greens, earth tones, maybe a pop of orange or yellow
- **Photos needed:** TJ with dogs, clean yards, before/after shots
- **Mobile-first:** Most leads will come from phones
- **Speed:** Keep it fast — no heavy graphics
- **SEO focus:** "pooper scooper St. Louis", "pet waste removal St. Louis", "dog poop cleanup [neighborhood]"
