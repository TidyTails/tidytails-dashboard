# Review Request Templates

*Copy-paste these to ask customers for reviews*

---

## Why Reviews Matter

- 93% of consumers read online reviews before buying
- Businesses with 5+ reviews get 270% more clicks
- Google ranks businesses with more reviews higher
- Reviews are social proof — they do the selling for you

**Goal:** Get all 4-5 current customers to leave a Google review this week.

---

## Template 1: Text Message (Casual)

Best for: Customers you have a friendly relationship with

```
Hey [Name]! Quick favor — would you mind leaving Tidy Tails a quick Google review? It really helps a small local business like mine get found by new customers.

Here's the link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Totally optional, no pressure at all. Thanks for being a great customer! 🐕

— TJ
```

---

## Template 2: Text Message (After Good Interaction)

Best for: Right after they compliment your service or you have a good exchange

```
Thanks [Name]! Really appreciate the kind words. 

Hey, if you have 30 seconds, would you mind putting that in a Google review? It makes a huge difference for a small business.

[GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

No worries if not — thanks either way!
```

---

## Template 3: Email (More Formal)

Best for: Customers you communicate with via email

```

As a small local business, reviews make a huge difference in helping other pet parents find us. Even just a sentence or two helps!

Here's the direct link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Thanks so much for being a Tidy Tails customer. I really appreciate your support!

Best,
TJ
Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal
```

## What to Ask For

Guide them on what to mention (optional, but helps):
- How long they've used the service
- Reliability ("always shows up on time")
- Quality ("yard always looks great")
- Personal touch ("TJ knows my dogs by name")
- Value ("worth every penny")

---

## When to Ask

**Best times:**
- Right after they compliment you
- After a particularly thorough cleanup
- After you've gone above and beyond
- When they renew or extend service

**Avoid:**
- When there's been an issue
- During bad weather days
- When they're clearly busy

---

## Getting Your Google Review Link

1. Search for your business on Google
2. Click "Write a review" 
3. Copy the URL
4. Or use: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[YOUR_PLACE_ID]`

*You'll get your Place ID after setting up Google Business Profile*
