# Customer Onboarding & Service Operations System
**Created:** March 10, 2026  
**Purpose:** Professional onboarding flow, service agreement, and ops templates that turn new leads into locked-in recurring customers

---

## Why This Matters

TJ has 4-5 recurring customers. Goal is 25-30 by August. That means onboarding ~25 new people in 5 months. Without a system:
- Customers don't know what to expect
- Pricing gets inconsistent
- No clear service agreement = easy cancellations
- First impressions are ad-hoc instead of professional

With a system:
- Every customer gets the same professional experience
- Clear expectations reduce complaints and churn
- Service agreement creates psychological commitment
- Welcome sequence builds relationship from day one
- TJ looks like a real company, not a guy with a bucket

## 3. New Customer Checklist

### Before First Visit
- [ ] Service agreement signed (or verbal agreement confirmed via text)
- [ ] Gate access confirmed (code, unlocked, etc.)
- [ ] Service day confirmed
- [ ] Pricing confirmed and agreed
- [ ] Added to customer tracker
- [ ] Welcome text sent
- [ ] Address added to route/schedule

### First Visit
- [ ] Extra thorough cleanup (first impression matters)
- [ ] Check for hazards (broken fence, aggressive dogs, etc.)
- [ ] Note yard size and typical waste level
- [ ] Send "first visit complete" text
- [ ] Take mental note of anything to mention (dog toys, yard features, etc.)

### Week 3
- [ ] Send Google review request text
- [ ] Check: any issues or complaints? Address immediately
- [ ] Confirm customer is happy with service day/time

### Month 1
- [ ] Send first invoice with payment instructions
- [ ] Include referral offer in message
- [ ] Update customer tracker with status
- [ ] Check: payment received on time?

### Ongoing (Monthly)
- [ ] Invoice by the 1st
- [ ] Follow up on late payments by the 5th
- [ ] Quarterly check-in text (see below)

## 7. First Visit Route Optimization Notes

When adding a new customer, consider:
- **Cluster by day:** Group nearby addresses on the same service day
- **Minimize drive time:** Order stops geographically, not alphabetically
- **Time windows:** If a customer needs a specific window, build the route around them
- **Equipment check:** Before leaving — bags, scooper, gloves, sanitizer, business cards
- **Leave a card:** Drop a business card or door hanger at 2-3 neighboring houses after each visit
