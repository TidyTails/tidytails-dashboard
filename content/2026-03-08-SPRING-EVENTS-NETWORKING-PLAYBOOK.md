## 🏪 PARTNERSHIP DROPS (Do This Week)

### Treats Unleashed — 20 Locations in STL

This is the biggest opportunity that costs $0. Treats Unleashed is the #1 natural pet store chain in St. Louis. Their customers are EXACTLY your target: people who spend premium money on their pets.

**Locations to hit first (TJ's service areas):**
1. **Ladue** — 22 N Euclid (high income)
2. **Chesterfield** — Original location (West County)
3. **O'Fallon** — Growing area
4. **Lake St. Louis** — 6436 Ronald Reagan Dr
5. **Creve Coeur/Des Peres** — Affluent suburbs

**What to do at each:**
1. Introduce yourself to the manager
2. Ask if you can leave a small stack of business cards or flyers by the register
3. Ask about their community bulletin board
4. Ask about upcoming in-store events where you could hand out flyers
5. Mention you'd be happy to do a cross-promotion (you recommend them, they recommend you)

**Script:**
> "Hey, I'm TJ from Tidy Tails. We do pet waste removal in [area]. I was wondering if I could leave some business cards here for your customers? We get asked all the time where to buy quality pet food and I always send people your way."

**Time: 15 minutes per store | Do 2-3 per week for 3 weeks = all 6 covered**

### Vet Clinics — Leave Flyers

Hit every vet within your service area. Vets see dog owners who are ALREADY spending money on their dogs. A flyer in the waiting room is free advertising to a captive, qualified audience.

**Priority clinics to approach:**
- Hometown Veterinary Partners, Crestwood (you'll already be at their event March 28)
- Any vet in Florissant, Hazelwood, Bridgeton (your core territory)
- Chesterfield Valley vets (if expanding west)

**What to bring:** 25 flyers + 25 business cards per clinic

### Dog Groomers & Doggy Daycares

Same play as vet clinics. Ask to leave cards. Offer cross-referrals.
