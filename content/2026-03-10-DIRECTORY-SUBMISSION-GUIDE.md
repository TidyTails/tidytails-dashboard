## WHY THIS MATTERS

The visibility audit showed **0 out of 6 searches** returned Tidy Tails. The #1 reason? TJ has almost zero "citations" — mentions of the business name, address, and phone number (NAP) across the web. ## 🔑 CRITICAL RULES FOR EVERY LISTING

### NAP Consistency (Name, Address, Phone)
**USE EXACTLY THE SAME INFO ON EVERY DIRECTORY:**

- **Business Name:** Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal
- **Address:** [TJ's business address — use GBP address]
- **Phone:** [TJ's business phone — use GBP phone]
- **Website:** tidytailspetwasteremoval.net
- **Email:** [TJ's business email]

⚠️ **DO NOT** use variations like:
- "Tidy Tails" (missing "Pet Waste Removal")
- "TidyTails" (missing space)
- Different phone numbers on different sites
- Different address formatting

**Why:** Google cross-references your info across directories. Inconsistent NAP = Google doesn't trust you = lower rankings.

### Business Description (Copy-Paste This)

**Short (under 160 chars):**
> Professional pet waste removal in St. Louis County, MO. Weekly, bi-weekly & one-time cleanups. Reliable, affordable, locally owned. Free quotes!

**Long (for directories that allow it):**
> Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal keeps St. Louis County yards clean so you don't have to. We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and one-time pet waste cleanup services for residential customers throughout St. Louis County and surrounding areas. Locally owned and operated, we're reliable, affordable, and committed to making your outdoor spaces enjoyable again. No contracts required. Contact us today for a free quote!
