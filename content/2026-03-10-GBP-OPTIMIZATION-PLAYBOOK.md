## SECTION 5: REVIEW GENERATION STRATEGY

### The Math
- TJ has ~8 customers. Even 5 reviews would put him ahead of most local competitors.
- Google prioritizes RECENT reviews (last 90 days) over total count.
- Goal: 1-2 reviews per month minimum.

### How to Ask (Templates)

**After-Service Text (Best conversion rate):**
```
Hey [Name]! Just finished up your yard — looking good! 🐕 Quick favor: if you're happy with the service, a Google review would really help a small business like mine. Here's the link: https://g.page/r/Ce_hqIHanGLvEAE/review

No pressure at all. Thanks for being a customer! — TJ
```

**For Long-Time Customers:**
```
Hey [Name], you've been with Tidy Tails for [X months] now and I really appreciate it. Would you mind leaving a quick Google review? Even a sentence or two helps more than you'd think. Here's the link: https://g.page/r/Ce_hqIHanGLvEAE/review

Thanks! — TJ
```

**In-Person Script (after service):**
"Hey, real quick — if you've been happy with the service, I'd really appreciate a Google review. I can text you the link right now. It takes like 30 seconds and it's huge for a one-man operation like this."

### Pro Tips:
1. **Ask right after service** — satisfaction is highest
2. **Send the direct link** — don't make them search for you
3. **Don't incentivize** — violates Google TOS, can get profile suspended
4. **Guide what to mention** — "If you could mention the area you're in or the service, that helps" (creates review justifications Google uses for ranking)
5. **Respond to EVERY review** — Google tracks response rate

### Review Response Templates

**Positive Review Response:**
```
Thanks so much, [Name]! Really appreciate you trusting Tidy Tails with your yard. See you next [service day]! 🐾
```

**Detailed Positive Response (vary these):**
```
This made my day! Keeping [City] yards clean is what we're all about. Thanks for being a great customer — [dog name] is always happy to see me too! 😄
```

**Must-Have Shots:**

3. ✅ Truck/vehicle with any branding
4. ✅ Equipment close-up (shows professionalism)

**File Naming:** Before uploading, rename files descriptively:
