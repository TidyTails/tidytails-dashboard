### Copy-Paste Follow-Up System for TJ's 27 Active Leads
*Created: March 8, 2026 — by Franklin*

---

## How This Works

You have **12 quoted leads** and **15 pre-quote leads** sitting in Salesforce. Each one is worth $3,000-$15,000+. The difference between closing 3 of them and closing 6 of them is literally $10-30K in commissions. And the difference is almost always **follow-up timing and messaging**.

**Rules:**
1. Never more than 5 texts before switching to "dormant" cadence
2. Each text adds NEW value (urgency, info, humor) — never just "checking in"
3. Text > Email for residential. Email for backup/formal follow-up.
4. Best times to text: Tue-Thu, 10 AM-2 PM or 5-7 PM
5. NEVER text "just checking in" — that's what every other salesperson does

## SEQUENCE A: QUOTED LEADS (Gave quote, haven't decided)
*For your 12 active quoted leads*

### Day 1 (Same day as quote)
**Text — The Recap**
> Hey [NAME]! Thanks for stopping by today. Just wanted to make sure you got everything you need on that [SIZE/MODEL]. Your quote is good for 30 days, and right now spring install slots are running about 2-3 weeks out. Let me know if any questions come up! — TJ

**Why it works:** Professional, sets the 30-day clock, plants urgency about install times.

---

### Day 3 — The Value Add
**Text — New Info**
> Hey [NAME] — quick thought on your [SIZE] shed. A lot of folks don't realize we can add windows, shelving, or a workbench during the build for way less than adding it after. If you're thinking about using it for [workshop/storage/hobby], might be worth a second look at the options. Happy to send you some ideas.

**Why it works:** Positions you as helpful, not pushy. Opens conversation about upsells naturally.

---

### Day 7 — The Urgency Nudge
**Text — Timing Play**

*Option A (if spring rush applies):*
> Hey [NAME]! Just a heads up — we're getting into spring booking season and install wait times are starting to stretch. Right now it's still 2-3 weeks, but by mid-April it's usually 6+. No pressure, but wanted you to know so you can plan accordingly. Your quote's still locked in. 👍

*Option B (if a promo is running):*
> Hey [NAME] — TuffShed just dropped a [PROMO DETAILS]. Your [SIZE] quote from last week would qualify. Want me to run the numbers with the discount applied? Could save you $[AMOUNT].

**Why it works:** Creates urgency without being salesy. The "no pressure" line is key.

---

### Day 14 — The Social Proof
**Text — Neighbor/Project Story**
> Hey [NAME]! Random — just finished an install in [NEARBY AREA] yesterday. The customer did a [SIZE] with [FEATURE] and it came out awesome. Reminded me of what you were looking at. If you want, I can send you a pic of how it turned out. Sometimes seeing a finished build helps make the decision easier.

**Why it works:** Real proof, local relevance, low-pressure offer. People love seeing finished projects.

---

### Day 21 — The Honest Check
**Text — Direct But Friendly**
> Hey [NAME] — I know you've been thinking about the shed for a few weeks. Totally get it — it's a big purchase. Just wanted to ask honestly: is it still on your radar, or did something change? Either way is totally fine. If timing's the issue, I can hold your quote and you can pull the trigger when you're ready. No weirdness either way. 👊

**Why it works:** Gives them permission to say no (which reduces pressure and often gets a YES). The "no weirdness" line is disarming.

---

### Day 30+ — Dormant Follow-Up (Monthly)
**Text — The Long Game**

*Month 2:*
> Hey [NAME]! Hope spring's treating you well. Just wanted to let you know your shed options are still available and [CURRENT PROMO OR SEASON NOTE]. If the timing ever lines up, you've got my number. Enjoy the weekend! 🌤️

*Month 3:*
> [NAME]! Summer's coming — if you're still thinking about that [SIZE], now's actually the best time before the summer install rush. Happy to re-quote if anything's changed. Either way, hope you're doing great! ☀️

**Why it works:** Keeps you top of mind without being annoying. One text per month is the sweet spot.

## SEQUENCE B: PRE-QUOTE LEADS (Haven't connected yet)
*For your 15 pre-quote leads who haven't been quoted*

### Attempt 1 — The Intro
**Text:**
> Hey [NAME]! This is TJ from TuffShed/Backyard Buildings. I saw you were interested in a [shed/garage/studio]. I'm the local guy here in St. Louis — would love to help you figure out what works for your space and budget. When's a good time to chat or swing by the HD lot? 🏠

**If no response after 2 days:**

### Attempt 2 — The Quick Question
**Text:**
> Hey [NAME]! TJ again from TuffShed. No worries if you're busy — just wanted to ask: are you looking for storage, a workshop, or something else? Knowing that helps me point you to the right models without wasting your time. 👍

**Why it works:** Easy to answer. One question. No commitment.

---

### Attempt 3 (Day 5) — The Photo Hook
**Text:**
> [NAME] — just wrapped a [SIZE] install in [AREA]. Turned out sharp. [ATTACH PHOTO IF YOU HAVE ONE] Want me to show you what options look like for your property? Takes 5 min. No obligation.

---

### Attempt 4 (Day 10) — The Voicemail + Text Combo
**Leave voicemail, then immediately text:**
> Hey [NAME], just left you a quick voicemail. I know shed shopping can feel overwhelming with all the options out there. Here's the short version: I build custom sheds on-site at your property, backed by Home Depot. Free to get a quote. Let me know if you want to chat — even just to get ballpark pricing to compare. 🔨

---

### Attempt 5 (Day 14) — The Breakup
**Text:**
> Hey [NAME]! I've reached out a few times and I totally get it — life's busy. I'll stop bugging you, but just know: if a shed or garage ever makes it back to the to-do list, I'm your local guy. Save my number and hit me up anytime. No expiration date. 🤙 — TJ

**Why it works:** The "breakup" text is statistically the highest-response follow-up. People feel guilty and often re-engage. The "save my number" plants a seed for future.

## SEQUENCE C: POST-INSTALL FOLLOW-UP
*Often forgotten. This is where referrals come from.*

### Day 1 After Install
**Text:**
> [NAME]! Hope you're loving the new [SIZE]! Quick question — does everything look good? Doors opening smooth, no issues? I want to make sure you're 100% happy before I close this out.

### Day 7 After Install
**Text:**
> Hey [NAME]! Just checking in — has the new shed been working out? If you've started setting it up, I'd love to see a pic of how you're using it! Also, if any neighbors or friends ask about it, feel free to send them my way. 🙏

### Day 30 After Install — The Review Ask
**Text:**
> Hey [NAME]! It's been about a month — how's the shed treating you? If you've been happy with the experience, I'd really appreciate a quick Google review. It helps other folks in [AREA] find us. Here's the link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]. Takes 30 seconds and means a lot. Thanks! ⭐

### Day 90 After Install — The Referral Plant
**Text:**
> Hey [NAME]! Hope the shed's been awesome. Quick thought — spring is when everyone starts thinking about their outdoor space. If anyone you know has been talking about needing storage/a workshop/a she-shed, I'd love an intro. I always take great care of referrals. Have a great [season]! 🌿

## ⚡ PRO TIPS

1. **Personalize EVERYTHING.** "[NAME], I remember you mentioned wanting space for your woodworking tools..." beats generic every time.

2. **Photos close deals.** Keep a folder of finished installs on your phone. Send them in follow-ups. Visual proof > words.

3. **The spring urgency is REAL.** Install times genuinely do stretch from 2-3 weeks to 6-8 weeks. Use it because it's true, not because it's a tactic.

4. **Text at the right time.** Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM-2 PM or 5-7 PM. Avoid Mondays (people are catching up) and Fridays (people check out early).

5. **Log every touch in Salesforce.** If you don't track it, you'll forget who's at what stage and double-text people (instant credibility killer).

6. **Use the competitive cheat sheet.** When someone says "I can get a portable for less," you have the exact response ready. Reference `content/2026-03-08-SHED-COMPETITIVE-PRICING-CHEATSHEET.md`.

7. **The breakup text works.** Seriously. It's the highest-response follow-up in sales. Use it on every lead that goes quiet.
