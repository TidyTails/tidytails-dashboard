## 🗣️ TJ's Objection Handling Cheat Sheet

### "I can get a portable shed for $2,000 less..."
> "Totally fair — and that might be the right move depending on what you need. But here's the difference: a portable shed is built in a factory and dropped off a truck. Our buildings are constructed ON your property, which means:
> - Custom fit to your exact site
> - No size restrictions from road transport
> - Proper foundation integration
> - 50-year siding warranty
> - Professional installation included in the price
> The $2K gap usually disappears when you factor in the foundation prep, delivery fees, and that a TuffShed lasts 2-3x longer. But no pressure — if a portable works for you, that's cool too."

### "Cardinal/Cook has rent-to-own with no credit check..."
> "We do too! RTO Flex Lease — no credit check, select models, low deposit. Plus we have 0% interest for 6 months if you want to pay it off quick. And 9.99% for up to 10 years if you want the lowest monthly payment. Most people don't realize we have that many options."

### "Why should I pay more when there's a shed on Facebook for half the price?"
> "Those are usually either used sheds (check the floor — soft spots = rot), big box kits you have to build yourself (hope you have a free weekend and a buddy), or listed at $1 to get you to call (the real price is 5x that). We're transparent. Price includes materials, professional construction on your site, and delivery. No surprises."

### "Backyard & Beyond has a 50-year warranty..."
> "On the siding, yes — that's LP SmartSide, same material we use. But check what the STRUCTURAL warranty covers. Our Premier Series has 7-year, PRO has 10-year full structural. And we're backed by TuffShed corporate + Home Depot — not a small shop that might not be around in 50 years."
