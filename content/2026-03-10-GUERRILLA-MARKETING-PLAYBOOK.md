### Nextdoor — THE #1 FREE CHANNEL ⭐⭐⭐

**Why Nextdoor is gold:**
- Hyper-local (neighbors see YOUR posts)
- Recommendation requests happen daily ("anyone know a good...")
- Business profiles are free
- Verified neighborhoods in service area: Kirkwood, Ballwin, Manchester, Ellisville, Des Peres, Chesterfield

**How to use Nextdoor:**
1. **Claim free business page** at business.nextdoor.com
2. **Post 2x/week** — helpful content, not ads
3. **Respond to every "looking for" recommendation request** about pet services
4. **Get 2 free Business Posts/month** (organic reach to neighbors)
5. **Ask existing customers to recommend on Nextdoor** — neighbor recommendations = highest trust

**Post ideas for Nextdoor:**
- "Spring yard cleanup reminder — dog waste from winter can harbor parasites 🐕"
- "Happy to help any neighbors dealing with the spring thaw mess — TJ from Tidy Tails"
- "Quick tip: pick up waste within 24 hours to prevent lawn burn spots"
- Respond to dog-related discussions naturally
