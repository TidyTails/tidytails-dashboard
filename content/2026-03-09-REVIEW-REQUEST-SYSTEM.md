*Created: March 9, 2026 | By: Franklin*

## Why This Matters
- TJ has **0 Google reviews**. Competitors have 10-50+.
- Reviews are the **#1 local ranking factor** for Google Maps.
- 0 reviews = invisible in search + zero social proof on the website.
- TJ has 4-5 happy recurring customers RIGHT NOW who haven't been asked.
- Goal: **5 reviews by end of March, 10 by end of April.**

| Trigger | Timing | Template |

## Pro Tips

1. **Text > Email** for review requests. 98% open rate vs 20%.
2. **Ask in person first, then follow up with the link via text.** "Hey, would you mind leaving a review? I'll text you the link." This gets verbal commitment first.
3. **Don't offer incentives for reviews** — violates Google's policy and can get reviews removed.
4. **Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours.** Google rewards engaged businesses.
5. **5 reviews is the magic number** — that's when the star rating shows in search results.
6. **Never ask for a "5-star review"** — just ask for "a review." Google flags businesses that solicit specific ratings.
7. **Best days to ask: Tuesday-Thursday.** People are in work mode and more likely to complete small tasks.
