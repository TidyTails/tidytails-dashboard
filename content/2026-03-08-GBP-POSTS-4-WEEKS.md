## ⚡ PRO TIPS

1. **Always add a photo.** Posts with photos get 10x more engagement than text-only.
2. **Use the CTA button.** "Call now" is best for service businesses.
3. **Post between 8-10 AM Monday.** Peak local search time.
4. **Don't copy-paste identical posts.** Google penalizes duplicate content. Each week above is unique.
5. **Respond to ANY reviews immediately.** Even a simple "Thank you!" signals activity to Google.
6. **After you get your first review**, start every post with your star rating: "⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Rated 5 stars by St. Louis pet owners"
