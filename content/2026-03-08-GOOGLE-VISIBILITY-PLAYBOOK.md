### TIER 1: FOUNDATION (Do This Week — 1-2 Hours Total)

#### Step 1: Claim Google Business Profile (30 min)
**This is the single most important thing you can do for visibility.**

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Add your business to Google"
4. Enter: **Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal**
5. Category: **Pet waste removal service** (primary)
   - Secondary categories: **Dog walker**, **Pet service**, **Cleaning service**
6. Since you go to customers (no storefront), select **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"**
7. Enter service areas:
   - St. Louis County, MO
   - St. Charles County, MO
   - Florissant, MO
   - Chesterfield, MO
   - Ballwin, MO
   - Manchester, MO
   - Ellisville, MO
   - Wildwood, MO
   - Maryland Heights, MO
   - Creve Coeur, MO
8. Phone: Your business number
9. Website: tidytailsstl.com
10. Verify (Google will offer postcard, phone, or video verification)

**PROFILE DETAILS TO FILL OUT:**

**Business Description (750 chars max):**
> Tidy Tails is St. Louis's locally owned pet waste removal service. Founded by TJ, a dog lover who handles every yard personally — no random technicians, no franchise fees. We offer weekly ($25/visit), bi-weekly ($45/visit), and monthly ($70/mo) yard cleanup for residential customers across St. Louis County, St. Charles, and surrounding areas. Every visit includes thorough waste removal, a text notification when complete, and a 100% satisfaction guarantee. No contracts ever. First cleanup is free. Serving Florissant, Chesterfield, Ballwin, Manchester, Wildwood, Maryland Heights, Creve Coeur, and beyond.

**Business Hours:** Set your normal operating hours (e.g., Mon-Sat 8am-5pm)

**Attributes to enable:**
- Locally owned
- Veteran-owned (if applicable)
- Identifies as small business
- Online appointments

#### Step 2: Add Photos to GBP (15 min)
Upload AT MINIMUM:
- **Logo** (the Tidy Tails logo — square, 720x720px minimum)
- **Cover photo** (you with a dog, or a clean yard shot)
- **3-5 "at work" photos** — you scooping, equipment, before/after yards
- **Your truck/vehicle** with any branding
- **A friendly photo of you** (customers want to see who's coming to their yard)

**Businesses with photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more clicks.**

💡 *Pro tip: Take a before/after photo at your next 3 service visits. These are GOLD for your profile.*

#### Step 3: Get Your First 5 Google Reviews (15 min to send, then wait)
**This is the #1 ranking factor for local businesses. Zero reviews = zero trust.**

**Text template to send to your 4-5 current customers:**

> Hey [Name]! Quick favor — would you mind leaving a quick Google review for Tidy Tails? It would really help other dog owners find us. Here's the link: [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
> 
> Even just a sentence or two about your experience means a lot. Thanks! — TJ

**How to get your review link:**
1. After GBP is verified, go to your Google Business Profile dashboard
2. Click "Ask for reviews" — Google gives you a short link
3. Save that link. You'll use it a lot.

**Goal: 5 reviews by end of March. 10 by end of April.**

### TIER 3: WEBSITE SEO (Week 3 — 30 Min)

Your website (tidytailsstl.com) is solid. A few tweaks to help Google find it:

#### Quick Wins:

**☐ Add service area pages** — Create individual pages or sections for each city:
- "Pet Waste Removal in Florissant, MO"
- "Pooper Scooper Service in Chesterfield, MO"
- "Dog Waste Cleanup in Ballwin & Manchester, MO"
- "Pet Waste Removal in Wildwood & West County"

Each page should have ~200 words with the city name mentioned naturally 3-5 times. This is how Google connects local searches to your site.

**☐ Add structured data (LocalBusiness schema)** — This helps Google understand your business:
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Tidy Tails Pet Waste Removal",
  "description": "Professional pet waste removal service serving St. Louis County and St. Charles County, Missouri.",
  "url": "https://tidytailsstl.com",
  "telephone": "YOUR-PHONE",
  "areaServed": [
    {"@type": "City", "name": "St. Louis", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Florissant", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Chesterfield", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Ballwin", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Wildwood", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Manchester", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Maryland Heights", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "City", "name": "Creve Coeur", "addressRegion": "MO"},
    {"@type": "County", "name": "St. Charles County", "addressRegion": "MO"}
  ],
  "priceRange": "$25-$135",
  "openingHours": "Mo-Sa 08:00-17:00",
  "sameAs": [
    "https://facebook.com/YOUR_PAGE",
    "https://instagram.com/YOUR_PAGE"
  ]
}
```

**☐ Add a blog post** — Even one post helps. Ideas:
- "Spring Yard Cleanup: Why March Is the Worst Month for Dog Waste in St. Louis"
- "Pet Waste Removal Cost in St. Louis: What to Expect in 2026"
- "Why Hiring a Pooper Scooper Service Is Worth It (From a St. Louis Dog Owner)"

**☐ Submit sitemap to Google Search Console**
1. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Add tidytailsstl.com as a property
3. Submit your sitemap (usually tidytailsstl.com/sitemap.xml)

### TIER 4: ONGOING (Weekly, 10 Min)

**☐ Post to Google Business Profile weekly** — GBP has a "Posts" feature. Use it.
- Share a tip, a seasonal reminder, a before/after photo, or a special offer
- Posts last 7 days and show in search results
- Example: "Spring cleanup season is here! 🌱 Book your first free cleanup this week."

**☐ Ask every new customer for a review** — Make it automatic
- After first service, text the review link
- Goal: 1-2 new reviews per month

**☐ Respond to all reviews** — Even positive ones
- "Thanks [Name]! Love keeping your yard clean for [dog name]. 🐕"
- Shows Google and customers you're active and engaged

**☐ Update GBP photos monthly** — Fresh photos signal an active business

**☐ Check Google Search Console** — See what searches lead to your site. Optimize for the ones that are close to ranking.
