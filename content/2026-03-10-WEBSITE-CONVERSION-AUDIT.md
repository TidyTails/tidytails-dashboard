## 10 Conversion Fixes — Ranked by Impact

### 🔴 FIX #1: FLIP THE PRICE ANCHORING (Critical)

**Problem:** The hero section says "Starting at $25/week." That's the WORST possible number to lead with. When a visitor is comparison-shopping:
- Poop2Scoop: "$18/visit" — looks cheaper
- Doggie Doodies: "$83/month" — looks cheaper
- Tidy Tails: "$25/week" — looks most expensive

**Reality:** TJ's $70/month plan is the CHEAPEST monthly option in the entire market. And his flat multi-dog rate destroys the competition at 2+ dogs. But nobody sees that because the site leads with the most expensive-looking number.

**Fix:**
```
CURRENT HERO: "Let us handle it. Starting at $25/week."

CHANGE TO: "Let us handle it. Plans from $70/month."
```

**Pricing card reorder:**
```
CURRENT ORDER:
1. Weekly ($25/wk) — positioned as primary
2. Bi-Weekly ($45)
3. Monthly ($70) — buried, marked as "minimal mess"
4. One-Time ($100)

CHANGE TO:
1. Monthly ($70/mo) ⭐ MOST POPULAR — "Perfect for 1 dog. Cheapest in STL!"
2. Weekly ($95/mo) — "Best for multi-dog homes. Same price, unlimited dogs."
3. Bi-Weekly ($45/visit) — "Lighter touch, every other week"
4. One-Time Deep Clean ($100) — "Spring cleanup, moving, events"
```

**Why $95/mo instead of $25/wk:**
- $95/mo is directly comparable to competitors ($83-90/mo range)
- It's only $12 more than Doggie Doodies — and TJ includes unlimited dogs
- Monthly billing reduces sticker shock and matches how people think about subscriptions
- Doggie Doodies charges $88/mo for 2 dogs. TJ at $95/mo for unlimited dogs is a STEAL.

**Impact:** HIGH — This single change reframes TJ from "most expensive" to "competitive with better value."

### 🔴 FIX #2: ADD SOCIAL PROOF TO HOMEPAGE (Critical)

**Problem:** Zero testimonials, zero review count, zero trust signals. Doggie Doodies has customer quotes on their homepage. When a visitor sees pricing but no proof that anyone is actually happy with the service, they bounce.

**Fix — Add a testimonials section between FAQ and Service Areas:**

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

came, I walked into my clean backyard and thought 'why didn't I do

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
"TJ is reliable, thorough, and my dogs love seeing him every week. 
Best money I spend each month."
— Mike, Chesterfield

[Google/Yelp badge showing review count]
```

**Action for TJ:** Text 2-3 happy customers and ask for a short quote + permission to use their first name and city. Even 2 reviews transforms the page.

**Impact:** HIGH — Social proof is the #1 conversion driver for local services.

### 🔴 FIX #3: ADD A FREE FIRST VISIT OFFER (Critical)

**Problem:** Doggie Doodies offers a FREE initial cleanup with plan purchase. TJ offers... nothing. For a service where you're letting a stranger into your backyard, the risk feels high. A free trial eliminates that.

**Fix — Replace "Get Your Free Quote" CTA with:**
```
🎉 First Cleanup FREE — Try Us Risk-Free

Your first visit is on us. If you love it, we'll set up your 
weekly schedule. If not, no charge, no awkwardness. 

[Book My Free Cleanup] → (links to contact/form)
```

**On pricing cards, add to the Weekly plan:**
```
Weekly Cleanup
$95/month
✓ First visit FREE
✓ Unlimited dogs, same price
✓ Cancel anytime
```

**Why this works:**
- Eliminates purchase anxiety ("what if I don't like it?")
- Creates reciprocity (free value → obligation to continue)
- Gives TJ a chance to WOW them in person
- Cost: ~30 min of TJ's time. ROI: ~$1,200/year if they convert

**Impact:** HIGH — Free trials convert 2-5x better than "get a quote" for home services.

### 🟡 FIX #5: HIGHLIGHT THE MULTI-DOG ADVANTAGE (Medium)

**Problem:** TJ's biggest competitive advantage — flat rate for ANY number of dogs — is barely mentioned. The pricing page says "Best for busy families & multiple dogs" on the weekly plan, but doesn't spell out the savings.

**Fix — Add a callout box above or within pricing:**
```
🐕🐕🐕 Got Multiple Dogs?

Most companies charge extra per dog. We don't. 

2 dogs at Doggie Doodies: $88/mo
2 dogs at Tidy Tails: $95/mo (just $7 more for flat rate)

3 dogs at Doggie Doodies: $93/mo  
3 dogs at Tidy Tails: $95/mo (SAME price)

4 dogs at Doggie Doodies: $98/mo
4 dogs at Tidy Tails: $95/mo (YOU SAVE $3/mo + no per-dog headaches)

One price. All dogs. Every visit.
```

**Impact:** MEDIUM — Differentiates TJ in a market where everyone charges per-dog. Multi-dog households are the highest-value customers.

### 🟡 FIX #6: ADD SPRING SEASONAL URGENCY (Medium)

**Problem:** It's March. Spring is THE highest-intent season for pet waste services. The site has zero seasonal content on the homepage. No banner, no spring special, no urgency.

**Fix — Add a top-of-page seasonal banner:**
```
🌸 SPRING CLEANUP SPECIAL: First visit FREE + 10% off your first month
Limited spots available for weekly service in March
[Claim Your Spot →]
```

**Why:** Urgency + seasonal relevance + scarcity ("limited spots") increases conversion. Update this quarterly.

**Impact:** MEDIUM — Seasonal urgency drives action from people who are "thinking about it."

**Problem:** Doggie Doodies mentions background checks, equipment disinfection, and gate photos. TJ does some of these things but doesn't mention them on the site.

**Fix — Add to "Why Choose" section or create a "What to Expect" section:**
```
What to Expect Every Visit:
✓ Same person every time (TJ — not a rotating crew)
✓ Text notification when the job's done
✓ Gate secured after every visit
✓ All equipment sanitized between properties
✓ Fully insured
```

**Even if TJ doesn't do ALL of these yet, he should start doing the easy ones:**
- Text when done → he already does this
- Gate secured → just take a photo, text it
- Equipment sanitized → add a sanitizer spray to the truck (costs $5)

**Impact:** LOWER — But eliminates objections from comparison shoppers.

### 🟢 FIX #8: ADD A CONTACT FORM TO THE HOMEPAGE (Lower)

**Problem:** The homepage says "Call, text, or fill out our form" but the contact form appears to be on a separate page or JS-rendered. Many visitors won't navigate to find it.

**Fix — Embed a simple form right on the homepage, after pricing:**
```
📝 Get Your Free Cleanup Scheduled

Name: [________]
Address/Zip: [________]  
# of Dogs: [1] [2] [3+]
Phone or Email: [________]
Best day for first visit: [________]

[Schedule My Free Visit →]
```

**Keep it SHORT — 4-5 fields max. Every extra field reduces completion by 10-15%.**

**Impact:** LOWER — Helps capture leads who prefer forms over calls/texts.

### 🟢 FIX #10: ADD DEODORIZING AS AN UPSELL (Lower)

**Problem:** Poop2Scoop offers deck/yard deodorizing for $4 extra. TJ has no upsell options. Adding a simple upsell increases average revenue per customer.

**Fix — Add to pricing page:**
```

🧴 Yard Deodorizing Treatment — $10/visit
Enzyme-based spray eliminates odors. Great after spring thaw or

**Impact:** LOWER — Revenue optimization, not conversion. But easy money.

## KEY INSIGHT

**TJ's website has a positioning problem, not a quality problem.** The copy is great, the personal brand is strong, the blog content is solid. But the site positions TJ as the most expensive option when he actually has the cheapest monthly plan AND the best multi-dog value in the market. 

Three changes — lead with monthly pricing, offer a free first visit, and add testimonials — would transform this from a "nice site" to a conversion machine. All three can be done in under an hour.
