## THE PROGRAM: "Scoop the Savings"

### How It Works
- **Current customer refers a friend** → friend signs up for weekly/biweekly service
- **Both people win** — referrer gets a reward, new customer gets a welcome discount
- No limit on referrals. The more you share, the more you save.

### Incentive Structure

| Referrals | Referrer Gets | New Customer Gets |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| 1st referral | **1 FREE week** of service | **First cleanup FREE** (already standard) |
| 2nd referral | **1 FREE week** of service | First cleanup FREE |
| 3rd referral | **1 FREE MONTH** of service | First cleanup FREE |
| 5+ referrals | **Permanent 10% off** all future service | First cleanup FREE |

### Why This Structure Works
- **Low barrier**: One referral = one free week. Easy to earn, easy to understand
- **Escalating rewards**: Creates a "game" — customers want to hit that 3rd referral for the free month
- **Permanent discount at 5**: Creates "VIP customers" who are locked in forever and keep referring
- **Both sides win**: New customer gets free first cleanup (removes risk), referrer gets tangible savings
- **Cost is minimal**: One free week costs TJ ~$25 in time. But a new weekly customer is worth $100+/month recurring

### The Math
- Cost of 1 free week: ~$25 (TJ's time)  
- Value of 1 new weekly customer: $100/month = $1,200/year  
- **Customer acquisition cost via referral: $25 (vs $50-100+ for paid ads)**
- Even if only 50% of referred people stick past month 1, ROI is massive

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Your best referrers are your newest customers.** They just experienced the "wow" moment. Hit them with the referral ask within the first 2 weeks.

2. **Make the reward IMMEDIATE.** Don't say "next month." Say "your next visit is free." Speed of reward = speed of referral.

3. **Name names (with permission).** "Sarah from Ballwin just referred her neighbor and got a free week!" Social proof drives action.

4. **Spring is your golden window.** Everyone's yard is gross right now. The next 6 weeks are prime referral season. Push hard.

5. **Stack with the review request.** After a customer refers someone AND leaves a Google review, give them an extra bonus week. Lock in that loyalty.
