## 📊 What Actually Works (Ranked by ROI)

### 1. 🏆 FACEBOOK/META ADS — The #1 Channel
**Why it's #1:** People don't search for "pet waste removal" because they don't know it exists. Facebook puts you in front of dog owners who NEED the service but haven't thought to look for it.

**Real numbers from Swoop Scoop (7-figure company):**
- 10,000+ leads generated since 2021
- 487 new customers in their best single month
- 2-5 leads per day consistently
- Average cost per lead for service businesses on Meta: ~$15-30

**How to set it up:**

| Setting | What to Use |
|---------|-------------|
| Campaign Objective | "Leads" or "Engagement" (NOT Awareness or Traffic) |
| Targeting Option 1 | Dog Lovers / Dog Interests |
| Targeting Option 2 | Advantage+ (let Meta's AI find buyers) |
| Targeting Option 3 | Lookalike audience from customer list (once you have 50+) |
| Geographic | 25-mile radius around STL service area |
| Budget to Start | $10-15/day ($300-450/month) |

**Ad Creative That Works:**
- ✅ Real photos of TJ working (phone photos outperform professional ones!)
- ✅ Photos next to the truck/equipment
- ✅ Call-out: "Hey dog owners in [Florissant/Ferguson/O'Fallon]!"
- ✅ Promotion: "Free first cleanup when you sign up for weekly service"
- ❌ NO stock images
- ❌ NO generic corporate copy

**Sample Ad Format:**
```
🐕 Hey Dog Owners in [City]!

Still spending your weekends picking up after your pup? 
We'll handle it for you — starting at just $17.50/week.

✅ Same-day service available
✅ Gate code? No problem
✅ Multi-dog households welcome (flat rate!)

🎉 FREE first cleanup when you sign up this month

Text "CLEAN" to [phone] or tap below 👇
```

**Budget Math:**
- $15/day = ~$450/month
- At $25/lead = ~18 leads/month
- At 30% conversion = ~5-6 new recurring customers/month
- Each customer = ~$70/month recurring
- ROI: Spend $450 → Gain $350-420/month RECURRING
- **Payback period: ~6 weeks, then pure profit**

### 2. 🔍 GOOGLE ADS (Search) — The Intent Catcher
**Why it works:** Once people SEE your Facebook ad, many will Google you later. Google Ads catches those high-intent searches.

**How it works:** Pay-per-click. You bid on keywords. You pay when someone clicks.

**Best keywords to target:**
- "pooper scooper service [city]" 
- "dog poop cleanup near me"
- "pet waste removal St. Louis"
- "dog waste removal service"

**Expected costs:**
- CPC (cost per click): $2-8 for pet waste keywords (LOW competition vs plumbing/HVAC at $20-50)
- Conversion rate: 5-10% of clicks → leads
- Cost per lead: ~$30-60

**Pro tips:**
- Track conversions (phone calls + form submissions) or you'll waste money
- Higher Quality Score = lower CPC. Make your landing page specifically about pet waste removal in STL
- Start with $10/day alongside FB ads
- Use "phrase match" and "exact match" keywords, NOT broad match

**Why Google is #2, not #1:**
- Low search volume for pet waste removal (people don't know to search for it)
- Works best AS A COMPLEMENT to Facebook ads
- Higher cost per lead than Facebook for this industry

### 3. 🗺️ GOOGLE LOCAL SERVICES ADS (LSAs) — Possible but Tricky

**The honest assessment:** Pet waste removal is NOT a direct LSA category. The closest eligible categories are:
- **Lawn Care** (includes "Yard cleanup" sub-category)
- **Pet Grooming/Pet Boarding** (pet services vertical)

**Could TJ qualify?** Possibly under "Lawn Care" → "Yard cleanup" but:
- May require a pesticide applicator license (Google requires it for ALL lawn care categories in many states, even if you don't spray anything)
- Requires general liability + professional liability insurance with COI
- Background check required (takes 2-3 weeks)
- Need a verified Google Business Profile first (TJ doesn't have one yet)

**My recommendation:** Skip LSAs for now. Get GBP verified and running Facebook ads first. Revisit LSAs once you have 20+ Google reviews and insurance documentation ready.

**Requirements to file away for later:**
- [ ] General liability insurance (COI in PDF)
- [ ] Professional liability insurance
- [ ] Business license
- [ ] Background check (owner)
- [ ] Verified Google Business Profile with reviews
- [ ] Possible pesticide applicator license (MO requirements)

---

### 4. 🏠 GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE — FREE and Non-Negotiable

**This is still the #1 blocker.** Before ANY paid ads, TJ needs:

1. **Claim/verify Google Business Profile** → google.com/business
2. **Category:** "Pet Waste Removal Service" or "Pet Services"
3. **Complete profile:** Photos, services, hours, description, service area
4. **NAP consistency:** Same Name, Address, Phone everywhere online
5. **Start collecting reviews** → Each review is worth its weight in gold

**Why this matters for paid ads:**
- Google Ads Quality Score partially depends on GBP
- LSAs require a linked GBP
- Customers who see FB ads will Google you — if nothing shows up, you lose the lead
- Swoop Scoop has 500+ five-star reviews. That's their moat.
