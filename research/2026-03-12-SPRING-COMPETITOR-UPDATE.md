## 🎯 SPRING 2026 COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE SUMMARY

### The Market Right Now:
1. **14+ companies** competing in STL pet waste removal
2. **Spring is peak signup season** — everyone is pushing promos
3. **Free first cleanup** is the standard hook (Doggie Doodies, POOP 911)
4. **No contracts** is table stakes — everyone offers it
5. **Satisfaction guarantees** are everywhere
6. **Commercial services** are the growth play (most competitors offer HOA/apartment)

### Where TJ Wins:
- ✅ **Lowest independent price** ($70 flat)
- ✅ **Multi-dog value** (flat rate = huge savings for 2-3 dog homes)
- ✅ **No contracts** ✓
- ✅ **Local, independent** (not a franchise)

### Where TJ Loses:
- ❌ **No free first cleanup offer** — everyone else has one
- ❌ **No customer portal/app** — Doggie Doodies has one
- ❌ **No text notifications** (before/after service)
- ❌ **No gate photos/proof of service**
- ❌ **No blog/SEO content**
- ❌ **No Google reviews** (critical for discovery)
- ❌ **No Yelp/Nextdoor presence** (competitors are listed)
- ❌ **No commercial services page**
- ❌ **No deck/patio deodorizing upsell**

## 🧮 COMPETITIVE VULNERABILITY: DOGGIE DOODIES

Their biggest weakness is still **unprofessional copy**:
- "INITAL" (should be INITIAL) — appears 6+ times on homepage
- "RESIDENTAL" (should be RESIDENTIAL)  
- "Guarentee" (should be GUARANTEE)
- "Progam" (should be PROGRAM)
- Using @hotmail.com for business email

This matters because: a customer comparing two services will notice. When TJ's site looks clean and Doggie Doodies has typos everywhere, that's a subtle trust signal. **TJ's professionalism IS a competitive advantage** — if people can find him.
