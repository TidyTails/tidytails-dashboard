## 💰 THE MATH: Why Tiered Pricing Matters

**Current (flat $70/mo, 5 customers):** $350/month

**If TJ had Doggie Doodies' pricing structure with same 5 customers:**
- Assume 2 have 1 dog ($83 each = $166)
- Assume 2 have 2 dogs ($88 each = $176)
- Assume 1 has 3 dogs ($100 = $100)
- **Total: $442/month (+26%)**

**At 25 customers with tiered pricing:**
- 10 × 1 dog @ $83 = $830
- 10 × 2 dogs @ $88 = $880
- 5 × 3 dogs @ $100 = $500
- **Total: $2,210/month vs. $1,750 at flat $70**
- **Extra $460/month = $5,520/year just from proper pricing**
