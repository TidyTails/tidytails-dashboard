## THE PROBLEM

TJ charges **$70/month flat** — no tiers, no add-ons, no per-dog pricing, no one-time service.

**Industry data says he's leaving money on the table:**
- National industry average: **$86–$95/month** (Poop Scoop Millionaire survey)
- 7-figure company Swoop Scoop: **$96–$105/month**, raises 10% annually
- STL competitor Poop2Scoop: **$78/month** + $4/dog surcharge
- STL competitor Poopy Paws (possibly dead): **$86/month**

**TJ's $70 flat rate is 18-26% BELOW the national average.** That's fine for customer acquisition in year one, but he needs a strategy to grow average revenue per customer (ARPC) as the business scales.

## RECOMMENDED SERVICE TIERS

### 🥉 BASIC — "The Essentials" — $70/month
*Current offering, rebranded as the entry tier*
- 1x weekly cleanup
- 1 dog included
- Standard service (scoop & haul)
- +$10/month per additional dog

**Why keep $70:** It's TJ's competitive weapon. Below Poop2Scoop ($78), way below Poopy Paws ($86). As a newer company building reviews, price leadership gets him in the door.

### 🥈 STANDARD — "The Clean Yard" — $95/month
*Best value tier — this is where most customers should land*
- 1x weekly cleanup
- Up to 2 dogs included
- Yard deodorizer spray (monthly, spring-fall)
- Priority scheduling (early in the route)
- Text notification when service is complete
- +$10/month per additional dog beyond 2

**Margin boost:** Deodorizer costs ~$2-3/application. Text notifications cost nothing. TJ nets ~$20/month more per customer.

### 🥇 PREMIUM — "The VIP Yard" — $135/month
*For multi-dog households and customers who want the best*
- 2x weekly cleanup
- Up to 3 dogs included
- Yard deodorizer spray (bi-weekly, spring-fall)
- Priority scheduling
- Service completion text + photo
- Seasonal deep clean included (spring + fall)
- +$10/month per additional dog beyond 3

**Margin boost:** Second visit adds ~20 min/week to a route TJ's already driving. Marginal cost is low. Nets $50-60/month more per customer.

## ONE-TIME SERVICES (Lead Generators)

### 🌱 SPRING CLEANUP — $99-$149 (based on yard size)
*The gateway drug to recurring service*
- Full yard cleanup of winter accumulation
- Deodorizer application
- Yard assessment report
- $25 off first month if they sign up for recurring

**Pricing logic:** Average initial cleanup takes 30-45 min. At TJ's target of $100/hr, $99-149 is fair and profitable. Swoop Scoop recommends flat-rate over hourly to avoid customer clock-watching.

**Conversion strategy:**
- Every spring cleanup ends with: "Hey, your yard looked rough — want me to keep it clean for $70/month so it never gets like that again?"
- Expected conversion: 25-35% of one-timers → recurring

### 🏡 ONE-TIME CLEANUP — $75-$125 (based on yard size)
*For events, real estate showings, or just had-enough moments*
- Small yard (under 1/4 acre): $75
- Medium yard (1/4 - 1/2 acre): $99
- Large yard (1/2+ acre): $125
- Multi-dog surcharge: +$25 for 3+ dogs

### 🎉 EVENT PREP CLEANUP — $125 flat
*BBQs, graduations, parties, open houses*
- Same-day or next-day service
- Complete yard sweep
- Deodorizer included
- Perfect upsell for spring/summer party season

## COMMERCIAL PRICING (Separate from Residential)

Commercial pricing should be **time-based, not flat-rate** until TJ has enough data for flat quotes.

### Formula:
1. Walk the property
2. Time how long cleanup takes
3. Multiply by $60/hour (minimum)
4. Add 20% overhead buffer
5. Round to nearest $50 for monthly quote

### Typical Commercial Rates:
| Property Type | Visits/Week | Monthly Range |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|
| Small complex (20-50 units) | 2x | $200-$400 |
| Medium complex (50-150 units) | 3x | $400-$800 |
| Large complex (150+ units) | 5x | $800-$1,500 |
| Dog park | 3-5x | $300-$600 |
| HOA common areas | 1-2x | $150-$350 |

**Key insight:** One medium apartment complex at $500/month = **7 residential customers' worth of revenue** on a single stop.

## PRICING PSYCHOLOGY & TACTICS

### 1. Anchor with Premium, Close with Standard
- Always show Premium first ($135) so Standard ($95) looks like a deal
- "Most Popular" badge on Standard tier
- This is textbook price anchoring — Poop2Scoop doesn't do this

### 2. The $70 Bridge
- Keep $70 Basic for price-sensitive customers and new acquisition
- Frame it as "no frills" — still great service, just without extras
- Once they see the value of Standard, many will upgrade

### 3. Annual Price Increases
- Plan 8-10% annual increases (Swoop Scoop does 10%)
- Grandfather early customers for 6 months as loyalty reward
- TJ's $70 → $77 in year 2 → $85 in year 3 (still competitive)
- Announce increases 30 days in advance, blame "fuel and insurance costs"

### 4. No Per-Dog Pricing on Marketing
- Advertise "$70/month" — clean, simple, memorable
- Per-dog surcharges are disclosed during quoting, not in ads
- Competitors like Poop2Scoop that show "+$4/dog" create friction

### 5. The Free First Cleanup
- POOP 911 uses this — it works
- TJ should match it for recurring signups only
- "Free first cleanup when you sign up for monthly service"
- Cost: 15-30 min of TJ's time. ROI: $840+/year per conversion

### The Math is Clear:
Tiered pricing + commercial + add-ons can **MORE THAN DOUBLE** TJ's monthly revenue at the same customer count.
