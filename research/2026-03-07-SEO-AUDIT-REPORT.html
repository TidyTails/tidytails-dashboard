🚨 Critical Issues

Not Ranking for ANY Target Keywords Critical

Searched "pet waste removal St Louis MO" — Tidy Tails is NOT in the top 10 results. DoodyCalls, POOP 911, Poop 2 Scoop, and even Petworks rank ahead. Even searching "tidy tails pet waste removal st louis" shows OTHER companies named "Tidy Tails" from Charleston, Atlanta, and Florida — not you.

🔧 Fix: Google Business Profile optimization + reviews + directory citations + backlinks (see Action Plan)

Zero or Near-Zero Google Reviews Critical

Google's Local Pack (the map results that appear first) is dominated by businesses with 10+ reviews. Without reviews, your Google Business Profile won't appear in map results, which is where 46% of local searches click first.

🔧 Fix: Use the Review Generator tool on your dashboard to text existing customers. Even 5 reviews dramatically changes your visibility.

Brand Name Collision — "Tidy Tails" Used by 6+ Companies Critical

There are at least 6 other pet waste removal companies named "Tidy Tails" nationwide (Charleston SC, Atlanta GA, Valrico FL, etc.). Google has to guess which one a searcher means. This dilutes your brand searches significantly.

🔧 Fix: ALWAYS use "Tidy Tails STL" in all marketing, profiles, and citations. The "STL" differentiator is key. Update your Google Business Profile name to include the differentiator if Google allows it.

⚠️ Warnings

Only 4 Blog Posts Warning

All 4 blog posts were published on the same day (March 6, 2026). Google prefers sites that publish consistently over time. Batch-publishing looks unnatural and doesn't build topical authority gradually.

🔧 Fix: Add 2 new blog posts per month. Backdate new ones a couple weeks apart. Focus on hyper-local topics (see Content Plan tab).

City Pages Are Thin Content Warning

Ferguson page is ~500 words and mostly duplicated pricing info. Google may see these as "doorway pages" (thin pages designed only to rank for specific terms). Each city page should have 800-1200 words of unique content about that specific area.

🔧 Fix: Add local context — nearby parks, dog-friendly spots, neighborhood character, local stats. Make each page genuinely useful for someone in that city.

No Backlinks (Likely) Warning

A new site with no external links pointing to it has no "authority" in Google's eyes. Your competitors (DoodyCalls, POOP 911) have hundreds of backlinks from national franchise networks.

🔧 Fix: Local directory listings (free), local press mentions, community partnerships. See Directories tab.

No Google Search Console or Analytics Detected Warning

The site HTML doesn't show Google Analytics or Search Console verification tags. Without these, you can't see what queries people use to find you, what your click-through rates are, or if Google is even indexing all your pages.

🔧 Fix: Set up Google Search Console (free, 10 min) and Google Analytics 4 (free, 15 min). Submit your sitemap at search.google.com/search-console.
