📅 The 7-Day Follow-Up Plan

Don't just ask once. Here's the proven sequence — persistent but not annoying.

Day 1 (Sunday After Service)
Send Template 1 or 4 right after their service while you're fresh in their mind. Best time: within 2 hours of finishing.
Day 3-4 (Tuesday or Wednesday)
If no review yet, send Template 2 (the check-in). This doesn't feel like a follow-up because it leads with "how's the yard?"
Day 7 (Next Sunday)
Last ask. After their service, casually mention in person: "Hey, did you get a chance to check that review link I sent? No worries if not!" In-person ask has the highest conversion rate.
After Day 7
Stop asking. Move to the next customer. Never make it awkward. They may still leave one later — the link is in their texts forever.
🎯 Target: Ask all 4-5 customers this week. Even a 50% response = 2-3 reviews = you're now visible on Google Maps. That's the goal.
