## 💵 COMMERCIAL PRICING GUIDE

Based on industry research (poopscoopsfornoobs.com):

### How to Bid:
1. **Walk the property** — time how long it takes
2. **Double your time** (first visits always take longer)
3. **Calculate:** Hours × $60/hr = Weekly price
4. **Monthly:** (Weekly × 52) ÷ 12

### Example Pricing:
| Property Size | Weekly Time | Weekly Price | Monthly Price |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| Small (50 units) | 1.5 hrs | $90 | $390 |
| Medium (100 units) | 3 hrs | $180 | $780 |
| Large (200+ units) | 5 hrs | $300 | $1,300 |

### Per-Unit Breakdown (for proposals):
- Calculate monthly ÷ number of units = cost per unit
- This makes it easy for property managers to budget
- Example: $780/month ÷ 100 units = $7.80/unit/month (sounds cheap to them!)

### What to Include in Proposal:
- Weekly common area scooping
- Pet waste station maintenance (restocking bags, emptying bins)
- Monthly detailed report
- Flexible scheduling
- Liability insurance (GET THIS if you don't have it)

### Questions to Ask the Property Manager:
1. How many units on the property?
2. What's your budget for this service?
3. Do you need waste stations maintained or just common areas?
4. Can I use your dumpster?
5. How many dog parks on property?
6. What's the total area to maintain?
