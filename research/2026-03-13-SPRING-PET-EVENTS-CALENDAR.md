## 💬 NETWORKING SCRIPTS

### At Adoption Events (talking to new adopters):
> "Congrats on the new family member! What kind of dog did you get? ... That's awesome. Hey, I run Tidy Tails — we do pet waste cleanup in [their area]. When you get tired of the backyard duty, hit me up. First cleanup is free so you can see how we work."

### At Pet Store Events (Treats Unleashed):
> "Love this place. Hey, I run a local pet waste removal service — Tidy Tails. Any chance you'd let me leave some cards or flyers here? We get asked all the time where to buy quality pet food, so we'd love to send business your way too."

### At Large Festivals (Bark in the Park):
> "Hey! You here with your dog? How many do you have? ... Nice. I'm TJ with Tidy Tails — we come out weekly and handle all the yard cleanup so you never have to deal with it. Here's my card. We're doing a spring special — first cleanup free."

### To Event Organizers (asking about vendor booths):
> "Hey, I love what you all are doing. I run Tidy Tails, a local pet waste removal service. Do you have any vendor spots available? We'd love to support the event and connect with dog owners."
