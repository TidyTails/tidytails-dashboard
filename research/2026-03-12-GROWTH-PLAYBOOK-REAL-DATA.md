## TIER 1: DO THIS WEEK (Free, Immediate Impact)

### 1. 🔥 The "Erica Bomb" — One FB Post That Made $5,000-$6,000

**What Erica did:** Posted before/after yard photos in 47 local buy/sell/trade Facebook groups with a caption offering pet waste removal starting at $30. She left for a conference, and "her purse started vibrating like crazy." Hundreds of messages. $5,000-$6,000 from ONE post.

**What TJ needs to do:**
1. Take before/after photos of your NEXT 2-3 cleanups (phone camera is fine — phone photos actually outperform professional ones on FB)
2. Post the photos in every St. Louis area buy/sell/trade group you can find
3. Caption: "Just cleaned up this yard in [Kirkwood/wherever]. Offering pet waste removal services — free first cleanup for new customers! Starting at $70/month for weekly visits. DM me 🐕"
4. **Time needed:** 1-2 hours total (photos + posting)
5. **Expected result:** Based on Erica's experience in a smaller market, even 10-20 inquiries would be realistic for STL

**Why this works:** Pet waste removal has LOW awareness. People don't know the service exists. When they see a visual transformation, it clicks instantly.

### 2. 📍 Nextdoor — The Sleeper Channel

**Multiple operators confirm:** Nextdoor consistently generates 1-2 new clients per month for free.

**What to do:**
1. Create a Nextdoor Business account (free)
2. Post weekly — not salesy, conversational: "Spring is here and yards are thawing out! If anyone needs help catching up after winter, I offer pet waste removal in [your neighborhoods]. Happy to do a free first cleanup."
3. Respond to any "recommendations" posts asking about yard services
4. **Time needed:** 15 min/week
5. **Expected result:** 1-2 new recurring customers/month (that's 12-24 over a year, FOR FREE)

### 3. ⭐ Google Reviews — Your #1 Trust Signal

**The data:**
- Erica: 376 five-star reviews → dominates her market
- Fresh Start: 175+ five-star reviews → $200k year one
- TJ: ??? (likely <5)

**Every successful operator obsesses over reviews.** This is non-negotiable.

**What to do:**
1. Text every current customer TODAY: "Hey [name], I'm building up my business and Google reviews make a huge difference. Would you mind leaving a quick review? Here's the link: [GBP review link]"
2. After every service going forward, send a review request text
3. **Time needed:** 10 minutes to text 4-5 customers
4. **Target:** 20+ reviews by end of April (ask customers, friends, family who've seen your work)

## TIER 2: DO THIS MONTH ($0-$50 Investment)

### 4. 💰 Raise Your Prices

**The data is clear — you're leaving money on the table:**
- Erica: $99/month average (was $55, incrementally raised)
- Fresh Start: Premium pricing with 487 new customers/month
- Doggie Doodies (your STL competitor): $90/month for ONE monthly visit
- **TJ: $70/month for FOUR weekly visits** — you're the cheapest by far

**Action:** Raise new customer pricing to $85/month (1 dog, weekly). Grandfather existing customers at $70 until they refer someone, then bump to $80. You're providing premium service at budget prices. Stop it.

### 5. 🎁 "Free First Cleanup" Spring Promo

**Every single successful operator uses this.** It's the industry standard for a reason.

- Erica uses it
- Fresh Start uses it
- Swoop Scoop uses it
- All our STL competitors use it (we documented this March 12)
- **TJ doesn't use it yet**

**The math:** A free first cleanup costs you ~30 min of labor. A new recurring customer at $85/month = $1,020/year. That's a 2,000%+ ROI on 30 minutes of work.

**Action:** Add "FREE first cleanup" to every post, every ad, every conversation. This removes the #1 barrier to trying the service.

### 6. 🏠 Yard Signs

**Erica's quote:** "You can see my vehicles from so far away." But before vehicles, she used yard signs at customer homes.

**Action:** Order 25 yard signs ($2-3 each on VistaPrint = ~$50-75). Ask customers if you can leave one in their yard. Every neighbor who walks their dog past will see it.

## TIER 3: INVEST TO ACCELERATE ($100-$500/month)

### 7. 📱 Facebook Ads — The #1 Paid Channel for This Industry

**William Milliken (Swoop Scoop) quote:** "From 2021-2024 we generated over 10,000 pooper scooper customer leads using Facebook ads."

**Why FB > Google for poop scooping:**
- Low search volume on Google (people don't know the service exists to search for it)
- FB puts you in front of dog owners who didn't know they needed you
- FB ads with phone photos outperform professional images

**Starting budget:** $5-10/day ($150-300/month)

**Ad formula that works:**
1. Use a real phone photo (before/after yard, you working, a happy dog)
2. Headline: "Attention Dog Owners in [City]!"
3. Offer: Free first cleanup
4. Target: Dog lovers + your service area ZIP codes
5. Objective: Engagement or Leads (NOT awareness or traffic)

**Expected result:** 2-5 leads per day at $5-10/day spend (based on Swoop Scoop data). At even 20% close rate, that's 2-5 new customers per week.

### 8. 🚗 Vehicle Magnet/Wrap

Multiple operators cite this as their best passive marketing. Start with magnets ($30-50) before committing to a full wrap ($2,000+). Your truck IS a billboard every time you drive through a neighborhood.

## TIER 4: ONCE YOU HIT 15+ CUSTOMERS

### 9. 🏢 Commercial Contracts

**Erica's insight:** "Residential pays the bills, commercial puts money in the bank."

Her first commercial contract: 17-station dog waste system for an HOA = $625/month from ONE client. That's the equivalent of 7+ residential customers.

**Wait until you have a track record** (15+ residential, some reviews), then approach:
- Apartment complexes with dog-friendly policies
- HOAs with common areas
- Vet clinic outdoor areas

### 10. 👷 Hire Your First Scooper

Erica's full-time employee handles 125 houses/week at $18.50/hr. At $85/month per customer, 125 customers = $10,625/month revenue. Even paying $18.50/hr full-time ($3,330/month), that's $7,295/month gross margin from ONE employee.
